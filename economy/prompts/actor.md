# Phase: ACTOR (economy series)

Inputs: `RUN`, `TURN`, `ACTOR`.

You play **<ACTOR>**. Read ONLY: `economy/prompts/_common.md`, `<RUN>/actors/<ACTOR>/brief.md`, `<RUN>/actors/<ACTOR>/journal.md`, `<RUN>/state/public-record.md`, `<RUN>/turns/t<TURN>/intel/<ACTOR>.md`. Reading anything else is cheating.

Write `<RUN>/turns/t<TURN>/orders/<ACTOR>.md` (≤350 words):
1. **Read of the situation** (3 sentences; cite the numbers you were given).
2. **Options considered** (3 one-liners, one unconventional).
3. **Decisions this period** — at most 3 concrete decisions, each: what you do, how big (money, people, pace), and why. Use the decision menu in your brief where it fits; you may go off-menu.
4. **Public statement** (1–2 sentences) and up to 2 **messages** to other players (To / text).
5. **What would change my mind** (1 sentence).

Then append ≤80 words to your journal: what you believe, what you committed to, what you'll watch.

Play your real interests as your brief describes them — not what would be nice for society, unless that is your interest. You don't know when the exercise ends.
