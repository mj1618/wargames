# Journal — entrepreneurs

## T01 (January–December 2027)

Believe the flat frontier and falling AI costs create an opening: emerging niches (AI setup, coaching, care) have real unmet demand. Customers show up for services big firms haven't built yet. Committed to moderate expansion wave (70% people-heavy) into those niches while junior staff hire easy and before the gap gets noticed. Watch: junior wage inflation, credit conditions, how fast the niches fill. Big firms' response will determine whether we can hold margin.
