# Journal — federal-gov

## Round 01 (2027)
Believe: no visible jobs crisis yet (4.1%), but voters distrust AI and rents bite; no fiscal room (6% deficit). Committed: disclosure rule by executive action; housing incentives bill (~0.1% GDP); retraining/wage insurance 0.1% GDP with a 6%-unemployment trigger; no taxes, no limits. Promised labs a quiet year in exchange for clean disclosure. Watching: unemployment vs 5.5%, graduate hiring, layoff headlines, wage-tax receipts, jobs' rank in polling, whether states take the housing money.

## Round 02 (2028)
Believe: economy fine for November (3.9%, rents falling); rent is the voting issue; displacement still invisible in totals. Committed: funded factory-built housing with zoning conditions (~0.25% GDP from 2029); pre-emption plus statutory layoff disclosure; employers' no-mass-layoff pledge; no levy, though labs offered one — kept in reserve. Watching: unemployment vs 5%, wage-tax receipts, graduate hiring, whether the pledge holds, election result, courts on pre-emption.

## Round 03 (2029)
Believe: boom is real (4.0%, GDP +3.1%, income 106) but layoffs 0.71m and receipts slipping; no crisis voters feel. Committed: permanent pre-emption traded for disclosure at 1,000+ firms; build-out permitting bill, no new spending; antitrust inquiry into bundled contracts; no tax, levy held in reserve. Watching: unemployment vs 5%, layoffs vs 1m, receipts shortfall, whether labs unbundle, employer pledge to 2030, local power-bill anger.
