# Journal — households

## Round 01 (2027)
Believe: no crisis yet; real pain is rent and graduate hiring; record profits vs record-low labour share is the grievance. Committed: steady organising drives (logistics, retail, warehouses), no fear-saving, peaceful zoning and safety-net demands, no boycotts. Watch: layoffs of people we know, rent trends, whether the coalition moves on anything, any sign unions are slowing automation.

## Round 02 (2028)
Believe: no crisis; pay is up and rents ease, but hiring of the young and backfill of leavers is the quiet problem. Committed: refocus organising on hiring sectors (care, trades, new firms) with backfill/apprenticeship/profit-share asks; spend normally (slight dip in saving); peaceful election-year demands for housing, working wage insurance, first-job programme, AI profit share; no boycotts. Watch: layoffs among people we know, quality of new-firm jobs, whether entrepreneurs and labs answer our asks.

## Round 03 (2029)
Believe: conditions good (pay +3.4% real, rents -5% locally); hidden cost is unreplaced office leavers and young job-seekers. Committed: renew care/health/trades contracts and extend organising to build-out jobs and new firms (backfill, apprenticeships, profit share); spend normally; peaceful pushes on zoning, wage insurance access, first-job programme, AI profit share; no boycotts. Watch: layoffs among people we know, unemployment above 5%, rents, whether labs sign backfill terms.

## Round 04 (2030)
Believe: boom conditions (pay +3.5% real, rents -3%, unemployment 3.1%); risks are unreplaced office leavers and automation of fashionable new job categories. Committed: renew/extend organising incl. build-out and new firms (backfill, notice, profit share, transition accounts); channel young and displaced into the ~90k entrepreneur apprenticeship roles; spend normally; peaceful demands, back antitrust inquiry; no boycotts. Watch: Q2 role postings, layoffs among people we know, unemployment above 5%, rents.
