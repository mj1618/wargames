# Intel — employers — round 01

**Period: January–December 2027.** Decide for the whole year.

**National numbers (end 2026):** unemployment 4.1%; participation 61.6%; 162.4m jobs; labour share of income 52.8% (record low); profits 14.9% of GDP (record); poverty 12.9%; homelessness about 22 per 10,000; federal deficit 6.0% of GDP, debt about 100%. Median household income, GDP and prices: index 100.

**Politics:** a growth-first coalition leads the federal government (pro-deployment, tax-averse, sceptical of new entitlements). The big states are resisting new housing; none is overriding local zoning.

**This year's news:** a flat year at the frontier — the new models are cheaper, not much smarter.

**Only you see this:** Your own books: the tools handle routine drafting, coding and claims work well and botch judgement calls. Headcount plans for 2027 are flat, with entry-level hiring cut. One of your insurers passed its automation saving into a 10% price cut in a pilot region and sold roughly 15% more policies there. Competitors are announcing AI programmes every week.

**Messages to you:** none.
