# Orders — ai-firms — round 01 (Jan–Dec 2027)

## Read of the situation
A flat frontier year is a demand year: three-quarters of enterprise use hands over whole tasks and spending still grows fast, so the money is in distribution. Pricing power is gone — Meridian's cut was matched within weeks by Helix, Lumen and open weights — so margin means lock-in. We are protected (growth-first coalition, 4.1% unemployment, profits a record 14.9% of GDP) but exposed: labour share a record-low 52.8%, two-thirds of the public want us slowed, customers hiring fewer juniors.

## Options considered
- Race flat-out plus price war: share up, margins thin, backlash fed.
- Steady pace, lock-in pricing, cheap political cover.
- Unconventional: offer a modest profits levy now for federal pre-emption of state rules.

## Decisions this period
1. **`deploy_pace`: race, +15% above baseline.** With a flat frontier the race is for enterprise seats. Not +30%: power is constrained and visible scale-up feeds moratorium talk.
2. **`ai_pricing`: lock-in** — bundled agent platforms, multi-year contracts, volume commitments. Token prices fall regardless; integration keeps savings with us and big customers. A price war only feeds open-weight rivals.
3. **Politics: $150m/yr lobbying and campaign money; joint $2bn/yr voluntary Worker Transition Fund** (retraining exposed juniors), framed as "no mandate needed". Aim: keep the growth-first coalition through 2028, pre-empt an AI-profits tax. Labs split on a levy; majority keeps it in reserve.

## Public statement
"This is the year AI gets cheaper for every American business. The labs jointly commit $2 billion a year to move workers into the roles AI creates — no new rules required."

## Messages
- **To: federal government.** We back your deployment agenda publicly and want federal pre-emption before 2028. If a profits levy is the price, better negotiated with you now than the opposition later.
- **To: employers.** Multi-year agreements at 2027 pricing, agents bundled; sign before the next model generation.

## What would change my mind
A moratorium or licensing bill nearing a majority, or an unemployment rise pinned on us, would move us to voluntary slow-down and a serious levy offer.
