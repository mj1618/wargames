# Orders — entrepreneurs — round 01

## Read of the situation

The frontier is flat but our input costs are falling every quarter, and customers are showing up for work nobody was doing two years ago. AI-setup and fix-it services, coaching, in-person care — these niches are hiring faster than they can find staff. Credit is normal and junior hiring is easy. This is a window to seize demand before the big firms notice.

## Options considered

1. **Conservative**: Hold hiring flat, let growth happen organically. Lower risk, leave money on the table.
2. **Expand aggressively into AI-only firms**: Maximize profit per founder with light staff and fleets of agents; scale fast and undercut incumbents.
3. **Aggressive people-heavy play into the hot niches**: Build services for the AI setup, coaching and care demand surge while staff is findable and demand is unmet.

## Decisions this period

**Decision 1: Expand new firm formation — moderate wave, +12% job creation above normal.** We're expanding into the hot niches. Cheaper AI means one person can do more; we're hiring junior staff while we can. This positions us for the profit from solving a real shortage.

**Decision 2: 70% people-heavy (AI setup, coaching, care services), 30% AI-only firms.** The people-heavy niches have unmet demand and higher customer willingness to pay for trust and human judgment. AI-only is efficient but leaves margins on the table. We split the bet.

**Decision 3: Push sectoral focus into AI services (setup, monitoring, fix), personal coaching, and health/care services.** These are the niches showing hiring pressure and customer willingness to pay. Factory-built housing is too capital-heavy and zoning-constrained for our move right now.

## Public statement

"Cheap AI and new customer demand are colliding. We're building the people-heavy services that matter — setting up systems, coaching, care. Hiring junior staff now while we can; the shortage for skilled people solving real problems will last."

## Messages

*To: Workers*  
"If you can fix things, teach people, or care for someone: there's hiring happening. Entry to advanced roles coming open as founders get serious about scaling."

*To: Policymakers*  
"Zoning and licensing in health and housing are still the walls. Remove them. The builders and carers are ready to move if you let us."

## What would change my mind

A sudden spike in junior wage inflation or credit drying up would force an immediate pullback to AI-only and cash conservation.
