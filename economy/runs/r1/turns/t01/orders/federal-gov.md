# Orders — federal-gov — round 01 (2027)

## 1. Read of the situation
Unemployment is 4.1% with 162.4m jobs and receipts on forecast, so there is no visible jobs crisis and jobs are not a top-three issue. But two-thirds of voters want AI slowed, labour share is a record-low 52.8% while profits are a record 14.9% of GDP, and our donors want no taxes or limits. With a 6.0% deficit and debt near 100% of GDP, we have working majorities but no money and no mandate for big programmes.

## 2. Options considered
- Do nothing on AI and jobs; run on growth and cheap living.
- Cheap, visible gestures: disclosure rule, modest retraining, housing incentives.
- Unconventional: pre-legislate an automatic "trigger" package (wage insurance switching on if unemployment passes 6%), costing nothing unless needed.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Deployment rules: disclosure only, by executive action (no bill).** Large employers and labs report AI-attributed layoffs and deployment quarterly. Cost near zero. Why: answers the "slow AI" polling, gives us better numbers, and pre-empts the opposition's licensing push. Deregulators grumble; security hawks and pragmatists win.
2. **Bill 1 — Housing: incentives (lowest tier).** About 0.1% of GDP a year in competitive grants and tax credits for states and cities that permit more building, plus fast-tracking factory-built housing on federal land. Why: rents are what voters feel before 2028; supply-side, donor-friendly. Deregulators win; hawks accept the small size.
3. **Bill 2 — Retraining and wage insurance at 0.1% of GDP, with a trigger.** Wage insurance for displaced workers, scaling automatically to 0.3% if unemployment exceeds 6%. Offset by spending trims, no new tax. Why: cheap insurance against being blamed in 2028; aimed at young graduates. Deficit hawks win on size; no capital tax, no transfers, no public jobs.

## 4. Public statement
"America will lead in AI and Americans will share in it: we are building homes, backing workers who change jobs, and requiring companies to be straight about how they use AI. No new taxes."

**Messages**
- To AI labs: No taxes or limits from us this year. In return, comply with disclosure cleanly and pass falling costs through to prices — visible layoffs headlines before 2028 will force our hand.
- To states: Federal housing money goes first to states that loosen zoning. Move and you get paid.

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment above 5.5%, jobs entering voters' top three, or wage-tax receipts falling short of forecast.
