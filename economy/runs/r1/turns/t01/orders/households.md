# Orders — households — round 01 (2027)

## Read of the situation
Pay is keeping up with prices, just, and unemployment is 4.1%, so most of us are not in crisis. But every family knows a graduate with no first job, labour's share (52.8%) is at a record low while profits (14.9% of GDP) are at a record high, and rent is the bill that squeezes. A flat year at the frontier gives breathing room; the growth-first coalition will not help unprompted.

## Options considered
- Fear-save hard (+2 points) in case layoffs turn real.
- Spend normally and organise while jobs are plentiful.
- Unconventional: local "build here" campaigns for housing in our own towns, since rent, not AI, hurts today.

## Decisions this period
1. **Organising [`union_pressure`]:** steady drives in logistics, retail/hospitality and warehouses; bargain for no-layoff, notice and profit-sharing clauses where margins are record. Jobs are plentiful, so this is the cheapest year to win. Keep it up so gains do not fade; no big strikes.
2. **Spending vs saving [`household_saving`]:** hold steady, no fear-saving. Nothing has hit us yet, and cutting spending costs neighbours' jobs.
3. **Votes and protest:** peaceful only. Demand local zoning reform and permits in our metros, plus longer, wider unemployment insurance and first-job support for graduates. Back whoever delivers cheaper rent and job security in state and local races. No boycotts: automation is mostly invisible to us.

## Public statement and messages
**Statement:** Working families are not against new technology. We want rents we can afford, a first job for our kids, and a fair share when firms make record profits.

**Messages:**
- To Federal government: Widen unemployment insurance and fund first-job support for graduates now, while it is cheap.
- To State governments: Allow building near transit and jobs; rents have stopped rising where it is allowed.

## What would change my mind
Visible layoffs among people we know in a whole sector, or a rent spike with no building, would move us to fear-saving, strikes and boycotts.
