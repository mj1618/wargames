# Intel — households — round 02

**Period: January–December 2028.** Decide for the whole year.

**National numbers (end 2027, provisional):** unemployment 3.9%; participation 61.6%; 163.0m jobs (+0.6m); GDP +2.1%; median household income index about 100.5; housing costs down about 2%, other prices flat; labour share 52.4%; deficit 6.0% of GDP, debt 102%. Announced layoffs about 0.46m. Poverty and homelessness for 2027 not yet published.

**This year's news:** housing breakthrough — factory-built homes win code approval in most states; costs per home fall sharply. Last year: Congress passed housing incentives and wage insurance (both start now); a court blocked the AI-layoff disclosure order. Federal elections are in November.

**Only you see this:** Your corner: pay rose about 1.6% faster than prices. Rent eased where building is allowed. Few people you know were laid off, but leavers are not replaced and graduates still struggle for a first job. The organising drives won little.
