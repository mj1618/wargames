# Orders — ai-firms — Round 02 (2028)

## 1. Read of the situation
Macro is benign for us: unemployment 3.9%, 163.0m jobs (+0.6m), GDP +2.1%, labour share 52.4% — nobody can pin a crisis on AI yet. Our data tells the real story: systems do twice last year's share of desk work, bundles sign well, and the only soft spot is small customers eyeing open weights. Election year: the government offers no taxes or limits for clean disclosure and visible pass-through, priced at no layoff headline before November.

## 2. Options considered
- Steady pace, hold pricing, bank the bundles: safe, but cedes the low end to open weights and the frontier to rivals.
- Race, cut routine-tier prices against open weights, lock enterprise into 3-year bundles with indemnity.
- Unconventional: offer a profits levy now for statutory pre-emption before the election. Majority rejects — worth more as a chip after November.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Pace: race, +20% above baseline.** Desk-work share is doubling; whoever scales enterprise deployment this year owns the installed base. Compute-bound, so enterprise before consumer.
2. **Pricing: two-tier.** Enterprise: 3-year premium bundles with capped error indemnity (up to annual contract value) as the lock-in sweetener employers asked for. Routine-task tier: list prices cut ~40% — denies open weights a foothold and visibly meets the pass-through ask.
3. **Politics: take the deal.** Voluntary quarterly workforce-impact data on our own definitions despite the court win; lobbying $200m, split across both coalitions as an election hedge; Worker Transition Fund stays $2bn/yr, $500m front-loaded into visible retraining in AI-exposed metros before November.

## 4. Public statement
"Routine AI work costs 40% less this year, and we will publish quarterly data on how our customers' workforces change — nobody has to force us."

Messages:
- **To federal-gov:** Accepted: 40% routine-tier cut and voluntary quarterly disclosure this year. We hold you to no taxes, no limits, and want state pre-emption drafted for 2029.
- **To employers:** Yes to 3-year routine-task pricing and capped indemnity. In return: shrink by attrition, no mass-layoff announcements before November.

## 5. What would change my mind
A lab breaking ranks on price, or a moratorium bill polling well enough that the levy must be spent early.
