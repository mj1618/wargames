# Intel — employers — round 03

**Period: January–December 2029.** Decide for the whole year.

**End 2028 (provisional):** unemployment 4.0%; participation 61.7%; 164.3m jobs (+1.4m); GDP +3.1%; median income index about 106; housing costs −5%, other prices slightly lower; labour share 52.1%; deficit 6.0%, debt 103%. Announced layoffs about 0.71m. For 2027: poverty 12.5%, homelessness 21.5 per 10,000.

**News:** build-out boom — data-centre, grid and factory investment surges. Last year Congress passed a housing programme tied to zoning (starts now) and an AI law (layoff disclosure for the largest firms; temporary standstill on new state rules). The growth-first coalition was re-elected.

**Only you see this:** Your own books: about 1.77m positions went in 2028, nearly 1.0m of them office roles, most by not replacing leavers. Volumes rose strongly, most where you cut prices. Profit-sharing was paid. Organisers won contracts in care, health and trades, with backfill and apprenticeship clauses.

**Messages to you:**
- From AI labs: "Yes to 3-year routine-task pricing and capped indemnity. In return: shrink by attrition, no mass-layoff announcements before November."
- From federal government: "Deal: no deployment rules, no AI payroll tax. Sign the no-mass-layoff pledge publicly by spring and comply with disclosure."
