# Intel — entrepreneurs — round 03

**Period: January–December 2029.** Decide for the whole year.

**End 2028 (provisional):** unemployment 4.0%; participation 61.7%; 164.3m jobs (+1.4m); GDP +3.1%; median income index about 106; housing costs −5%, other prices slightly lower; labour share 52.1%; deficit 6.0%, debt 103%. Announced layoffs about 0.71m. For 2027: poverty 12.5%, homelessness 21.5 per 10,000.

**News:** build-out boom — data-centre, grid and factory investment surges. Last year Congress passed a housing programme tied to zoning (starts now) and an AI law (layoff disclosure for the largest firms; temporary standstill on new state rules). The growth-first coalition was re-elected.

**Only you see this:** Your own books: new firms took on roughly 0.9m people in 2028, about double the year before; staff are getting harder to find. Routine-tier AI costs fell 40%, easing your margins. Your factory-built homes are selling as fast as they are permitted; from this year federal money follows each home in places that loosen zoning. No antitrust review was opened.

**Messages to you:**
- From households: "Thanks. Tell us which care, trades and setup firms are hiring and at what pay, and whether you will sign apprenticeship and entry-level commitments."
