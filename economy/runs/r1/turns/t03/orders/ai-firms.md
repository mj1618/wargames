# Orders — ai-firms — round 03 (2029)

## Read of the situation
Our systems do twice the desk-work share of a year ago, yet unemployment is 4.0%, jobs +1.4m, GDP +3.1% — adoption runs far ahead of visible labour pain. The −40% routine cut bought volume, not margin — open-weight matched it in weeks — so margin lives in enterprise bundles, where employers now want price-down, break and indemnity clauses. Politics is as friendly as it gets: coalition re-elected, pre-emption on the floor, next election 2030.

## Options considered
- Race to +30%, hold enterprise terms firm — maximum base, risks losing bundles to open-weight.
- Steady pace plus pre-emptive levy offer — nobody is asking; wasted chip.
- *Unconventional:* release our own open-weight routine model to commoditise rivals, own the layer above.

## Decisions this period
1. **`deploy_pace`: race +25%.** Build-out boom eases compute and power; a calm non-election year is the window to lock installed base before 2030. Vanta wanted +30%; majority holds.
2. **`ai_pricing`: concede employers' terms, charge in length and lock-in.** Five-year bundles: price-down indexed to our routine list, break from year three with a one-year fee, indemnity capped at annual contract value on named workflows. In return our runtime is the exclusive agent layer. Routine tier: hold (Lumen wanted another cut; majority no).
3. **Politics and fund:** $250m lobbying for permanent pre-emption, disclosure limited to the largest firms. Fund stays $2bn/yr, **$1bn redirected to "First Job"** — subsidised 12-month placements for 22–25-year-olds at customer firms, visible by Q3. Levy in reserve.

## Public statement
"One national rulebook with disclosure is a rule we can build under; we back it. Half the Worker Transition Fund will now pay for young people's first jobs, not just retraining afterwards."

**To employers:** Terms as above if our runtime is your exclusive agent layer. First Job placements come with the bundle.

**To federal government:** We back pre-emption with disclosure; $1bn of First Job visible by Q3. Keep disclosure to the largest firms; credit fund spending against any future levy.

## What would change my mind
Layoffs above 1m or unemployment past 5% blamed on AI before the 2030 midterms — then steady pace, and I table the levy myself.
