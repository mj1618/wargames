# Orders — federal-gov — round 03 (2029)

## 1. Read of the situation
We were re-elected on a boom: unemployment 4.0%, 164.3m jobs (+1.4m), GDP +3.1%, median income index about 106, housing costs −5%. The warning lights are quiet ones: announced layoffs rose from 0.46m to 0.71m, labour share slipped to 52.1%, and receipts are a touch under forecast with the deficit stuck at 6.0% and debt at 103%. Nothing voters feel yet justifies taxes or limits, but the revenue base is eroding under us.

## 2. Options considered
- Coast: implement housing, pass nothing, bank the boom.
- Lock in the deployment regime: permanent pre-emption traded for wider disclosure, plus a build-out permitting bill.
- Unconventional: take the labs' earlier levy offer now, while they are rich and we are safe, as a small build-out fee earmarked to deficit reduction.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Bill 1 — Permanent AI framework.** Convert the standstill into permanent pre-emption of state AI deployment rules; in exchange extend layoff disclosure from the largest firms to all firms over 1,000 staff, quarterly. `deploy_regulation` stays at disclosure. Deregulators win; labs get their ask, we get the data.
2. **Bill 2 — Build-out and grid permitting.** Fast-track federal permits for data centres, transmission and factory-built housing plants; no new spending; ratepayer protection so local bills do not rise. Security hawks and deficit hawks win. Housing programme (~0.25% GDP) runs as legislated.
3. **Executive action — competition cases.** Open an antitrust inquiry into multi-year bundled enterprise contracts and small-firm access to frontier models (`competition_policy`: cases only). No taxes, no levy, no new transfers this year. Deregulators split; the pro-competition wing wins.

## 4. Public statement
"America is building again: more jobs, higher incomes, cheaper homes. We will keep AI rules national, permits fast and markets open to every founder."

**Messages**
- To AI labs: "Permanent pre-emption is drafted. Price: disclosure widened to 1,000+ firms and unbundled access terms for small firms. Settle that voluntarily and the inquiry stays an inquiry."
- To Employers: "No profit tax, no mandate this year. Keep the pledge through November 2030; put your shorter-week support in writing."

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment above 5%, announced layoffs above 1m, or wage-tax receipts more than 0.3% of GDP under forecast would put the levy and wage insurance expansion on the table.
