# Orders — households — round 03 (2029)

## Read of the situation
Life is getting a bit easier for most of us: pay is up about 3.4% above prices, rents are down about 5% where factory-built homes are going up, and unemployment sits at 4.0% with 1.4m more jobs. But labour share is still 52.1% and profits are huge, and office leavers still are not replaced, so young people and mid-career office workers carry the hidden cost. Our organising wins in care, health and trades fade unless renewed, and the build-out boom is a chance to lock in terms while jobs are plentiful.

## Options considered
1. Keep the 2028 course: renew contracts, spend normally, push peaceful demands.
2. Go harder: strikes and boycotts of AI services while the boom gives us leverage.
3. Unconventional: bargain for local "backfill pools" and shared apprenticeship funds, plus profit-share clauses at data-centre and grid builders.

## Decisions this period
1. **Renew and extend organising (union_pressure, steady-to-up).** Keep and renew the care, health and trades contracts, and push into build-out jobs (data-centre construction, grid, factory fit-out) and new firms. Asks: backfill of leavers, apprenticeship slots, profit-sharing, shorter-week top-ups. Jobs are plentiful, so this is the best moment; a lapse loses what we won.
2. **Keep spending normally (household_saving: no fear-saving, hold at slight dip).** Nobody we know is being laid off in droves, and cutting back would only cost our neighbours' jobs. We save only a little extra in office-heavy households that are nervous.
3. **Peaceful political push, no boycotts.** Use the new housing programme to press local governments to actually permit building, demand wage insurance be made easier to access, a first-job programme for under-25s, and an AI profit share or dividend. Backing the worker-first coalition at state level and showing up at zoning hearings. Boycotts held in reserve: conditions are good and it would cost the care and trades jobs we are winning.

## Public statement and messages
**Statement:** Pay and rent are finally moving our way; we want that made permanent with backfill, apprenticeships and a share of AI profits, not left to the next downturn.
**To: ai-firms** — Your Worker Transition Fund and no-layoff pledge are fine, but sign backfill and apprenticeship terms into contracts, and share profits with the people whose work trained your systems.
**To: federal-gov** — Make wage insurance easier to claim and fund a first-job programme for under-25s now, while the budget and jobs are healthy.

## What would change my mind
Layoffs among people we know, a jump in unemployment past 5%, or rents rising again would push us toward saving more, striking and boycotting.
