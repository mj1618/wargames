# Intel — ai-firms — round 04

**Period: January–December 2030.**

**End 2029 (provisional):** unemployment 3.1%; 166.8m jobs (+2.5m); GDP +4.3%; median income index about 109; housing costs −3%; labour share 52.1%; deficit 5.8%, debt 103%. Announced layoffs about 0.95m. For 2028: poverty 11.7%, homelessness 18.7 per 10,000.

**News:** new-work mirage — last year's fashionable new job category is itself being automated. In 2029 Congress made pre-emption permanent, widened layoff disclosure to firms over 1,000 staff and fast-tracked permits; an antitrust inquiry into bundled AI contracts opened.

**Only you see this:** Your systems can do noticeably more desk work than a year ago; customers use well under half of what the systems can do. Most large customers signed the five-year exclusive bundles. The antitrust inquiry has asked for your contract terms and small-firm access prices.

**Messages to you:**
- From federal government: "Permanent pre-emption is drafted. Price: disclosure widened to 1,000+ firms and unbundled access terms for small firms. Settle that voluntarily and the inquiry stays an inquiry."
- From households: "Your Worker Transition Fund and no-layoff pledge are fine, but sign backfill and apprenticeship terms into contracts, and share profits with the people whose work trained your systems."
