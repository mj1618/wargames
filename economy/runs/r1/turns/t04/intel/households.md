# Intel — households — round 04

**Period: January–December 2030.** Decide for the whole year.

**End 2029 (provisional):** unemployment 3.1%; 166.8m jobs (+2.5m); GDP +4.3%; median income index about 109; housing costs −3%; labour share 52.1%; deficit 5.8%, debt 103%. Announced layoffs about 0.95m. For 2028: poverty 11.7%, homelessness 18.7 per 10,000.

**News:** new-work mirage — last year's fashionable new job category is itself being automated. In 2029 Congress made pre-emption permanent, widened layoff disclosure to firms over 1,000 staff and fast-tracked permits; an antitrust inquiry into bundled AI contracts opened.

**Only you see this:** Your corner: pay rose 3.5% faster than prices; rent fell about 3% more. Work is easy to find. The organising drive worked again, now covering build-out sites; it needs renewing to hold. Office leavers are still not replaced, and people who retrained into AI setup and coaching hear those jobs may not last. Wage insurance was not changed; no federal first-job programme, though the labs' own placements began.
