# Orders — ai-firms — round 04 (2030)

## Read of the situation
Unemployment 3.1%, 166.8m jobs (+2.5m), GDP +4.3%: a boom, so a midterm backlash is unlikely. Most large customers are on five-year exclusive bundles, so the installed base is locked; the constraint is adoption depth — customers use well under half of what the systems can do. The antitrust inquiry is the one credible legal threat, priced at small-firm unbundling.

## Options considered
- Fight the inquiry — risks a case that reopens the exclusives.
- Settle on small-firm terms, keep the enterprise runtime — concede the commoditised tier, protect the margin.
- Unconventional: offer a profits levy for a statutory safe harbour on bundles — too dear while nobody asks.

## Decisions this period
1. **Settle the inquiry voluntarily.** Publish standard terms and an unbundled small-firm tier: per-seat, no exclusivity, 12-month maximum, at routine-tier prices (already −40%). Five-year bundles grandfathered. Why: the small tier earns little; the exclusives earn most of the margin.
2. **Pace: race +10%** (down from +25%); a third of planned capex growth goes to deployment and integration teams, plus usage-based expansion pricing inside bundles. Why: capability outruns usage; revenue comes from pulling customers up the curve. Foreign rivals stop us going slower.
3. **Politics.** Lobbying $300m for the midterm, both coalitions: defend pre-emption and the settlement, oppose any contract-mandate or levy bill. Fund $2bn/yr (First Job $1bn). A 5% discount for customers who sign backfill/apprenticeship ratios — cheap, pre-empts a mandate. No profit-sharing with training-data contributors. Levy in reserve.

## Public statement
"We are opening our terms to every business in America, large or small, and backing young workers into first jobs. Open access and open books — no mandate required."

## Messages
- **To federal government:** "Accepted. Standard terms and a small-firm unbundled tier published within 90 days; existing contracts grandfathered. In return the inquiry closes as an inquiry and pre-emption stands."
- **To households:** "Apprenticeship and backfill terms go into contracts as a priced option; First Job stays at $1bn. Profit-sharing for training data, no."

## What would change my mind
An open-weight provider winning enterprise renewals on unbundled terms would make the exclusives a liability; I would unbundle first.
