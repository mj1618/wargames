# Orders — federal-gov — round 04 (2030)

## 1. Read of the situation
We enter the November midterms on a boom: unemployment 3.1%, jobs +2.5m, GDP +4.3%, income index about 109, housing costs −3%, deficit 5.8%. The quiet warnings are louder: announced layoffs about 0.95m, labour share stuck at 52.1%, and last year's new job category is itself being automated. Big states still have not loosened zoning.

## 2. Options considered
- Coast to November and pass nothing.
- Buy cheap insurance: a small first-job and wage-insurance bill; steer housing money to states that build.
- Unconventional: a pre-election "boom dividend" cheque funded by a lab levy.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Bill 1 — First Job and wage insurance.** Raise `retraining_pct_gdp` from 0.1% to 0.2% from 2031: an under-25 first-job programme matching the labs' fund, simpler wage-insurance claims, trigger lowered from 6% to 5% unemployment. No new tax; lab fund spending credited against any future levy. Security hawks and deregulators beat deficit hawks: small, visible before November, answers households.
2. **Bill 2 — Build-where-welcome housing.** No new money. Funds unclaimed by non-reforming states are reallocated yearly to states and cities that permit; federal land released for factory-built homes. `housing_policy` unchanged. Deregulators win.
3. **Executive — competition.** Keep the bundling inquiry open with a settlement offer: unbundled small-firm access and exit rights from five-year exclusives. `competition_policy`: cases. No deployment rules, no payroll or profit tax.

## 4. Public statement
"Record jobs, rising pay, cheaper homes. Now every young American gets a first job in the AI economy, and states that build get the money."

**Messages**
- To AI labs: "Fund credit against a future levy is in the bill. In return: $1bn of First Job visible by Q3 and unbundled small-firm terms, or the inquiry becomes a case."
- To Employers: "No deployment rules, no AI payroll tax in 2030. Sign by spring; hold layoffs under last year's through November."

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment above 5%, layoffs well above 1m, losing a chamber, or receipts 0.3% of GDP under forecast would bring the levy and larger transfers forward.
