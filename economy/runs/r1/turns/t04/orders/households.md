# Orders — households — round 04 (2030)

## Read of the situation
Life is good for most of us: pay is up 3.5% over prices, rents fell another 3%, unemployment is 3.1% with 2.5m more jobs, and work is easy to find. But labour share is stuck at 52.1%, office leavers are still not replaced, and retrainees in AI setup and coaching hear their jobs are already being automated. Our organising wins, now including build-out sites, fade unless renewed, and tight labour markets are when leverage is greatest.

## Options considered
1. Hold course: renew and extend organising, spend normally, press peacefully.
2. Go hard: strikes and boycotts of AI services while employers are short of staff.
3. Unconventional: bargain for portable "re-skilling and transition accounts" funded per worker by employers, so new-economy jobs that get automated don't leave retrainees empty-handed.

## Decisions this period
1. **Renew and extend organising (union_pressure, steady-to-up).** Re-sign care, health, trades and build-out contracts and push into new firms. Asks: backfill of leavers, no-layoff and notice clauses, profit-share, and portable transition accounts. Staff shortages make this the best bargaining window we will get.
2. **Take up the entrepreneurs' apprenticeship offer and spend normally (household_saving: hold at slight dip).** Steer young people and displaced office workers into the roughly 90k care, trades and housing-construction entry roles ($18-24/hr) via union hiring halls and community groups, and pressure firms to publish roles by Q2. Nobody we know is being laid off in droves, and saving more would only cost neighbours' jobs. Office-heavy households that feel nervous save a little extra.
3. **Peaceful political push, no boycotts.** Use the 2030 cycle to demand easier wage-insurance access, a federal first-job programme for under-25s, and an AI profit dividend. Turn up at zoning hearings to hold the rent gains. Back the antitrust inquiry into bundled AI contracts, since lock-in is how the gains stay with the labs. Boycotts stay in reserve: they would cost the care and trades jobs we are winning.

## Public statement and messages
**Statement:** Pay and rent are moving our way; make it last with backfill, real apprenticeships and a share of AI profits before the next downturn.
**To: entrepreneurs** — We will send people to your care, trades and housing roles; publish them by Q2 with pay ladders and training time, and honour union hiring-hall referrals.
**To: federal-gov** — Fund a first-job programme and ease wage-insurance access now, while jobs and the budget are healthy.

## What would change my mind
Layoffs among people we know, unemployment rising past 5%, or rents turning up again would push us toward saving more, striking and boycotting.
