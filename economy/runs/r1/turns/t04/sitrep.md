# Sitrep — round 04 (January–December 2030)

**Headline: the boom ends — 2.2m fewer people in work, pay flat, growth-first holds Congress with thinner margins.**

**Jobs.** About 5.74m jobs went: 2.37m to automation, 3.17m because last year's investment surge stopped and the central bank tightened. 2.92m were created. Employment fell to 164.7m. Unemployment 3.69% (3.23%); employment rate 58.98% (59.99%) — most leavers left the labour force, not the dole queue. Machines do 14.1% of desk work, of 33.3% possible.

**Pay and prices.** Real pay +0.2%; hours shorter. Median real household income 108.4 (109.3); bottom fifth 108.2. Housing costs fell another 3%. Labour's share dropped from 52.1% to 50.4%; top-1% share 21.1%.

**Business.** GDP +1.5%. Labs slowed their race, opened an unbundled small-firm tier and kept the big exclusive contracts. Employers kept automating by attrition and raised profit-sharing. Half of new start-ups were AI-only; new firms hired about 1.5m.

**Government.** Congress passed a first-job and wage-insurance bill (from 2031) and redirected unclaimed housing money to places that build. Deficit 6.0%, debt 105%. Poverty 11.0%; homelessness 16.7 per 10,000.

**What mattered most.** The end of the build-out boom (+1.5% of spending gone) plus the central bank's squeeze. The "mirage" event cut new work by 30% just as entrepreneurs pivoted away from hiring. The election roll (20% to lose) kept the coalition; Congress became divided.

**What a household would have noticed:** rent kept falling, but the raises stopped and the office job a neighbour left was never advertised again.
