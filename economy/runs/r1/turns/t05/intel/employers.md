# Intel — employers — round 05

**Period: January 2031–December 2032.** One set of decisions for both years. Elections November 2032.

**End 2030 (provisional):** unemployment 3.8%; 164.7m jobs (−2.2m); GDP +1.5%; median income index about 109; housing costs −3%; labour share 50.4%; deficit 6.0%, debt 105%. Announced layoffs about 2.3m. For 2029: poverty 10.9%, homelessness 16.9 per 10,000.

**News:** open-weight price war — free models match last year's best; AI service prices are collapsing. Congress passed a first-job and wage-insurance bill (from 2031); growth-first kept Congress, narrowly.

**Only you see this:** Your own books: about 5.7m positions went in 2030 (2.2m office, 1.4m retail and hospitality), many because orders fell, mostly by attrition. Pay rose 0.2% faster than prices; vacancies fill easily again. Margins widened. Organisers won again. Your no-mass-layoff pledge ran out in November.

**Messages to you:**
- From federal government: "No deployment rules, no AI payroll tax in 2030. Sign by spring; hold layoffs under last year's through November."
- From entrepreneurs: "You're seeing the same wage math. Don't outbid each other; your business model doesn't depend on it."
