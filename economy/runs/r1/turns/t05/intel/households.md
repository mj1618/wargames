# Intel — households — round 05

**Period: January 2031–December 2032.** One set of decisions for both years. Elections November 2032.

**End 2030 (provisional):** unemployment 3.8%; 164.7m jobs (−2.2m); GDP +1.5%; median income index about 109; housing costs −3%; labour share 50.4%; deficit 6.0%, debt 105%. Announced layoffs about 2.3m. For 2029: poverty 10.9%, homelessness 16.9 per 10,000.

**News:** open-weight price war — free models match last year's best; AI service prices are collapsing. Congress passed a first-job and wage-insurance bill (from 2031); growth-first kept Congress, narrowly.

**Only you see this:** Your corner: pay rose only 0.2% faster than prices; rent fell about 3% more. Work is harder to find: build-out sites, shops and restaurants cut back, and many who left jobs stopped looking. Some AI setup and coaching jobs vanished. The organising drive worked again and profit-sharing rose; it needs renewing to hold. The first-job programme you asked for starts in 2031.

**Messages to you:**
- From AI labs: "Apprenticeship and backfill terms go into contracts as a priced option; First Job stays at $1bn. Profit-sharing for training data, no."
- From entrepreneurs: "We're shifting where jobs go. Organise AI-only startup teams if you want a seat at the table."
