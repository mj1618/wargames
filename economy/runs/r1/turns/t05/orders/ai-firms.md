# Orders — ai-firms — round 05 (2031–2032)

## Read of the situation
Free open-weight models match last year's best and prices are collapsing: the routine tier is worth nothing, small firms are gone, and our only moat is the gap between what our systems can do and the well-under-half customers use. Employers accept the year-three break but refuse exclusivity; with the inquiry open, exclusive runtime is dead regardless. Jobs fell 2.2m, layoffs hit 2.3m, labour share is 50.4%, growth-first holds Congress narrowly before November 2032 — the cheap window for a settlement is now.

## Options considered
- Fight for exclusivity through the inquiry: lose slowly, hand worker-first a villain.
- Full price war on open-weight: burns margin on a tier already free.
- Unconventional: propose a statutory levy ourselves while the friendly coalition still writes it.

## Decisions this period
1. **Deploy pace: race +20%.** Distance from free models is the product; capex into frontier training plus deployment teams converting unused capability into billed work.
2. **Pricing: drop exclusivity, move up-stack.** Accept year-three break and multi-sourcing; exclusives become non-exclusive at renewal. Routine tier free. Revenue shifts to usage-priced agentic tiers and outcome contracts.
3. **Politics: take the government's deal and extend it.** $1bn First Job by Q3 2031; small-firm unbundled terms binding; inquiry closes by consent decree. Labs split 3–1; majority also offers a 3% fund-creditable levy on AI revenue for a statutory guarantee of no deployment limits or licensing. Lobbying $350m for 2032, 60/40 toward growth-first.

## Public statement
"We are ending exclusive contracts, making our basic tier free for every business, and funding first jobs for 100,000 young workers. We will pay a fair levy if Congress guarantees the freedom to build."

**To federal government:** First Job $1bn by Q3 and binding small-firm terms, agreed. Close the inquiry by consent decree and we publicly back a 3% fund-creditable levy tied to a statutory no-deployment-limits guarantee before November 2032.

**To employers:** Exclusivity is off; year-three break accepted. Agentic tiers priced per completed task; 15% discount for First Job participants.

## What would change my mind
A moratorium or licensing bill reaching a committee vote: we slow to steady and raise the levy offer rather than lose the right to deploy.
