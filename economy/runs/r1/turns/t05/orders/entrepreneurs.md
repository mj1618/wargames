# Orders — entrepreneurs — turn 05

## Read of the situation

End 2030: 164.7m jobs (down 2.2m), unemployment 3.8%. The wage spiral broke—wages rose only 0.2% faster than prices and staff are now easy to find. Free open-weight models have collapsed our AI input costs and service prices are crashing, fundamentally shifting the economics of AI-only firms from marginal to highly profitable.

New firm hiring totalled 1.5m in 2030 (down from 1.6m in 2029), showing momentum is slowing. Housing sells as fast as permitted where zoning allows; unclaimed federal housing funds now redirect to permitting states. The antitrust inquiry remains open, giving leverage to smaller players pushing for access parity.

## Options considered

1. **Aggressive scale of AI-only startups** — free models mean near-zero marginal cost and margin expansion; the window is open now.
2. **Pivot back toward people-heavy care and trades** — households explicitly requesting this, federal first-job bill available, labor costs just dropped.
3. **Wait on housing until states zone** — big states still resist; only capture where permitted and federal money flows.

## Decisions this period

1. **Scale AI-only startups aggressively to 65% of new business formation.** Free models have reset the margin calculation. We're launching 1.2m person-equivalents in AI-only firms (fleets of agents, minimal staff), capturing the structural shift before incumbent labs adjust pricing or bundling. High margin, fast growth.

2. **Grow people-heavy care and trades at 25% expansion.** Honour the household request: publish care and trades roles (200k positions) by Q2 with clear pay ladders and training time. Prioritise federal first-job programme slots and union hiring-hall referrals. Labour is now cheap and available; this locks in supply before others spot it.

3. **Hold housing push pending state zoning moves; deploy capital in permitting states.** Big states still block. Focus on states that permit and direct unclaimed federal incentives there. This preserves capital for AI-only scaling where the real margin is.

## Public statement

"The collapse in AI input costs has opened a structural opportunity. We're scaling AI-only startups aggressively while simultaneously building care and trades roles with published pay ladders and union partnerships. We're partnering with households and federal programmes to capture growth across both labour-light and labour-intensive fronts."

## Messages

**To: Households**  
We're publishing 200k care and trades roles by Q2 with pay ladders and training time. We'll honour union hiring-hall referrals and prioritise federal first-job programme slots. This partnership captures the wage-insurance opportunity for both of us.

**To: Congress / Policy Makers**  
The antitrust inquiry is working—open-weight competition has made AI input costs free. We need continued access parity and the first-job programme properly funded to scale labour-intensive work. Keep the door open.

## What would change my mind

If demand collapsed again (as in 2030), we'd immediately pivot to margin defence: cut unprofitable people-heavy lines and focus entirely on high-margin AI-only firms and housing where federal incentives flow.
