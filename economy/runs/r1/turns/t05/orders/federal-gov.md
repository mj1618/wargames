# Orders — federal-gov — round 05 (2031–32)

## 1. Read of the situation
Displacement is now in the numbers: 164.7m jobs (−2.2m), layoffs 2.3m, labour share 50.4%, though unemployment reads only 3.8% because people left the workforce. Receipts slipped to 17.0% of GDP with the deficit at 6.0% and debt 105%, so there is no room for unfunded cheques. With thin margins (small bills a coin-toss, large ones long shots) and an election in November 2032, we need visible, cheap, employer-backed help.

## 2. Options considered
- Stand pat: rely on the 2031 first-job and wage-insurance law and cheaper living.
- Work-sharing subsidy plus a lab-funded jobs levy: small, pre-election, donor-tolerable.
- Unconventional: a universal "AI dividend" cheque funded by a capital tax — voters love it, but it cannot pass and donors would revolt.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Work-sharing bill (vote 1).** Federal subsidy so employers cut hours, not jobs: about 0.15% of GDP a year from 2031, plus easier credit for new firms that hire people. Employers already back shorter weeks in writing. Faction: deregulators and security hawks over deficit hawks.
2. **Transition levy bill (vote 2).** Call in the reserved levy: 2 points on AI profits (`capital_tax_pct` = 2), credited against Worker Transition Fund contributions, ring-fenced to pay for work-sharing and to lower the wage-insurance trigger from 5% to 4.5% and count lost participation. Roughly deficit-neutral. Faction: deficit hawks win; deregulators lose narrowly.
3. **Close the bundling inquiry by settlement (no vote).** Accept the labs' standard terms and small-firm tier; in exchange, exclusives may not be renewed beyond their current term. Pre-emption stands. No new deployment rules.

## 4. Public statement
"The boom made living cheaper; now we make sure the work is shared. Hours, not jobs, and the firms that profit from AI will help pay for the transition."

**Messages**
- To AI labs: Inquiry closes on your terms plus no renewal of exclusives. We need the 2-point levy you once offered, with full credit for your Fund; back it and pre-emption stays untouched.
- To employers: No deployment rules, no general profit tax. Support the work-sharing bill publicly and extend the pledge through November 2032.

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment above 5% or layoffs above 3m in 2031 would push us to public jobs and targeted transfers despite the deficit.
