# Orders — households — round 05 (2031–2032)

## 1. Read of the situation
The headline is fine (unemployment 3.8%) but we know it hides 2.2m fewer jobs, a participation drop to 61.2% and labour share down to 50.4%. In our corner pay beat prices by only 0.2%, rents fell 3%, shops, restaurants and build-out sites cut back, and people stopped looking rather than being counted. The investment boom is over, last year's "new jobs" are being automated, and the first-job and wage-insurance bill only starts in 2031 and November 2032 is our chance to be heard.

## 2. Options considered
- Renew organising and widen it to every sector, bargaining for backfill, notice and profit share.
- Cut back and save hard to protect against a layoff wave (costs others' jobs, and ours).
- Unconventional: form worker-owned co-ops and union seats inside AI-only startups, and a boycott of AI-run checkouts and care homes in selected cities.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Renew and extend organising** (`union_pressure`): renew the care, health, trades and logistics contracts that carry our profit-share gains, and expand into shops, restaurants and the remaining build-out sites. Asks: backfill of leavers, 90-day notice before automation, profit share, transition accounts. Sustained effort, since the drive fades unless kept up. Take the labs' priced backfill option as a floor, not a ceiling.
2. **Spending vs saving** (`household_saving`): modest precaution, saving rate up about 1 point. Work is harder to find, but a full fear-cut would crash the shops our neighbours work in. Spend normally on essentials and enjoy cheaper AI services.
3. **Votes, protest and a limited boycott**: peaceful mass demands: fund the first-job and wage-insurance programmes in full, tax AI profits to pay for them, back the antitrust case on bundles, and push a "no-layoff-without-notice" law. Run a targeted boycott of AI-run checkouts and care-home robots in a handful of cities where shops have shed staff. Use the 2032 vote as leverage: back whichever side commits in writing to funding and protecting those programmes.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Public:** "Profits are at records and our share is at a record low. Pay for the first-job and wage-insurance programmes with AI profits, and give workers notice and a seat before machines replace us."

**To AI labs:** Accept backfill and apprenticeship terms as a priced option, but we still want profit-share on the gains from our work and $1bn is too small. Reopen it before the election.
**To entrepreneurs:** We will join AI-only startup teams where there is a stake. Offer worker equity and a seat on the board and we will fill the roles.

## 5. What would change my mind
If unemployment rises toward 5% or people we know get laid off in numbers, we save harder and escalate; if firms sign real backfill and notice terms, we drop the boycott.
