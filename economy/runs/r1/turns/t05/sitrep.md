# Sitrep — round 05 (January 2031–December 2032)

**Headline: free AI, cheap services and a wave of new work — 5m more people in work, incomes jump, Congress does nothing and is re-elected.**

**Jobs.** 6.9m jobs went over two years, 6.1m of them to automation (3.8m in offices). 10.6m were created: 6.4m in new kinds of work, 3.8m because cheaper output sold more. Employment rose to 169.7m. Unemployment 3.01% (3.69%); employment rate 60.3% (58.98%), above 2026. Machines now do 24.4% of desk work, of 48.7% possible.

**Pay and prices.** Real pay +7.8%; weekly hours 33.7. Median real household income 117.7 (108.4); bottom fifth 116.5. Housing costs fell 5%; AI and professional services 3%. Labour's share slipped to 49.9%; top-1% share 21.3%.

**Business.** GDP grew 5.2% a year. Labs raced, ended exclusive contracts and gave the basic tier away. Employers automated faster, by attrition. Start-ups went 65% AI-only yet hired heavily in care and trades.

**Government.** Both bills failed: the work-sharing subsidy and the 2-point AI-profit levy. The bundling inquiry closed by consent decree. Deficit 5.3%, debt 102%. Poverty 9.5%; homelessness 15.1 per 10,000.

**What mattered most.** The open-weight price war plus the labs dropping lock-in pushed savings into prices and new firms. The dice went against every worker-side move — organising drive, boycott, both bills — and it made little difference this period.

**What a household would have noticed:** a raise that beat prices, cheaper rent, and help-wanted signs back — in jobs that did not exist five years ago.
