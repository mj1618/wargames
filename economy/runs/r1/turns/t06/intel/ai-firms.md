# Intel — ai-firms — round 06

**Period: January 2033–December 2034.** One set of decisions for both years. Elections November 2034.

**End 2032 (provisional):** unemployment 3.2%; 169.7m jobs (+5.0m over two years); GDP +5.2% a year; median income index about 119; housing costs −5%; labour share 49.9%; deficit 5.3%, debt 102%. Announced layoffs about 2.8m over two years. For 2030: poverty 11.0%, homelessness 16.7 per 10,000.

**News:** housing squeeze — AI wealth is concentrating in a few metros and bidding up land; insurers are pulling out of others. Congress rejected the work-sharing subsidy and the 2-point AI-profit levy. Growth-first kept the presidency and widened its majorities.

**Only you see this:** Your systems can do far more desk work than two years ago; customers use about half of it. Routine-tier revenue is gone; agentic and outcome contracts grew fast. The inquiry closed by consent decree: no renewal of exclusives, small-firm terms binding. The levy you offered to accept died with the bill.

**Messages to you:**
- From federal government: "Inquiry closes on your terms plus no renewal of exclusives. We need the 2-point levy you once offered, with full credit for your Fund; back it and pre-emption stays untouched."
- From households: "Accept backfill and apprenticeship terms as a priced option, but we still want profit-share on the gains from our work and $1bn is too small. Reopen it before the election."
