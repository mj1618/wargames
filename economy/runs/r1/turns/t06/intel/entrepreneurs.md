# Intel — entrepreneurs — round 06

**Period: January 2033–December 2034.** One set of decisions for both years. Elections November 2034.

**End 2032 (provisional):** unemployment 3.2%; 169.7m jobs (+5.0m over two years); GDP +5.2% a year; median income index about 119; housing costs −5%; labour share 49.9%; deficit 5.3%, debt 102%. Announced layoffs about 2.8m over two years. For 2030: poverty 11.0%, homelessness 16.7 per 10,000.

**News:** housing squeeze — AI wealth is concentrating in a few metros and bidding up land; insurers are pulling out of others. Congress rejected the work-sharing subsidy and the 2-point AI-profit levy. Growth-first kept the presidency and widened its majorities.

**Only you see this:** New firms hired about 6.4m over the two years, far above 2030; new kinds of customers keep turning up. Free models made AI-only firms cheap to start, and crowded. Staff are scarce again and pay is rising. The small-firm credit failed with the work-sharing bill. Land prices are jumping in the boom metros.

**Messages to you:**
- From employers: "We set pay and hiring independently; we will not coordinate wages with anyone (antitrust exposure). Compete for the people you need."
- From households: "We will join AI-only startup teams where there is a stake. Offer worker equity and a seat on the board and we will fill the roles."
