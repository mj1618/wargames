# Intel — entrepreneurs — round 06

**Period: January 2033–December 2034.** One set of decisions. Elections November 2034.

**End 2032 (provisional):** unemployment 3.2%; 169.7m jobs (+5.0m in two years); GDP +5.2% a year; median income about 119; housing costs −5%; labour share 49.9%; deficit 5.3%, debt 102%; announced layoffs 2.8m. For 2030: poverty 11.0%, homelessness 16.7 per 10,000.

**News:** housing squeeze — AI wealth bids up land in a few metros; insurers quit others. Congress rejected the work-sharing subsidy and AI-profit levy. Growth-first kept the presidency, with wider majorities.

**Only you see this:** New firms hired about 6.4m over two years, far above 2030; new kinds of customers keep appearing. AI-only niches are crowded. Staff are scarce again. The small-firm credit failed. Land prices are jumping in boom metros.

**Messages to you:**
- From employers: "We set pay and hiring independently; we will not coordinate wages with anyone (antitrust exposure). Compete for the people you need."
- From households: "We will join AI-only startup teams where there is a stake. Offer worker equity and a seat on the board and we will fill the roles."
