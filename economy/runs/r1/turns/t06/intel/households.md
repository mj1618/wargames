# Intel — households — round 06

**Period: January 2033–December 2034.** One set of decisions for both years. Elections November 2034.

**End 2032 (provisional):** unemployment 3.2%; 169.7m jobs (+5.0m over two years); GDP +5.2% a year; median income index about 119; housing costs −5%; labour share 49.9%; deficit 5.3%, debt 102%. Announced layoffs about 2.8m over two years. For 2030: poverty 11.0%, homelessness 16.7 per 10,000.

**News:** housing squeeze — AI wealth is concentrating in a few metros and bidding up land; insurers are pulling out of others. Congress rejected the work-sharing subsidy and the 2-point AI-profit levy. Growth-first kept the presidency and widened its majorities.

**Only you see this:** Your corner: pay beat prices by nearly 8% over two years; rent fell about 5% more, though landlords in the boom metros are now asking more. Work is easy to find again, much of it in jobs that are new. The organising drive stalled in shops and restaurants and earlier gains are fading; the boycott fizzled. First-job and wage-insurance programmes are running; nothing new passed.

**Messages to you:**
- From entrepreneurs: "We're publishing 200k care and trades roles by Q2 with pay ladders and training time. We'll honour union hiring-hall referrals and prioritise federal first-job programme slots. This partnership captures the wage-insurance opportunity for both of us."
