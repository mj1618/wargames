# Orders — ai-firms — round 06 (2033–2034)

## Read of the situation
The macro is as good as it gets: unemployment 3.2%, 169.7m jobs (+5.0m), GDP +5.2%, and growth-first widened its majorities, so nothing we dislike passes before November 2034. The fuses are distributional: labour share 49.9%, 2.8m layoffs, a housing squeeze blamed on "AI wealth". Our moat is the unused half of frontier capability; routine revenue is zero, outcome contracts are the business.

## Options considered
- Refuse the levy and ride the majorities: saves ~$1bn/yr, burns our only ally while it faces a 5.3% deficit.
- Voluntary slow-down to defuse the wealth backlash (unconventional): doesn't hold against foreign labs.
- Take the levy, make the Fund the credit vehicle, spend part on housing.

## Decisions this period
1. **Deploy & pricing:** race +15%, capex weighted to reliability and deployment teams — adoption depth is the constraint. Outcome contracts (we take 20–30% of measured savings) become the standard tier; basic tier stays free; backfill/apprenticeship offered as a priced option. [`deploy_pace` race +15%; `ai_pricing` premium/outcome]
2. **Politics:** publicly back the 2-point AI-profits levy with full Fund credit, conditional on pre-emption untouched and a 10-year review; we ask for a deployment safe harbour but sign without it. Lobbying $400m for 2034, three-quarters growth-first, rest to worker-first moderates.
3. **Fund:** $3bn/yr (or the full creditable amount): First Job $1.5bn, apprenticeships $0.5bn, $1bn factory-built housing in squeezed metros. Profit-share refused — the levy is the profit-share. Labs split on housing; majority carries it.

## Public statement
"We will pay a dedicated AI levy, credited against the $3bn a year our Fund spends on first jobs, apprenticeships and homes where AI has driven up land. Deployment rules stay national."

## Messages
- **To federal government:** Yes to the 2-point levy with full Fund credit, pre-emption untouched, 10-year review. A safe harbour would be welcome; we sign either way. Co-announce before the budget.
- **To households:** Fund goes to $3bn/yr; backfill and apprenticeship priced into every contract. No profit-share — the levy is your share, and it is statutory.

## What would change my mind
A moratorium or licensing bill polling above 50% with growth-first votes attached.
