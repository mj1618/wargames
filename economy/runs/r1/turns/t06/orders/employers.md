# Orders — employers — round 06 (2033-2034)

## 1. Read of the situation
Margins held and volumes grew where we cut prices, with unemployment at 3.2% and 5.0m jobs added, so the model works. But vacancies are hard to refill and pay claims are rising, which says attrition-based shrinkage is running into a thin labour pool, not slack. Labour share is 49.9% and the 2.8m announced layoffs keep us visible ahead of the November 2034 elections; our pledge has lapsed and the government wants it renewed.

## 2. Options considered
- Keep the 2031-32 playbook: +25% adoption, 30% layoff share, quiet attrition.
- Slow down and rebuild hiring to ease the vacancy problem, at the cost of cost-savings.
- Unconventional: put the 15% First Job discount and per-task pricing to work by moving routine hiring-pipeline roles to agentic tasks, and fund a joint apprenticeship scheme with rivals (no wage terms, so no antitrust exposure).

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Adoption and shedding:** hold adoption about +25% in office/routine work, cautious in health and transport; take agentic per-task pricing on a dual-source basis. Keep layoff_share at about 30%, using attrition. Hours_share stays 20%, with the low-pay top-up. Why: cheap tools, tight labour and a record margin.
2. **Pay and prices:** profit-sharing 2.0-2.5% of payroll, targeted at roles where vacancies are hardest to fill and claims are rising. Price cuts in 6-8 contested lines, passing on about half the savings. About 30k entry-level hires through First Job, claiming the 15% discount.
3. **Politics:** sign a pledge of no mass layoffs through the November 2034 elections (the lapsed one is refreshed), and publicly support the work-sharing bill, since hours_share already does the same job. Oppose the AI-profit levy and any deployment rules. We make no wage-coordination moves.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** "Employers will keep hiring, keep sharing gains and avoid mass layoffs through the 2034 election."
- To federal government: Pledge renewed through November 2034; we back work-sharing in return for no general profit tax and no deployment rules.
- To AI labs: Agentic per-task terms and the First Job discount accepted; dual-source, no exclusivity, with the year-three break.

## 5. What would change my mind
A sharp demand drop, or a credible profit levy or layoff bill with real votes, would make us accelerate cost cuts or lobby harder.
