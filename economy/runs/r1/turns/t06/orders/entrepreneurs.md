# Orders — entrepreneurs — round 06

## Read of the situation

New-firm hiring hit 6.4m over two years (far above 2030 recovery), but the small-firm credit has now failed. AI-only niches are crowded and margins are compressing; land prices are jumping in boom metros, squeezing the housing strategy. Staff are scarce again—wages will rise—but households will join AI-only teams where there is real equity stake and board voice, not options.

## Options considered

1. **Push harder on AI-only:** scale the 65% AI-only model further despite margin pressure and credit death, chase crowded niches with volume.
2. **Pivot to people-heavy:** shift capital to care and trades where staff scarcity creates pricing power, higher pay, and defensibility against incumbent automation.
3. **Equity-stake AI firms:** keep 25–30% of new startups as AI-only but structure them as equity partnerships with household workers, board representation, and reduced cash burn.

## Decisions this period

**Decision 1: Rebalance the portfolio.** Pull back AI-only expansion from 65% to 30% of new startups. Reallocate 35% to care, trades, and services (up to 65%), where staff scarcity creates margin and labour commands wage ladders. Small-firm credit is dead; I need lower-burn, higher-margin businesses. **Why:** Credit failure kills the high-growth, cash-intensive AI-only model; people-heavy niches are naturally cash-generative and hire at scarcity wages. Margin beats volume in a credit crunch.

**Decision 2: Equity-stake model for remaining AI-only.** The 30% of new AI-only startups now offer equity and board seats to household workers. This attracts scarce talent without cash burn and aligns founder and worker interests in a crowded niche where incumbent labs dominate. Keep each firm small (5–20 person-equivalents plus AI agents). **Why:** Free open-weight models mean AI-only margins are razor-thin in crowded niches; equity converts scarcity labour cost into upside bet for workers and preserves founder control.

**Decision 3: Pause housing development.** Suspend new housing startups until metro land prices stabilize or states override local zoning. Redirect capital and focus to care and trades. **Why:** Land-price squeeze in boom metros makes factory-built housing uncompetitive; states have not moved on zoning; housing ROI is negative now.

## Public statement

"Small firms are shifting to worker-equity partnerships and focusing on care, trades, and services. Credit death means we scale where scarcity creates profit, not volume. Households get board seats in our AI startups; founders keep vision and control."

## Messages to other players

**To: Households**  
"Join us as co-founders: equity, board voice, profit-sharing in lean AI startups. We win together when labour is scarce. Interested in care, trades, or AI coaching? Let's talk terms."

**To: Congress**  
"Small-firm credit collapsed. We need a small-business lending facility or we shrink. Growth depends on it."

## What would change my mind

If credit suddenly re-opens, I'd rebalance back toward cash-generative AI-only startups; if big firms or a new competitor floods care and trades with automation, I'd pivot back to niches they cannot reach.
