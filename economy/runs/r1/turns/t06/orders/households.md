# Orders — households — round 06 (2033-34)

## 1. Read of the situation
Unemployment is 3.2%, pay beat prices by nearly 8% over two years and rents fell about 5% more, so most of us feel better off than at any point in this decade. But the labour share is 49.9% and falling, the top tenth who own the shares are collecting the gains, and our organising drive stalled and its earlier wins are fading. Boom-metro rents are turning up again and the November 2034 midterms are coming; this is the time to lock in terms while jobs are plentiful and we have leverage.

## 2. Options considered
- Coast on good times: spend freely, do nothing organised.
- Rebuild organising on hiring-hall deals with the new firms offering roles, pushing profit-share and notice clauses.
- (Unconventional) Cooperative bargaining pools: tenants and workers in boom metros bargain together over rent and wages, using the hiring halls as the base.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Rebuild organising through the hiring-hall route.** Take up the entrepreneurs' offer of 200k care and trades roles: union referrals into them, in exchange for pay ladders, training time, backfill and 90-day notice of automation, with profit-share where firms are profitable. Also fund and staff drives in shops, restaurants, build-out sites and care at a steady pace; renew expiring contracts first. Jobs are plentiful, so this is when it works; if we skip it, the fade continues.
2. **Spend normally, saving down about 1 point.** Pay is up and work is easy to find, so there is no reason to hoard; we also won't hold back out of fear of jobs we can't see vanishing. We keep a rainy-day cushion through the wage-insurance and first-job programme rather than cash.
3. **Peaceful political push ahead of the 2034 midterms.** Demand: fund first-job and wage insurance from an AI-profit contribution (the rejected levy again), zoning reform in boom metros, and backing for the AI-contracts consent decree to be enforced. We vote for candidates who commit in writing. No boycotts: the last one fizzled and conditions don't justify it.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** Good times are not shared times: we welcome new jobs, and we'll organise them so the pay ladder, training and notice are written down, not promised.

**Messages:**
- To entrepreneurs: Yes to hiring-hall referrals into the 200k roles. We need pay ladders, training hours and 90-day automation notice in writing, and we will help fill slots from the unemployed and young first.
- To federal-gov: Fund the first-job and wage-insurance slots properly and tie the zoning money to boom metros. Voters will remember it in November 2034.

## 5. What would change my mind
If unemployment rises toward 5%, people we know start losing jobs, or the entrepreneurs' roles don't materialise, I'd shift to saving more and escalate organising and protest.
