# Sitrep — round 06 (January 2033–December 2034)

**Headline: the boom runs on — 4m more people in work and incomes up again — yet voters hand Congress to the worker-first coalition.**

**Jobs.** 9.1m jobs went over two years, 8.4m of them to automation (5.0m in offices). 11.7m were created: 6.4m in new kinds of work, 4.7m because cheaper output sold more. Employment rose to 173.7m. Unemployment 2.93% (3.01%); employment rate 61.2% (60.3%). Machines now do 36.9% of desk work, of 63.6% possible.

**Pay and prices.** Real pay +5.9%; weekly hours 33.3. Median real household income 125.2 (117.7); bottom fifth 122.8. Housing costs still fell 2% despite the land squeeze; AI and professional services fell 4%. Labour's share slid to 48.3%; top-1% share 21.9%.

**Business.** GDP grew 5.5% a year. Labs raced and made outcome contracts standard. Employers automated by attrition and renewed their pledge. Start-ups swung back to care and trades.

**Government.** Congress passed zoning pre-emption in squeezed metros with an insurance backstop, and a no-deployment-limits guarantee tied to a $6bn labs' Fund (the labs offered $3bn). Deficit 4.7%, debt 97%. Poverty 8.6%; homelessness 13.8 per 10,000.

**What mattered most.** The housing bill passed and survived court, blunting the squeeze. Then a 15% election roll came up: growth-first lost Congress in November 2034.

**What a household would have noticed:** another real raise, plenty of job offers, rent flat rather than falling — and the office jobs their parents had quietly disappearing.
