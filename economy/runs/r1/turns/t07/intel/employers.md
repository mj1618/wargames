# Intel — employers — round 07

**Period: January 2035–December 2036.** One set of decisions. Elections November 2036.

**End 2034 (provisional):** unemployment 2.8%; 173.7m jobs (+4.0m in two years); GDP +5.5% a year; median income about 125; housing costs −2%; labour share 48.3%; deficit 4.7%, debt 97%; announced layoffs 3.6m. For 2032: poverty 9.5%, homelessness 15.1 per 10,000.

**News:** a hit new industry — an unpredicted kind of service takes off and hires in large numbers. Congress passed metro zoning pre-emption with an insurance backstop, and a no-deployment-limits guarantee conditional on a $6bn Fund. Worker-first won Congress; the presidency stays growth-first.

**Only you see this:** You removed about a third more positions than in 2031–32, mostly by attrition. Vacancies are harder to fill and pay claims keep rising. Margins widened. Your pledge lapsed in November 2034. The work-sharing bill was not tabled.

**Messages to you:**
- From federal government: "No payroll tax, no deployment rules, wage insurance fast-tracked. Renew the no-mass-layoff pledge through 2034 and keep cuts to attrition."
