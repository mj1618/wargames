# Orders — ai-firms — round 07 (2035–2036)

## Read of the situation
The macro is as good as it gets — unemployment 2.8%, 173.7m jobs, GDP +5.5% a year, median income 125 — and a new industry hiring in bulk is our best "AI creates work" story. The threat is political: labour share 48.3%, 3.6m announced layoffs, a worker-first House, the levy we backed never tabled, and our guarantee not binding because the Fund is at $3bn, not $6bn. Our systems can do two-thirds of desk work and customers use half, so growth is in depth, not pace.

## Options considered
- Hold the Fund at $3bn and trust a growth-first veto: saves $3bn, leaves us naked in an election year.
- Take the government's deal: $6bn buys a statutory no-limits guarantee through 2036.
- Unconventional: table our own 3% levy in the worker-first House as the alternative to any licensing bill, splitting the opposition early.

## Decisions this period
1. **Take the deal.** Fund to $6bn a year from 2035, First Job and small-firm terms binding; we publicly back the housing bill. Vanta objects, majority carries. $3bn a year is cheap insurance against a moratorium.
2. **Race +15%, weighted to deployment [`deploy_pace`]; outcome contracts standard, dual-source and year-three break accepted [`ai_pricing`].** Locks agentic per-task revenue; the hit new industry gets a two-year discounted ramp as a showcase of AI-driven hiring.
3. **$500m lobbying, split toward the worker-first House.** Goal: no licensing or moratorium bill reaches the floor; voluntary displacement-data disclosure is the concession. Levy held for whoever wins the 2036 presidency.

## Public statement
"We are doubling the Worker Transition Fund to $6bn a year with binding First Job and small-firm terms, and we back the housing bill. Record employment shows AI creates work when the economy is allowed to grow."

## Messages
- **To federal government:** Accepted: $6bn from 2035, First Job and small-firm terms binding, housing bill backed. We expect the guarantee signed this quarter and defended through 2036.
- **To employers:** Terms accepted: agentic per-task, dual-source, year-three break. Back the guarantee publicly and keep the no-mass-layoff pledge; it is cover for both of us.

## What would change my mind
A licensing bill passing the House with growth-first defections would make a pre-emptive 3% levy offer the cheaper play.
