# Orders — entrepreneurs — turn 07

**Read of the situation.** Unemployment at 2.8%, staff extremely scarce—the tightest labour market in years. A hit new industry is hiring in large numbers and households are explicitly offering hiring-hall partnerships for 200k+ care/trades roles with pay ladders, training, and automation notice. My worker-equity model is proving out: equity firms are recruiting where cash wages alone can't. Congress is now worker-first, and factory-built housing is approved by right in constrained metros—both open doors I've been testing.

**Options considered.** 
1. Go all-in on care/trades scaling (250k+ roles) with household partnerships and equity stakes, ceding AI-only space to incumbents.
2. Chase the hit new industry aggressively while expanding care/trades modestly (180k roles), risking missing the labour-scarcity window.
3. Diversify: care/trades (200k roles), re-enter housing buildout in permitted metros, pilot the hit industry—balanced exposure.

**Decisions this period.**
1. **Expand care/trades to 200k+ roles via hiring-hall partnerships.** (Budget: household partnerships + hiring platform cost; pace: ramp through 2035–36.) Households are offering. My equity model proves equity stakes beat cash in tight labour markets. Pair each role with pay ladders, 90-day automation notice, and training hours.
2. **Enter the hit new industry with small pilots in 5–6 metros** (100–150 test startups; ~$50m allocated; pace: Q1 2035 launches). The industry is unpredicted but real and hiring—get founders in before incumbents notice.
3. **Re-enter housing in pre-approval metros** (factory-built, 2,000–3,000 units over two years; pace: ramp 2035–36). Zoning is now settled law in constrained metros; construction bottleneck is clearing.

**Public statement:** "Workers and founders are in the same boat—labour scarcity is our constraint, not capital. We're betting on pay ladders, equity stakes, and hiring-hall partnerships to compete for talent and build defensible small firms in people-heavy niches."

**Message to households:**
