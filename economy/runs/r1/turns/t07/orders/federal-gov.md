# Orders — federal-gov — round 07 (2035–36)

## 1. Read of the situation
The boom is still ours to run on: unemployment 2.8%, 173.7m jobs (+4.0m), income 125, deficit down to 4.7%, debt 97%. But we lost Congress to worker-first, labour share is 48.3%, layoffs hit 3.6m and receipts slipped to 16.8%. The labs paid only $3bn of $6bn, so the no-limits guarantee is not in force and Congress now holds the pen.

## 2. Options considered
- Veto everything and run in 2036 on the boom against an "anti-growth" Congress.
- Triangulate: sign a small, lab-agreed levy and worker measures to pre-empt anything bigger.
- Unconventional: offer Congress a universal "AI dividend" cheque funded by the levy, landing October 2036.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Levy-and-lending bill.** 2-point levy on AI profits (`capital_tax_pct` = 2), full Fund credit, pre-emption untouched, 10-year review; proceeds seed a small-business lending facility (~0.1% GDP) and fully fund first-job and wage-insurance slots (`retraining_pct_gdp` 0.3). Co-announced with the labs before the budget. Fiscal moderates and security hawks win; deregulators lose. Why: Congress will tax anyway — better our 2 points than their 10.
2. **Work-sharing and boom-metro housing bill.** Work-sharing subsidy (~0.15% GDP) with employer backing; zoning money tied to boom metros. Worker-first can vote yes; employers get what they asked for. Deficit hawks grumble; paid for by the levy and growth.
3. **Veto line, by executive action.** Publicly pledge vetoes of any general profit tax, deployment limits or repeal of pre-emption; keep disclosure rules as they are. Deregulators win. Why: donors need certainty, and the labs' $3bn shortfall no longer buys a statutory guarantee — the veto is the guarantee.

## 4. Public statement
"The boom is working; now it pays its way. A modest levy the industry itself supports will fund first jobs, small-business credit and shorter hours instead of layoffs — with no limits on American AI."

**Messages**
- To AI labs: "Deal: 2 points, full credit, pre-emption safe, review in ten years. Co-announce in February. Fund the missing $3bn or my veto is your only shield."
- To employers: "Work-sharing is in our bill; no general profit tax, no deployment rules will get my signature. Renew the pledge through November 2036."

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment above 4.5% or receipts below 16.5% before mid-2036 — then I move to a larger levy and a pre-election cheque.
