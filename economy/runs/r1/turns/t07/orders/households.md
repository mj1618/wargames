# Orders — households — round 07 (2035-36)

## 1. Read of the situation
Work is easy to find (unemployment 2.8%, +4.0m jobs, pay about 6% ahead of prices), but labour share is 48.3%, a record low, and office openings keep thinning. Our referral drive filled roles yet won no contracts, so we have jobs without bargaining power. The labs offer a $3bn-a-year Fund but no profit share; a statutory levy can be repealed, and the 2-point levy was rejected in 2032.

## 2. Options considered
- Keep running referral drives as before.
- Use the tight labour market to bargain for notice, backfill, apprenticeship and profit-share clauses.
- Unconventional: take the entrepreneurs' equity offer as pooled union or community equity, so the bottom half finally owns shares.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Organising with leverage [`union_pressure`]:** Drives in care, trades, build-out and entrepreneurs' new firms, aiming at written contracts (90-day notice, backfill, apprenticeship quotas, profit-share), not just referrals. Roll needed.
2. **Pilot the co-founder offer:** 5 to 10 startups first, equity and profit-share held in a pooled union or community fund, and no clause barring organising. It gives us a stake in the gains.
3. **Votes and protest:** Peaceful push on the new Congress to legislate the AI levy, the Fund's backfill and apprenticeship terms, and first-job and wage insurance, before November 2036. Back whoever commits in writing. Spend normally (saving -1pt). No boycott while jobs are plentiful.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** Jobs are there but the gains are not shared. We will organise where the work is, take a stake in new firms, and want the AI levy and Fund written into law.

- **To AI labs:** Backfill and apprenticeship terms are welcome. Put the Fund in writing at $3bn a year for five years and don't oppose a statutory levy.
- **To entrepreneurs:** Interested in a pilot. Equity goes to a pooled fund, with 90-day notice and no bar on organising.

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment heading toward 5%, or the Fund proving a pledge that vanishes when profits dip, would push us to saving and disruptive protest.
