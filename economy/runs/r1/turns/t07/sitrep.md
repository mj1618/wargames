# Sitrep — round 07 (January 2035–December 2036)

**Headline: a hit new industry and a worker-first Congress cap the boom — 5.7m more people in work, 3.7m vacancies nobody could fill, and the first levy on AI profits.**

**Jobs.** 10.6m jobs went over two years, 9.9m to automation (5.5m in offices). 14.9m were created; new kinds of work alone offered 13.1m openings, and 3.7m went unfilled. Employment reached 179.4m. Unemployment 2.93% (unchanged); employment rate 62.7% (61.2%). Machines do 48.8% of desk work, of 78.4% possible; robots 10.4% of physical work.

**Pay and prices.** Real pay +6.2%; weekly hours 32.8. Median real household income 135.3 (125.2); bottom fifth 132.5. Housing costs fell 6%; AI and professional services 4%. Labour's share fell to 45.8%; top-1% share 22.9%.

**Business.** GDP grew 6.5% a year. Labs raced, kept outcome contracts and doubled their Fund to $6bn. Employers slowed automation slightly and renewed their pledge. Start-ups hired heavily in care and trades.

**Government.** Congress passed a 2-point AI-profit levy with small-business lending and first-job money, plus work-sharing and boom-metro housing; a court let the levy stand. Deficit 4.0%, debt 90%. Poverty 7.3%; homelessness 12.2 per 10,000.

**What mattered most.** The event: new work doubled. The organising drive finally won, helping the levy through.

**What a household would have noticed:** bigger pay, cheaper rent, employers begging for staff — and a smaller slice of a much bigger pie. The November 2036 election falls outside the exercise.
