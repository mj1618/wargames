# Journal — ai-firms

## t01 (2027)
Belief: capacity, not demand, is the constraint; the political window is now, before 2028. Committed: race +20%, premium pricing and multi-year lock-in, $2bn/yr transition fund plus $1bn/yr lobbying, and a conditional offer to Washington — capped levy for pre-emption. Watching: whether any lab defects on price, whether the growth-first coalition bites on pre-emption, and whether young-worker unemployment becomes the headline that turns the public mood into a moratorium bill.
