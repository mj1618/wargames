# Journal — employers

Believe: automation pays (pilot: -10% price, +12% volume, margin up); political risk low under growth-first Washington; headline risk is the main threat. Committed: company-wide drive (+20%), layoff_share to ~40% (attrition/freezes), ~half of savings passed to price in elastic services, hours_share 10%, offered voluntary pledge for no mandatory rules. Watch: rival moves, sales trend vs price cuts, youth-unemployment headlines, any bill on deployment rules or automation tax.
