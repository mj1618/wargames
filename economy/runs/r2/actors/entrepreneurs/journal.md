# Journal — entrepreneurs

## Turn 01 (January 2027)

We're betting on real demand for services and expertise over automation theater. The 2026 "AI supervisor" crash showed that hiring people just to manage machines doesn't work—customers want the work *done*, not supervised. We're shifting capital from AI-only specs to people-heavy services where we're actually seeing traction. Factory-built housing is the play: states are loosening zoning right now, construction is a labor bottleneck AI can genuinely help with, and every house built creates supply-side relief for the whole economy. We'll watch whether credit stays cheap and whether states follow through on zoning. If both hold, we scale fast.
