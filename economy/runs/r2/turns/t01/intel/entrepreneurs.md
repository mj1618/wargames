# Intel — Entrepreneurs — January to December 2027

**National numbers (end 2026):** unemployment 4.1%; participation 61.6%; 162.4m jobs (59.1% of adults); labour share 52.8% (record low); profits 14.9% of GDP (record); top-1% income share about 20%; poverty 12.9%; homelessness 22 per 10,000; federal deficit 6.0% of GDP, debt about 100%. Median income, GDP and prices: index 100.

**Washington:** the growth-first coalition leads after the midterms — pro-deployment, tax-averse, sceptical of new entitlements. **States:** several big states are already overriding local zoning to get homes built.

**In the news — the new-work mirage:** last year's fashionable new job category, the "AI supervisor" roles hired through 2026, is itself being automated; several large employers have closed those teams.

**From your own pipeline:** New kinds of customer are turning up — households paying for things they never bought before, small firms wanting bespoke services with a person attached. It is a steady trickle, not a rush: about one founder in three in your network reports demand they could not have named two years ago, and most of those are hiring a handful of people, not hundreds.

Credit is available on ordinary terms.
