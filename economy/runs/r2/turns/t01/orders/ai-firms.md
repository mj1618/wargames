# Orders — AI firms — t01 (2027)

## Read of the situation
Enterprise demand runs ahead of our capacity, so chips and power bind, not customers; profits at a record 14.9% of GDP and labour share at 52.8% say the savings land with us. Unemployment at 4.1% means no crisis yet justifies a rule, but the 19% drop in young hiring and two-thirds of the public wanting a slowdown are its raw material. The growth-first coalition holds Washington until November 2028: our window for a friendly framework.

## Options considered
- Price war for share: pointless while capacity-bound.
- Voluntary 15% slow-down: unenforceable against foreign and open-weight rivals.
- Unconventional: a "deployment compact" — accept a small capped profits levy for federal pre-emption and no deployment limits.

## Decisions this period
1. **deploy_pace: Race, +20% above baseline.** All marginal capex into compute and power; capacity decides who owns the enterprise market. One lab preferred steady; overruled.
2. **ai_pricing: premium, bundling, lock-in.** Hold list prices; sell multi-year enterprise agreements with volume commitments and bundled tooling. The one-third cut was only half-matched; not repeated.
3. **Politics and cover: $3bn/yr.** $2bn/yr into a jointly branded Worker Transition Fund (retraining, wage-insurance pilots); $1bn/yr lobbying and campaign money for the growth-first coalition. Asks: federal pre-emption of state AI-employment laws, no licensing, no deployment limits. A capped levy is offered only for pre-emption.

## Public statement
"AI is the strongest productivity tool America has had in a generation. We are committing $2bn a year to help workers into the jobs it creates, and ask Washington for one national rulebook, not fifty."

## Messages
- **To Federal government:** We will publicly back a capped AI-profits levy (at most 5%, sunset 2032) for worker transition, if the same bill pre-empts state AI-employment rules and sets no deployment or licensing limits.
- **To Employers:** Multi-year capacity agreements signed in 2027 get priority allocation and locked pricing through 2029.

## What would change my mind
A moratorium bill with bipartisan sponsors, or unemployment above 5.5% pinned on AI, would make me trade pace for a deal.
