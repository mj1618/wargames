# Orders — Federal government — 2027 (t01)

## 1. Read of the situation
Unemployment is 4.1% with 162.4m jobs, so there is no visible jobs crisis to legislate against — but labour's share is a record-low 52.8%, profits a record 14.9% of GDP, and two-thirds of voters want AI slowed. The deficit is already 6.0% with debt near 100% of GDP and 70% of revenue resting on wages, so we cannot buy our way to 2028. The weak spots voters feel are rents and young graduates' hiring, not the headline rate.

## 2. Options considered
- Do nothing federal; let states and the market run, and bank growth.
- Light-touch AI framework plus a housing supply push: cheap, visible, coalition-friendly.
- Unconventional: a small "AI dividend" cheque funded by a capital tax — steals the opposition's best 2028 issue, but donors and deficit hawks would revolt.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Bill 1 — American AI Leadership and Workers Act.** `deploy_regulation` = disclosure only (large firms report AI-attributed layoffs and deployment quarterly), pre-empting stricter state rules; paired with `retraining_pct_gdp` = 0.1% (wage insurance and retraining aimed at under-30s and displaced office workers), starting 2028. No capital tax (`capital_tax_pct` = 0). Why: answers the "slow it down" mood at minimal cost, keeps donors, and gives us better data. Deregulators and security hawks win; deficit hawks swallow 0.1%.
2. **Bill 2 — Housing supply.** `housing_policy` = incentives, edging to funded building with zoning conditions: about 0.15% of GDP in grants, only to states and cities that override restrictive zoning; fast-track factory-built housing. Why: rents are the cost-of-living issue we can move before November 2028, and it is deregulation. Deregulators win.
3. **Executive, no Congress.** A few antitrust cases (`competition_policy` = cases) on AI pricing to show token savings reach customers; no transfers, public jobs or work-time changes. Deficit hawks win.

## 4. Public statement
"America will lead in AI and Americans will share in it: we are building homes, backing workers who need a new start, and requiring companies to be straight about how they use these systems."

**Messages**
- To states: federal housing money goes first to states that pre-empt local zoning; tell us what you can break ground on by mid-2028.
- To the AI labs: we are holding the line at disclosure and no new tax; in return, report layoffs honestly and keep hiring visible. Do not embarrass us before 2028.

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment above about 5.5%, a clear fall in wage-tax receipts, or street disorder would make cheques and a capital tax live options.
