# Orders — households — T01 (2027)

## 1. Read of the situation
Unemployment is 4.1% and 162.4m people are working, so most of us still have a paycheck, but labour's share is 52.8% and profits are 14.9% of GDP: the gains are going elsewhere. Our kids are feeling it first: graduates cannot land a first desk job and "AI" is the stock reason for layoffs, while UI covers only 40% of pay for 26 weeks. One bright spot on our streets: new apartment blocks went up and rents are flat, so bills are not getting worse right now.

## 2. Options considered
- Wait and watch; nothing has hit most of us yet.
- Organise now while jobs are plentiful and bargaining power is as good as it will get.
- Cut spending and hoard cash against what's coming (unconventional alternative: neighbourhood mutual-aid funds and tool/childcare co-ops that pool savings instead of each household hoarding).

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Organising drive (`union_pressure`), moderate scale.** Concentrate on health/care, logistics and big-office workplaces where AI task-handoff is spreading. Ask for AI-change notice (90 days), no-layoff or redeployment-with-pay clauses, and a share of productivity gains. Doing it while unemployment is low is the cheap time to do it; if it fades without upkeep, we accept that.
2. **Hold saving where it is (`household_saving`: no change).** Most of us are not yet scared enough to cut spending, and hoarding would cost our neighbours' jobs. Those who can will quietly top up emergency funds; start local mutual-aid pools for the unemployed and graduates.
3. **Vote and protest, peaceful only.** Demand wage insurance, longer and wider UI, and AI-layoff notice rules; back worker-first candidates locally and keep pressing for housing to be built (rents flat is the one thing helping). No boycotts yet: nothing visible has taken a job from us in our street.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** "Working families built these profits. We are not against new technology; we want notice, a share of the gains, and a floor under people who lose their jobs."

- **To Government (growth-first):** Support a modest AI-layoff notice and wage-insurance bill now; if you wait until layoffs are visible the anger will be harder to manage.
- **To Firms:** Talk to us about no-layoff and gain-sharing clauses before announcing cuts; workers who feel included will not sabotage deployment.

## 5. What would change my mind
If layoffs among people we know turn from graduates into mid-career workers, or rents start climbing again, we will raise saving, escalate to strikes and consider boycotts.
