# Sitrep — round 01 (2027)

**Jobs.** The first year employment fell. Automation and its knock-on spending cuts removed 2.25m jobs, 1.15m of them office and professional; 1.01m appeared, half from cheaper services selling more and half from new kinds of work. Even with population growth, employment dropped 0.6m to 161.8m. Because employers mostly froze hiring instead of firing, unemployment only rose to 4.3%; the share of adults working fell from 59.1% to 58.65%.

**Pay and prices.** Real pay rose 1.0%. Goods and services got slightly cheaper; housing costs fell 2.3% as building ran ahead. Median real income 100.6; bottom fifth 100.6.

**Businesses.** The labs raced: machines could do 16.6% of desk work by December, and firms used 4.5%, triple a year ago. Labour's share fell a full point to 51.7%; the top-1% share rose to 20.5%. GDP grew 1.9%.

**Government.** Both bills passed: layoff disclosure with pre-emption of state rules (plus retraining from 2028) and housing grants (from 2028). Antitrust cases opened. Deficit 6.2%, debt 102.4%.

**Daily life.** Poverty 12.7%, homelessness 20.7 per 10,000. The union drive won contracts.

**What mattered most.** The labs' race plus employers' company-wide drive, in a world where firms adopt fast. And attrition over layoffs, which kept the damage out of the unemployment rate.

**What a household would have noticed:** rent easing, pay edging up, and a graduate at home who cannot get a first interview.
