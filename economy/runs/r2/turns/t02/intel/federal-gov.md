# Intel — Federal government — round 02

**January–December 2028.** Federal elections in November.

**Numbers (end 2027).** Unemployment 4.5%; participation 61.3%; 161.8m jobs (down 0.6m); GDP +1.9%; housing costs down 2%, other prices flat; median real income 101.6; labour share 51.7%; deficit 6.2%. AI-cited layoffs: about 0.9m. Poverty 12.7%, homelessness 20.7 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Generation leap.** A new model generation does week-long professional projects unaided.

**Only you see.** Both bills passed and survived court, as did the antitrust cases. Filings show more roles closed by not hiring than by layoff. Wage-tax receipts are slipping (revenue 17.2% of GDP). Retraining and housing grants start this year.

**Messages.**
