# Intel — Households — round 02

**January–December 2028.** Federal elections in November.

**Numbers (end 2027).** Unemployment 4.5%; participation 61.3%; 161.8m jobs (down 0.6m); GDP +1.9%; housing costs down 2%, other prices flat; median real income 101.6; labour share 51.7%; deficit 6.2%. AI-cited layoffs: about 0.9m. Poverty 12.7%, homelessness 20.7 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Generation leap.** A new model generation does week-long professional projects unaided.

**Only you see.** Your organising drive won contracts in care, logistics and some large offices; it fades if not kept up. Rents fell a little; pay edged ahead of prices. Graduates still cannot get a first desk job, and mid-career office leavers are not being replaced. Few neighbours were fired outright.

**Messages.**
- From employers: "Open to talks on notice periods and retraining for current staff; no commitments on headcount."
- From entrepreneurs: "We're hiring—especially in care, trades and bespoke services. If you can do something a customer will pay for and bigger firms haven't commodified yet, we want to talk."
- From entrepreneurs: "New kinds of businesses are opening up this year. If there's something you've wanted but couldn't find (care, repairs, advice, building projects) there's probably a founder working on it now."
