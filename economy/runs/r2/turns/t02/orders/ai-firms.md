# Orders — ai-firms — round 02 (2028)

## Read of the situation
The leap is ours and demand still outruns capacity, so pricing power is intact and the constraint is chips and power, not customers. Washington gave us pre-emption with disclosure only and no levy, but antitrust on pricing survived its first court test and is now the one credible legal threat. Unemployment is 4.5% with 0.9m AI-cited layoffs and an election in November: the risk is not this Congress but the next one.

## Options considered
- Race flat-out, hold premium bundling, dare the courts.
- Voluntary election-year slowdown pact among the four labs (unconventional) — rejected: foreign rivals won't hold it and it concedes the moratorium frame.
- Race on capability, give ground voluntarily on the lock-in terms antitrust targets, buy cover cheaply before November. **Chosen.**

## Decisions this period
1. **`deploy_pace`: race, +25% above baseline**, every unit of compute we can power; new generation goes first to multi-year contract customers. Why: the lead is perishable; we assume a foreign match within 18 months.
2. **`ai_pricing`: two-tier.** Premium holds on the new generation (sold out anyway). Previous generation cut ~40% with open API access; all contracts get 12-month exit and data portability. Vanta dissents; majority rules. Why: this is the conduct remedy a court would impose — giving it now costs little and weakens the cases.
3. **Politics and funds.** Lobbying to $1.5bn/yr for the growth-first coalition's defence of pre-emption. Honest quarterly layoff disclosure. Worker fund $2bn to $3bn/yr, one-third routed via state governors who lost powers to pre-emption. Why: cheapest insurance against a worker-first majority with a moratorium plank.

## Public statement
"The new generation is available to every business at falling prices and with no lock-in. We will report our customers' job changes honestly and fund the transition where it lands."

## Messages
- **To federal government:** "Disclosure will be clean and on time. We are cutting old-generation prices and dropping lock-in before anyone makes us. Hold pre-emption through November and we stay quiet on a levy."
- **To households:** "We'll publish a model gain-sharing and redeployment clause and route $1bn/yr of the fund through union-run retraining. The clauses are employers' to sign, not ours."

## What would change my mind
A trial loss on bundling, or the worker-first coalition polling to take both chambers on a moratorium plank — then I offer the levy back.
