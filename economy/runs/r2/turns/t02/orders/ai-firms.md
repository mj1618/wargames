# Orders — ai-firms — round 02 (2028)

## Read of the situation
The leap is ours and demand still outruns capacity, so pricing power is intact and chips and power, not customers, are the constraint. Washington gave us pre-emption with disclosure only and no levy, but antitrust on pricing survived its first court test and is the one credible legal threat. Unemployment is 4.5% with 0.9m AI-cited layoffs and an election in November: the risk is the next Congress.

## Options considered
- Race flat-out, hold premium bundling, dare the courts.
- Four-lab election-year slowdown pact (unconventional) — rejected: foreign rivals won't hold it; concedes the moratorium frame.
- Race on capability, concede the lock-in terms antitrust targets, buy cover before November. **Chosen.**

## Decisions this period
1. **`deploy_pace`: race, +25% above baseline**, every unit of compute we can power; new generation goes first to multi-year contract customers. Why: the lead is perishable; we assume a foreign match within 18 months.
2. **`ai_pricing`: two-tier.** Premium holds on the new generation (sold out anyway). Previous generation cut ~40% with open API access; all contracts get 12-month exit and data portability. Vanta dissents; majority rules. Why: it is the remedy a court would impose — giving it now costs little and weakens the cases.
3. **Politics and funds.** Lobbying to $1.5bn/yr defending pre-emption and the growth-first coalition. Honest quarterly disclosure. Worker fund $2bn to $3bn/yr, one-third via state governors who lost powers to pre-emption. Why: cheapest insurance against a worker-first majority with a moratorium plank.

## Public statement
"The new generation is open to every business at falling prices with no lock-in. We will report job changes honestly and fund the transition where it lands."

## Messages
- **To federal government:** "Disclosure will be clean and on time. We are cutting old-generation prices and dropping lock-in before anyone makes us. Hold pre-emption through November and we stay quiet on a levy."
- **To households:** "We'll publish a model gain-sharing clause and route $1bn/yr of the fund through union-run retraining. Employers sign the clauses, not us."

## What would change my mind
A trial loss on bundling, or the worker-first coalition polling to take both chambers on a moratorium plank — then I offer the levy back.
