# Orders — ai-firms — round 02 (2028)

## Read of the situation
The leap is ours and demand still outruns capacity, so pricing power is intact and the binding constraint is chips and power, not customers. Washington gave us pre-emption with disclosure only and no levy — better than we offered — but antitrust on pricing has survived its first court test, which is now the one credible legal threat to our model. Unemployment is 4.5% with 0.9m AI-cited layoffs and an election in November: the risk is not this Congress but the next one.

## Options considered
- Race flat-out, hold premium bundling, dare the antitrust courts.
- Voluntary election-year slowdown pact among the four labs (unconventional) — rejected: foreign rivals won't hold it and it concedes the moratorium frame.
- Race on capability, give ground voluntarily on the lock-in terms the antitrust cases target, buy political cover cheaply before November. **Chosen.**

## Decisions this period
1. **`deploy_pace`: race, +25% above baseline**, every unit of compute we can power. New generation allocated first to multi-year contract customers. Why: the lead is perishable; a foreign match within 18 months is our planning assumption.
2. **`ai_pricing`: two-tier.** Premium pricing holds on the new generation (capacity is sold out anyway). Previous generation cut ~40% with open API access, and all contracts get 12-month exit and full data portability. Vanta dissents on exit terms; majority rules. Why: this is the conduct remedy a court would impose — giving it now costs little and weakens the cases.
3. **Politics and funds.** Lobbying to $1.5bn/yr, aimed at the growth-first coalition's November defence of pre-emption. Full, honest quarterly layoff disclosure as asked. Worker fund raised from $2bn to $3bn/yr, one-third routed through state governors who lost rule-making powers to pre-emption. Why: cheapest insurance against a worker-first majority with a moratorium plank.

## Public statement
"The new generation is available to every business, large or small, at falling prices and with no lock-in. We will report our customers' job changes honestly and triple-fund the transition where it lands."

## Messages
- **To federal government:** "Disclosure will be clean and on time. We are cutting old-generation prices and dropping lock-in before anyone makes us. Hold pre-emption through November and we stay quiet on a levy."
- **To households:** "We'll publish a model gain-sharing and redeployment clause and fund $1bn/yr of the transition fund through union-run retraining. Clauses themselves are employers' to sign, not ours."

## What would change my mind
A loss at trial on bundling, or the worker-first coalition polling to take both chambers on an explicit moratorium plank, and I would offer the levy back.
