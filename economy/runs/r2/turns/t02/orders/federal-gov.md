# Orders — Federal government — round 02 (2028)

## 1. Read of the situation
Unemployment is 4.5% and jobs fell 0.6m, with about 0.9m AI-cited layoffs and more roles closed by not hiring: visible enough to hurt in November, not yet a crisis. Median income (101.6) and housing costs (down 2%) are our good news; labour share at 51.7% and wage-tax receipts slipping (revenue 17.2%, deficit 6.2%) are the bad. The generation leap means the numbers get worse after the vote, and the opposition will pitch cheques.

## 2. Options considered
- Stand pat: run on income growth and cheaper housing, pass nothing.
- Take the labs' deal: capped levy funding worker support, in exchange for pre-emption and no deployment limits.
- Unconventional: a universal "AI dividend" cheque mailed before November, deficit-financed.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Bill 1 — Worker Transition Act.** `capital_tax_pct` = 5 on AI profits, sunset 2032, as the labs offered; proceeds fund `retraining_pct_gdp` raised from 0.1 to 0.4 (wage insurance, extended to never-hired graduates) plus 60-day AI-layoff notice. Keeps pre-emption; no licensing. Roughly deficit-neutral, starts 2029. Deregulators lose on the levy; deficit hawks and security hawks win.
2. **Bill 2 — Work-sharing and new-firm credit.** Subsidy letting employers cut hours instead of jobs, plus easier small-business credit; about 0.15% of GDP. Employers get their voluntary no-mass-layoff pledge written in as the condition for the subsidy. Deregulators win: nothing mandatory.
3. **Executive action.** Front-load the 2028 housing grants and retraining enrolment into swing metros before November; keep antitrust cases moving on AI pricing. No universal cheque: deficit hawks win, bond market first.

## 4. Public statement
"America leads in AI, and the firms that profit will pay for the workers who carry the change: notice, wage insurance and cheaper homes, with no new taxes on families."

**Messages**
- To AI firms: Deal accepted at 5%, sunset 2032, pre-emption kept, no licensing. We need your public backing and donor calm before the floor vote.
- To households: Your notice and wage-insurance bill is moving this year, paid for by AI profits, not by you.

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment past 5.5% or a failed bond auction before November would push me to targeted transfers or to drop the spending respectively.
