# Sitrep — round 02 (2028)

**Jobs.** Employment fell again, faster. 3.91m jobs were destroyed (1.92m office and professional) and 2.35m created, 1.33m of them in new kinds of work. After population growth, employment dropped 0.9m to 160.9m. Employers again used freezes more than firing, so unemployment only edged up to 4.4%; the share of adults working fell from 58.65% to 58.1%, a full point below 2026.

**Pay and prices.** Real pay rose 1.2%. Housing costs fell another 2.9% as federal grants and state zoning overrides took hold. Median real income 101.2; bottom fifth 101.3.

**Businesses.** After the generation leap machines could do 31.5% of desk work, nearly double a year earlier; firms used 9.5%, also double. Labour's share fell 1.4 points to 50.3%; top-1% share 21.1%. GDP grew 2.6%.

**Government.** The Worker Transition Act passed at half size (2.5% levy on AI profits, smaller wage insurance, 30-day notice, from 2029). The work-sharing and new-firm credit bill passed whole. The growth-first coalition kept its lead in November, but Washington is now divided. Deficit 6.4%, debt 104.3%.

**Daily life.** Poverty 12.6%, homelessness 19.0 per 10,000. The office organising drive failed.

**What mattered most.** The generation leap on top of a faster race and faster adoption. And the election draw: the same coalition, with much less room to legislate.

**What a household would have noticed:** rent down again, a small profit-share cheque, and whole office teams quietly not being replaced.
