# Intel — Households — round 03

**January–December 2029.** Growth-first still leads after November, but Congress is now divided.

**Numbers (end 2028).** Unemployment 4.3%; participation 60.8%; 160.9m jobs (down 0.9m); GDP +2.6%; housing costs down 3%, other prices slightly lower; median real income 100.7; labour share 50.3%; deficit 6.4%. AI-cited layoffs: about 1.6m. Poverty 12.6%, homelessness 19.0 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Cheap services catch on.** Households start buying AI-delivered tutoring, legal help and home design in bulk, with people in the loop.

**Only you see.** The office organising drive stalled; existing contracts fade if not kept up. Rents fell again; some employers paid small profit-shares. Whole office teams are not being replaced; small new firms are hiring. Wage insurance and 30-day notice start this year, smaller than promised.

**Messages.**
- From AI firms: "We'll publish a model gain-sharing clause and route $1bn/yr of the fund through union-run retraining. Employers sign the clauses, not us."
- From employers: "Open to gain-sharing and hours reductions with pay protection; no blanket no-layoff clause. Let's draft the framework together before any announcement."
- From federal government: "Your notice and wage-insurance bill is moving this year, paid for by AI profits, not by you."
