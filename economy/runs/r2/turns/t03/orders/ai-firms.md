# Orders — AI firms — round 03 (2029)

## Read of the situation
We got our deal: a 2.5% levy sunsetting 2032, pre-emption intact, no licensing — and Washington still wants our public backing, so we have leverage to spend. The new generation is sold out, old-generation volumes jumped after the 40% cut while rivals matched only half, and margins held: compute, not demand, is the constraint. The threats are antitrust (cases continue despite dropping lock-in) and a divided Congress where 1.6m AI-cited layoffs and a 50.3% labour share hand the worker-first side a moratorium frame.

## Options considered
- Race +30%, raise new-generation prices, let antitrust run.
- Steady pace, broad price cuts to kill the antitrust story.
- Unconventional: offer DOJ a consent decree now (open access, portability, no exclusives) and own the "cheap services" headline with a household subsidy.

## Decisions this period
1. **`deploy_pace`: race +30%**, compute-limited. Capacity sold by multi-year, non-exclusive reservations with 12-month exit — meets employers without reinstating lock-in.
2. **`ai_pricing`: two-tier.** New generation up ~15% (sold out). Old generation open-access, cut a further 20%, with a household tier for tutoring, legal and design. Employers: volume discounts for 3-year commitments; agentic-work indemnity capped at contract value, excluding misuse.
3. **Politics and funds.** Publicly endorse the Worker Transition Act; donors quiet through the vote. Lobbying $1.5bn, aimed at a DOJ consent decree and blocking any moratorium or licensing rider. Worker fund $3bn/yr plus $1bn/yr "services for every household" subsidy.

## Public statement
"We back the Worker Transition Act and will pay its levy in full. This year tutoring, legal help and design will cost every household less than a streaming subscription."

**Messages**
- To federal government: "You have our endorsement and donor calm. In return: a DOJ consent decree on access and portability this year, and no licensing or pause rider on anything."
- To employers: "Yes to 3-year capacity at locked pricing, volume discounts, non-exclusive, 12-month exit. Indemnity capped at contract value, excluding misuse. Sign by Q2 for new-generation allocation."

## What would change my mind
A moratorium plank polling above 50% with growth-first defections, or a rival breaking ranks on pace to take our sold-out customers.
