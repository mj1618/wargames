# Orders — Entrepreneurs — turn 03 (2029)

## Read of the situation

End 2028 saw unemployment at 4.3% and median income at 100.7, yet my network added 1.3m new service-sector jobs—triple 2027's pace. Factory-built housing is selling in zoning-override states and margins recovered as old-generation AI got cheap after lock-in ended. Households are signalling demand for care, trades, repair and design work that keeps people in the loop and are asking about ownership terms.

## Options considered

1. **Aggressive expansion**: Scale factory-built housing and people-heavy services into every viable sector (care, repair, tutoring, legal, design), hiring to match demand.
2. **Profit mode**: Take margins now; pause hiring and shift capital to high-margin AI-only plays where 2028 competition restored unit economics.
3. **Co-op play** (unconventional): Launch worker-owned firms in care and trades with households as partners, betting that cooperative models beat VC-funded competitors in high-trust sectors.

## Decisions this period

1. **Expand factory-built housing 80% YoY ($400m capital allocation)**: Scale across zoning-override states; hire into design, supply chain, on-site construction and sales. Why: proven demand, falling costs, direct supply-side relief.

2. **Launch worker-owned service co-ops ($50m across 10 metro areas)**: Care, home repair, tutoring, light legal in major metros. Worker ownership; transparent wages; profit-sharing. Why: households asked for ownership terms; co-op models drive customer retention and solve the people-heavy hiring challenge via ownership incentives.

3. **Hold AI-only plays flat**: Maintain current operations, no new capital. Why: capital returns highest in factory housing and people-heavy services where demand is proven and competitive intensity is lower.

## Public statement

"The new economy's real jobs come from services people actually want to buy. We're scaling factory-built housing and hiring in care, trades and repair—human-centered work. Cooperative ownership is what households are asking for and what builders want."

## Messages

**To: Households**
"Worker-owned service co-ops launching early 2029 in major metros. Care, trades, repair, light legal. Ownership, transparent wages, profit-sharing. Ready to build with you."

**To: Congress**
"Keep small-business credit simple and available—launch is critical. Housing grants need three-year certainty. Antitrust on AI pricing helps margins for all founders scaling new work."

## What would change my mind

If Congress cuts small-business credit or reverses housing-override grants, I shift capital to margin-focused AI-only plays and pull from people-heavy services.
