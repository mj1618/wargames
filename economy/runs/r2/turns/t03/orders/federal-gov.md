# Orders — federal-gov — round 03 (2029)

## 1. Read of the situation
Unemployment reads 4.3%, but jobs fell another 0.9m to 160.9m, participation is 60.8% and AI-cited layoffs hit 1.6m: people are leaving the count, not finding work. Voters still feel cheaper living (housing costs down 3%, median income 100.7), which is why we kept our lead. Revenue slipped to 17.0% and the deficit is 6.4%, and with a divided Congress only half of small bills pass.

## 2. Options considered
- Sit tight: let the Worker Transition Act and work-sharing start, pass nothing.
- Small bipartisan bills on housing and new firms; antitrust by executive action.
- Unconventional: a universal "AI dividend" cheque funded by tripling the levy — popular, but dead in this Congress and breaks our deal with the labs.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Housing Supply Act (small bill).** Move `housing_policy` to funded building with zoning conditions: +0.15% of GDP a year from 2030, favouring factory-built homes. Falling rents are our best re-election asset and worker-first members will vote for it. Deregulators win; deficit hawks get a five-year sunset.
2. **New Firms and Young Workers Act (small bill).** Easier credit for new firms plus a first-job hiring credit for under-27s, about 0.1% of GDP, from 2030. Targets the participation leak where it is visible (graduates) without a new entitlement. Growth-first deregulators win; labour gets the hiring credit.
3. **Executive action, no vote.** Press the AI pricing antitrust cases toward open-access settlements (`competition_policy`: cases, seeking open-access terms by consent). Hold pre-emption and the 2.5% levy unchanged; no cheques, no public jobs. Security hawks and deficit hawks win.

## 4. Public statement
"Living costs are falling and we intend to keep it that way: more homes, more new businesses, a first job for every graduate — and AI firms that compete rather than lock customers in."

**To AI firms:** Pre-emption holds and the levy stays at 2.5%. In return, settle the pricing cases on open-access terms and keep disclosure clean.

**To entrepreneurs:** Credit bill and open-access antitrust are yours this year. Deliver visible hiring and homes before 2030, and say publicly who did it.

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment past 5.5%, participation below 60%, or revenue under 16.7% would push us to a larger levy funding targeted transfers.
