# Intel — Entrepreneurs — round 04

**January–December 2030.** Growth-first leads a divided Congress; congressional elections in November.

**Numbers (end 2029).** Unemployment 3.5%; participation 61.1%; 164.0m jobs (up 3.1m); GDP +5.9%; housing costs down about 1%, professional services cheaper; median real income 106.0; labour share 50.1%; deficit 6.0%. AI-cited layoffs: about 1.5m. Poverty 11.6%, homelessness 17.8 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Financial wobble.** AI shares fall by a third; credit tightens for a year.

**Only you see.** Firms in your network added about 2.8m jobs in new kinds of work in 2029, double 2028. The co-ops launched in ten metros and filled quickly. The factory-housing expansion is complete; further gains now depend on policy, not on you. The Housing Supply Act (funded building, favouring factory-built homes) and a half-size new-firms and first-job credit start this year. The pricing cases settled on open access. Investors are now pulling back: new funding rounds are smaller and slower.

**Messages.**
- From federal government: "Credit bill and open-access antitrust are yours this year. Deliver visible hiring and homes before 2030, and say publicly who did it."
