# Intel — Households — round 04

**January–December 2030.** Growth-first leads a divided Congress; congressional elections in November.

**Numbers (end 2029).** Unemployment 3.5%; participation 61.1%; 164.0m jobs (up 3.1m); GDP +5.9%; housing costs down about 1%, professional services cheaper; median real income 106.0; labour share 50.1%; deficit 6.0%. AI-cited layoffs: about 1.5m. Poverty 11.6%, homelessness 17.8 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Financial wobble.** AI shares fall by a third; credit tightens for a year.

**Only you see.** The targeted drive failed; existing contracts were kept up but fade if not renewed. Small firms and co-ops hired heavily; people who had stopped looking came back to work. Profit-share cheques grew; rent fell again; hours are slightly shorter. Wage insurance is paying out, at the smaller size. Some neighbours' savings fell with the share market, and loans are harder to get.
