# Orders — AI firms — round 04 (2030)

## Read of the situation
The economy is our best argument: unemployment 3.5%, 164.0m jobs (up 3.1m), GDP +5.9%, median income 106 — displacement is a hard sell in an election year. The threat is now the balance sheet: shares down a third, credit tight, while new-gen stays sold out and old-gen volumes surge. Pre-emption holds, levy 2.5% to 2032, cases settled — this year's job is financing the race without the equity market.

## Options considered
- Baseline pace, hoard cash (Vanta's view; the rest call it ceding the frontier).
- Race +30% on expensive debt (too dear).
- Unconventional: finance compute off customers' balance sheets via prepaid capacity, plus federal loan guarantees.

## Decisions this period
1. **Pace [`deploy_pace`]: race +10%**, concentrated on new-gen, funded by prepaid capacity not equity. Vanta would slow further; majority holds.
2. **Pricing [`ai_pricing`]: new-gen premium +10%** (sold out); **old-gen cut 20%**, no lock-in, portable per the consent decree — offered as a **3-year prepaid capacity contract** (pay up front, get the floor price). This is our financing.
3. **Politics and funds:** lobbying $1.5bn — defend pre-emption and the 2032 sunset, block a moratorium plank in November, seek loan guarantees and faster power permitting. Worker fund **held at $3bn/yr**; the $1bn household subsidy **ends** (volumes grew without it).

## Public statement
"Three million new jobs and the fastest growth in a generation — that is what deployment looks like. Prices keep falling and the worker fund keeps paying, in good markets and bad."

## Messages
- **To employers:** "Agreed: old-gen down 20%, no lock-in, portable, as 3-year prepaid capacity at the floor price; sign this quarter and the floor is locked."
- **To federal government:** "Cases settled, levy paid, disclosure clean. Credit now constrains American compute: we ask for data-centre loan guarantees and faster power permitting; in return old-gen prices keep falling and the fund stays at $3bn."

## What would change my mind
A rival racing +30% on cheap sovereign capital, or a moratorium plank polling above 40%, pushes us to +30% regardless.
