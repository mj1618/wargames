# Orders — federal-gov — round 04 (2030)

## 1. Read of the situation
The numbers are the best we have had: unemployment 3.5%, jobs up 3.1m to 164.0m, GDP +5.9%, median income 106.0, poverty 11.6%, deficit back to 6.0%. The one visible threat before November is the wobble — AI shares down a third and credit tightening — which could choke the new firms and home-building our record rests on. Labour share is 50.1% and AI-cited layoffs still ran 1.5m, but voters are not feeling that yet; with half of small bills passing, we defend what works.

## 2. Options considered
- Do nothing: run on the boom and let the wobble pass.
- Small credit-and-housing certainty bill plus a consent decree by executive action.
- Unconventional: a public fund buying AI shares in the dip, dividends to households later — hawks would kill it and it looks like a bailout.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Main Street Credit and Housing Certainty Act (small bill).** Federal loan guarantees for new and small firms and factory-home builders while credit is tight; restore the New Firms credit to full size (+0.05% of GDP, total 0.1%); lock Housing Supply Act grants for three years. Simple application, no new entitlement. Deregulators win; deficit hawks get guarantees rather than outlays.
2. **Consent decree, no vote.** DOJ files a consent decree with the labs on open access and portability this year (`competition_policy`: open-access terms by consent). No licensing, no pause rider, no break-ups; no rescue for AI shareholders. Security hawks and deregulators win.
3. **Wage insurance delivered, no vote.** Order payment within 30 days at the promised level, funded from the 2.5% levy; audit layoff disclosure. Levy rate, pre-emption and `transfers_pct_gdp` (zero) unchanged. Deficit hawks win; labour gets delivery.

## 4. Public statement
"More jobs, higher incomes, cheaper homes. Wall Street's wobble will not reach Main Street: credit for new businesses stays open, and no bailouts for speculators."

**To AI firms:** Decree on access and portability this year; no licensing or pause rider. Keep hiring and prices steady through the wobble.

**To households:** Wage insurance paid within 30 days, at the promised level, from the levy. Disclosure gets audited.

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment past 5%, a credit crunch that stops home-building, or revenue under 16.7% would move us to a larger levy funding targeted transfers.
