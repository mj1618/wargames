# Sitrep — round 04 (2030)

**Jobs.** The worst year so far. 8.95m jobs were destroyed: 3.56m by automation, 5.32m because spending fell. Only 3.20m were created (1.87m in new kinds of work). Employment fell 5.1m to 158.9m. Unemployment rose from 3.5% to 4.5%, but that understates it: most who lost work left the labour force. The share of adults working fell from 59.0% to 56.9%. Office jobs fell 1.9m, retail 1.7m.

**Pay and prices.** Real pay fell 1.1%. Housing costs fell 4.7%; professional services got cheaper. Median real income fell from 106.0 to 102.8; bottom fifth 103.0. Weekly hours 33.8.

**Businesses.** Machines could do 53% of desk work and did 21%; robots could do 22% of physical work, up from 10%. GDP grew 0.5%. Labour's share fell three points to 47.1%; top-1% share 22.3%.

**Government.** The Main Street credit bill failed. Deficit 6.5%, debt 106.7%. In November growth-first kept its lead; Congress stays divided.

**Daily life.** Poverty 12.2%, homelessness 16.7 per 10,000, still falling with rents. The union renewal drive gained nothing.

**What mattered most.** The financial wobble arriving as 2029's spending boost ended: lost wages snowballed into lost sales. And founders pulling back just then, so new work fell by a third while automation carried on at nearly the same pace.

**What a household would have noticed:** a neighbour quietly out of work and not looking, shops closing, a smaller pay packet, and cheaper rent.
