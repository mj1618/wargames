# Intel — Households — round 05

**January 2031–December 2032.** Growth-first kept its lead in November 2030; Congress still divided. Presidential and congressional elections November 2032.

**Numbers (end 2030).** Unemployment 4.3%, since revised to 4.7% (this period's headline: **Data revision**); participation 59.6%; 158.9m jobs (down 5.1m); GDP +0.5%; median real income 101.8; labour share 47.1%; deficit 6.5%. Announced layoffs about 3.6m. Poverty 12.2%, homelessness 16.7 per 10,000.

**Only you see.** The renewal drive gained nothing; contracts fade unless renewed. Many neighbours lost work; most stopped looking. Pay and profit-share cheques shrank; rent fell sharply. Co-ops kept hiring but lack credit.

**Messages.**
- From employers: "Agree to draft the framework now. We can commit to 60-day notice, redeployment support and quarterly aggregate pay-out reporting; a binding 90-day floor is not agreeable yet."
- From federal government: "Wage insurance paid within 30 days, at the promised level, from the levy. Disclosure gets audited."
- From entrepreneurs (via co-op networks): "We're hiring in care and trades. Co-ops are filling fast because they work: ownership, predictable work, real demand. If you're looking for a job that can't be moved overseas or replaced by automation next year, the skilled trades and care services are it."
