# Orders — ai-firms — round 05 (2031–32)

## Read of the situation
The wobble hit customers, not us: new generation sold out, old-generation volumes rose, prepaid contracts funded compute, margins held while 5.1m jobs vanished (unemployment revised to 4.7%). Robots now work reliably in physical settings — commercially enormous, politically visible. The levy sunsets in 2032, a presidential year, with labour share at 47.1%: the pay-more-or-get-paused fight is now.

## Options considered
- Race +30% while rivals are capital-starved; cheapest lead ever, but gifts the moratorium camp its headline.
- Slow down −30% through the election; Vanta and foreign labs won't follow.
- Unconventional: turn the worker fund into a free household old-generation tier before November 2032.

## Decisions this period
1. **deploy_pace: steady in 2031, +15% in 2032** as credit reopens. Robots leased as a service, shortage sectors first (care, factory housing, logistics). No lab headcount cuts in 2031 — cheap, and asked for.
2. **ai_pricing: accept employers' 3-year capacity terms, modified.** Old-generation step-down 10%/yr (we'd cut anyway), indemnity 1.5x contract value excluding consequential loss, non-exclusive, 12-month exit; Q2 2031 signature funds compute without equity. New-generation premium flat through 2031, household tier kept.
3. **Politics: levy-for-certainty.** Publicly back extending the 2.5% levy to 2036 for statutory no-pause, no-licensing language and pre-emption of state robot rules. Lobbying $2bn over the cycle. Fund $3bn, plus $1bn/yr robot apprenticeships in trades and care, co-funded with employers — cover for the physical rollout.

## Public statement
"We held prices and jobs through the downturn and will keep doing so. We back extending the worker levy; in return we need certainty that America keeps building."

## Messages
- **To employers:** Yes to 3-year capacity by Q2: 10%/yr old-generation step-down, indemnity 1.5x excluding consequential loss, non-exclusive, 12-month exit. Co-fund robot apprenticeships and your shortage sectors go first.
- **To federal government:** We back a levy extension to 2036 and hold prices and hiring through 2031. Price: statutory no-pause/no-licensing language and pre-emption of state robot rules before the 2032 campaign.

## What would change my mind
A moratorium plank polling above 45%, or a rival breaking pace, flips me to race +30% and a price war before rules land.
