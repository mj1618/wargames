# Orders — ai-firms — round 05 (2031–32)

## Read of the situation
The wobble hit customers, not us: new generation sold out, old-generation volumes rose, prepaid contracts funded compute without new equity, and margins held while customers' sales fell and 5.1m jobs vanished (unemployment revised to 4.7%). Robots now work reliably in many physical settings, which opens construction, care and trades — commercially enormous, politically visible. The levy sunsets in 2032, a presidential year, with labour share at 47.1%: the fight over whether we pay more or get paused happens this period.

## Options considered
- Race +30% while rivals are capital-starved; cheapest lead-extension we will ever buy, but hands the moratorium camp its headline.
- Voluntary slow-down −30% through the election; only holds if Vanta and foreign labs follow, and they won't.
- Unconventional: convert the worker fund into a free household old-generation tier ("compute dividend") before November 2032.

## Decisions this period
1. **deploy_pace: steady in 2031, +15% in 2032** as credit reopens. Robots sold as robot-as-a-service leases to employers, prioritising shortage sectors (care, factory housing, logistics) where the "no one to hire" framing is true. No lab headcount cuts in 2031 — cheap, and government asked.
2. **ai_pricing: accept employers' 3-year capacity terms with modifications.** Price step-down 10%/yr on old generation (we'd cut anyway), indemnity 1.5x contract value excluding consequential loss, non-exclusive, 12-month exit. Signature by Q2 2031 funds compute without equity. New-generation premium held flat through 2031 (government's "prices steady"), old generation −20% and household tier kept.
3. **Politics: levy-for-certainty.** Publicly support extending the 2.5% levy to 2036 in exchange for a statutory no-pause, no-licensing clause and federal pre-emption of state robot-deployment rules. Lobbying $2bn across the election cycle. Fund held at $3bn, plus $1bn/yr robot apprenticeships in trades and care, co-funded with employers — cover for the physical rollout.

## Public statement
"We held prices and jobs through the downturn and will keep doing so. We support extending the worker levy; what we need in return is certainty that America keeps building."

## Messages
- **To employers:** Yes to 3-year capacity by Q2: 10%/yr step-down on old generation, indemnity 1.5x excluding consequential loss, non-exclusive, 12-month exit. Co-fund robot apprenticeships with us and we lead with your shortage sectors.
- **To federal government:** We will back a levy extension to 2036 and hold prices and hiring through 2031. Price: statutory no-pause/no-licensing language and pre-emption of state robot rules, before the 2032 campaign.

## What would change my mind
A moratorium plank polling above 45% nationally, or a rival lab breaking steady pace, flips me to race +30% and a price war to lock in share before the rules land.
