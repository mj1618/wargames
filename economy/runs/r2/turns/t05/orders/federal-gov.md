# Orders — Federal government — round 05 (2031–32)

## 1. Read of the situation
The crisis is now in the numbers: 158.9m jobs (down 5.1m), 3.6m announced layoffs, unemployment revised to 4.7%, participation 59.6% because most who lost work simply left. Median income fell from 106.0 to 101.8 and labour share is 47.1%; cheaper housing (−5%) is our only good story. Revenue is 16.9%, deficit 6.5%, debt 107%, the levy expires in 2032 and we face a presidential election in November 2032 with a third-chance on medium bills.

## 2. Options considered
- Hold course: renew the 2.5% levy, small credit bill, run on cheaper living.
- Cheques before November: targeted transfers funded partly by a bigger levy.
- Unconventional: trade the labs' data-centre guarantees for an equity-style "compute dividend" paid to households.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **American Transition Dividend Act (medium bill).** Renew the levy to 2036 and raise it to 6%; pay transfers of 1.0% of GDP aimed at the bottom two-fifths, plus wage insurance extended to 52 weeks for leavers who re-enter job search. Cheques land in 2032. Why: voters punish 5.1m lost jobs; the opposition will offer cheques if we don't. Faction: populists and fiscal pragmatists beat the deficit hawks, who get the levy as offset.
2. **Build and Hire Act (small bill).** Full-size new-firms credit and loan guarantees (0.15% GDP), plus data-centre loan guarantees and faster power permitting — conditional on the labs' fund rising to $5bn and public backing for bill 1. Why: credit is the bottleneck for both hiring and compute. Faction: security hawks and deregulators win.
3. **Executive action.** Fast-track federal power permitting now; order the statistics office to publish monthly labour-force-exit and AI-layoff data. No licensing, no public jobs, pre-emption held.

## 4. Public statement
"The boom's gains will reach every household: a transition dividend paid for by AI profits, credit for the firms that hire, and homes that keep getting cheaper."

**Messages**
- To AI firms: "You get guarantees and permitting. Price: 6% levy to 2036, fund at $5bn, and you say so publicly. Otherwise November brings someone who licenses you."
- To entrepreneurs: "New-firms credit is back, full size, with guarantees. We need visible hiring numbers by mid-2032 — announce them."

## 5. What would change my mind
Bond yields spiking or revenue under 16.5% would shrink the dividend; unemployment past 6% would push me to public jobs and sector licensing.
