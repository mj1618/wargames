# Intel — Federal government — round 06

**January 2033–December 2034.** Growth-first kept the presidency in November 2032; Congress still divided. Congressional elections November 2034.

**Numbers (end 2032).** Unemployment 3.6%; participation 60.3%; 163.8m jobs (up 4.9m); GDP +6.2% a year; median real income 113.2; labour share 46.0%; deficit 5.7%. Headline: **Housing squeeze** — AI wealth bids up land in a few metros; insurers pull out of others.

**Only you see.** The Transition Dividend Act failed: the levy expired at the end of 2032 and no dividend was paid. Build and Hire passed without data-centre guarantees (the labs' fund stopped at $4bn). Revenue 16.8%; debt about 102%. Wage-insurance claims fell. Half of small bills pass, a third of medium ones.

**Messages.**
- From AI firms: "We back a levy extension to 2036 and hold prices and hiring through 2031. Price: statutory no-pause/no-licensing language and pre-emption of state robot rules before the 2032 campaign."
- From entrepreneurs: "The Main Street credit bill's failure means we're slowing growth to sustainable rates. You have the policy tools—small-business credit guarantees, housing funding—but they're not deployed. If federal credit flows again, growth accelerates. Until then, we're at 1m jobs/year."
