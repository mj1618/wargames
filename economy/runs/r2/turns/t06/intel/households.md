# Intel — Households — round 06

**January 2033–December 2034.** Growth-first kept the presidency in November 2032; Congress still divided. Congressional elections November 2034.

**Numbers (end 2032).** Unemployment 3.6%; participation 60.3%; 163.8m jobs (up 4.9m); GDP +6.2% a year; median real income 113.2; labour share 46.0%; deficit 5.7%. Headline: **Housing squeeze** — AI wealth bids up land in a few metros; insurers pull out of others.

**Only you see.** The drive in care, trades and co-op shops won contracts with 60-day notice as a floor; they fade unless renewed. Work is easy to find, mostly outside offices. Pay and profit-share cheques grew; the week is shorter. Rent fell, but is now rising in the richest metros. The Transition Dividend Act failed: no cheques.

**Messages.**
- From employers: "Seats accepted for automation plans, 90-day notice and retraining. Signing this quarter; tell us when the committees are formed."
- From entrepreneurs (via co-op networks): "We're testing household-owned equity co-ops: you own shares, you fund expansion, you share returns. This isn't a substitute for fair public credit—it's a hedge against policy failure. Pilot in care, trades, and co-op services; if it works, we scale it."
