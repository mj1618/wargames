# Orders — ai-firms — round 06 (2033–34)

## Read of the situation
Unemployment 3.6%, 163.8m jobs and GDP growing 6.2% a year is the best shield we have ever had, and the levy lapsed for nothing. But labour share at 46.0% plus the housing-squeeze headline make "AI wealth" the 2034 story, and the licensing threat is credible with Congress divided and no no-pause language on the books. Employers are signing prepaid capacity this quarter, which funds the scale-up.

## Options considered
- Refuse the levy and bank on the jobs numbers (Vanta's preference).
- Take the 6% deal as offered.
- Unconventional: put part of the fund into housing in the metros we are inflating.

## Decisions this period
1. **Pace [`deploy_pace`: race +20% 2033, +25% 2034].** Sign employers' terms: floor price locked, 2x indemnity, 3-year prepay; that finances the step-up. Robot leasing scaled hardest in care, logistics and construction, at about 70% of labour cost.
2. **Pricing [`ai_pricing`: premium new-gen, old-gen cut 25%].** Prior generation cut 25% with portability. Robots as a service, no lock-in, to pre-empt a second antitrust round.
3. **Politics: counter, not refuse.** Offer a 4% levy to 2036 and the fund at $5bn/yr ($3.5bn transition and apprenticeships, $1.5bn factory-built housing in data-centre metros) for statutory no-licence/no-pause language and data-centre loan guarantees. Fallback: accept 5% if the clause is in bill text. Endorsement follows the text. Lobbying $2.5bn on the 2034 House map. Labs split 3–1 (Vanta against); majority carries.

## Public statement
"Four million new jobs in two years, and $5bn a year into workers and housing. We will back a fair levy with clear rules — not a licence that hands the frontier to foreign labs."

**To federal government:** 4% to 2036, $5bn fund, public endorsement — conditional on statutory no-licence/no-pause and the guarantees. 5% is our ceiling, only with the clause in text.

**To employers:** Terms accepted as sent. Countersign this quarter and 2033 capacity is yours first.

## What would change my mind
A licensing plank polling above 50%, or a rival breaking pace, and I take 6% at once.
