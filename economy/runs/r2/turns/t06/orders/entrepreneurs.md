# Orders — entrepreneurs — round 06

## Read of the situation
The Build and Hire Act delivered: full new-firms credit with guarantees, and we've already added 4.6m jobs over two years—double our original plan. Equity co-ops are filling with trained workers, households are asking how much credit we need to scale, and AI-only firms aren't hiring at all, which means the margin-over-jobs world isn't our future. The housing squeeze is real in rich metros, but that's not where we need to be—it's the secondary metros and rural areas where credit and scalable service models will dominate.

## Options considered
1. Consolidate: Hold at current scale, milk the co-ops for cash flow, avoid the land squeeze.
2. Blitz secondary metros: Max out new-firms credit, triple co-op count in 50+ secondary metros before AI wealth spreads there.
3. Land hedge (unconventional): Partner with rural co-ops to acquire and hold factory-housing sites as we scale, turning credit into assets before the squeeze reaches them.

## Decisions this period

**Scale co-ops aggressively into secondary metros.** We're taking full new-firms credit (roughly $12–15bn available to our network) and deploying 60% of it into 50+ secondary metros—places like Des Moines, Memphis, Albuquerque, Cedar Rapids. These are where demand for care, trades, repair and craft work is real and land is still cheap. Hiring: target 2.5m jobs over the next two years, mostly people-heavy (co-op model). Pace: open new co-ops every 2–4 weeks per region, staffed by trained workers from households' pipeline.

**Hold AI-only flat.** It's not hiring and margins aren't worth the risk in this policy window. Redirect that capital to people-heavy expansion.

**Tell government and households how much credit we need.** We need the full $12–15bn to hit 2.5m jobs. We're announcing visible hiring: 500k new jobs in 2033, another 1.5m by end 2034.

## Public statement
"We're ready to scale. The Build and Hire Act worked—co-ops are hiring hundreds of thousands. We'll add another 2m jobs in care, trades and services over the next two years if the credit flows. We're going into secondary metros where customers and workers are, not where land is already bid up."

## Messages
**To Federal Government:** We need the full $12–15bn in new-firms credit to hit 2.5m jobs. We're committed to visible hiring: 500k jobs in 2033, 1.5m by end 2034. Secondary metros are our focus.

**To Households:** Send us trained workers. We're hiring 2.5m over two years. Co-ops need skilled care, trades, and repair workers—we'll match them with credit-backed franchises in 50+ metros.

## What would change my mind
If Congress cuts new-firms credit or the housing squeeze spreads to secondary metros and land costs spike nationwide, we pivot back to margin and AI-only.
