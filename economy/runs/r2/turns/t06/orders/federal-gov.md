# Orders — federal-gov — round 06 (2033–34)

## 1. Read of the situation
Voters are in a boom: unemployment 3.6%, jobs up 4.9m, GDP +6.2% a year, median income 113.2 — that is why we kept the presidency. The felt pain is now land and insurance (the housing squeeze), not jobs; labour share at 46.0% and participation at 60.3% are still statistics, not street anger. Revenue is 16.8% with deficit 5.7%, the levy has lapsed leaving wage insurance unfunded, and only half of small bills and a third of medium ones pass.

## 2. Options considered
- Go big on housing: pre-emption plus Housing First — likely fails in a divided Congress.
- Two modest bills: funded housing/insurance backstop, and a small levy renewal on the labs' terms.
- Unconventional: a national land-value surcharge on the AI-boom metros, rebated as rent cheques — donors and homeowners would kill it.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Metro Homes and Insurance Act (medium bill).** Raise funded building with zoning conditions by 0.2% of GDP, aimed at the bid-up metros, plus a federal reinsurance backstop (capped, about 0.05% of GDP) where insurers have withdrawn. Starts 2034, before November. Deregulators win on zoning; deficit hawks get the cap.
2. **Levy Renewal Act (small bill).** Restore the 2.5% AI-profits levy to 2036, ring-fenced for wage insurance, in exchange for statutory no-licensing language and pre-emption of state robot rules. Security hawks and deregulators win; it costs the Treasury nothing and the labs have said they back it.
3. **Executive action, no vote.** Push the Build and Hire loan guarantees out at full pace — target $40bn of new-firm credit over two years — and open antitrust inquiries into land-banking and insurer withdrawal in the squeezed metros. No cheques, no public jobs, no capital tax: nothing in the numbers demands them.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Public:** "Incomes are at a record. Our job now is to make sure a home and an insurance policy are within reach in every city the boom touches."

- **To AI firms:** "Deal accepted at 2.5% to 2036 with no-licensing and robot pre-emption. We need your public backing and votes delivered, plus the fund's missing $1bn."
- **To entrepreneurs:** "Guarantees are flowing now under Build and Hire. Show us 1.5m jobs a year and housing starts in the squeezed metros."

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment past 5%, median income falling, or revenue under 16.5% would move me to targeted cheques and a larger levy.
