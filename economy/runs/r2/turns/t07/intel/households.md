# Intel — Households — round 07

**January 2035–December 2036.** Growth-first kept its lead in November 2034; Congress is now gridlocked. Elections November 2036.

**Numbers (end 2034).** Unemployment 4.2%; participation 59.3%; 161.5m jobs (down 2.4m); GDP +5.1% a year; median real income 116.5; labour share 41.8%; deficit 5.8%. For 2032: poverty 10.4%, homelessness 15.3 per 10,000. Headline: **A state pilot works** — a large state's wage-insurance-plus-cash pilot shows faster re-employment at modest cost.

**Only you see.** The drive to extend contracts won nothing new; existing contracts were renewed and hold, but fade unless renewed again. Hourly pay and profit-share cheques grew; the week is down to 32.3 hours. Rent fell overall. More neighbours have stopped looking for work than are counted unemployed. No dividend: the levy renewal failed.
