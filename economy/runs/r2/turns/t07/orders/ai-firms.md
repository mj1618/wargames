# Orders — ai-firms — round 07 (2035–36)

## Read of the situation
Jobs fell 2.4m, labour share is 41.8% and announced layoffs ran 5.5m, yet GDP is growing 5.1% a year and median income is 116.5 — our customers are winning and the public is not sure it is. The Levy Renewal Act failed, so we pay nothing, but we also hold no statutory shield against licensing or a pause, and Congress is gridlocked until the November 2036 election decides the frame. The state wage-insurance-plus-cash pilot working is the cheapest political cover on offer: someone will scale it, and we would rather be its funder than its target.

## Options considered
- Voluntary 15% slow-down in 2036 to defuse the election — rejected; foreign labs will not match and the capacity deal is already sold.
- Walk away from Washington, pocket the levy savings, lobby against everything — tempting in gridlock, naked if worker-first wins 2036.
- Unconventional: pay the 2.5% voluntarily into state-run wage-insurance-plus-cash schemes, making the levy ours before it is theirs — partly adopted below.

## Decisions this period
1. **deploy_pace: race**, +25% in 2035 and +20% in 2036 on the countersigned employer capacity deal (floor locked, 2x indemnity). Robot leasing goes first to care, construction and logistics, where shortages blunt the displacement story. Compute is prepaid; gridlock means no rule bites before 2037.
2. **ai_pricing: premium on new generation (+10%), old generation −25% with portability.** Robot-as-a-service priced at about 60% of loaded labour cost with a 12-month exit. Frontier margin, old-gen volume, antitrust kept quiet.
3. **Politics and fund.** Accept the government's deal and back it publicly. Lobbying $2.5bn: $1bn to whip votes for the no-licensing bill, $1bn into 2036 campaigns against any moratorium or licensing plank in both coalitions, $0.5bn in state capitals against state levies and for data-centre permitting. Fund to $5bn/yr: the missing $1bn starts in 2035 as dollar-for-dollar matching to states scaling the pilot; a further $1bn/yr sits in escrow, released the day no-licensing and robot pre-emption become law.

## Public statement
We back the Act: a 2.5% contribution to 2036, written into law, in exchange for one national rulebook. Starting now, we match every dollar a state puts into scaling the wage-insurance-plus-cash pilot that worked.

## Messages
- **To federal government:** Yes. Public backing today; the $1bn starts in 2035 via state pilot matching; a further $1bn/yr is escrowed and released when no-licensing and robot pre-emption are law. $1bn of our lobbying goes to your whip count. If the bill is not on the floor by mid-2036 we treat the deal as lapsed.
- **To employers:** Floor and 2x indemnity honoured; robot leasing to your shortage sectors first at 60% of loaded labour cost. Keep the 60-day notice and attrition path — a layoff headline in election year costs us both.

## What would change my mind
A moratorium or licensing plank polling above 50% and adopted by either coalition by mid-2036: then 2036 goes to steady pace and the escrowed $1bn is released early.
