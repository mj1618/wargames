# Orders — ai-firms — round 07 (2035–36)

## Read of the situation
Jobs fell 2.4m and labour share is 41.8%, yet GDP grows 5.1% a year and median income is 116.5 — our customers are winning and the public is unsure it is. The Levy Renewal Act failed: no levy but no statutory shield, and gridlock lasts until November 2036 sets the frame. The state pilot is the cheapest cover going; better to fund it than be taxed for it.

## Options considered
- Voluntary 15% slow-down in 2036 — foreign labs won't match.
- Walk away from Washington, pocket the savings — naked if worker-first wins.
- Unconventional: pay the 2.5% voluntarily into state wage-insurance-plus-cash schemes, making the levy ours — partly adopted.

## Decisions this period
1. **deploy_pace: race**, +25% in 2035, +20% in 2036, on the countersigned employer deal. Robot leasing goes first to care, construction and logistics, where shortages blunt the displacement story.
2. **ai_pricing: new generation +10% premium, old generation −25% portable.** Robot-as-a-service at 60% of loaded labour cost, 12-month exit.
3. **Politics and fund.** Accept the deal; back it publicly. Lobbying $2.5bn: $1bn to whip the bill, $1bn into 2036 campaigns against any moratorium or licensing plank, $0.5bn against state levies. Fund to $5bn/yr: the missing $1bn matches states scaling the pilot from 2035; $1bn/yr more escrowed until no-licensing and robot pre-emption are law.

## Public statement
We back the Act: 2.5% to 2036, in law, for one national rulebook. We will match every dollar a state puts into scaling the pilot that worked.

## Messages
- **To federal government:** Yes. Backing today; $1bn from 2035 via state matching; $1bn/yr more escrowed until the language is law; $1bn of lobbying to your whip count. No floor vote by mid-2036, the deal lapses.
- **To employers:** Floor and indemnity honoured; robots to your shortage sectors first. Keep attrition — an election-year layoff headline costs us both.

## What would change my mind
A moratorium or licensing plank polling above 50% and adopted by either coalition by mid-2036: 2036 goes steady, escrow releases early.
