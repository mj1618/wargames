# Orders — Entrepreneurs — Turn 07 (2035–36)

## Read of the situation

We delivered 6.1m jobs over the last two years, exceeding our 2.5m target and proving the secondary-metro model works. Government and households are asking us to scale further and also address housing in expensive metros. Congress is gridlocked, which threatens credit certainty and could stall the full Build and Hire implementation we depend on.

## Options considered

1. **Max scale in secondary metros**: Keep the 1.5m/year pace, stay people-heavy services, let housing squeeze resolve itself through migration and time.
2. **Split the bet**: 1m jobs/year in secondary metros, pilot factory-built and co-op housing ventures in three expensive metros to show government we're trying.
3. **Defensive regroup**: Hold at 1m jobs/year, tighten margins in AI-only, wait for credit clarification after November 2036 elections.

Choosing option 2: we deliver the 1.5m number and signal willingness to tackle the metro squeeze, but hedge on credit risk.

## Decisions this period

**1. Scale to 1.5m jobs/year in secondary metros.** People-heavy services (care, trades, repair, supervising robots on-site): 85% of new hiring; AI-only stays flat. $35bn of capital deployed into 75+ metros. Why: Households and government are watching; the co-op model de-risks credit tightness.

**2. Pilot factory-built and co-op housing in three expensive metros** (Bay Area, NYC, LA): $8bn capital, 50k housing units over two years, anchored by worker-owned service co-ops (childcare, elder care, home repair). Why: Addresses federal pressure, shows we're not ignoring the squeeze, and the co-op model creates jobs and ownership stability.

**3. Push Congress for Build and Hire credit stability and antitrust enforcement on AI pricing.** If credit tightens, we scale back to 1m/year and shift to margin mode. Why: Gridlock is our real risk; if guarantees fail, secondary metros will empty.

## Public statement

"Entrepreneurs are on track to deliver 1.5 million jobs per year as requested. We're also testing factory-built and co-op housing in three expensive metros to prove the model works at scale. Full Build and Hire credit deployment is the prerequisite."

**Messages:**
- **To federal government**: "We're scaling to 1.5m/year in secondary metros and piloting co-op housing in expensive metros. Build and Hire must stay stable; if credit tightens, we pull back."
- **To households**: "Co-op board seats and capped fees are permanent. We're testing housing ventures; you control equity and surplus."

## What would change my mind

If Congressional gridlock cuts Build and Hire credit by more than 30%, or customer incomes drop below 2034 levels, I pull back to 1m/year and go margin-only.
