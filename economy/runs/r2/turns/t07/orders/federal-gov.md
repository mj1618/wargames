# Orders — federal-gov — round 07 (2035–36)

## 1. Read of the situation
Voters still feel a boom (income 116.5, GDP +5.1%, unemployment 4.2%), but jobs fell 2.4m, participation is 59.3% and labour share 41.8%. Revenue has dropped to 16.0% with a 5.8% deficit and wage insurance is unfunded since the levy died. Gridlock means only small bills have a real chance (three in ten; large one in twenty), and November 2036 is ahead.

## 2. Options considered
- Go big: a national dividend funded by a 5% levy — one-in-twenty odds, donors furious.
- Go small: copy the state pilot through a federal match, paid by the labs' 4% levy.
- Unconventional: no federal programme at all — hand states a levy-funded block grant and let them own the results and the blame.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **State Transition Match Act (small bill).** Federal match for any state running the wage-insurance-plus-cash pilot model: `retraining_pct_gdp` 0.2, aimed at displaced workers, first payments mid-2036. Funded by `capital_tax_pct` 4 on AI profits to 2036, with the statutory no-licence/no-pause clause in text. Fiscal moderates and deregulators win; it restores revenue and gives a pre-election cheque with a proven model.
2. **New-Firms Credit top-up (small bill).** $13bn over two years for new-firms credit, weighted to secondary metros, disbursed only against verified hires. Deregulators win; visible job creation before November.
3. **Executive action, no Congress.** Extend layoff disclosure to robot-leasing deployments; push Metro Homes money and loan guarantees out within 90 days; keep `deploy_regulation` at disclosure and `competition_policy` at open-access. No universal cheques, no public jobs, no licensing — deficit hawks win.

## 4. Public statement
"A state proved that fast wage insurance plus cash gets people back to work at modest cost. We will match every state that does it, paid for by the firms doing best — not by borrowing."

**Messages**
- To AI firms: "Accepted at 4% to 2036 with the clause in text. We need the $5bn fund announced and your endorsement on the day the bill is filed, not after."
- To entrepreneurs: "$13bn credit bill filed, secondary-metro weighted, paid against verified hires. Publish your 1.5m job count by metro now — we need it for the vote."

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment past 5.5%, income falling, or bond yields jumping on 16.0% revenue would push me to a larger levy and a national programme.
