# Journal — federal-gov

Believe: displacement not yet visible to voters; rent is. Committed: housing bill (funded building with zoning conditions, ~0.2% GDP, direct to upzoning metros), small-business credit plus work-sharing (~0.05%), disclosure-only AI rule; no capital tax, no transfers. Told labs the no-tax line holds only if rents and layoffs stay quiet. ## Round 02 (2028)
Believe: displacement is still a hiring freeze; rent and the election dominate; the wobble squeezes receipts. Committed: Build Act (power and factory-built home permitting), AI Workforce Notice Act (90-day notice, statutory reporting, state pre-emption, 0.1% GDP wage insurance from 2029, labs' $5bn); no levy, no rescue, no transfers. Watch: November result, unemployment past 5%, AI layoffs past 1m, capital-gains receipts, state backlash to pre-emption, whether labs pay.

## Round 03 (2029)
Now worker-first. Believe: 3.8m jobs lost and falling participation are the real story; the 4.5% revision hides exits. Committed: Worker Security Act (1% GDP targeted transfers, 0.4% retraining/wage insurance, 8-point capital levy, from 2030); statutory reporting plus 1m public jobs; antitrust cases; no licensing this year. Watch: both votes, bond yields, whether labs pay the $5bn and report in Q2, employers' layoff cap, jobs and participation, 2030 midterm mood.
