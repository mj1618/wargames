# Journal — households

**R01 (2027).** Belief: jobs still mostly safe; the pain is rent, insurance and kids' entry-level prospects; gains are going to profits. Committed: medium-large organising drive (no-layoff, AI notice, profit-sharing); saving rate held; tenant unions and pro-permit turnout, peaceful protest. Watch: layoffs among people we know, entry-level hiring, whether rents and insurance keep climbing, whether any employer or government answers our messages.

**R02 (2028).** Belief: our jobs mostly safe but hiring of the young is stuck; shares and retirement accounts down, credit tight. Committed: accept employers' hours/retraining talks, strike pause only until June; saving +1; peaceful election-year turnout on entry jobs, rents, AI-profit tax; messages to employers and entrepreneurs. Watch: signed deal by June, layoffs among people we know, graduate hiring, rents and insurance.

**R03 (2029).** Belief: May deal (layoff cap, ~1% payroll profit pool) is real but fragile; jobs down 3.8m, many stopped looking, rents easing, our side now holds Washington. Committed: medium enforcement/extension organising drive, no strikes unless deal broken; saving held; peaceful lobbying for wage insurance on time, first-job programme, AI-profit tax; no boycotts. Watch: layoffs after the cap, pool payouts, youth hiring, whether the new government delivers.

**R04 (2030).** Belief: jobs and hours recovering, rents falling, wage insurance live; labour share still below start; our leverage is highest while hiring is strong. Committed: accept employers' package by May (35% hours, top-up, entry cohort, retraining) without a blanket no-strike pledge, with published data and 12-month review; back entrepreneurs' upzoning/licensing deal with local hiring and land-trust stakes; saving down 1; peaceful lobbying for first-job programme and AI-profit levy. Watch: layoff cap compliance, pool payouts, cohort hiring, rents, new industry wages.

**R05 (2031–32).** Belief: tight labour market, rents and poverty falling, but labour share barely up and attrition is how jobs vanish; our leverage peaks now. Committed: medium-large organising drive on attrition floors and bargaining reform, no strikes unless cap/pool broken; saving down 1; peaceful lobbying and 2032 turnout for bargaining reform, levy and first-job funding; no boycotts. Watch: unemployment above 4.5%, entry hiring, employer data sharing, whether Congress passes bargaining reform.

**R06 (2033–34).** Belief: pay beats prices ~3%, rents and poverty falling, but labour share flat and the package is still unsigned; consolidation raises concentration risk. Committed: medium-large organising drive to sign the package (bounded no-strike clause, annual review), saving down 1, peaceful 2034 midterm push for bargaining reform and consolidation scrutiny, conditional support for entrepreneurs' zoning deal, no boycotts. Watch: unemployment over 4.5%, cap breaches, price or hiring effects of the merger, bargaining bill.

**R07 (2035–36).** Belief: pay beats prices ~3%, rents and poverty falling, but labour share 51.1% and attrition quietly cuts entry jobs; the package and bargaining reform are law but our membership is thin. Committed: accept employers' data plus 25% entry-hire floor with a review to lift it to 35%; medium organising drive on automated-decision accountability (liability ruling), no strikes or boycotts; peaceful turnout for bargaining vote, first-job funding; saving down 1. Watch: unemployment over 4.5%, cap/pool breaches, whether the floor is honoured, membership numbers.
