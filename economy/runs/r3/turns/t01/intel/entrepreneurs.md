# Intel — entrepreneurs — round 01

**Period: January–December 2027.** Decide for the whole year.

**The numbers (end 2026).** Unemployment 4.1%; participation 61.6%; 162.4m jobs; labour share 52.8% (record low); profits 14.9% of GDP (record high); poverty 12.9%; homelessness about 22 per 10,000; federal deficit 6.0% of GDP, debt about 100%; median household income, GDP and prices at index 100.

**Politics.** A growth-first coalition leads Washington after the midterms: pro-deployment, tax-averse, sceptical of new entitlements. The big, expensive states are resisting new housing.

**This year's headline: Housing squeeze.** AI wealth is concentrating in a few metros and bidding up land; insurers are pulling out of others. Rents and house prices are jumping.

**Only you see.** A few of you report customers you have never had before — small firms and households buying services that did not exist two years ago — but others say the same about every fad, and it is too early to tell which this is. Seed money is plentiful for anything with AI in the pitch. Commercial rents in the boom metros are climbing fast; outside them, landlords are offering deals.
