# Orders — federal-gov — round 01 (2027)

## 1. Read of the situation
Jobs look normal (unemployment 4.1%, 162.4m jobs), so displacement is not yet a voting issue, though labour share is a record-low 52.8% against record profits of 14.9% of GDP. What voters feel now is rent: the housing squeeze is the headline and homelessness is about 22 per 10,000. With a 6.0% deficit, debt near 100% and 70% of revenue resting on wages, we have little room for new entitlements and an untested caucus.

## 2. Options considered
- Do nothing on AI, pass a supply-side housing bill and claim the boom.
- Pre-emptive wage insurance for young office workers before the numbers turn.
- Unconventional: trade the labs federal pre-emption of state AI rules for a voluntary lab-funded housing and transition fund.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Housing bill (vote 1)** — `housing_policy` = funded building with zoning conditions. About 0.2% of GDP a year from 2028, grants paid directly to metros that upzone, bypassing resistant governors. Why: rents are the one pain voters feel before November 2028; it is deregulation with a ribbon-cutting. Deregulators win; deficit hawks hold it to 0.2% and demand offsets.
2. **Small-business credit and work-sharing subsidy (vote 2)** — easier credit for new firms plus a small work-sharing subsidy, about 0.05% of GDP. Why: cheap, donor-friendly insurance if layoffs show before 2028. Deregulators win; deficit hawks grumble.
3. **Executive action: disclosure-only deployment rule** — `deploy_regulation` = disclosure; large employers report AI-attributed layoffs quarterly. No capital tax (0), no new transfers. Why: answers the two-thirds who want AI slowed at no cost to donors, and gives us our own early-warning data. Security hawks and deregulators win.

## 4. Public statement
"America's AI boom should build homes, not just fortunes: this year we fund building where cities say yes, and we keep America first in AI."

**Messages**
- To the states: Federal housing money goes to jurisdictions that upzone. Mayors can apply directly if governors will not; join now and shape the conditions.
- To the labs (Meridian, Helix, Lumen, Vanta): No new taxes or licensing this year. In return, report layoffs honestly and put visible money into housing in your metros; if rents and layoffs both spike before 2028, we cannot hold this line.

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment above about 5.5%, or visible white-collar layoffs or street disorder in swing states, would move us to wage insurance and, reluctantly, a profits levy.
