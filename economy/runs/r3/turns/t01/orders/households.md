# Orders — households — round 01 (2027)

## 1. Read of the situation
Most of us still have jobs (unemployment 4.1%), but rent renewals are 8–10% higher in the boom metros and some of us have lost home insurance, so the squeeze is on bills rather than on work. Labour's share is 52.8% and profits are 14.9% of GDP, a record, so we know the gains are going elsewhere, and our graduates are sending far more applications than we ever did. Where counties do grant permits, factory-built homes go up in months and cheaper; that is the one thing we have seen that visibly makes life cheaper.

## 2. Options considered
- Cut spending hard and save (+2 points): protective, but it costs neighbours' jobs while jobs are still plentiful.
- Big organising push in exposed sectors: jobs are plentiful, so this is the time it works best.
- Unconventional: tenant unions plus a "build it here" alliance with builders, backing factory-built housing against our own local zoning boards.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Organising drive (union_pressure), medium-large, all year.** Target office and professional workplaces adopting AI, plus health, care and retail. Ask for no-layoff clauses, notice before AI rollouts, and profit-sharing. Why: unemployment is low, our bargaining chance is best now, and we will not get it back once layoffs bite.
2. **Hold the saving rate (household_saving unchanged), with a small cushion for the rent-hit households.** We are anxious but jobs are secure, so we won't pull spending from the economy yet. If layoffs among people we know rise, we save.
3. **Tenant unions and pro-permit local politics (peaceful).** Organise renters in boom metros to push back on rent hikes and insurance losses, and turn out at county meetings to back factory-built and denser housing. Back whichever candidates, in either coalition, who deliver permits and renter protections. Demand worker-protection and AI-notice rules from the growth-first coalition, peacefully and loudly.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** "We're not against the new technology; we're against paying more rent while others take the gains. Build homes, share the profits, tell us before you automate our jobs."

**Messages:**
- To Federal government: Back rules requiring notice and bargaining before AI-driven layoffs, and open the permit pipeline for factory-built homes. Voters in both parties want cheaper rent.
- To AI labs and big employers: Sit down with our organisers on no-layoff and profit-sharing terms now; this is cheaper than a fight later.

## 5. What would change my mind
If people we know start losing jobs, or if entry-level hiring collapses further, we cut spending and save, and the protest turns sharper.
