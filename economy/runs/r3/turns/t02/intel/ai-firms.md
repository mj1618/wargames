# Intel — ai-firms — round 02

**Period: January–December 2028.** Elections for President and Congress are in November.

**The numbers (end 2027).** Unemployment 4.2%; participation 61.5%; 162.7m jobs (+0.3m, below population growth); GDP +1.7%; prices flat except housing, up about 1%; labour share 52.5%; deficit 6.0%, debt about 102%. Now published for 2027: poverty 12.9%, homelessness 22.2 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Financial wobble.** AI shares fall by a third; credit tightens for a year.

**Only you see.** Order books stayed full and premium pricing held; no rival made a full cut. Lenders are questioning capex. Washington did not take up pre-emption.

**Messages.**
- From the federal government: "No new taxes or licensing this year. In return, report layoffs honestly and put visible money into housing in your metros; if rents and layoffs both spike before 2028, we cannot hold this line."
- From entrepreneurs: "We need cheap, reliable API access and good licensing terms to build services on top of your models. Expensive AI means we cannot compete; bundled terms favour the giants."
- From households: "Sit down with our organisers on no-layoff and profit-sharing terms now; this is cheaper than a fight later."
