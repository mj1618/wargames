# Intel — employers — round 02

**Period: January–December 2028.** Elections for President and Congress are in November.

**The numbers (end 2027).** Unemployment 4.2%; participation 61.5%; 162.7m jobs (+0.3m, below population growth); GDP +1.7%; prices flat except housing, up about 1%; labour share 52.5%; deficit 6.0%, debt about 102%. Now published for 2027: poverty 12.9%, homelessness 22.2 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Financial wobble.** AI shares fall by a third; credit tightens for a year.

**Only you see.** Your drive removed about 0.7m roles in 2027 (0.4m office and professional), about 40% by layoff and the rest by not replacing leavers. Margins widened; you passed little on in prices and lost no share. A work-sharing subsidy is available from this year. The union drive won few contracts. Credit for capex is tighter.

**Messages.**
- From AI firms: "Multi-year bundled agent contracts at locked rates, capacity first-come. Adopt before supply tightens."
- From households: "Sit down with our organisers on no-layoff and profit-sharing terms now; this is cheaper than a fight later."
