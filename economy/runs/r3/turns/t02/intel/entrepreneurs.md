# Intel — entrepreneurs — round 02

**Period: January–December 2028.** Elections for President and Congress are in November.

**The numbers (end 2027).** Unemployment 4.2%; participation 61.5%; 162.7m jobs (+0.3m, below population growth); GDP +1.7%; prices flat except housing, up about 1%; labour share 52.5%; deficit 6.0%, debt about 102%. Now published for 2027: poverty 12.9%, homelessness 22.2 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Financial wobble.** AI shares fall by a third; credit tightens for a year.

**Only you see.** Your new firms hired about 0.3m people in 2027, mostly in new kinds of service work; the AI-only firms made money but hired almost nobody. Model access stayed expensive and bundled in favour of big buyers. Seed capital is now drying up: investors are cutting cheques by about a third. Federal small-business credit opens this year. Boom-metro commercial rents rose but stayed under your 25% threshold.

**Messages.** None addressed to you.
