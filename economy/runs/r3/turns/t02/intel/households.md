# Intel — households — round 02

**Period: January–December 2028.** Elections for President and Congress are in November.

**The numbers (end 2027).** Unemployment 4.2%; participation 61.5%; 162.7m jobs (+0.3m, below population growth); GDP +1.7%; prices flat except housing, up about 1%; labour share 52.5%; deficit 6.0%, debt about 102%. Now published for 2027: poverty 12.9%, homelessness 22.2 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Financial wobble.** AI shares fall by a third; credit tightens for a year.

**Only you see.** The organising drive won few contracts: employers avoided layoffs and simply stopped hiring, so there was little to bargain over. Few people you know lost jobs; many graduates you know are still looking. Rent rose roughly in line with pay. Retirement accounts are down with the share market. Federal housing grants for cities that upzone start this year.

**Messages.**
- From employers: "We are open to talks on hours reductions with retraining funds for roles we automate, provided no strike action disrupts rollout."
- From entrepreneurs: "We are creating jobs — 15–20% above normal net new hires, heavily weighted to care, trades, and people-heavy services. We also need credit to stay cheap and housing/zoning rules to ease."
