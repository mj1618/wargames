# Orders — employers — round 02 (2028)

## 1. Read of the situation
Our 2027 drive removed about 0.7m roles, margins widened and we lost no share with one-third price pass-through, so the model works. But AI shares are down a third and capex credit is tight, so disappointing projects cost more this year. Labour share is a record-low 52.5% and November elections could empower a worker-first coalition that taxes profits.

## 2. Options considered
- Keep the +20% drive and lock in the labs' multi-year bundles.
- Slow to normal speed and conserve cash through the squeeze.
- Unconventional: use the work-sharing subsidy so government money funds shorter weeks, buying political cover before the election.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Automation pace: from +20% to normal, cash-funded.** Prioritise projects paying back within 18 months (claims, back-office, service). Sign only 12-month agent contracts, not locked multi-year bundles: labs' capacity is not scarce while their valuations are down.
2. **Hours and layoffs: hours_share from 10% to about 25%** in office and professional units using the subsidy, partial pay top-up for the lowest paid. layoff_share falls from about 40% to about 30%; the rest of the shedding continues through freezes and attrition. Thins layoff headlines before November.
3. **Politics: partial deal with households.** Announce a cap on involuntary mass layoffs (with distress exits) and a profit-sharing pool of about 1% of payroll, in exchange for a public line that new deployment rules are unnecessary. Price pass-through stays at one-third.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** "Large employers are using AI to protect jobs and share gains through shorter weeks, a layoff cap and profit-sharing. Rules written in an election year would slow the investment that sustains them."

- **To households:** We will meet on a layoff cap and a small profit-sharing pool; not on wage indexation or automation bans.
- **To federal government:** We back the work-sharing programme and a voluntary transition-report standard in return for no deployment mandates or AI-profit taxes.

## 5. What would change my mind
Falling sales, or an opposition lead on a profit-tax platform, would push us to share more; a rival gaining share by cutting prices would push us back to +20%.
