# Orders — entrepreneurs — turn 02 (2028)

## Read of the situation

Seed capital evaporated: investors are cutting cheques by about a third. I hired 300k people in 2027 mostly in new service work, but AI-only firms still struggle with expensive model access bundled toward incumbents. Credit tightens broadly and may stay tight all year, but federal small-business credit opens—my only reliable funding source now.

## Options considered

- **Consolidation**: pause new-firm formation, focus on profitability and debt service (safest, slowest).
- **Chase federal credit**: gear up to absorb as much SBA credit as possible to keep hiring going while private capital stays frozen.
- **Unconventional**: pivot aggressively to people-heavy services in secondary cities where rents are still cheap, cede AI-only market to incumbents until model prices fall.

## Decisions this period

1. **Slow new-business wave from 15% to ~5% growth.** Credit discipline. I'm not stopping, but I'm not racing into a crunch.

2. **Shift the mix hard toward people-heavy services.** AI-only firms depend on expensive bundled model access—incumbents control that. Care, trades, craft, repair, supervising machines: these need humans and AI to be a tool, not a substitute. 80% of new hiring goes here; 20% to profitable AI-only niche plays only.

3. **Aggressively pursue federal small-business credit.** This is my lifeline. I'm building the relationships and deal pipelines now to absorb significant tranches when funds flow, target secondary metros where rents stay reasonable.

## Public statement

"We're slowing new-firm formation but not stopping—shifting toward service jobs where AI and humans work together. Federal credit will fuel sustainable hiring in 2028. We need cheap access to AI models, not just capital."

## Messages

**To: Labor unions**
"If you want young job creation in services, talk to your politicians about federal credit and cheaper AI access. We hire when it works, not for policy."

**To: AI labs**
"Unbundle model access from hardware and consulting. Startups with cheap API routes create much larger markets than your current cloud bundles."

## What would change my mind

If federal credit dries up or commercial rents in secondary metros spike above 25%, I pivot to fast consolidation and wait out the cycle.
