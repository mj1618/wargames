# Orders — federal-gov — round 02 (2028)

## 1. Read of the situation
Unemployment is 4.2% and jobs grew only 0.3m, below population growth, so displacement is a hiring freeze, not yet a layoff wave voters feel. Rent is what they feel: housing prices up about 1% while everything else is flat, homelessness 22.2 per 10,000. The wobble (AI shares down a third) cuts capital-gains receipts with the deficit already 6.0% and debt 102%, in an election year.

## 2. Options considered
- Stand pat and campaign on last year's housing bill.
- Take the labs' deal whole: pre-empt state AI-labour rules, fast-track power, bank $5bn.
- Unconventional: trade pre-emption for a federal layoff-notice standard, so the growth-first government owns the worker-protection issue before the opposition does.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Bill 1 — Build Act (permitting).** Fast-track power permitting for data centres and grid, plus a federal permit pathway for factory-built homes, attached as a condition to the 2028 upzoning grants. No new money. Why: cheaper rent is the one demand voters in both parties share; labs and security hawks get power. Deregulators win.
2. **Bill 2 — AI Workforce Notice Act.** One federal standard: 90 days' notice and reporting of AI-attributed layoffs at firms over 500 staff (restores the blocked disclosure rule by statute, gives us data), consultation but no bargaining mandate; pre-empts state AI-labour rules. Wage insurance at 0.1% of GDP from 2029, part-funded by the labs' fund at $5bn. No profits levy, no payroll levy. Why: visible protection before November at near-zero budget cost. Security hawks and deregulators win on pre-emption; deficit hawks swallow 0.1%.
3. **No rescue, no new transfers.** No support for AI share prices or lenders; small-business credit stays at last year's scale. Deficit hawks win.

## 4. Public statement
"We are building homes and power faster, and no American will be replaced by a machine without notice and help. We will not tax the industry that keeps America ahead."

**Messages**
- To AI firms: You get pre-emption and power permitting; it comes with a federal notice rule. Commit the $5bn publicly before the vote.
- To households: Notice, reporting and factory-built homes are in this year's bills. Bargaining is not; hold us to the rest.

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment above 5%, layoffs citing AI above 1m, or a bond-market scare from falling receipts would move me to transfers or a levy.
