# Intel — ai-firms — round 03

**Period: January–December 2029.**

**The numbers (end 2028).** Unemployment 4.9% (now revised to 4.5%); participation 60.3%; 158.9m jobs (−3.8m); GDP −0.8%; prices flat, housing costs down about 4%; labour share 50.7%; deficit 6.7%, debt about 108%. Announced layoffs about 1.9m (0.3m citing AI). Published for 2028: poverty 13.4%, homelessness 20.0 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Data revision.** 2028 unemployment was overstated by 0.4 points.

**Politics.** The worker-first coalition won the Presidency and Congress in November.

**Only you see.** Enterprise customers trimmed budgets as their own sales fell; premium pricing held and no rival made a full cut. The developer tier launched and filled quickly. Lenders forced a pause on transition-fund payments. Pre-emption of state AI-labour rules is now law; the notice rule is blocked in court. The incoming majority campaigned on taxing AI profits.

**Messages.**
- From the federal government (outgoing): "You get pre-emption and power permitting; it comes with a federal notice rule. Commit the $5bn publicly before the vote."
- From entrepreneurs: "Unbundle model access from hardware and consulting. Startups with cheap API routes create much larger markets than your current cloud bundles."
