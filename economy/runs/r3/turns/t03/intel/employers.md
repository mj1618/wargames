# Intel — employers — round 03

**Period: January–December 2029.**

**The numbers (end 2028).** Unemployment 4.9% (now revised to 4.5%); participation 60.3%; 158.9m jobs (−3.8m); GDP −0.8%; prices flat, housing costs down about 4%; labour share 50.7%; deficit 6.7%, debt about 108%. Announced layoffs about 1.9m (0.3m citing AI). Published for 2028: poverty 13.4%, homelessness 20.0 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Data revision.** 2028 unemployment was overstated by 0.4 points.

**Politics.** The worker-first coalition won the Presidency and Congress in November.

**Only you see.** You shed about 4.8m roles in 2028: about 0.7m to automation, the rest because sales fell. About 30% went by layoff; a quarter of the labour saving was taken as shorter weeks under the subsidy. The layoff cap and profit-sharing pool were signed with the unions in May. Margins per sale widened, but volumes fell. Credit is easing.

**Messages.**
- From households: "Yes to talks. We want hours cuts without lost pay, entry-level hiring, and retraining on paid time. No strikes while talks are live, but they end if talks stall by June."
