# Intel — entrepreneurs — round 03

**Period: January–December 2029.**

**The numbers (end 2028).** Unemployment 4.9% (now revised to 4.5%); participation 60.3%; 158.9m jobs (−3.8m); GDP −0.8%; prices flat, housing costs down about 4%; labour share 50.7%; deficit 6.7%, debt about 108%. Announced layoffs about 1.9m (0.3m citing AI). Published for 2028: poverty 13.4%, homelessness 20.0 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Data revision.** 2028 unemployment was overstated by 0.4 points.

**Politics.** The worker-first coalition won the Presidency and Congress in November.

**Only you see.** Your new firms hired about 0.4m people in 2028, mostly people-heavy services; federal credit flowed and replaced much of the lost seed money. The labs' developer tier cut your model costs, but it is capped and enterprise bundles still favour big buyers. Commercial rents fell. Customers were cautious all year. Private investors are starting to return.

**Messages.**
- From AI firms: "Developer tier launches Q1, published terms, no bundling. Tell us where licensing still blocks you."
- From households: "Hire the young people we know. We will back zoning reform and cheap credit if hires are local and permanent."
