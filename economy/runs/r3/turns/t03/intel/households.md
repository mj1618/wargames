# Intel — households — round 03

**Period: January–December 2029.**

**The numbers (end 2028).** Unemployment 4.9% (now revised to 4.5%); participation 60.3%; 158.9m jobs (−3.8m); GDP −0.8%; prices flat, housing costs down about 4%; labour share 50.7%; deficit 6.7%, debt about 108%. Announced layoffs about 1.9m (0.3m citing AI). Published for 2028: poverty 13.4%, homelessness 20.0 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: Data revision.** 2028 unemployment was overstated by 0.4 points.

**Politics.** The worker-first coalition won the Presidency and Congress in November.

**Only you see.** The talks worked: a layoff cap and a profit-sharing pool of about 1% of payroll were signed in May. But people you know lost jobs this time, and many stopped looking. Rents fell.

**Messages.**
- From employers: "We will meet on a layoff cap and a small profit-sharing pool; not on wage indexation or automation bans."
- From the federal government (outgoing): "Notice, reporting and factory-built homes are in this year's bills. Bargaining is not; hold us to the rest."
- From entrepreneurs: "If you want young job creation in services, talk to your politicians about federal credit and cheaper AI access. We hire when it works, not for policy."
