# Orders — ai-firms — round 03 (2029)

## Read of the situation
The downturn was mild (unemployment revised 4.9%→4.5%, GDP −0.8%), premium held and no rival cut, so demand is intact and lenders remain the brake. The real event is political: a worker-first Presidency and Congress that campaigned on taxing AI profits, while our $2bn fund paid nothing in 2028. Pre-emption is law and the notice rule blocked; both are now targets, and a moratorium is the case to insure against.

## Options considered
- Dig in: fight any levy, keep the fund dormant, race on the revision.
- Buy the deal: accept a modest profits levy for statutory no-licence, no-cap language. (Majority view.)
- Unconventional: unbundle the API and seed thousands of startups so the AI economy has its own voters before the levy vote.

## Decisions this period
1. **Pace: modest race (+10%); capex +10% where lenders allow.** The revision and a full developer tier justify re-accelerating; credit is too thin for +20%.
2. **Pricing: premium holds for enterprise; developer tier becomes an unbundled API ~45% below list for firms under $100m.** Small margin cost; widens the market and builds a constituency.
3. **Politics: $1.2bn. Publicly offer a 4% levy on AI profits earmarked for wage insurance, in exchange for a statutory ban on deployment caps and licensing plus preserved pre-emption. The fund restarts and actually pays $2bn this year, creditable against any levy.** Helix and Vanta want to fight; Meridian and Lumen carry the majority: a levy we shape beats one we lose.

## Public statement
"We will pay a fair share on AI profits to fund wage insurance — and ask Congress to guarantee in return that America keeps building. Our fund pays out this year, no conditions."

## Messages
- **To federal government:** We back a 4% AI-profits levy for wage insurance if the bill bars deployment caps and licensing and preserves pre-emption. Fund pays $2bn in 2029, creditable. Name your number.
- **To entrepreneurs:** Unbundled API ships Q2, ~45% below list for firms under $100m. Help us show Congress what it creates.

## What would change my mind
A levy above ~8% or one carrying licensing flips us to full opposition and a slow-down.
