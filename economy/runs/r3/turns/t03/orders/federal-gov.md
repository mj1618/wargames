# Orders — federal-gov — round 03 (2029)

## 1. Read of the situation
Jobs fell 3.8m and participation dropped to 60.3%, so the revision to 4.5% unemployment means people are leaving the workforce, not that things are fine. Poverty rose to 13.4% and labour's share fell to 50.7% while the deficit hit 6.7% and debt 108%. We won on this; the labs paid nothing from their fund and there is still no official layoff data.

## 2. Options considered
- Keep the inherited settlement, add nothing, protect the bond market.
- A paid-for worker package: targeted cheques, wage insurance, a modest AI-profit levy.
- Unconventional: a national dividend funded by federal equity stakes in the labs.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Worker Security Act (bill 1).** Transfers of 1.0% of GDP aimed at the bottom two-fifths (`transfers_pct_gdp`=1.0, targeted); retraining and wage insurance raised to 0.4% of GDP; work-sharing subsidy doubled. Paid for by an 8-point levy on capital/AI profits (`capital_tax_pct`=8). All starts 2030, so cheques land before the midterms. Labour and progressives win on substance; fiscal moderates win on size and the pay-for.
2. **Transition Reporting and Jobs Act (bill 2).** Statutory 90-day notice and AI-layoff reporting, written to survive the court; 1m public jobs in care and construction (`public_jobs_m`=1.0, about 0.2% of GDP). Deployment rules stay at disclosure — no licensing this year. Fiscal moderates win on rules; labour wins on jobs.
3. **Executive action.** Open antitrust cases on lab pricing and compute access (`competition_policy`=cases); keep housing at funded building with zoning conditions and steer the labs' $1bn to upzoning metros. Progressives win.

## 4. Public statement
"Four million jobs are gone and the people who lost them will not be told the numbers look fine. Those profiting most from AI will help pay for those it displaces."

**Messages**
- To AI firms: "No licensing this year, as agreed. Reporting becomes law, and an 8-point profit levy is coming; pay the promised $5bn and it stays at 8."
- To employers: "Work-sharing doubles and there are no deployment mandates. Reporting will be statutory, not voluntary. Hold your layoff cap."

## 5. What would change my mind
Bond yields spiking or the levy failing its vote would make me cut the transfer; another 2m jobs lost would push me to licensing and a bigger levy.
