# Intel — employers — round 04

**Period: January–December 2030.**

**The numbers (end 2029).** Unemployment 3.4%; participation 61.1%; 163.8m jobs (+4.9m); GDP +4.8%; prices down slightly, housing costs down about 2%; labour share 52.1%; deficit 6.2%, debt about 107%. Reported AI layoffs about 0.35m. Published for 2029: poverty 12.0%, homelessness 18.6 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: A hit new industry.** An unforeseen new service takes off and hires in large numbers.

**Only you see.** Sales recovered and you rehired about 4m people; automation removed about 0.9m roles, over half in offices. A third of the labour saving went into shorter weeks with the top-up; the pool costs about 1.5% of payroll. The 0.4m entry-level cohort started. Applicants are scarce and pay is rising. No profit tax passed.

**Messages.**
- From households: "We accept no wage indexation or automation ban for now; we will watch the cap closely, and any firm that breaks it gets a campaign."
- From the federal government: "Work-sharing doubles and there are no deployment mandates. Reporting will be statutory, not voluntary. Hold your layoff cap." (The doubling did not pass.)
