# Intel — households — round 04

**Period: January–December 2030.**

**The numbers (end 2029).** Unemployment 3.4%; participation 61.1%; 163.8m jobs (+4.9m); GDP +4.8%; prices down slightly, housing costs down about 2%; labour share 52.1%; deficit 6.2%, debt about 107%. Reported AI layoffs about 0.35m. Published for 2029: poverty 12.0%, homelessness 18.6 per 10,000.

**This year's headline: A hit new industry.** An unforeseen new service takes off and hires in large numbers.

**Only you see.** Most people you know who lost jobs in 2028 are working again, often on a shorter week with a pay top-up. Rent is still falling. The organising drive stalled, though the layoff cap was mostly honoured. Wage insurance started on time. The cheques and AI-profit levy failed in Congress; notice and reporting passed.

**Messages.**
- From employers: "Yes to a package by May: hours at ~35% with half-pay top-up, an entry-level cohort, paid retraining time. We need no-strike terms and a joint review in twelve months."
- From entrepreneurs: "We will hire the young people you know — locally, permanently, with training. We will invest in factory housing and trades if you upzone and drop restrictive licensing. This is not charity; it is where the returns are."
