# Orders — entrepreneurs — turn 04

## Read of the situation

The economy is in strong recovery: unemployment at 3.4%, 163.8m jobs (up 4.9m from 2028), and a new unforeseen industry is hiring in large numbers. I hired 700k people last year as customers returned and credit flowed back. The unbundled API cuts my model costs by 45%, but labour is now the constraint—wages are rising sharply and workers are harder to find.

## Options considered

1. Flood the emerging industry with aggressive hiring regardless of wage pressure; maximize opportunity capture.
2. Shift entirely to AI-only firms to defend margins as labour costs spike.
3. **Hybrid leverage** (unconventional): use the cheap API to lock in cost advantages in people-heavy services while entering the new industry selectively where I can command premium pricing and recruit efficiently.

## Decisions this period

**1. Aggressive unbundled API migration.** Shift 50% of existing operations and 70% of new hires to the cheaper tier; this locks in margin before all competitors move. Investment: $0.5bn in integration and early-adoption support. Pace: Q2 2030 launch. Why: cost lock-in is one-time; first-mover advantage on a 45% reduction is decisive.

**2. Selective entry into the hit new industry.** Identify 2–3 subsegments with the best wage-to-revenue ratios; hire 200k people there at market rates. Investment: $1.2bn in recruitment, training, and working capital. Why: entrepreneurs win by seeing new demand first; wage inflation means picking winners carefully, not hiring everywhere.

**3. Expand factory housing.** Continue aggressive development in permissive states (non-big-four); target 80k new units in 2030. Investment: joint ventures, modest equity. Why: demand is there, zoning remains bottled in resistant states, so regional consolidation of advantage is durable.

## Public statement

The unbundled API and the emerging new industry prove small and young firms can still create opportunity at scale. We'll invest in both—lower costs and new demand are good for founders.

## Messages

**To:** AI firms  
**Text:** "Committed to the unbundled tier. Can you ship Q1 availability for early partners and guarantee API stability at scale? We'll commit $X million in volume."

**To:** Growth-first coalition / Republicans in Congress  
**Text:** "Factory housing and the new industry justify our bet: cheap AI tools and flexible hiring let entrepreneurs create jobs at scale. Compute-access antitrust would help us compete with the labs."

## What would change my mind

If wage growth exceeds 15% annual or the new industry stalls, I will cut hiring and consolidate into capital-light AI-only operations.
