# Orders — federal-gov — round 04 (2030)

## 1. Read of the situation
The numbers are the best of my term: unemployment 3.4%, 163.8m jobs (+4.9m), GDP +4.8%, poverty 12.0%, homelessness 18.6 per 10,000, and a new industry is hiring. But the Worker Security Act failed, so my base has nothing but reporting and 1m public jobs to show before November, and the labs' fund paid $2bn, not $5bn. Deficit 6.2% and debt 107% leave no room for unfunded cheques, and voters feel no crisis that would pass a big bill.

## 2. Options considered
- Re-run the Worker Security Act at full size and campaign on its failure.
- Take the labs' levy offer, slightly raised, and bank a signed law before the midterms.
- Unconventional: no bills at all; run purely on the boom and executive enforcement.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Wage Insurance and First Job Act (bill 1).** 5-point AI-profits levy (`capital_tax_pct` 5; fund payments creditable), paying for `retraining_pct_gdp` 0.4 — wage insurance on schedule plus a first-job programme for 18–25s — from 2031. In exchange: no federal deployment caps or licensing for three years (sunset, not permanent) and pre-emption kept. Deficit-neutral. Fiscal moderates and labour win; progressives lose on licensing.
2. **Public jobs: deliver, do not expand.** Hire the 1m (`public_jobs_m` 1) quickly, half the places reserved for under-25s and housing construction, visible in swing districts by autumn. No second spending bill; no transfers. Fiscal moderates win.
3. **Executive enforcement.** Staff the notice-and-reporting statute fully and publish quarterly AI-layoff figures; keep the antitrust cases running as leverage for the levy vote. Bargaining-rights reform becomes a 2031 campaign pledge, not a 2030 vote. Labour loses on timing.

## 4. Public statement
"Jobs are up nearly five million and poverty is down. Now we lock in wage insurance and a first job for every young worker, paid for by the firms that gain most."

**Messages**
- To AI firms: "Five points, not four; fund creditable; no caps or licensing for three years, pre-emption stays. Deliver your votes and pay the full $5bn."
- To households: "Wage insurance, a working notice rule and a first-job programme come this year. Bargaining reform is the 2031 bill — help us hold Congress."

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment back above 4.5% or reported AI layoffs above 1m would make me drop the licensing truce and revive targeted cheques.
