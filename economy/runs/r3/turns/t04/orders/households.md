# Orders — households — round 04 (2030)

## 1. Read of the situation
Work is coming back: unemployment is 3.4%, 4.9m more jobs, most people we know from 2028 are working again, though often on shorter weeks with top-ups. Rents are still falling, wage insurance started on time, and the new hit industry is hiring, so jobs are plentiful, which is when we have the most bargaining power. But the cheques and AI-profit levy failed, our organising drive stalled, and labour's share (52.1%) is still below the 52.8% we started at. The well-off top tenth, who own the shares, did fine in the 4.8% boom; the rest of us need that gain in pay, not just in profits.

## 2. Options considered
- Sign the employers' package as offered, no-strike terms and all.
- Hold out and strike while labour is scarce, to win more.
- Unconventional: take the entrepreneurs' offer, and ask tenant groups and unions to co-own the new factory housing and trades training through community land trusts.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Accept the employers' package by May, with changes** (small-medium organising effort, to win it and police it). We take ~35% hours with half-pay top-up, the entry-level cohort and paid retraining time. We will not give a blanket no-strike pledge. We pledge no strikes while the layoff cap, pool payouts and cohort hiring numbers are met and published, and we agree to the 12-month joint review. We also ask to extend the ~1% profit pool to cover firms that skipped it. Why: it is a real, visible gain at a time when more jobs are coming.
2. **Take the entrepreneurs' housing and trades deal.** Tenant unions and community groups campaign peacefully for upzoning and for replacing restrictive trade licences with a short apprenticeship. We make local hiring and a seat for community land trusts a condition. Why: cheaper rent and real jobs for our kids matter more than any cheque.
3. **Spend, don't hoard** (saving rate down 1 point). Jobs are safer and rents are falling, so we hold off on cutting back, which would hurt other people's jobs. We still push, peacefully, for the first-job programme, the 1m public jobs and a new try at the AI-profit levy. No boycotts.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** "Jobs are back. Now pay and hours must follow: we will sign deals that deliver, and we will watch that they do."
- **To employers:** Yes to a package by May, with published layoff, cohort and pool data, but no blanket no-strike clause.
- **To entrepreneurs:** We will back upzoning and fewer licensing rules if hiring is local and permanent and community land trusts get a stake.

## 5. What would change my mind
Broken layoff caps or empty pool payouts, a fresh wave of layoffs, or youth hiring that stays stuck despite the boom.
