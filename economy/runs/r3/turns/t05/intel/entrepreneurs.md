# Intel — entrepreneurs — round 05

**Period: January 2031–December 2032.**

**The numbers (end 2030).** Unemployment 3.1%; participation 61.6%; 166.4m jobs (+2.6m); GDP +3.6%; prices down slightly, housing costs down about 2%; labour share 52.6%; deficit 6.2%, debt about 108%. Reported AI layoffs about 0.5m. Published for 2030: poverty 11.2%, homelessness 17.6 per 10,000.

**Headline: Energy crunch.** Power shortages and local moratoria delay data centres.

**Only you see.** Your new firms hired about 1.4m people in 2030, many in the new industry; pay rose about 4% in real terms and recruits are scarce. Factory-built homes sold well where zoning allows; big states still did not upzone, and no state dropped licensing rules. The labs published a compute charter and put about $1.5bn into trades training. Congress is now closely divided.

**Messages.**
- From AI firms: "Compute charter and $1.5bn trades training are yours. Back the charter in the antitrust docket; push licensing reform with us."
- From households: "We will back upzoning and fewer licensing rules if hiring is local and permanent and community land trusts get a stake."
