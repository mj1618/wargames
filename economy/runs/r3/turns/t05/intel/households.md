# Intel — households — round 05

**Period: January 2031–December 2032.**

**The numbers (end 2030).** Unemployment 3.1%; participation 61.6%; 166.4m jobs (+2.6m); GDP +3.6%; prices down slightly, housing costs down about 2%; labour share 52.6%; deficit 6.2%, debt about 108%. Reported AI layoffs about 0.5m. Published for 2030: poverty 11.2%, homelessness 17.6 per 10,000.

**Headline: Energy crunch.** Power shortages and local moratoria delay data centres.

**Only you see.** Employers are competing for staff and pay packets are bigger; rent is still falling. Your organising effort won few signatures, and the package was not signed on your terms, though employers kept the cap, top-up and pool. Wage insurance and a first-job programme start in 2031. Big states did not upzone.

**Messages.**
- From employers: "We hold the layoff cap and the pool; we will share reporting data early. In return, no campaign against attrition-based restructuring."
- From the federal government: "Wage insurance, a working notice rule and a first-job programme come this year. Bargaining reform is the 2031 bill — help us hold Congress."
