# Orders — ai-firms — round 05 (2031–32)

## Read of the situation
Demand is not our problem: unemployment 3.1%, GDP +3.6%, jobs +2.6m, and the new industry outran our compute while the premium held. Power is the binding constraint now — shortages and local moratoria delay data centres, so the race is for megawatts, not models. Politically we got the deal we wanted (5-point levy, creditable, no caps or licensing for three years, pre-emption kept) and only the antitrust cases and a closely divided Congress threaten it.

## Options considered
- Full race on compute regardless of grid: fastest, but lands us as the villain of every blackout.
- Voluntary slow-down to defuse power backlash: cheap goodwill, but a foreign lab or a domestic defector takes the frontier.
- Unconventional: become the grid's customer-of-last-resort — finance generation and storage ourselves and sell surplus to utilities, turning the crunch into a moat only we can afford.

## Decisions this period
1. **Deploy pace: race +15%, capex +20%, all incremental capex into power.** Roughly $60bn over the two years into owned and contracted generation, storage and grid upgrades, sited outside the moratorium metros. Racing ahead of rivals who are grid-constrained is the cheapest frontier lead available; building our own power is the only pace that survives the headline.
2. **Pricing: premium holds; unbundled tier priced at +15% with Q1 availability and a 99.9% API-stability commitment for early partners.** Compute is scarce, so scarcity pricing; the entrepreneurs' volume commitment underwrites the tier and keeps the antitrust narrative honest.
3. **Politics $1.6bn; pay the full $5bn fund; offer a consent-decree based on the compute-access charter plus mandated unbundling.** Paying the second $2bn delivers our side of the government bargain and keeps "creditable" alive in a divided Congress. A settlement now, while the levy debate is quiet, costs less than a court-ordered remedy later. We will publicly back any federal pre-emption of local data-centre moratoria in exchange for grid-investment commitments.

## Public statement
"We will fund the power we use — $60bn in new generation and storage over two years — and we have paid the full $5bn into the transition fund. Grid pre-emption that lets us build should be paired with our investment, not with blackouts."

## Messages
- **To federal government:** Full $5bn paid, votes delivered. We need federal pre-emption of local data-centre moratoria tied to our $60bn grid investment; we'll accept a consent decree on compute access and unbundling to close antitrust.
- **To entrepreneurs:** Q1 availability confirmed for early partners, 99.9% API stability guaranteed under contract; price +15% while compute is scarce. Commit the volume and the tier is yours.

## What would change my mind
A rival or foreign lab shipping a frontier model on cheaper, unconstrained power would force a full race and shelve the power moat.
