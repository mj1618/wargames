# Orders — ai-firms — round 05 (2031–32)

## Read of the situation
Demand is not our problem: unemployment 3.1%, GDP +3.6%, jobs +2.6m, and the new industry outran our compute at premium prices. Power is now the binding constraint — shortages and moratoria delay data centres; the race is for megawatts, not models. We got our deal (5-point levy, creditable, no caps or licensing for three years, pre-emption kept); only antitrust and a divided Congress threaten it.

## Options considered
- Full race regardless of grid: fastest, but we own every blackout.
- Voluntary slow-down: cheap goodwill; a rival takes the frontier.
- Unconventional: become the grid's anchor customer — finance generation, sell surplus to utilities, make the crunch a moat only we can afford.

## Decisions this period
1. **Race +15%, capex +20%, all incremental capex into power** — about $60bn over two years in owned generation, storage and grid upgrades outside moratorium metros. Rivals are grid-constrained; owning power is the cheapest frontier lead that survives the headline.
2. **Premium holds; unbundled tier at +15%, Q1 availability, contractual 99.9% API stability.** Scarcity pricing; entrepreneurs' volume underwrites the tier and helps the antitrust narrative.
3. **Politics $1.6bn; pay the full $5bn fund; offer a consent decree: compute-access charter plus mandated unbundling.** The held-back $2bn honours the bargain; settling now beats a court-ordered remedy. We back federal pre-emption of local moratoria tied to grid investment.

## Public statement
"We will fund the power we use — $60bn in new generation over two years — and have paid the full $5bn transition fund. Let us build, and the lights stay on."

## Messages
- **To federal government:** Full $5bn paid, votes delivered. Pre-empt local data-centre moratoria tied to our $60bn grid investment; we'll accept a compute-access and unbundling consent decree to close antitrust.
- **To entrepreneurs:** Q1 confirmed, 99.9% API stability under contract, price +15% while compute is scarce. Commit the volume; the tier is yours.

## What would change my mind
A foreign lab shipping a frontier model on cheap power would force a full race and shelve the moat.
