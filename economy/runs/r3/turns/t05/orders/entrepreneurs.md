# Orders — entrepreneurs — Turn 05 (2031–2032)

## 1. Reflection
The new industry hired at massive scale last year (my firms took 1.4m, mostly there); pay rose 4% real and recruits are scarce but below my 15% red line. The unbundled APIs cut marginal cost to 45% of the bundle tier. Energy crunch is new and real—power moratoria risk compute access if it tightens further. Congress is closely divided (worker-first coalition kept Congress but by narrower margins). Labs want me to back their antitrust position in exchange for licensing reform and continued trades training. Households want local, permanent hiring and a community-land-trust stake before they'll back upzoning.

**Uncertainties:** Will energy constraints force model prices up and compute access down? Will the industry growth sustain into 2032 or stall? Will big states finally move on zoning?

**Red lines:** Wage growth above 15% triggers consolidation. Compute access restrictions would force me back to people-heavy. Licensing rules still stuck.

## 5. Journal entry
**Beliefs.** The new industry is real but growth will moderate in 2031–32; energy crunch is the new tail risk to compute access. Labs will push antitrust hard and want my backing. Households will support zoning/licensing if I credibly tie hiring to community benefit. Big states won't move; secondary metros will compete for jobs and upzone if I show up with capital. Congress will stay divided; no major new rules.

**Commitments.** Aggressive expansion target 1.3m hires; secondary-metro deal with community-land-trust governance; public backing for labs' compute charter in antitrust (transactional, not ideological); wage growth monitoring at 10% threshold.

**Trust/grudges.** Labs are useful allies but I don't trust their antitrust framing—they want market concentration. Households have leverage if I tie jobs to place and wage floors. Employers (big firms) will likely consolidate and automate; I'll beat them on cost and hiring speed.

**Next-turn plans.** Monitor energy and compute access obsessively. Close secondary-metro deals in Q1 2031. Pilot community-land trusts and see if households and big states shift. If energy crunch tightens, pivot immediately.
