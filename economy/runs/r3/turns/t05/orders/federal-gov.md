# Orders — federal-gov — round 05 (2031–32)

## 1. Read of the situation
The boom is holding: unemployment 3.1%, 166.4m jobs (+2.6m), poverty 11.2%, homelessness 17.6 per 10,000, though the deficit is stuck at 6.2% and debt at 108%. The only thing voters can feel going wrong before November 2032 is power: shortages and moratoria, with data centres blamed for bills. Margins are thinner, the labs paid $3bn not $5bn, and AI layoffs are creeping up (0.5m) without yet being a street issue.

## 2. Options considered
- Bank the win: implement the Act, pass nothing, run on the numbers.
- Energy bill with a household rebate: fix the visible problem, cheque before the election.
- Unconventional: trade the antitrust cases for a binding consent decree and the labs' cash, turning a court fight into a campaign deliverable.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Grid and Household Power Act (bill 1).** Fast permitting for transmission and new generation; large data centres pay their own grid connection and capacity costs; about 0.1% of GDP a year, including a power-bill rebate for the bottom two-fifths paid in 2032. Passes with growth-first votes. Security hawks and fiscal moderates win; progressives get the rebate.
2. **Deliver the 2030 Act, no new tax (no second bill).** Levy stays at 5 points; wage insurance and first-job places open in 2031, targeted at 300,000 18–25s by mid-2032. Public jobs held at 1m. No transfers, no licensing: truce honoured. Fiscal moderates win; labour's bargaining reform waits until after 2032.
3. **Antitrust settlement on our terms (executive).** Offer the labs a consent decree: their compute-access charter made binding and court-enforceable, with price-reporting; levy rate held at 5 through 2033; fund payments creditable up to half, only if the full $5bn a year is paid. If they pay $2bn, cases go to trial. Labour and progressives win.

## 4. Public statement
"Jobs are up, poverty is down, and now we keep the lights on and the bills low: the companies using the power will pay for the grid, not families."

**To AI firms:** Levy stays at 5, not 8, through 2033; half-credit for the fund if you pay $5bn; charter becomes an enforceable decree. Back the grid bill and you get permitting.

**To employers:** Accepted: fund apprenticeship slots alongside first-job. The levy is law; no deployment mandates this period.

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment past 4.5% or AI layoffs past 1m before mid-2032 would make me bring targeted cheques and a higher levy to a vote.
