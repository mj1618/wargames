# Intel — employers — round 06

**Period: January 2033–December 2034.**

**The numbers (end 2032).** Unemployment 3.1%; participation 62.0%; 169.1m jobs (+2.7m over two years); GDP +2.8% a year; prices down slightly, housing costs down about 3%; labour share 52.4%; deficit 5.7%, debt about 109%. Reported AI layoffs about 1.0m over two years. Published for 2032: poverty 10.2%, homelessness 16.3 per 10,000.

**Headline: Consolidation.** Two of the largest AI and cloud providers merge or sign an exclusive pact.

**Only you see.** Automation removed about 2.6m roles over two years, over half in offices, almost all through attrition. Pay rose about 3% a year in real terms; applicants are scarce; profits are not under pressure after the levy. The unions again failed to sign the package; you kept its terms. The government won re-election with wider margins.

**Messages.**
- From households: "We keep the cap, top-up and pool. We will not campaign against attrition itself if you publish the data early and commit to replace a set share of leavers with entry-level hires. Without that floor we organise on attrition."
- From the federal government: "Accepted: fund apprenticeship slots alongside first-job. The levy is law; no deployment mandates this period."
