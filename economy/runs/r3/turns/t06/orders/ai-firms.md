# Orders — ai-firms — round 06 (2033–34)

## Read of the situation
The economy absorbs us: unemployment 3.1%, 169.1m jobs, GDP +2.8% a year, poverty 10.2% — the backlash case is weak. Our costs are political and physical: the levy holds at 5 only through 2033, the decree is binding, and the crunch cost capacity our $60bn of generation is only now replacing. The consolidation headline is the danger: a merger weeks after a decree reopens the cases and gives the wider worker-first majority a reason to go back to 8.

## Options considered
- Oppose the merger and let antitrust block it — protects three labs, hands customers to a foreign rival.
- Back it unconditionally — strongest for the pair, reopens decree and levy.
- Unconventional: convert the merger into a pre-cleared compute pact bound to the charter, paid for with the fund.

## Decisions this period
1. **Pace: race +15%, capex +15%** (~$70bn), weighted to finishing owned generation and interconnects now permitting is on offer. Power, not demand, is the limit.
2. **Pricing: premium holds; unbundled tier frozen three years** for firms under $100m. Seat splits 2–2 on consolidation; the revenue majority backs an exclusive compute-and-cloud pact, pre-notified to the agencies, the merged entity accepting charter, price reporting and the freeze as decree amendments.
3. **Politics: $1.8bn.** Back grid permitting publicly; accept levy at 5 for 2033; offer $5bn/yr fund through 2035 for a 5-point lock to 2035 and no licensing. Push state licensing reform wherever we lobby.

## Public statement
"Our generation is coming online, small-firm prices are frozen for three years, our fund pays $5bn a year. Consolidation will happen under the decree, not around it."

**To federal government:** Grid backing is yours. Levy lock at 5 through 2035 and no licensing; we pay $5bn/yr and pre-clear consolidation under the decree.

**To entrepreneurs:** Unbundled tier frozen three years; licensing reform in every state we lobby. Back the charter in filings and bring the secondary-metro deals.

## What would change my mind
If the agencies move to block the pact outright or the levy returns to 8, the seat drops the merger and settles.
