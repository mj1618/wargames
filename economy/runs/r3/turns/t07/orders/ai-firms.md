# Orders — ai-firms — round 07 (2035–36)

## Read of the situation
The boom holds (GDP +3.1%/yr, unemployment 3.5%) but cover is thinning: labour share 51.1%, 2.0m AI layoffs in two years, and the levy at 5 rests on an executive commitment with a 2035 review, not statute. The liability ruling is the live threat: customers stall if every automated decision is an uncapped tort. It is also a moat: liability favours whoever carries insurance, audit trails and indemnity — us, not open-weight or small rivals.

## Options considered
- Fight the ruling (appeals, pre-emption bill) — slow, looks like dodging.
- Absorb it: sell indemnified, audited deployments at a premium.
- Unconventional: fund a no-fault compensation pool for automation harms, vaccine-injury style, to pre-empt a statutory one on worse terms.

## Decisions this period
1. **Pace [`deploy_pace`]: steady, capex +10%.** Generation is on line. Hold while liability is unsettled — a headline harm case mid-race is the moratorium scenario.
2. **Pricing [`ai_pricing`]: premium holds, plus an "assured" enterprise tier** (~25% above standard) bundling capped indemnity, audit logs and insurance. Unbundled small-firm tier stays frozen through 2037; open access honoured. Lock-in through assurance, not access denial.
3. **Politics and funds: $2.2bn politics; $5bn/yr fund extended to 2038 plus a $1.5bn/yr no-fault harm pool**, as a package for a standards-based liability safe harbour (certified systems, capped damages), a statutory levy lock at 5 and no licensing. We do not fight the bargaining bill.

## Public statement
"Firms should answer for harm their systems cause. We are funding a $1.5bn-a-year no-fault compensation pool and asking Congress for clear standards so responsible deployers know where they stand."

## Messages
- **To federal government:** Trade honoured — no fight on bargaining. In return: statutory levy lock at 5 and a certified-systems safe harbour; fund to 2038 plus the harm pool.
- **To entrepreneurs:** Unbundled tier frozen through 2037, open access statutory. Commit the volume; firms under $100m get the assured tier at cost-plus.

## What would change my mind
A rival breaking price or shipping uninsured at scale — then we race and litigate rather than carry the pool alone.
