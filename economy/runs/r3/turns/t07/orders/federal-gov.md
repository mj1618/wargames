# Orders — federal-gov — round 07 (2035–36)

## 1. Read of the situation
The boom holds (GDP +3.1% a year, poverty 9.6%, homelessness 15.5) but the labour side is softening: unemployment 3.5% from 3.1%, only +1.0m jobs in two years, labour share 51.1%, and AI layoffs at about 1.0m a year — my own tripwire. The liability ruling hands me leverage: firms now want a statute more than I do. November 2036 is a presidential election and the deficit is 5.5% with debt at 110%.

## 2. Options considered
- Stand pat: levy at 5, let the courts handle liability, run on the boom.
- Trade a liability safe harbour for a modest levy step that pays for visible worker benefits before November 2036.
- Unconventional: turn the levy into a citizens' dividend fund paying every adult a small annual cheque.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Automated Decisions Accountability Act (bill 1).** Codify the ruling: full liability stays, with a safe harbour for firms that audit, disclose and offer human review; single federal standard. No licensing, no caps. Fiscal moderates and labour win; progressives wanted strict liability untouched.
2. **Levy Review and Apprenticeship Act (bill 2).** Honour the lock: levy stays 5 through 2036, steps to 7 from 2037. From 2036: wage insurance and apprenticeship co-funding with employers rise to 0.6% of GDP total, plus secondary-metro zoning grants (~0.1%). Benefits before the election, cost after. Labour wins; deficit hawks get it paid for from 2037.
3. **Executive.** Antitrust division reviews the merger on compute-access terms under the decree and Open Access Act; pre-clearance only with frozen small-firm prices. No new public jobs, no transfers.

## 4. Public statement
"If a machine decides, someone answers for it — and firms that play straight get clear rules. The boom will pay for apprenticeships and wage insurance for the million workers a year it displaces."

**To AI firms:** Lock at 5 holds through 2036 and no licensing. The price of the liability safe harbour is 7 from 2037; pre-clearance needs small-firm price freeze kept.
**To employers:** Apprenticeship co-funding accepted, no mandates. Back the safe harbour bill and the 2037 step.

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment past 4.5% or AI layoffs past 1.5m a year moves me to targeted transfers; bond yields spiking or donors bolting drops the 2037 step.
