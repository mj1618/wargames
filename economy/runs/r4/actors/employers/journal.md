# Journal — employers

## 2027 (turn 01)
Belief: record margins are the prize; liability ruling slows everyone equally, so no first-mover edge in regulated lines. Price-cut pilot is unproven. Committed to: normal adoption pace, named sign-off on automated decisions, layoff_share down to ~45% via attrition and freezes, matched price tests, a safe-harbour-for-pledge offer to Washington. Will watch: rival share or margin moves, any profits-tax or deployment-rule talk, insurer pricing, and young-graduate hiring data.

## 2028 (turn 02)
Belief: cheap open-weight tools make faster adoption the best earnings lever; the election and the layoff-reporting rule make visible layoffs costly. Committed to: company-wide drive on audited stacks, layoff_share held near 45%, hours_share toward 15-20%, a conditional entry-level hiring floor and no-mass-layoff pledge, safe-harbour backing, price cuts only where rivals cut. Will watch: rival margins and share, insurer standards, deployment-rule and profits-tax odds, graduate hiring data.
