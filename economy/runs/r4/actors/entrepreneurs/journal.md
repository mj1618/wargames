# Journal — entrepreneurs

## Turn 01 (January 2027)

We see the liability ruling as opportunity: big firms will pay for oversight. Hired aggressively into new AI-supervision roles, bet ~30% on high-margin people-heavy services (care, tutoring, design). Credit is good, entry-level hiring is easy. Watching: whether customers stick with paid human services; whether Congress moves on antitrust; credit conditions if employment slips.

## Turn 02 (January 2028)

The open-weight collapse is a windfall: inputs now free, margins suddenly viable for founders. We're accelerating AI-only firm creation (better per-founder returns) while locking in people-heavy services (customers proved willing to pay, liability = our asset). Housing looks like a genuine high-upside opportunity in zoning-friendly states. Watching: whether customer willingness to pay for human oversight persists as prices fall and wages stagnate; credit remaining normal; Congress moving on incumbent gatekeeping or antitrust.
