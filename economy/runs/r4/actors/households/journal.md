# Journal — households

## Turn 01 (2027)
Believe: jobs are okay for now; the pain is young graduates. Rents flat with free months is a real relief. Committed to: modest organising drives (no-layoff, human-sign-off clauses using the liability ruling), saving up only 0.5 point, backing youth hiring and apprenticeship policy, no strikes or protests yet. Watching: layoffs among people we know, rents, entry-level hiring, whether the growth-first Congress cuts protections.

## Turn 02 (2028)
Believe: pay and rents are fine for now, but unfilled jobs and stuck graduates are the real warning; labour share is at a record low. Committed to: renewing and widening no-layoff and human-sign-off drives (no strikes), saving flat, making youth hiring and stronger wage insurance the 2028 vote demand, peaceful rallies only. Watching: layoffs among people we know, unemployment toward 5%, any move on liability, whether the opposition keeps its youth-hiring promise.

## Turn 03 (2029)
Believe: pay and rents are fine, but labour share keeps falling and quiet attrition will speed up with week-long AI projects. Committed to: a third organising wave extended to hiring freezes (no strikes), saving flat, taking supervision-role jobs as protected classes, refusing Employers' no-boycott/no-rules pledge. Watching: layoffs near us, entry-level intake, whether Government passes youth hiring or a notice rule before 2030.
