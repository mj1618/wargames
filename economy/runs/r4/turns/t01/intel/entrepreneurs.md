# Intel — Entrepreneurs — January to December 2027

**Numbers everyone has (end 2026):** unemployment 4.1%; participation 61.6%; 162.4m jobs (59.1% of adults); labour share of income 52.8% (record low); corporate profits 14.9% of GDP (record); poverty 12.9%; homelessness about 22 per 10,000; federal deficit 6.0% of GDP, debt about 100%. Income, GDP and prices: index 100.

**Washington:** a growth-first coalition leads Congress and the White House (pro-deployment, tax-averse, wary of new entitlements). **States:** mixed on housing; some big states loosen zoning, others resist.

**This year's headline — Liability ruling:** courts have held firms fully liable for harm done by automated decisions.

**From your own seat:** AI tools cost you less than a year ago and credit is normal. Hiring is easy at entry level, harder for experienced trades. The liability ruling makes big firms slower to automate decisions, and some now want outside people to check and sign off machine work. One market observation: founders report customers turning up for services that did not exist two years ago — people who supervise and fix AI systems for small firms, in-person tutoring and care bundles, bespoke design-and-build — and paying for the human part.
