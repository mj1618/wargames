# Orders — AI firms — Turn 01 (2027)

## Read of the situation
Demand is fine: unemployment 4.1%, profits a record 14.9% of GDP, a growth-first coalition in Washington, and compute and power are our binding limit. The liability ruling is the one real threat: enterprise customers are pausing fully automated workflows and asking us to share liability. Our data says a 20% price cut gets only half-matched and the rest sticks as margin, so no price war.

## Options considered
- Race flat-out, ignore liability (customers stall anyway).
- Voluntary slow-down to defuse public mood (rivals won't hold; existential).
- Unconventional: make the ruling a moat — sell indemnified autonomy only our balance sheets can underwrite.

## Decisions this period
1. **`deploy_pace`: race, +15% above baseline** — every chip and megawatt we can get. Why: the frontier is the whole game and the government is pro-deployment; speed costs nothing politically this year.
2. **`ai_pricing`: premium, bundled** — hold enterprise prices; launch an "Assured Autonomy" tier (about +20%) with contractual indemnity for automated decisions inside audited workflows, plus logging and escalation tooling. Why: customers want liability shared; we capture it as margin and lock-in, and it hurts smaller rivals more.
3. **Politics, about $300m this year** — lobby for a federal safe-harbour: firms meeting an audited "accountable automation" standard get limited liability, pre-empting state suits. Sweetener: a pooled **$2bn/yr worker-transition fund** from the four labs, in exchange for no deployment limits and no profits levy.

## Public statement
"We back clear federal rules for accountable automation and will put $2 billion a year into helping workers into the jobs AI creates. Slowing America down is not a plan."

## Messages
- **To: government.** We will back an accountable-automation standard and pay the transition fund if the bill includes liability safe-harbour and no deployment limits or profits levy. We can help draft it this quarter.
- **To: employers.** Indemnified autonomy tier ships this quarter; we carry liability within audited workflows. Resume paused deployments with us.

## What would change my mind
A moratorium bill with majority support, or a rival breaking the half-match pricing pattern — then we fight on price.
