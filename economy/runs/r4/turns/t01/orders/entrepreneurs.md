# Orders — Entrepreneurs — Turn 01

## Read of the situation

Unemployment at 4.1%, labour participation at 61.6%, and 162.4m jobs exist—a tight labour market. AI tools just got cheaper and credit is normal. The liability ruling is the real prize: courts have made firms liable for automated decisions, which means the big incumbents now need people to check and sign off machine work—a new market we can supply.

## Options considered

- **Conservative:** Wait for the market to fully shape up; build traditional small businesses in existing niches.
- **Aggressive expansion:** Push hard into both AI-only firms (high margin, no jobs) and people-heavy services capturing the new supervision and care demand.
- **Bet on disruption:** Go all-in on housing-construction services and trades, assuming building bottlenecks will break. *(Unconventional—depends on states moving on zoning.)*

## Decisions this period

**1. Rapid expansion into AI-supervision services.** The liability ruling just opened a new labour market. We'll fund and launch firms offering people to oversee and sign off on automated decisions for small and mid-sized companies. Budget: ~$200m to seed 1000+ new founder-led teams; expect this to drive 8-10% new job creation in this niche by year-end.

**2. Build people-heavy service bundles.** Founders are reporting real demand for in-person tutoring, care, and bespoke design-and-build. We'll shift ~30% of capital toward these human-contact businesses—they pay, customers are showing up, and big firms can't easily undercut us here. Expect ~4-5% new hiring in services.

**3. Lobby for cheap AI and antitrust.** Push Congress to keep token prices accessible to small firms and investigate the dominant labs' lock-in on API pricing and talent.

## Public statement

Entrepreneurs are hiring and investing now. The liability ruling is good for us—it created real jobs supervising machines. We're moving fast into AI-oversight services and the care and custom-work markets where we already see demand.

**Message to Congress:** Keep AI inputs cheap and competitive. Antitrust action against dominant labs helps everyone else.

## What would change my mind

If credit tightens or customers in the new niches suddenly dry up, I pull back to 25% below baseline hiring.
