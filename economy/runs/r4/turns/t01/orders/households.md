# Orders — households — Turn 01 (2027)

## 1. Read of the situation
Most of us still have jobs: unemployment is 4.1%, pay and hours are flat, but labour's share is a record-low 52.8% while profits sit at a record 14.9%. The ones getting hurt are our kids: graduates sending hundreds of applications for office jobs. Rents have stopped rising and landlords are offering free months, which is the one thing that visibly makes life cheaper.

## 2. Options considered
- Do nothing and keep spending as normal.
- Start organising now, while jobs are still plentiful and bosses can't easily replace us.
- Save hard out of fear (rejected for now: nothing has hit us yet).
- Unconventional: bargain for "a person signs off" clauses, using the liability ruling as leverage, so human jobs become the legally safe choice.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Organising drive (union_pressure), modest.** Back drives in office, health/care and logistics workplaces, focused on no-layoff and human-sign-off clauses that cite the new liability ruling. Roll needed. Jobs are plentiful, so this is the cheapest time to try. Aim for a handful of large drives, not a wave of strikes. No strikes yet: we have no grievance big enough to hold people together.
2. **Spending/saving: hold steady, very small cushion.** Nudge saving up about 0.5 point (not the full 2). Households with young adults at home are quietly saving more. We don't cut back broadly, because that costs our own neighbours their jobs and nothing has hit yet.
3. **Votes and demands.** Press for the worker-first side: youth hiring support, apprenticeships and entry-level pathways, and protection of the liability rule. No protests yet. We save the anger for when layoffs arrive.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** "Keep a human accountable for every machine decision, and don't pull up the ladder from young people trying to start work. We'll take cheaper rent, but we want a share of the gains too."

**Messages:**
- To Congress (worker-first opposition): Jobs for under-25s are the issue that will move voters in 2028. Build a youth hiring and apprenticeship bill now.
- To Employers: Keep entry-level hiring open and a person in the loop. It is legally safer and we will remember who did it.

## 5. What would change my mind
A visible wave of layoffs among people we know (not just graduates), or rents and bills rising again, would make me save hard, strike and protest.
