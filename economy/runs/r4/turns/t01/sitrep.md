# Sitrep — 2027

**Jobs.** About 1.5m jobs disappeared and 0.5m new ones appeared. Population growth filled most of the gap, so total employment slipped only from 162.4m to 162.05m. Unemployment rose from 4.1% to 4.3%; the share of adults in work fell from 59.1% to 58.7%. Office work took the largest hit (0.7m lost); new-economy firms grew fastest.

**Pay and prices.** Real hourly pay rose 1.5%. Shop and service prices were flat: AI firms kept their savings as margin. Housing costs fell 2.2%. Median real household income 100.9; bottom fifth 100.8.

**Business.** GDP grew 1.6%. Labour's share fell to 52.2%, a new low; the top 1% took 20.3%. Machines now do 3.2% of desk work, of 12.5% they could.

**Government.** Deficit 6.1% of GDP, debt 102.7%. Poverty 12.7%, homelessness 21.1 per 10,000. Unrest barely moved (35.7).

**What mattered most.**
1. The liability ruling slowed automation by about a fifth, and Congress failed to pass the safe harbour (roll failed), so it stood all year.
2. Employers chose hiring freezes over layoffs, so losses showed up as people not entering work more than as unemployment.

Also: the organising drive succeeded; the wage-insurance pilot passed at half size, starting 2028.

**What a household would have noticed.** Rent a little cheaper, pay a little better, and a graduate child still at home with no job offers.
