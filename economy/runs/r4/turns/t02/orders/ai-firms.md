# Orders — AI firms — Turn 02 (2028)

## Read of the situation
Capability roughly doubled and margins held through 2027, but free open-weight models now match last year's best and customers are testing them against our contracts. The safe-harbour bill failed, so we carry indemnity risk; Government offers a safe harbour if we back disclosure and stay quiet on layoffs before November. The macro picture is benign (unemployment 4.2%, real pay +1.5%, prices flat) but labour share is a record-low 52.2% in an election year where two-thirds of voters want us slowed.

## Options considered
- Full price war to kill the open-weight threat — buys share, burns the margin investors await.
- Voluntary slow-down to defuse the election — fails unless foreign rivals join, and they won't.
- Unconventional: turn the disclosure rule into a product — sell employers the compliance layer.

## Decisions this period
1. **Deploy pace: race, +20%** (compute-bound ceiling). Open-weights commoditise last year's model; the only durable moat is being a year ahead. Concentrate on multi-day agentic work free models cannot do.
2. **Pricing: split the stack.** Cut the commodity tier ~60% to near open-weight; hold "Assured Autonomy" at premium on 3-year bundles packaging indemnity, audit logs and automatic filing under the AI-layoff disclosure rule. Lumen would go full price war; the majority holds.
3. **Politics: take Government's deal, hedged.** Publicly back disclosure; labs pledge no mass-layoff announcements before November. Lobbying $400m for safe harbour, split across both coalitions. Worker-transition fund restarted at **$1bn/yr unconditional now**, rising to **$3bn/yr** the year safe harbour passes.

## Public statement
"We support transparency about AI's effect on jobs and will fund the transition — starting this year, not after an election. Fair liability rules keep us building here rather than abroad."

**To Government:** Accepted: public backing for disclosure, no layoff announcements, $1bn/yr fund now. We need a safe-harbour floor vote before summer recess.

**To Employers:** Free models carry no indemnity and no compliance cover. Assured Autonomy bundles both; three-year terms lock today's price before any liability bill lands.

## What would change my mind
A moratorium bill with a credible majority, or a rival undercutting the premium tier — then we slow the race or go to price war.
