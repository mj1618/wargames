# Orders — employers — 2028 (turn 02)

## 1. Read of the situation
Margins are at a record (profits 14.9% of GDP, labour share 52.2%), and open-weight models matching last year's best are collapsing AI service prices, so our automation costs are falling just as the liability risk is being priced into insurance. We shed about 1.5m posts in 2027, under half by layoff, and sign-off rules slowed roll-outs by roughly a fifth. The election is in November 2028, the deficit is 6.1%, and a visible layoff wave or scandal this year invites a worker-first sweep and a profits tax.

## 2. Options considered
- Hold the line: normal pace, keep attrition-led shedding, wait for insurer standards.
- Accelerate hard on cheap open-weight tools plus the indemnified tier, and take the full cost saving before rivals do.
- Unconventional: sign a public, time-limited entry-level hiring and no-mass-layoff pledge through the election, traded for deployment-rule restraint, and use the cheap tools to cut cost without cutting heads this year.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Adoption_speed: company-wide drive (about +15–20%).** Move the paused deployments onto indemnified-tier or open-weight stacks with audited named sign-off, where insurers will quote cover. Cheap tokens make this the best earnings lever and rivals will do it anyway. Keep sign-off in health, transport and credit decisions.
2. **Layoff_share: hold near 45%, no step-up before November.** Keep shedding through freezes and not replacing leavers, not announced layoffs. The reporting rule makes layoffs visible and an election year makes them costly. Raise hours_share modestly toward 15–20% in back-office units, with a partial pay top-up, as a cheap display of good faith.
3. **Politics and pledge.** Offer a 2028 entry-level hiring floor (graduate intake held at 2027 levels, about 90% of seats funded) and a no-mass-layoff pledge, conditional on no deployment rules or AI-profits tax this Congress. Back the safe-harbour bill again, tied to the audited sign-off standard. Pass about half of the cost saving to prices only where a rival has already cut, to protect share.

Pay: keep wage_sharing at modest profit-sharing for retained staff, with no broad raises.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** "Large employers will keep a person accountable for every automated decision, hold graduate hiring this year, and put the savings into lower prices and sturdier firms."
- **To AI firms:** We will resume deployments on the indemnified tier if the audit standard is published and the price per task falls with open-weight levels.
- **To Households:** We have held entry-level intake and sign-off. In return we ask for no consumer boycotts and no support for deployment rules.

## 5. What would change my mind
A rival visibly taking share or margin through deeper cuts, or a clear sign that a profits tax or deployment rule will pass regardless of our pledge, would make me drop the pledge and move to mass restructuring.
