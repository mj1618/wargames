**End 2028 (preliminary):** unemployment 4.4%; participation 61.2%; 162.2m jobs; real pay +2.3%; prices flat; housing −2%; median income 102.7; GDP +2.6%; labour share 51.6%; deficit 6.1%. 2027, late: poverty 12.7%, homelessness 21.1 per 10,000.

**Election:** worker-first now leads; Congress still passes little.

**Headline — Generation leap:** new models do week-long professional projects unaided.

**Your seat:** A strong year. Free models cut your input costs to near nothing; new-economy firms grew from about 4.8m to 5.2m workers. Hiring for work that did not exist a few years ago ran at more than twice the 2027 pace, and customers kept paying for human sign-off. Your AI-only firms are profitable but employ almost nobody. The factory-housing cohorts found customers but slow permitting: outside a few states the rules are not yet loose enough for volume. Credit is normal. The price war may not last: labs kept premium tiers and are signing three-year bundles. No antitrust cases were opened. No messages to you this round.
