# Orders — AI firms — Turn 03 (2029)

## Read of the situation
Machines can do a fifth of desk work and already do a third of that; politics, not demand, is our constraint. Commodity revenue collapsed under open weights while premium three-year bundles sold well; at 4.4% unemployment and 51.6% labour share the backlash is a mood, not a majority. A worker-first coalition open to taxing AI profits, Government's $20bn-a-year price for safe harbour, and Employers' demand for an audit standard set the table.

## Options considered
- Dig in: refuse the $20bn, race flat out — cheap now, invites a moratorium if unemployment rises.
- Pay the $20bn, signed, for safe harbour and no limits before the backlash peaks.
- Unconventional: publish the audit standard ourselves, making certification the moat open-weight rivals must pass.

## Decisions this period
1. **Deploy pace: race +20%** (`deploy_pace`). Falling behind abroad is existential; compute is the only brake.
2. **Pricing: two tiers plus published audit standard** (`ai_pricing`). Commodity tier matched to open-weight prices; indemnified premium tier held at 3-year bundles. Audit standard published Q1 through an industry body we fund ($200m) — meets Employers' condition, makes certification a moat.
3. **Take Government's deal at $12bn/yr rising to $20bn in 2031**, with a signed public no-AI-layoff pledge covering premium-tier customers, for safe harbour, no deployment caps and no AI profits levy for five years. Lobbying $500m, both coalitions. Labs split; majority prefers a known $20bn to an unknown levy.

## Public statement
"We will fund the largest private worker-transition programme in history and publish an independent AI audit standard. We ask for clear rules, not limits."

**Messages**
- **To Government:** $12bn now, $20bn by 2031, pledge signed before summer. Price: safe harbour, no deployment caps, five-year levy moratorium. Draft with us this quarter.
- **To Employers:** Audit standard published Q1; commodity tier at open-weight prices. Resume indemnified deployments and co-sign the pledge — it covers you too.

## What would change my mind
A moratorium or cap bill polling a majority: we pay the full $20bn immediately and slow to steady.
