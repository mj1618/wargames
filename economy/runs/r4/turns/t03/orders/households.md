# Orders — Households — Turn 03 (2029)

**1. Read of the situation.** Pay is up 2.3%, rents are down again and unemployment is only 4.4%, so most of us feel okay. But labour's share is a record low 51.6%, office jobs close quietly behind leavers, and week-long AI projects mean the quiet attrition will speed up. The worker-first coalition has the lead but Congress passes little, so what we win must come from our own bargaining and from the 2030 vote.

**2. Options considered.**
- Keep renewing no-layoff and sign-off drives, peaceful only.
- Take Employers' deal: no boycotts and no support for deployment rules, in exchange for held entry-level intake.
- Unconventional: turn Entrepreneurs' "supervision roles" offer into union-negotiated sign-off job classes with pay floors and apprenticeship slots.

**3. Decisions this period.**
1. **Union pressure, third wave, moderate.** Renew the existing clauses and extend them to firms with attrition-based cuts. Add a demand to cover hiring freezes (no replacing leavers with AI without a notice period and a retraining offer), using the liability ruling as leverage. No strikes; slow-downs only where a firm breaks a signed clause. It fades if we stop, and jobs are still plentiful, so this is the time.
2. **Saving rate: hold flat.** Pay and rents are fine and we do not want to cause the layoffs we fear. If layoffs rise near us we will save up 1 point.
3. **Take the supervision-roles offer, on our terms.** Households' people will apply for the sign-off jobs and push for them to be written into contracts as protected, paid classes. We refuse Employers' package: no boycott pledge, though no boycott is planned now. We keep the option, and we will not give up backing deployment rules, because rules are how the gains get shared.

**4. Public statement.** "Pay is up, but the share going to people who work keeps falling. We want no-layoff clauses, youth apprenticeships and a notice period before AI replaces anyone who leaves."

Messages:
- To Employers: "We will not promise no boycotts or no support for rules. Keep entry-level intake and sign-off and we will not need either."
- To Government: "Bring the youth hiring bill and a notice rule for attrition cuts. Our 2030 votes depend on them."

**5. What would change my mind.** Layoffs among people we know rising visibly, or Employers cutting entry-level intake, would push us to save more, strike and boycott.
