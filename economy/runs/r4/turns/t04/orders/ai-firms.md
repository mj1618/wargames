# Orders — ai-firms — Turn 04 (2030)

## Read of the situation
The 3-point levy is law and court-proof, so fighting it buys nothing; the question is what we get for not fighting. Three years without a safe harbour leaves premium bundles on one-year terms ("one-year terms only, with price protection") and commodity revenue thin, while the economy is strong (GDP +3.3%, unemployment 4.1%). The robot recall is the moratorium precursor we feared; whoever writes robot certification owns the next moat.

## Options considered
- Refuse Government's price, run out the clock, litigate case by case.
- Pay the $5bn and take the floor vote — with the levy sunk, this is cheap.
- Unconventional: make our audit standard the legal bar for robot recertification — a rule that hurts cheaper entrants more than us.

## Decisions this period
1. **Pace: race +20% on frontier models; robot lines −30% while recertifying.** Labs split (Vanta wants to ship through); majority holds. $300m pooled into an industry Robot Safety Certification body merged with the audit standard, offered to regulators as the recertification regime.
2. **Accept Government's deal.** $5bn/yr fund from the day safe harbour is signed, $2.5bn escrowed now; freeze disclosure in statute (binds employers, not us); no fight on the levy. $300m lobbying for the vote before recess. The fund is a fraction of the levy and buys indemnity certainty plus cover against a post-recall moratorium.
3. **Pricing: meet Employers.** Premium indemnified tier on one-year terms with a renewal price cap, conditional on audit certification; three-year bundles 15% off for co-signers of the safe-harbour letter. Commodity at open-weight parity.

## Public statement
"We will fund worker transition at $5bn a year and submit every robot line to independent recertification. In return we ask Congress for clear liability rules for certified systems."

**To Government:** Terms accepted: $5bn/yr from signature, $2.5bn escrowed now, freeze disclosure in statute, no fight on the levy. Schedule the vote before recess; escrow lapses if it slips to 2031.
**To Employers:** One-year terms with a renewal cap, done. Co-sign the safe-harbour letter and the three-year bundle is 15% off with full indemnity.

## What would change my mind
A scheduled vote that fails again, or a moratorium bill polling a majority, ends the deal and sends the fund to litigation reserves.
