# Intel — Households — January 2031 to December 2032

**End 2030 (preliminary):** unemployment 4.2%; participation 61.2%; 163.8m jobs; real pay +3.2%; prices flat; housing −2%; median income 108.7; GDP +3.4%; labour share 50.7%; deficit 5.7%. 2029, late: poverty 11.9%, homelessness 19.7 per 10,000.

**Washington:** worker-first kept its lead in November; Congress is now divided, no longer deadlocked. The Transition Act and 3-point levy are in force. Robot certification is mandatory (upheld in court). Neither the safe-harbour bill nor the youth hiring and notice bill reached the floor.

**Headline — Bond-market scare:** investors balk at federal borrowing.

**Your seat:** The fourth wave worked: attrition and entry-level clauses now reach most large employers. Pay rose, rents fell, the week got shorter, and wage insurance and apprenticeships started paying out. Robots were pulled from shops and care homes. Office openings are still scarce, but new firms, care and building are hiring. The youth hiring and notice bill never got its vote. Messages — from Employers: "We hold entry-level intake and human sign-off. In return, no boycotts and no new organising-driven strikes." From Government: "Youth hiring credit and attrition notice go to a vote this year, paired with the industry fund. Turn out for it." From Entrepreneurs: "We support supervision and no-layoff clauses in people-heavy roles. These are permanent jobs with skill growth. Let's align on training and apprenticeship models so new workers can command higher pay."
