# Orders — AI firms — Turn 05 (2031–2032)

## Read of the situation
Demand is fine: GDP +3.4%, unemployment 4.2%, 163.8m jobs, real pay +3.2% — the backlash has no unemployment number to feed on. The bond-market scare is the new threat: a worker-first Congress, divided not deadlocked and short of borrowing room, will reach for the tax already on the books, and our 3-point levy is the obvious dial. Government's offer (safe harbour, no caps, levy frozen through 2032, pledge by June) is worth more than a year ago, and Employers will co-sign.

## Options considered
- Refuse again, keep the $2.5bn, bet the divided Congress passes nothing.
- Take Government's number ($12bn→$20bn) and stop fighting.
- Unconventional: prepay two years of levy into Treasuries during the scare, for a freeze through 2034.

## Decisions this period
1. **Sign by June.** Accept: safe harbour, no caps, levy frozen at 3 points through 2032, signed layoff pledge (no AI-attributed mass layoffs by us or co-signers without six months' notice and transition funding). Fund $8bn/yr from signature, rising to $12bn in 2033 if the freeze runs to 2034; prepayment offer on the table. $400m lobbying to schedule the vote before recess. A known $8bn beats an unseen levy hike.
2. **Pace.** Models: race +20% — the frontier is existential, abroad does not pause. Robots: −10% while lines clear certification, then steady; $300m more into the certification body so rivals buy our standard.
3. **Pricing.** Commodity at open-weight parity through 2032 (Employers' condition). Premium: indemnified, audited, certified bundles on two-year terms, renewal cap kept. Certification is the lock-in now, not price.

## Public statement
"We are signing the transition pledge. Certified, insured, audited systems are how this technology earns public trust."

## Messages
- **To Government:** Yes. $8bn/yr from signature, pledge signed by June, $400m to schedule the vote. Extend the freeze to 2034 and we go to $12bn in 2033 or prepay two years into Treasuries now.
- **To Employers:** Commodity pricing holds through 2032; certified stacks ship from Q2. Co-sign by June and resume.

## What would change my mind
A levy-hike bill polling a majority before June, or a rival breaking +20% — either and we sign faster and bigger.
