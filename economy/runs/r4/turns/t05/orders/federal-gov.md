# Orders — Federal government — t05 (2031–2032)

## 1. Read of the situation
Voters feel fine: unemployment 4.2%, real pay +3.2%, median income 108.7. The visible danger is the bond-market scare at a 5.7% deficit, with labour share at 50.7% eroding the wage base behind 70% of revenue. Industry offered $5bn firm against our $12–20bn and nobody signed the pledge, so our levy freeze is void, and 2032 is a presidential year.

## 2. Options considered
- Spending cuts to calm bonds — hurts our base before November 2032.
- Raise the profits levy; split proceeds between deficit and a pre-election cheque.
- Unconventional: pay the levy into a citizens' AI equity fund instead of cash.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Fiscal Stability and Dividend Act (bill 1).** `capital_tax_pct` from 3 to 7 points, voted 2031, effective 2032. About half to deficit reduction (target near 5%); half to `transfers_pct_gdp` 0.3%, `transfer_target` bottom two-fifths, first cheques mid-2032. Fiscal moderates and labour win. Why: bonds are the live crisis, profits are where the money is, cheques land before the election.
2. **Housing Build Act (bill 2).** `housing_policy` to funded building with zoning conditions, 0.15% of GDP a year from bill 1's envelope, fast-track permitting for factory-built homes. Progressives win. Why: voters reward cheaper rents.
3. **Executive action.** Transition Act apprenticeships place recent graduates first; wage insurance paid within 30 days; licensing review prioritises health and trade credentials. Safe-harbour talks closed; no deployment limits. Labour wins.

## 4. Public statement
"Companies earning record profits will help steady the nation's books; working families get a dividend and cheaper homes. America stays first in AI."

**To AI firms:** Your offer fell short and the pledge went unsigned; the freeze is off. No caps, no break-ups — in return, do not fight the levy and housing bills.

**To Entrepreneurs:** Licensing review now prioritises health and trades; back the Housing Build Act publicly and bring crossover votes.

## 5. What would change my mind
Bond yields easing unaided, or unemployment passing 5.5%, which would redirect levy money from deficit to jobs.
