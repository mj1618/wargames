# Intel — Entrepreneurs — January 2033 to December 2034

**End 2032 (preliminary):** unemployment 4.1%; participation 61.2%; 164.9m jobs; real pay +3.2% a year; housing −2% a year; median income 113.3; GDP +3.3% a year; labour share 49.4%; deficit 5.4%. 2030, late: poverty 11.4%, homelessness 18.7 per 10,000.

**Washington:** worker-first kept the presidency and its lead, with a firmer majority. Profits levy now 7 points (upheld in court), funding a lower-income dividend; Housing Build Act passed.

**Headline — A state pilot works:** a large state's wage-insurance-plus-cash pilot shows faster re-employment at modest cost.

**Your seat:** New-economy firms added about 1.5m workers over two years (6.7m to 8.2m); supervision, care and trades posts filled quickly. Factory-built homes kept going up in friendly states, and federal building money with zoning conditions now reaches more of them. Credit held up; the bond scare passed. The 7-point levy applies to your profits. The licensing review now puts health and trade credentials first. Messages — from Government: "Licensing review now prioritises health and trades; back the Housing Build Act publicly and bring crossover votes."
