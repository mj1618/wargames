**Headline — A state pilot works:** a large state's wage-insurance-plus-cash pilot shows faster re-employment at modest cost.

**Your seat:** The fifth wave stalled at mid-size employers; existing clauses at large sites hold. Pay rose, rents fell, the week got shorter, and lower-income households got a dividend cheque.
