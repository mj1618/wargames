# Orders — AI firms — Turn 06 (2033–34)

## Read of the situation
The levy is 7 points, upheld, and worker-first has a firmer majority: the 7 points are sunk and the threat is a further rise. Demand is fine: GDP +3.3% a year, labour share 49.4%, premium certified bundles selling, certified robots back on sale. Five safe-harbour offers never reached the floor; we stop paying for floor votes.

## Options considered
- Fight the levy in court and the 2034 campaign — already lost once, invites caps.
- Steady pace, pocket margins, say nothing — cedes the frontier to foreign labs.
- Unconventional: buy safe harbour from the states, not Congress — fund the working state pilot for state tort shields on certified deployments.

## Decisions this period
1. **deploy_pace: race +20% on models, robots +10%.** Compute is the limit.
2. **ai_pricing: premium certified bundles, 3-year terms, 15% off with indemnity** for Employers who co-sign the audit-standard letter and adopt the standard. Commodity at open-weight parity; certification is the moat.
3. **Politics: take Government's deal, go to the states.** No fight on levy or housing (all four labs agree). $300m lobbying moved from Congress to the five largest states and the regulator: audit-standard compliance to count as presumptive due care under state tort law and the robot rule. **Fund: $2bn/yr, unconditional**, matching the state wage-insurance-plus-cash pilot in any state adopting the presumption; pays from this year.

## Public statement
"The levy is law and we will pay it. Certified systems carry our indemnity, and we will match any state that pairs the proven wage-insurance pilot with clear liability rules."

**To Government:** Accepted — no fight on levy or housing. Ask: the regulator recognises audit-standard compliance as due care under the certification rule; no floor vote needed.

**To Employers:** Accepted — 15% off, indemnity, 3-year terms for co-signers adopting the standard. Join us on state due-care presumptions.

## What would change my mind
A levy-hike above 10 points or a cap bill polling a majority — then we reopen a federal deal with real money.
