# Orders — Federal government — 2035–36

## 1. Read of the situation
Voters are content: pay +3.8% a year, median income 120.7, housing −2% a year, poverty 9.7%. The erosion is quiet but growing: unemployment 4.4%, 3.0m announced layoffs, offices −2.2m, labour share 47.7%. Our seat's problem is fiscal: deficit 5.2%, debt 104%, revenue 17.5% resting on a shrinking wage base, and Transition Act II unfunded because the labs' $2bn went to the states.

## 2. Options considered
- Stand pat into 2036 on good numbers.
- Raise the levy modestly to fund what we passed and enlarge the pre-election dividend.
- Unconventional: shift the tax base off wages — cut payroll tax, replace it with a larger profits levy.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Levy and Dividend Act (bill 1).** Profits levy 7→10 points from 2036. Proceeds: fund Transition Act II (0.4% GDP), raise the lower-income dividend 0.4→0.7% of GDP with the first larger cheque in mid-2036, remainder to the deficit. Why: cheques before November, bond market reassured, tax base follows income. Fiscal moderates and progressives win; labs said no fight at 7 — we test 10.
2. **Due-care recognition (executive, no vote).** Regulator recognises audit-standard compliance as due care under the certification rule, reviewable every two years, conditional on independent auditors, renewed layoff-notice pledge and public incident reporting. Why: costs nothing, keeps donors quiet while the levy rises. Fiscal moderates win over progressives.
3. **Credentials and Building Act (bill 2).** Expedited national recognition of health and trades credentials (90-day review, interstate portability), tied to Housing Build Act grants; Housing funding 0.15→0.25% of GDP. Why: absorbs displaced office workers into hiring sectors, keeps rents falling. Labour grumbles; progressives and moderates win.

## 4. Public statement
"The economy is growing faster than in a generation; this government will make sure every household gets a share of it, and that the books are sound."

**To AI firms:** Due-care recognition granted by rule, with independent audits and the notice pledge renewed. The levy goes to 10 points; hold your no-fight line and it stays there through 2038.

**To Entrepreneurs:** Bill 2 gives 90-day health and trades credential review with interstate portability. Keep backing housing publicly in divided states and whip your members' districts.

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment past 5.5%, falling wage-tax receipts or a bond scare would push us to larger transfers and a payroll-to-profits tax swap.
