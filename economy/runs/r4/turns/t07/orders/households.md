# Orders — Households — Turn 07 (2035-36)

## 1. Read of the situation
Pay (+3.8% a year), rents (-2% a year), the dividend and a 33-hour week make daily life better, and poverty fell to 9.7%. But labour share is 47.7%, another record low, unemployment rose to 4.4%, and robots in warehouses and stockrooms are now taking retail and logistics posts of people we know. The sixth wave worked because intake is written into contracts, so we should lock that in and not squander it.

## 2. Options considered
- Coast on the good pay and dividend and do nothing new.
- Seventh wave aimed at retail and logistics (robot-exposed), with notice, redeployment and intake clauses.
- (Unconventional) Make the dividend and wage insurance a worker-run "transition cooperative" that pays retraining stipends and helps people relocate to hiring areas.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Seventh organising wave in retail and logistics, no strikes.** Roughly the same pace as wave six, focused on robot-exposed warehouses and stockrooms. Demands: 6-month notice before robot deployment, redeployment to care, construction or trades with pay protected, and some of the savings flowing to entry-level hiring. Why: that is where our neighbours are losing posts now, and clauses have worked where we have won them.
2. **Confirm the truce with Employers.** We accept the truce through 2036 if they publish quarterly entry-level intake. Our measure: intake no lower than the average of 2032-34, reported by employer, with a union audit right. No boycott while they comply; a targeted boycott of non-signing robot deployers is held in reserve.
3. **Savings flat; vote and lobby.** No change in saving. Peaceful rallies only. Our demand ahead of November 2036 is that the industry fund adds to wage insurance (as Government agreed), that Transition Act II is protected from cuts, and that robot-displacement notice rules are passed.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** "Pay and rents are better and we welcome it, but workers should share in the gains. Robots in warehouses must come with notice, redeployment and jobs for young people."

- **To Employers:** "Truce holds through 2036 if you publish quarterly entry-level intake, with the 2032-34 average as the floor and a union audit right."
- **To Government:** "Thanks for agreeing the fund adds to wage insurance. Please put that in writing and add robot-displacement notice rules before the 2036 vote."

## 5. What would change my mind
Rising layoffs among people we know, or Employers cutting intake or refusing audit, would push us toward strikes, boycotts and a harder vote.
