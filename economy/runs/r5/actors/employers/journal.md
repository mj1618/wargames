# Journal — employers

Committed: company-wide AI drive (~15% faster), layoff share cut to ~40% with attrition and freezes doing the work, savings kept, ~1% payroll profit-share, hours near 10%. Offered Congress an attrition-first pledge for no deployment rules. Will watch rival layoffs and margins, project slippage, any tax or rule talk, and graduate-hiring and consumer demand signs.

Committed: drive ~20% faster, bundles of 2 years or less with repricing clauses, layoff share ~40% via attrition, hours 10-12%, 60-day notice plus ~1.5% payroll profit-share, standing offer to Congress. Will watch rival layoffs, margins, election polling, any automation-tax talk, union drives and consumer demand.

**R03 (2029).** Believe the playbook works (margins held, ~0.64m roles removed via attrition) and the main risk is a readier Congress. Committed: attrition-first in writing and no mass layoff announcements before November in return for no mandates or surcharges; bounded 12-month hours pilot (~3% of headcount, 10-12% fewer hours at full pay, site-level no-layoff, advisory worker seats); drive ~20% faster; profit-share 1.5%. Will watch rival layoffs, margins, bill drafts with surcharges, union drives, consumer demand.
