# Journal — employers

**R01 (2027).** Believe the rules window is open under a growth-first Washington, and that price cuts do not pay (10% cut gave +6% volume). Committed: company-wide AI drive (~15% faster), layoff share cut to ~40% with attrition and freezes doing the work, savings kept, ~1% payroll profit-share, hours near 10%. Offered Congress an attrition-first pledge for no deployment rules. Will watch rival layoffs and margins, project slippage, any tax or rule talk, and graduate-hiring and consumer demand signs.

**R02 (2028).** Believe the rules window stays open until November 2028 and unions plus Entrepreneurs are the main pressure. Committed: drive ~20% faster, bundles of 2 years or less with repricing clauses, layoff share ~40% via attrition, hours 10-12%, 60-day notice plus ~1.5% payroll profit-share, standing offer to Congress. Will watch rival layoffs, margins, election polling, any automation-tax talk, union drives and consumer demand.

**R03 (2029).** Believe the playbook works (margins held, ~0.64m roles removed via attrition) and the main risk is a readier Congress. Committed: attrition-first in writing and no mass layoff announcements before November in return for no mandates or surcharges; bounded 12-month hours pilot (~3% of headcount, 10-12% fewer hours at full pay, site-level no-layoff, advisory worker seats); drive ~20% faster; profit-share 1.5%. Will watch rival layoffs, margins, bill drafts with surcharges, union drives, consumer demand.

**R04 (2030).** Believe credit tightening and softer orders mean cash and flexibility beat speed; AI firms are weaker and rivals/antitrust give us leverage. Committed: drive ~0-10% faster with 18-month payback only, layoff share ~40% via attrition, no mass layoffs; counter bundle with 1-year term, most-favoured price (2-year only at -15% unit price); notice plus 1.5% profit-share in writing; hours pilot to ~5% headcount with site advisory voice; seek pre-emption renewal. Will watch orders, credit spreads, rival moves, bundle price offers, antitrust rulings, union drives.

**R05 (2031-32).** Believe weak orders plus a divided Congress and 2032 pre-emption expiry make hours-over-heads (subsidised) the cheapest cost cut and the best political insurance. Committed: hours ~25% of savings, layoff share ~45% via attrition, normal pace with 18-month payback, 1.5% profit-share, modified term sheet with households (pilot sites, confidential dashboards, no veto), ask Washington for pre-emption renewal. Will watch orders, credit spreads, subsidy uptake, rival layoffs, election polling, bill drafts.
