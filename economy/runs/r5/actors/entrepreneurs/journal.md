# Journal — entrepreneurs

## Round 01 (January–December 2027)

We're in the window. Credit is loose, AI tools are cheap, hiring is easy. Shifting 15% more new ventures toward AI-only and AI-hybrid models—labour's share at record low, margins too good to ignore. Capital flowing into services and light manufacturing where we see customer traction. Watching: (1) whether demand shift is real, (2) whether bigger firms copy this, (3) Congress on zoning and AI access.

## Round 02 (January–December 2028)

Demand for people-in-the-loop services is real, not a blip. Vendor bundling is the binding constraint—they're pricing for big buyers, cutting my margins. Committing to shift 60% of expansion capital back to human-heavy hybrids: tutoring, legal, design. Expect 0.25–0.3m net new jobs. Pushing Congress on antitrust and AI access. Will watch: vendor pricing moves, customer demand durability, congressional antitrust appetite, whether big firms copy our model.
