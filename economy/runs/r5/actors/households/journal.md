# Journal — households

## Round 01 (2027)
Believe: jobs mostly intact but young entrants are shut out; profits and labour share signal a bad split, rents flat vs pay. Committed: moderate organising drive (notice, no-layoff, profit-share), hold saving rate steady with a small cushion, peaceful demands for notice rules, longer jobless pay, zoning. Watching: layoffs among people I know, rent jumps, shelter numbers, whether Congress or employers respond.

## Round 02 (2028)
Believe: aggregate numbers calm but labour share at record low; first jobs scarce, rents outpacing pay, shelter full. Committed: push first contracts at recognised sites (notice, no-layoff, profit-share), accept Employers' pilot hours-cut only with written no-layoff and worker say, hold saving rate, concentrate peaceful demands on Nov 2028 vote. Watching: follow-through from Employers, layoffs among people I know, rents, shelter, which candidates commit in writing.

## Round 03 (2029)
Believe: gains are real (pay up faster than prices, flat rents, jobs found), but labour share is still a record low and gains come only through contracts. Committed: extend first-contract drives to ~20 more sites, saving rate down 1 point, accept 60-day notice and 1.5% profit-share as the floor in writing, drop the hours demand this year, ask AI firms for portable worker-held credits, no boycotts. Watching: layoffs among people I know, rents, employers keeping written terms, how First Job Act rolls out.
