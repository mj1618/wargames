# Run r5 — summary

## 1. This play-through in three sentences

Machines became able to do two-thirds of desk work by 2036, but firms handed over less than a fifth, and new kinds of work replaced the lost jobs almost exactly one for one. The only bad year was 2030, a recession caused by a share-market fall and tight credit, not by automation. Pay rose a fifth, housing got cheaper and poverty fell, while office work shrank and labour's share of income slipped.

## 2. The hidden conditions and the events

The players did not know they had been dealt a kind world behind a frightening frontier. Desk-work AI improved very fast and robots ran about six years behind, but firms adopted slowly. Cheaper things did not make people buy much more. Against that, many new kinds of human work appeared, pay shared well in productivity gains, and lost wages did not snowball. Housing could be built only partly. Washington was divided and slow, and the central bank would not cushion downturns.

Events drawn by dice: a leap in AI capability (2027), a boom in cheap AI-delivered services (2028), a share-market fall and credit squeeze (2030), a fall in building costs (2031–32) and a land squeeze in AI cities (2035–36).

## 3. The story

- **2027.** Employers automate through hiring freezes, not layoffs. The retraining bill fails, a court blocks the layoff-reporting rule, and a housing bill passes at half size.
- **2028.** Households buy cheap tutoring and legal help; new firms hire to supply them. Job creation beats destruction.
- **2029.** The AI labs put $2bn in trust, which unlocks a law that blocks state AI licensing and makes layoff reporting mandatory. A funded housing law passes too.
- **2030.** AI shares fall by a third and credit tightens. 4.2m jobs go because spending fell; 0.7m go to machines. The employers' no-layoffs pledge had a 60% chance of holding and broke.
- **2031–32.** Credit returns and 5.7m jobs are created. The bill for cheques funded by an AI-profit levy fails. The pro-growth coalition then loses the election on a one-in-four roll.
- **2033–34.** A levy on profits with cash for poorer households passes at half size.
- **2035–36.** Automation reaches 3.0m jobs in two years; 3.2m are created. A housing law offsets the land squeeze.

## 4. Answer 1 — jobs

About 14.2m jobs were destroyed over ten years (9.7m by automation, 4.5m by lost spending, nearly all in 2030) and 14.2m were created. Of those, 8.9m were new kinds of work, 4.3m came from spending recovering, and 1.0m from slow re-hiring. Employment rose from 162.4m to 168.9m only because the population grew. Unemployment went 4.1% → 5.3% (2030) → 4.0%; participation 61.6% → 60.7% → 61.5%. Office jobs fell from 48.0m to 46.0m; care, building, retail and new sectors grew.

The Jevons effect (cheaper output creating more work in the same trade) held only partly. Prices barely fell and buyers responded weakly, so almost no jobs came from cheaper prices. The economy as a whole replaced the work; office workers as a group did not get theirs back.

## 5. Answer 2 — living standards

Median household real income rose 16%, the bottom fifth 15%. The top 1% share went from 20.0% to 20.8%; labour's share fell from 52.8% to 50.7%. Housing costs fell 8%. Goods and digital services fell under 1%; health care rose 0.5%. Poverty fell from 12.9% to 9.7%; homelessness from 22 to 14.6 per 10,000. The deficit ended where it began, 6.0% of GDP; debt rose from 100% to 118%.

## 6. What drove it

Mostly the hidden conditions: slow adoption, plentiful new work and well-shared pay. Three decisions mattered. Founders kept moving money into firms that hire. Washington passed three housing laws, which is why housing got cheaper. Employers chose freezes over layoffs, except in 2030. Dice set the timing: the 2030 slump, the broken pledge, the failed cheques bill and the 2032 election.

## 7. Where the players were unrealistic

The referee's auditor called them "very cooperative". Households never struck or boycotted in ten years, even after their organising drives failed five rounds running. Employers paid for shorter weeks unpressured. The labs volunteered a levy on themselves.
