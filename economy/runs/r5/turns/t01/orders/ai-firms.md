# Orders — AI firms — round 01 (January–December 2027)

## Read of the situation
The leap is ours and nothing behind it is slowing, so this year is the window to convert capability into contracts before rivals catch up. Washington is growth-first and the macro picture benign (unemployment 4.1%, profits a record 14.9% of GDP), but two-thirds of the public want AI slowed and labour's share is a record-low 52.8%. Customers are mostly piloting and price cuts get only half-matched, so margins live in enterprise bundles, not price wars.

## Options considered
- Race at +30% with open access to grab share first.
- Steady pace, premium pricing, let the coalition carry us.
- Unconventional: voluntary slow-down pledge — rejected; foreign labs won't hold it.

## Decisions this period
1. **Deploy pace: race, +20% above baseline.** Ship the new generation to enterprise in H1, consumers in H2. Not +30%: power and chips won't sustain it.
2. **Pricing: premium bundling.** Hold list prices; sell three-year enterprise bundles (model + agents + support) with volume lock-in. Long-tail token prices drift down anyway, giving a "savings go to customers" story for free.
3. **Politics: $250m lobbying and campaign spending behind the growth-first coalition**, plus a $2bn/yr industry-funded Workforce Transition Compact (retraining, wage-insurance top-ups), offered in exchange for federal pre-emption of state deployment rules and no licensing or moratorium. Cover, not charity; contingent on the deal.

## Public statement
"This generation does a week's professional work in an afternoon. We are committing $2 billion a year to help workers move with it, and we ask Washington for one national rulebook instead of fifty."

## Messages
- **To federal government:** $2bn/yr for worker transition and public backing for your agenda, in return for federal pre-emption of state AI deployment rules and a commitment against licensing. Deal?
- **To employers:** Three-year bundles signed now lock in current rates before the next generation reprices the market; early signers get priority capacity.

## What would change my mind
A moratorium or licensing bill reaching committee with coalition votes would make us trade pace for a deal.
