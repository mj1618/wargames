# Orders — households — round 01 (2027)

## 1. Read of the situation
Most of us still have jobs (unemployment 4.1%), so this is not yet panic. But labour's share is a record-low 52.8% while profits sit at a record 14.9% of GDP, and our kids with new degrees cannot land a first job. Rents rose in line with pay and the shelter is as full as last year, so nothing is getting easier. The "Generation leap" headline tells us it will not stay quiet.

## 2. Options considered
- Wait and see until layoffs hit people we know.
- Organise now while jobs are still plentiful and bargaining is easier.
- (Unconventional) Pool neighbourhood and union funds into a shared rent and emergency fund, so a layoff does not become eviction.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Organising drive (`union_pressure`: moderate).** Focus on offices, call centres, logistics and warehouses, where AI exposure is highest and the bosses are already talking about automation. Ask for 90-day notice of AI-driven layoffs, no-layoff or redeployment-with-pay clauses, and a share of productivity gains as profit-sharing or shorter weeks. No strikes yet: they work best while jobs are plentiful, but we have little cash, so we start with drives and bargaining. Keep it up all year, because it fades if we don't.
2. **Spending vs saving (`household_saving`: hold).** We are worried but not frightened. Cutting back now would cost our neighbours' jobs. Quietly build a little extra cushion (a month of rent) without a big change. We would cut spending hard only if layoffs reach people we know.
3. **Votes and demands.** Demand that the new Congress pass notice-and-severance rules, stronger unemployment insurance (longer than 26 weeks, wider coverage), and help for first-job seekers. Back state and local candidates who allow building near jobs; rent is what actually hurts. Peaceful protest only, and local fights over data-centre power bills.

No boycotts this year. We would not boycott AI services that make our lives cheaper without a visible reason.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** "Record profits and record-low worker pay is not a plan. Give us notice, a share of the gains, and rents we can afford."
- **To: Labs and large employers** — We will bargain for notice and sharing now; if we have to, we will fight later. Better to deal now.
- **To: Federal government** — Pass notice, jobless-pay and first-job support while it is cheap to do so, before the layoffs make it expensive.

## 5. What would change my mind
Layoffs reaching people we know, or a sharp rent spike, would push us to save hard, strike and protest; cheap goods and visible new jobs would soften us.
