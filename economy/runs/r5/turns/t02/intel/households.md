# Intel — Households — round 02 (January–December 2028)

**Published, end 2027:** unemployment 4.0%; participation 61.6%; employment 162.8m (59.0% of adults); GDP +1.6%; median real income index 100.2; prices flat; labour share 52.6%; deficit 6.0%, debt 103% of GDP. Announced AI-linked layoffs about 170,000.

**Washington:** Transition Act failed; half-size housing incentives start 2028; court blocked the layoff-disclosure order. Elections November 2028.

**Headline — Cheap services catch on:** households buy AI-delivered tutoring, legal help and home design in bulk, with people in the loop.

**Only you see:** Your organising drive won recognition at a string of sites; it fades if not kept up. Pay rose a little faster than prices; rents slightly more. Few people you know were laid off, but first jobs are scarcer. The shelter is as full as last year.

**Messages to you**
- From Employers: "Open to talks on hours reductions with unchanged weekly pay in pilot units, if you agree to flexibility on task redesign."
- From Entrepreneurs: "We need to hire people to build and run these new firms. Cheap AI means one founder can create roles that didn't exist a year ago. Retrain for the supervisor and scaling jobs, not the ones that never were."
