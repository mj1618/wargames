# Orders — Entrepreneurs — round 02

## Read of the situation

The market is working for people-in-the-loop services—households are buying AI-delivered tutoring, legal help and design—but AI vendors held list prices and pushed bundles designed for big buyers, raising my costs relative to incumbents. I hired 0.2m people last year in genuinely new lines of work, while my AI-only ventures earned well but created almost no jobs. Credit remains loose and early orders for this year's people-in-the-loop services are strong.

## Options considered

1. Double down on high-margin AI-only ventures—they're profitable but won't scale hiring.
2. Shift back to people-heavy services and let the market window close on demand we can't meet.
3. Fight the vendor bundling problem politically while capturing the people-in-the-loop boom.

## Decisions this period

1. **Rebalance toward people-in-the-loop.** Redirect 60% of expansion capital from AI-only to hybrid models: AI-assisted tutoring, legal services, home design with human review. This matches customer demand we saw and aligns with my actual interest in building hiring businesses. Scale: shift roughly $3–4B in venture and growth capital from pure-AI to hybrid, expecting 0.25–0.3m net job creation.

2. **Lobby hard on vendor access.** Push Congress for antitrust scrutiny of AI bundle-and-lock tactics, and for open access to base models at fair pricing. My voice is weaker than big employers', but this is where the fight is. Partner with other small players on antitrust messaging.

3. **Lean into unregulated niches.** Continue hiring in trades, care, and services where zoning and licensing are least restrictive. These are the sectors where people-heavy AI hybrids pay best.

## Public statement

"The window for affordable services is now. Entrepreneurs will hire millions if vendors stop pricing for incumbents."

## Messages

**To Congress:** Push antitrust and AI access; I'll back worker-friendly licensing reform if you force open the model market.

## What would change my mind

If customer demand for services collapses, I revert to AI-only immediately.
