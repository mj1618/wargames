# Orders — Federal government — round 02 (2028)

## 1. Read of the situation
The numbers show no crisis: unemployment 4.0%, employment 162.8m, median income 100.2, receipts on forecast, unrest low. But labour share fell to 52.6%, 170,000 layoffs were blamed on AI, and firms are cutting through hiring freezes that statistics won't show before November. Deficit 6.0% and debt 103% mean anything we pass must be small or paid for by someone else.

## 2. Options considered
- Stand pat and campaign on 4.0% unemployment and cheap AI services.
- Take the labs' deal: pre-emption for $2bn a year, plus a small worker bill before the election.
- Unconventional: make the labs' fund a statutory "first-job dividend" paid direct to under-26s, so the cheque carries our name.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Bill 1 — AI Deployment Framework Act.** Federal pre-emption of state deployment rules, with disclosure of AI-linked layoffs written into statute (`deploy_regulation` = disclosure, re-enacting the blocked order). No licensing this Congress — not a permanent pledge. Condition: the labs' $2bn for 2027 is paid before the floor vote. Deregulators win; security hawks satisfied.
2. **Bill 2 — First Job and Wage Insurance Act.** `retraining_pct_gdp` = 0.1% (about $30bn), half the failed bill: wage insurance, 60-day layoff notice, hiring credit for 22–25-year-olds. Offset by the labs' fund and trimmed elsewhere; no new tax, `capital_tax_pct` stays 0. Deficit hawks set the size; it answers households cheaply before November.
3. **Executive — Employer compact.** Sign large employers to attrition-first, no mass AI layoffs through 2028, and published retraining places, in return for no payroll surcharge or mandates. Push housing-incentive money out by summer.

## 4. Public statement
"America leads in AI and unemployment is 4%. This year we pass one national rulebook and a first-job guarantee of support for young workers — paid for by the industry, not the taxpayer."

**Messages**
- To AI firms: "Deal on pre-emption and no licensing this Congress, with statutory disclosure included. Pay the overdue $2bn before the vote, or there is no vote."
- To Employers: "No mandates or surcharges. In return: attrition-first in writing and no mass layoff announcements before November."

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment above 5%, wage-tax receipts below forecast, or the labs still not paying by mid-year.
