# Intel — AI firms — round 03 (January–December 2029)

**Published, end 2028:** unemployment 3.8%; participation 61.6%; employment 163.7m (59.1% of adults); GDP +2.0%; median real income index 101.2; prices flat; labour share 52.6%; deficit 6.0%. Announced AI-linked layoffs about 260,000.

**Washington:** First Job and Wage Insurance Act passed, starts 2029; Framework Act never voted. Growth-first kept its lead; the new Congress looks readier to legislate.

**Headline — Data revision:** 2028 unemployment was overstated by 0.4 points; revised to 3.4%.

**Only you see:** Consumer and small-business volumes jumped after the price cuts; enterprise bundles held their margins. Employers are refusing three-year terms. Customers still use a small fraction of what the models can do. The $500m training fund is paid; the $2bn Compact is not, and the pre-emption bill died over who moves first. Unions now hold first contracts at several of your biggest customers.

**Messages to you**
- From Federal government: "Deal on pre-emption and no licensing this Congress, with statutory disclosure included. Pay the overdue $2bn before the vote, or there is no vote."
- From Employers: "We will sign 1-2 year bundles at current rates with a repricing clause. We will not sign 3-year lock-ins."
