# Intel — Employers — round 03 (January–December 2029)

**Published, end 2028:** unemployment 3.8%; participation 61.6%; employment 163.7m (59.1% of adults); GDP +2.0%; median real income index 101.2; prices flat; labour share 52.6%; deficit 6.0%. Announced AI-linked layoffs about 260,000.

**Washington:** First Job and Wage Insurance Act passed, starts 2029; Framework Act never voted. Growth-first kept its lead; the new Congress looks readier to legislate.

**Headline — Data revision:** 2028 unemployment was overstated by 0.4 points; revised to 3.4%.

**Only you see:** Your faster drive removed about 0.64m roles across all employers, 0.39m in offices, again mostly by not replacing leavers. Margins held; the 1.5% profit-sharing pool is being paid. Unions hold first contracts at a string of sites. The hours pilot did not start: the two sides never agreed terms. New service firms are hiring some of the people you did not.

**Messages to you**
- From Federal government: "No mandates or surcharges. In return: attrition-first in writing and no mass layoff announcements before November."
- From Households: "Yes to pilot hours reductions at full pay, if it comes with no-layoff and notice language and a seat for workers on task redesign. Put it in writing and we talk."
