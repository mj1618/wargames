# Intel — Entrepreneurs — round 03 (January–December 2029)

**Published, end 2028:** unemployment 3.8%; participation 61.6%; employment 163.7m (59.1% of adults); GDP +2.0%; median real income index 101.2; prices flat; labour share 52.6%; deficit 6.0%. Announced AI-linked layoffs about 260,000.

**Washington:** First Job and Wage Insurance Act passed, starts 2029; Framework Act never voted. Growth-first kept its lead; the new Congress looks readier to legislate.

**Headline — Data revision:** 2028 unemployment was overstated by 0.4 points; revised to 3.4%.

**Only you see:** New firms and new lines of work took on about 0.5m people in 2028, more than twice last year; a further 0.3m jobs across the economy came from the surge in household orders, which may not repeat. Hybrid people-in-the-loop services filled their order books. Cheaper consumer and small-business AI access lowered your costs; enterprise bundles still favour incumbents. Washington took no antitrust or open-access action. Credit is easy.
