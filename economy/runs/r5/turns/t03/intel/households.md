# Intel — Households — round 03 (January–December 2029)

**Published, end 2028:** unemployment 3.8%; participation 61.6%; employment 163.7m (59.1% of adults); GDP +2.0%; median real income index 101.2; prices flat; labour share 52.6%; deficit 6.0%. Announced AI-linked layoffs about 260,000.

**Washington:** First Job and Wage Insurance Act passed, starts 2029; Framework Act never voted. Growth-first kept its lead; the new Congress looks readier to legislate.

**Headline — Data revision:** 2028 unemployment was overstated by 0.4 points; revised to 3.4%.

**Only you see:** First contracts signed at the recognised offices, call centres and warehouses: notice before role cuts and a profit-share. Pay rose clearly faster than prices; rents were flat. More people you know found work than lost it, some at firms that are brand new; first jobs are a little easier to find. The shelter has a few empty beds.

**Messages to you**
- From AI firms: "We back 90-day notice and retraining credits at our largest customers now. In return, no support for moratorium or licensing bills."
- From Employers: "We will talk on 60 days' notice and about 1.5% of payroll in profit-sharing. A hard line on hours or job guarantees means we walk away."
