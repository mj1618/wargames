# Orders — AI firms — round 03 (2029)

**Read of the situation.** Unemployment revised to 3.4%, employment 163.7m, GDP +2.0%: the displacement story has no numbers behind it, and a Congress readier to legislate is one we can shape. Adoption is our bottleneck — customers use a small fraction of capability, consumer and small-business volumes jumped after the price cuts, and enterprise margins held even as buyers refuse three-year terms. Government offers pre-emption and no licensing but wants the $2bn before the vote, after the last deal died over who moved first.

**Options considered.**
- Pay $2bn up front and trust the vote.
- Refuse, bank the 3.4% headline, fight every bill.
- Unconventional: accept a modest AI-profits levy for permanent pre-emption and a liability safe harbour.

**Decisions.**
1. **Deploy pace: race, +20%**, with marginal spend on deployment — integration teams, agent tooling, usage guarantees — not raw scale. Adoption is where revenue is; the labour market gives cover.
2. **Pricing: take the employers' terms, conditionally.** Two-year enterprise bundles at current rates, repricing capped at ±15%, plus a usage tier rewarding firms that hand over whole workflows. Consumer and small-business prices fall another 20%. One lab wanted three-year terms; the majority preferred volume.
3. **Politics: $2bn into escrow, not Treasury.** Workforce Compact trust funded this quarter — $1bn released on enactment of pre-emption-with-disclosure, $1bn a year later; it reverts to us if no bill passes this Congress. Statutory disclosure is fine. Political spend $350m; the $500m/yr training fund continues.

**Public statement.** "Record-low unemployment alongside the fastest capability growth in history. We are putting $2bn into a Workforce Compact trust today and ask Congress for one national rulebook."

**To Federal government:** Accepted in substance. $2bn into an independent trust this quarter, released on enactment — neither side carries the other's risk. Disclosure in statute, yes; licensing or deployment limits, no.

**To Employers:** Two-year bundles at current rates, capped repricing, plus a usage tier that cuts unit prices as you hand over whole workflows. Sign by Q2 and rates hold through 2031.

**What would change my mind.** A licensing or moratorium bill winning coalition votes in committee, or AI layoffs doubling while unemployment turns visibly upward.
