# Orders — Federal government — round 03 (2029)

## 1. Read of the situation
Unemployment is 3.8% published and revised down to 3.4%, GDP grew 2.0%, median income is 101.2 and receipts are on forecast: voters feel no jobs crisis, and we were just re-elected. The weak spots are slow-burning: labour share stuck at a record-low 52.6%, AI-linked layoffs up from 170,000 to 260,000, and a 6.0% deficit with debt near 105%. The labs paid $500m but not the $2bn, so they still owe us; we have a working majority for small and medium bills only.

## 2. Options considered
- Bank the win: pass the Framework Act on the labs' terms and do nothing else.
- Use the honeymoon for a medium housing bill, the one cost-of-living item voters feel.
- Unconventional: side with entrepreneurs against our own donors and open the model market by antitrust.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **AI Deployment Framework Act (bill 1, medium).** Pre-empts state licensing through 2030 and makes workforce disclosure statutory. Money first: the $2bn Compact goes into escrow before the floor vote, or no vote. No capital tax, no deployment limits. Deregulators and security hawks win; deficit hawks get it free.
2. **Housing Supply Act (bill 2, medium).** Funded building with zoning conditions, 0.2% of GDP a year from 2030, offset by trimming unused retraining authority so the deficit does not rise. Rents are the visible grievance before 2030. Deficit hawks set the size; deregulators win the zoning strings.
3. **Competition, by executive action.** Open two antitrust cases on enterprise bundling and exclusive access terms; no break-ups, no mandates yet. Cheap, popular, and leverage if the labs stall on escrow. Deregulators grumble; security hawks get a carve-out for national-security work.

## 4. Public statement
"Jobs are up, pay is up, and America leads in AI. This year we build homes and write one national rulebook, with the industry paying its share for workers."

**Messages**
- To AI firms: "Pre-emption is on the calendar. $2bn in escrow before the vote; no escrow, no vote, and the bundling cases widen."
- To Entrepreneurs: "Two bundling cases open this year. Back the Framework Act publicly and open-access terms are next."

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment past 5%, receipts falling below forecast, or graduate joblessness becoming a street issue would move me to wage insurance at scale and a harder line on the labs.
