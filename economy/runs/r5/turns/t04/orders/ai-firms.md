# Orders — AI firms — round 04 (2030)

## Read of the situation
Shares are down a third and lenders want tighter terms, so capital, not capability, binds this year. Customers use about a sixth of model capability, so another leap earns nothing; adoption tooling does. Government wants a second $2bn escrow for pre-emption beyond 2030, bundling cases as the stick — the 2029 deal again, with $1bn still due into the trust.

## Options considered
- Race on, borrow at worse terms, bet the wobble is short.
- Pay nothing more, fight pre-emption in court, risk 50 state licensing regimes in 2031.
- Unconventional: unbundle ourselves before the courts do — make the antitrust liability the SMB growth story.

## Decisions this period
1. **deploy_pace: steady** (baseline, down from +20%). Training capex growth frozen; freed money goes to deployment tooling, integration partners and inference capacity. Rationale: one-sixth utilisation means the shortfall is adoption; credit says stop borrowing.
2. **ai_pricing: split.** Enterprise two-year bundles held as signed (cash flow). New à-la-carte usage tier for SMB and consumers at −25%, no bundling or exclusivity. Rationale: that is where volume grew, and it hands the antitrust courts a remedy already in place.
3. **Politics, $300m spend.** Pay the $1bn due. Offer a further $2bn into the same escrow, released on enactment, for pre-emption through 2034 plus a consent-decree path on bundling. Publicly back portable retraining credits (Households' ask — cheap cover). Labs' majority agrees; Vanta grumbles on pricing but goes along.

## Public statement
"We are moderating our build-out, opening usage-based access to small businesses and consumers, and backing retraining credits that travel with the worker."

**Messages**
- To Federal government: "$1bn paid this quarter. $2bn more into the trust under the 2029 release terms, for pre-emption through 2034 and a negotiated consent decree on bundling. Escrow before the vote, as before."
- To Entrepreneurs: "Usage tier at −25%, no bundles, live Q2. Scale on it and say so publicly."

## What would change my mind
A rival, domestic or foreign, racing through the credit squeeze forces us back to +20%.
