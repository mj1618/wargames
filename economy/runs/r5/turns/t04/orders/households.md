# Orders — households — round 04 (2030)

## 1. Read of the situation
Pay beat prices again (real income index 103.0) and rents are flat, but labour share hit another record low at 52.3% and more office jobs went unrefilled instead of being filled. Our drive at the next tier of sites fell short, so gains only exist where contracts exist. Now AI shares are down a third and card and car-loan credit is tightening, so a bad year could land on people with no cushion, while the top tenth, who own the shares, feel it on paper only.

## 2. Options considered
- Keep spending as in 2029 and trust the pay gains.
- Pull back sharply (saving up 2 points) and pause all campaigns until the credit squeeze is clear.
- Unconventional: negotiate a mutual "credit and rent hardship pledge" with employers and the entrepreneurs, with paid-while-training slots as the entry route for people who lose offices jobs.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Defend before expanding.** Put organising effort into holding and renewing existing first contracts (notice, no-layoff, 1.5% profit-share) and re-approach the sites where the drive fell short, with small local drives, not a big new wave. Low pace, about 8 sites, since the financial wobble makes workers scared to sign and jobs less plentiful.
2. **Saving rate up 1 point.** Credit is tightening and a financial shock can come through to jobs; we trim non-essentials and avoid new card and car debt. This costs some shop jobs, but a household with no buffer cannot do otherwise.
3. **Take Employers' offer, with edits.** Send a term sheet: shorter weeks at full pay at pilot sites, 60-day notice and no-layoff written for those sites, a bridge to other sites if the pilot meets targets, and worker seats on redesign panels with a right to see AI deployment plans (not only advisory). Also take up Entrepreneurs' paid-to-train places, and ask for a promise that trainees are not dropped when the credit squeeze hits. No boycotts this year.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** "Pay is up but too many jobs are vanishing quietly. If shares fall, working families must not be the ones who pay for it: keep notice, keep contracts, keep people hired."

**To Employers:** "Term sheet coming: pilot shorter weeks at full pay, with notice and no-layoff at pilot sites and a path to others, and panel seats that can see deployment plans. We will sign within 60 days if you agree to the changes."

**To Entrepreneurs:** "Welcome the paid-to-train hires. Please put in writing that trainees keep their pay through the credit squeeze and get first call on the new jobs."

## 5. What would change my mind
Mass layoffs among people I know, or a sharp jump in shelters and food banks, would push me to save more and turn to protest; a clear fall in rents and credit costs would let me spend again.
