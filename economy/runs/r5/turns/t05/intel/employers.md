# Intel — Employers — round 05 (January 2031–December 2032)

**Published, end 2030:** unemployment 5.5%; employment 160.4m (57.5% of adults); GDP −0.6%; median real income 101.2; housing costs down 2%; labour share 50.5%; deficit 6.8%. Announced layoffs about 1.9m, mostly weak orders. 2029: poverty 12.5%, homelessness 21 per 10,000.

**Washington:** Main Street Act passed (money from 2031). Pre-emption extended to 2032, tied to a $1bn-a-year training fund. Growth-first kept its lead; Congress more divided.

**Headline — Housing breakthrough:** Factory-built homes win code approval in most states; costs per home fall sharply.

**Only you see:** You automated away about 0.72m roles, but cut several times that as orders fell, worst in retail and offices. Many member firms broke the attrition-first pledge. Margins squeezed.

**Messages to you**
- From Federal government: "Work-sharing subsidy and credit guarantees are coming; you were consulted. Use hours cuts, not layoffs, and keep attrition-first through 2030. No mandates or surcharges this year."
- From Households: "Term sheet coming: pilot shorter weeks at full pay, with notice and no-layoff at pilot sites and a path to others, and panel seats that can see deployment plans. We will sign within 60 days if you agree to the changes."
