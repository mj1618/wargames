# Orders — Federal government — round 05 (2031–2032)

## 1. Read of the situation
The crisis is now in the numbers: unemployment 5.5%, 3.6m jobs gone in a year (164.0m to 160.4m), GDP −0.6%, labour share 50.5%. The budget is squeezed from both sides — receipts 17.0%, spending 23.8%, deficit 6.8%, debt 112% — and the central bank did not cut. Employers broke attrition-first, unrest is rising, and we face voters in November 2032.

## 2. Options considered
- Hold the line: let the Main Street Act work, protect the bond market, hope for recovery by 2032.
- Temporary, targeted relief cheques plus a housing build-out, with the bill arriving after the election.
- Unconventional: trade the labs pre-emption through 2034 for open-access terms and a standing levy paid into a worker dividend.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Paycheck Bridge Act (bill 1).** Cash transfers to the bottom two-fifths at 1.0% of GDP a year for 2032–33, sunsetting; retraining and wage insurance up to 0.4% of GDP; work-sharing subsidy extended. Part-funded by a 4-point levy on capital/AI profits starting 2033. Pre-emption to 2034 rides this bill only if the $2bn is in escrow first. *Security hawks and pragmatists beat the deficit hawks; deregulators get pre-emption.*
2. **Factory Homes Act (bill 2).** Housing Supply Act doubled to 0.4% of GDP, tied to factory-built homes and zoning conditions; targets construction jobs and rents in laid-off metros. *Deregulators win: supply, not subsidy.*
3. **Consent decree on bundling (executive).** Settle the two cases only with open-access terms and a cheap small-business tier written in; otherwise litigate. No AI bailout.

## 4. Public statement
"Families who lost a pay cheque will get a bridge this year, and we will build homes at a price working people can afford. Those who profit most from this technology will help pay for it."

**Messages**
- To AI firms: "Yes to 2034 — escrow $2bn first, and the decree carries open access plus an SMB tier. A 4-point levy from 2033 is the price of no licensing."
- To Entrepreneurs: "Open access goes into the decree. Back the Paycheck Bridge Act publicly."

## 5. What would change my mind
Bond yields spiking on the deficit, or unemployment falling back under 5% by mid-2031, would shrink the transfers; unemployment past 7% would make them universal.
