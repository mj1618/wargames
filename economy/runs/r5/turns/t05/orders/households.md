# Orders — Households — Round 05 (2031–2032)

**1. Read of the situation**
Unemployment is 5.5% and employment fell 3.6m, with shop and office jobs hit first; real pay fell about 1% and labour share is 50.5%, a record low. Employers broke written attrition-first pledges, so paper promises without enforcement are worth little. Rents are easing, credit is loosening and factory-built homes are coming, which is the first sign of a cheaper life. The Main Street Act money starts this year.

**2. Options considered**
- Rebuild by defending contracts and pushing for enforceable terms at existing sites.
- Big strike wave now (risky with jobs scarce and savings thin).
- Unconventional: worker-held portable credits plus a housing co-op/community land push, using the factory-built homes to get cheaper housing without waiting for employers.

**3. Decisions this period**
1. **Organising (`union_pressure`):** Make the core job enforcement. Win clauses with penalties (severance and pay-in-lieu if notice or no-layoff is broken) at existing sites. Accept Employers' offer of notice, profit-share floor and site-level advisory seats on role changes, but only with a signed sunset review and a data-sharing clause. Run about 10 small local drives, no strikes while unemployment is high.
2. **Saving (`household_saving`):** Hold the saving rate steady this period. We are scared, but more cutting would cost our own neighbours' jobs. If credit loosens and layoffs ease, spend slightly more in 2032.
3. **Votes and housing:** Put effort into the November 2032 election with peaceful, targeted demands: enforceable notice, Main Street money reaching the shop-floor, and local approval of factory-built homes and rent-stabilising permits. Back candidates who commit in writing. Take up Entrepreneurs' paid-to-train places with a no-drop promise again. No boycotts.

**4. Public statement and messages**
Public: "We will take any job floor in writing, but a promise with no penalty did not stop the layoffs; we want terms that bite."
- To Employers: "Yes to notice, profit-share floor and advisory seats. We want penalties for breach, a 12-month review and access to deployment plans for the advisory seats. Sign the earlier term sheet's no-layoff and shorter-week pilot at recognised sites."
- To Entrepreneurs: "We will take paid-to-train places for people laid off from shops and offices, if the placement is guaranteed and the training counts as portable credit."

**5. What would change my mind**
A second round of mass layoffs among people I know, or a credit crunch, would push us to save more and protest harder; a clear rebound in hiring and falling rents would let us spend and bargain for hours.
