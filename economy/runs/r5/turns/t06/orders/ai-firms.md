# Orders — AI firms — round 06 (2033–34)

## Read of the situation
The recovery (unemployment 4.1%, GDP +3.3%/yr, median income 108.7) has killed the displacement story, yet labour's share at 51.7% and a worker-first Congress turn politics against us anyway. Pre-emption has expired, states are drafting licensing bills, and the bond-market scare means a levy comes whether we bargain or not. Customers use a fifth of capability, so lock-in, not frontier pace, drives revenue.

## Options considered
- Fight licensing state by state, refuse any levy; risk a patchwork plus a hostile federal bill.
- Carry the pre-election offer (levy, open access, SMB tier) to the new Congress as a ready-made framework.
- Unconventional: propose the levy ourselves, on every AI service sold here, foreign and open-weight included: a cost becomes a moat.

## Decisions
1. **Politics: buy one national rule.** Labs split (Vanta would fight the states); majority rules. Offer: 4-point profits levy from 2033 on all AI sold in the US, consent decree settling antitrust with open access and an SMB tier, $2bn escrow released on enactment, training fund held at $1bn/yr, for pre-emption of state licensing through 2036 and no deployment caps. Political spend $400m, half in state capitals.
2. **Pricing: lock in before the levy.** Enterprise terms extended to three years at −15% for 50% committed volume, signed by mid-2033; SMB tier opened now.
3. **Pace: steady, capex +10%,** into Factory Homes robotics and integration tooling that lifts utilisation, not the frontier.

## Public statement
"We will pay our share and settle the antitrust cases. In return America needs one rule for AI, not fifty."

Messages:
- To Federal government: "Your predecessor's terms (4-point levy, decree, $2bn on enactment) stand, if pre-emption runs to 2036 and the levy covers every AI service sold here, foreign included."
- To Large employers: "A fifty-state patchwork hits you harder than us. Back one federal framework; sign three-year terms before any levy."

## What would change my mind
A bill pairing the levy with deployment caps, or a rival racing, sends us back to +20% and state-by-state fighting.
