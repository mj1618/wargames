# Employers — round 06 orders (2033–2034)

**1. Read of the situation.** Orders have recovered, we removed ~1.75m roles via attrition and hiring more than offset it, so margins are healthy; but the work-sharing subsidy behind our 8% shorter-week cohort has ended, and a worker-first Congress is readier to legislate. A bond-market scare means credit could tighten again, and several states are drafting AI licensing bills now that pre-emption has lapsed.

**2. Options considered.**
- Hold course: normal pace, attrition-led, keep the shorter-week pilot as is.
- Accept the AI firms' offer (2 years at -15% for 40% volume growth) and drive harder to bank the savings early.
- Unconventional: trade the Households' penalties and review for a federal pre-emption-plus-notice deal, and fund the shorter-week sites ourselves as a branded "stability" employer.

**3. Decisions this period.**
- **Automation pace:** normal to slightly faster (~+10%), 18-month payback hurdle. Take the AI firms' deal only in modified form: 2 years, -15%, but volume commitment capped at ~20% growth (not 40%) with a step-down if orders soften, MFN within cohort, and rate protection whatever the cases decide. Hold the cheaper cost line without locking in bond-scare risk.
- **Hours and layoffs:** keep layoff share ~45% via attrition and freezes, no mass layoffs. Keep shorter weeks at ~8% of headcount, self-funded at pilot sites only (top-up phased to ~half pay-loss), not extended company-wide without a subsidy.
- **Households' term sheet:** sign notice (60 days), 1.5% profit-share floor, advisory seats with access to deployment plans (under NDA), a 12-month review, and a capped breach remedy (back-pay style, not open penalties). Sign the no-layoff and shorter-week pilot at recognised sites only. Seek federal pre-emption renewal and a state-licensing safe harbour in return.

**4. Public statement.** "We are keeping hiring and pay stable, sharing gains with workers and meeting every notice commitment; a single national standard is the way to keep it that way." 

Messages:
- **To AI firms:** Counter: 2 years at -15%, 20% committed growth with a step-down trigger on weak orders, MFN in cohort, rate hold; we will sign in 60 days.
- **To Households:** We accept notice, profit-share floor, advisory seats and 12-month review; capped remedies and pilot-site no-layoff only. Dashboards stay confidential.

**5. What would change my mind.** Credit spreads widening sharply or orders falling again would push me to cut the pace and defer the AI deal; a bill with real surcharges would push me to fund the pre-emption fight harder.
