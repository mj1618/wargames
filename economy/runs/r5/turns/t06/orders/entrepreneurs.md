# Orders — Entrepreneurs — round 06

## Read of the situation

New firms hired 1.9m net workers over 2031-32, tripling the crisis-year rate. Housing is booming and credit has recovered to normal. Worker-first Congress is in place and signaling readiness to pass laws; households are directly asking for guaranteed paid training placements to absorb displaced workers.

## Options considered

1. **Aggressive:** Push expansion to 20% above normal, flood housing and hybrid services, back Paycheck Bridge and open API access hard.
2. **Cautious:** Hold at current capacity, wait for bond-market clarity, focus on execution quality over growth.
3. **Unconventional:** Spin up a nationwide training platform guaranteeing job placement; sell workforce services to government directly.

## Decisions this period

1. **Expand new firm formation by 15%** (toward the upper bound), with 75% in people-heavy hybrids and 25% in AI-only. Focus capital in housing (50%), tradesfolk, and paid-to-train services. Expect 0.35–0.4m net new jobs.

2. **Create training placement guarantees**—build formal partnerships with households and government for paid-to-train programs: $600m/year committed, guaranteeing placement and portable credits for displaced workers, targeting 100,000 placements by end 2033.

3. **Push Congress hard:** Back Paycheck Bridge Act publicly; demand open API access to AI models and antitrust action against bundled enterprise licensing; lobby for training credits as portable across employers and geographies.

## Public statement

We're ready to hire and train at scale. Congress should unblock open access to AI and pass Paycheck Bridge—these are the conditions for a labor-led recovery.

## Messages to other players

**To Federal Government:** We'll hit 100,000 paid-to-train placements if Congress passes Paycheck Bridge and mandates open API access. Back these bills and we'll deliver the jobs.

**To Households:** We're committing $600m/year to guaranteed paid-to-train places. If Congress passes Paycheck Bridge, we'll extend to 150,000 placements by 2035. Training counts toward portable credentials.

## What would change my mind

If the bond market breaks and credit tightens again, we'll pause growth until conditions clear.
