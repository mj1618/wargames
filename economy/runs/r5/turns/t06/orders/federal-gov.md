# Orders — Federal government — round 06 (2033–34)

## 1. Read of the situation
The recovery is real and voters feel it: unemployment 4.1%, income 108.7, housing costs down 4%. But labour's share is 51.7%, the deficit is 6.2% (receipts 17.2%, spending 23.4%) and investors are balking at our borrowing. We now lead as worker-first with medium bills usually passing, and the base expects something after 2030's broken employer pledges.

## 2. Options considered
- Revive Paycheck Bridge transfers on borrowed money — bond market says no.
- Pay-as-you-go: a capital levy that funds a modest worker package and trims the deficit.
- Unconventional: trade pre-emption to 2034 for the $2bn plus a consent decree — rejected; labs refuse open access and our base would revolt.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Fair Share and Stability Act (medium bill).** `capital_tax_pct` = 6 from 2034. Half the proceeds go to deficit reduction; the rest funds bottom-two-fifths transfers at `transfers_pct_gdp` = 0.5 and `retraining_pct_gdp` = 0.3. No net new borrowing. Fiscal moderates win on size, labour on the levy.
2. **Main Street and Work-Sharing Renewal (small bill).** 0.15% of GDP a year for 2034–35, inside the levy envelope: work-sharing subsidy with quarterly employer reporting and a clawback for layoffs, plus trainee subsidies and working capital for housing startups. Labour and moderates agree.
3. **No pre-emption renewal; antitrust continues (no Congress needed).** States may license. Bundling cases proceed to trial unless the labs accept open-access terms; `competition_policy` stays at cases, open-access mandate threatened. Progressives win.

## 4. Public statement
"The recovery is here; now it gets shared and paid for. Not one dollar of new borrowing: those who gained most from AI will fund the bridge for those who gained least."

**Messages**
- To AI firms: "Pre-emption is not for sale at $2bn. Accept open access by consent decree and we will discuss a single federal licensing standard; otherwise fifty states and a trial."
- To Employers: "Work-sharing money is renewed with reporting and clawbacks. No mandates this Congress if layoffs stay below the 2030 pace."

## 5. What would change my mind
Bond yields spiking further or the levy failing its vote would make me drop transfers and pass deficit reduction alone; unemployment past 6% would make me go bigger.
