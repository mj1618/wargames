# Orders — Entrepreneurs — round 07 (Jan 2035–Dec 2036)

## Read of the situation

New firms hired 2.4m over the past two years—our people-heavy hybrid model is scaling. Credit is normal, Main Street subsidies and housing capital run through 2035, and the worker-first Congress backs trainee guarantees. The bind: land is getting dear in big metros where jobs concentrate, and the Fair Share Act took effect without mandating cheaper API access—our margin pressure remains.

## Options considered

1. **Aggressive secondary metros play**: Push housing and training in loosening-zoning states and secondary metros where land is cheaper; maintain 70% people-heavy hybrids, expand hiring 15% above normal, commit trainee cohorts now before Main Street expires.
2. **Consolidation and shift**: Slow hiring, reduce housing, shift toward AI-only in metros to dodge land costs.
3. **Double down on antitrust**: Bet that antitrust cases force API access, making people-heavy hybrids permanently cheaper than incumbent labour; use this year to scale before that happens.

**Chosen: Option 1 + push hard on Option 3.**

## Decisions this period

1. **Hiring expansion**: +15% above normal (hire 1.8–2.0m over two years). Maintain 70% people-heavy hybrids (tutoring, trades, care, design, supervision), 30% AI-only (margins). Tradesfolk and hybrid services the core.

2. **Housing pivot**: Capital allocation shifts to 40% housing (down from 50% due to metro land costs). Focus on loosening-zoning states and secondary metros where land and labour are cheaper; housing working capital runs out end-2035, so commit builds now. Expect 15–20% cost reduction vs metro premium.

3. **Trainee guarantee lock-in**: Commit another $600m/year cohort starting now (2035 contracts), first-year pay guaranteed, households named as first call on placements. Commit before Main Street expires end-2035; aim to place 150–200k trainees over the two-year cycle.

## Public statement

"New firms are hiring at pace and scaling trainee guarantees. We're moving housing and trades expansion to secondary metros and loosening-zoning states—where land and credit work. Watch for announcements."

## Messages

**To Congress:** Main Street trainee subsidies and housing capital expire end-2035. Renew them—the model works at 2.4m new hires per cycle but depends on the money. Mandate open API access: cheaper inputs mean more jobs, not fewer.

**To Households:** Every offer names first-year-pay guarantee and puts you as first call on new hires. Signing committed trainees now.

## What would change my mind

If Main Street money gets clawed back before end-2035, or if API prices spike, forcing us to abandon people-heavy hybrids for AI-only.
