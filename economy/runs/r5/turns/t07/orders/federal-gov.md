# Orders — Federal government — round 07 (2035–2036)

## 1. Read of the situation
The numbers voters feel are good: unemployment 3.8%, median income 111.9, poverty 11.4%, homelessness 17. The one live grievance is the headline — land bid up in AI metros, insurers leaving others — and the election is November 2036. The budget is stuck (receipts 17.3%, spending 23.4%, deficit 6.1%, debt 116%), so anything new must pay for itself.

## 2. Options considered
- Coast: no new bills, run on the recovery.
- Housing bill plus a levy-for-pre-emption deal with the labs.
- Unconventional: a federal land-value surcharge on the AI metros, rebated to renters.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Metro Homes and Insurance Act (bill 1, medium).** Housing to top rung: zoning pre-emption in high-cost metros plus Housing First, funded building raised to 0.5% of GDP from 2036, and a federal reinsurance backstop for states that insurers are leaving. Paid for by decision 2. Why: it is the only problem visible in the streets before 2036. Progressives and labour win; fiscal moderates get the pay-for.
2. **AI Services Levy and Open Access Act (bill 2, medium).** Take the labs' deal with an edit: levy from 3 to 4 points, covering every AI service sold here including foreign; consent decree with mandated open API access; $2bn paid on enactment into paid-to-train placements; in exchange a single federal licensing standard pre-empting state licensing through 2036 only. Extra revenue: half to housing, half to the deficit. No Paycheck Bridge revival — at 3.8% there is no case. Fiscal moderates win; labour swallows pre-emption for the levy and open access.
3. **Let Main Street/work-sharing money lapse after 2035** (saves 0.15% of GDP), keeping retraining at current size. Why: bond market first, and credit is no longer tight.

## 4. Public statement
"The recovery is real; now we make it affordable to live where the jobs are. We will build homes, keep families insured, and make every AI seller pay the same fair share."

**Messages**
- To AI firms: "Deal: 4 points, all sellers, decree with open API, $2bn on enactment, pre-emption to end-2036 via a federal standard. No decree, no pre-emption — and we go to trial."
- To Entrepreneurs: "Open API mandate is in our bill. No Paycheck Bridge this Congress; the $2bn funds your placements. Show 100,000 by mid-2036."

## 5. What would change my mind
Unemployment past 5% or a bond-yield spike would flip bill 2's proceeds to transfers or to the deficit respectively.
