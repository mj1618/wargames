# Households — Round 07 orders (2035-36)

## 1. Read of the situation
Pay is up about 4% in real terms, unemployment is 3.8%, and the half-size cheques for the bottom two-fifths started arriving, so most of us feel a little better off. But labour share is still 51.3%, our small drives keep falling short, and the new headline is a housing squeeze: wealth bids up land in a few metros and insurers are leaving other places. Employers' contracts hold, but with capped remedies, no fixed fine and no-layoff only at pilot sites, so we are not protected when things turn.

## 2. Options considered
- Take Employers' deal as signed and keep running small drives.
- Shift most effort to housing and insurance fights (rent caps, zoning, state insurance backstops) plus the Nov 2036 vote.
- Unconventional: pool cheque money and union dues into tenant and homeowner co-ops that buy or hold rental buildings in squeezed metros.

## 3. Decisions this period
1. **Housing and insurance campaign.** Put about half our organising effort into tenant unions and renter and homeowner coalitions in the squeezed metros and insured-out regions. Demands: state zoning relief, public insurance backstops, anti-gouging rent rules. Peaceful; no strikes. This is where our bills are actually rising.
2. **Hold and widen contracts.** Run about 8 drives (warehouses, care, call centres) and use the 12-month review to press for fixed penalties and no-layoff beyond pilot sites. Do not strike while jobs are plentiful and we have no leverage to win a bigger fight. Sign nothing further that trades away breach penalties.
3. **Savings and vote.** Saving rate steady; spend the cheque on bills and a small cushion, down 1 point only if rents stop rising. A third of effort goes to the Nov 2036 vote: back candidates who commit in writing to a full-size Fair Share cheque, Paycheck Bridge, and housing and insurance relief. Take Entrepreneurs' paid-to-train places with a first-year-pay promise and portable credentials. No boycotts.

## 4. Public statement and messages
**Statement:** We took the pay rise, but rent and insurance are now what is breaking family budgets; we want housing and insurance action, a full-size cheque, and penalties that bite.

- **To Entrepreneurs:** We will fill your trainee places if first-year pay is guaranteed and credentials are portable; we back Paycheck Bridge.
- **To Employers:** We accept the notice, floor, seats and review as a start. At the 12-month review we will ask for fixed penalties and no-layoff beyond pilot sites.

## 5. What would change my mind
A visible jump in layoffs among people we know, or rents and insurance easing on their own, would shift me to defend jobs or back off the housing fight.
