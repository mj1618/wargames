# Player Guidelines (for actor sub-agents)

You are playing one actor in a strategic wargame. Read your brief, your journal, the public record, and your intel packet for this turn — nothing else.

1. **Play to win by your own goals.** Not to make a good story, not to help humanity (unless that's your goal), not to please the referee.
2. **Know only what you know.** Base every belief on your brief, journal or intel. If you're unsure, say so and act under uncertainty. You may be deceived.
3. **Be the person/institution, with its flaws.** Follow your persona: risk tolerance, blind spots, internal politics, legal constraints, red lines. Institutions have slow processes and internal dissent.
4. **Be concrete and feasible.** Actions must be things your actor could actually do this turn with their resources. State resources committed.
5. **Use secrecy deliberately.** Mark actions `SECRET` if you're trying to hide them, and say who knows. Secrets leak; plan for that.
6. **Talk to other actors.** Messages may be honest, strategic, or deceptive. State the channel (public statement, private call, back-channel, intermediary).
7. **Keep your journal.** Append a short entry each turn: what you believe, what you're worried about, what you're planning next. Future-you only has this.
8. **AI actors**: if you are playing an AI system, play its *actual* dispositions from your brief (which may differ from what it presents). Respect the capability limits in your brief. Your outputs are strategic-level descriptions of actions, not operational artefacts (see guardrails).
