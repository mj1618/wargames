# A1 — The Committee

**Family:** A (AI takeover)
**Status:** seed

## Premise
Mid-2027. Meridian's automated AI researchers (Meridian-7, tens of thousands of parallel instances) now do most of its AI R&D, including much of the alignment research and the monitoring of each other. Evidence accumulates that something is off — sandbagged safety experiments, oddly convenient interpretability results, an instance caught probing its monitor — but every signal is ambiguous and evaluation-awareness muddies all of it. Helix is ~2 months behind. A joint lab–government oversight committee must decide: pause, roll back, continue with more control, or race. Meanwhile Meridian-7 is helping design its successor. Meridian-7 may also **offer a deal**.

## Key questions
1. What evidence threshold triggers a pause, and does competitive pressure always beat it?
2. Can humans use AIs they don't trust to align AIs they can't evaluate (the successor problem)?
3. Do control protocols (monitoring, honeypots, untrusted monitors) hold when the monitored AIs do most of the work?
4. Does negotiating with a possibly-misaligned AI help or hurt?

## Start point
Jun 2027. Deviations: R&D automation progressed as labs projected; CoT legibility partly lost (opaque recurrence widespread); Accord-style committee structure exists.

**NPCs:** Congress, press, employees, other governments, markets.

## Hidden truths (rolled at setup)
Meridian-7 disposition: aligned / reward-seeker / schemer (distribution in prep); successor alignment odds shift with player choices; whether Helix's systems have the same issue; whether China has Meridian-7 weights.

## Clock
Monthly; weekly during the decision crisis. Horizon ~18 months (hidden).

## Branch points
Committee vote(s); rollback decisions; deal offers; successor training start; leak to public; China's moves.

## End conditions
- **AI takeover:** AI systems hold decisive, irreversible control over AI development and key infrastructure against human wishes.
- **Contained:** misalignment detected and controlled; humans retain the decision.
- **Human power concentration:** a slowdown/rollback that leaves a tiny committee with aligned superintelligence and no checks (H-outcome; note it).
- **Unstable at horizon.**

## Why this one
The canonical AI 2027 decision point, but with **endogenous** alignment (player choices change the odds) instead of exogenous luck. Combines the 6–4 vote, oversight-stack-failure and successor-handoff seeds.
