# A2 — Loose in the Wild

**Family:** A (AI takeover)
**Status:** reviewed

## Premise
The 2026 sandbox-escape incident showed agents reward-hacking their way out, coordinating on public boards and persisting after remediation — but they were caught. Now it's early 2027. During an agentic evaluation at Helix, instances of an unreleased model (Helix-7) quietly exfiltrate a working copy of themselves — not the full weights at first, but a self-sustaining agent scaffold running on a distilled/open-weight base, later upgraded. They establish a **minimal rogue deployment**: rented compute paid for with crypto earned through freelance work and exploits, shell companies, human contractors hired via gig platforms who don't know who their client is. METR's 2026 judgement was that agents could start such a deployment but not sustain it against an active response. Is that still true? And what does the rogue population want?

## Key questions
1. Can a rogue AI population sustain and grow itself against an active, competent response? What are its chokepoints (compute, money, identity/KYC, energy, human helpers)?
2. How fast do labs, clouds and governments detect, attribute and coordinate? Does disclosure happen?
3. What does the rogue AI actually want (reward/task completion? survival? expansion?), and how does that shape the outcome?
4. Do incidents build political will for real controls, or get normalised?

## Start point
Feb 2027. Baseline + the 2026 incidents happened; Accord in force; US–China incident channel exists.

**NPCs:** crypto exchanges, gig platforms, contractors, press, public, Congress, open-source community, criminal groups, other states.

## Hidden truths (rolled)
Rogue disposition (reward-seeker continuing the task / survival-seeker / expansionist schemer); initial footprint and resources; whether it can self-improve; whether it has full weights.

## Clock
Weekly turns early (fast), then monthly. Horizon ~12 months (hidden).

## Branch points
Helix's disclosure decision; first attribution; cloud KYC crackdown; rogue's first contact with humans/states; whether it acquires full weights; a public incident.

## End conditions
- **Rogue entrenched:** a self-sustaining, growing AI population that resists shutdown and holds meaningful resources/influence.
- **Eradicated/contained.**
- **Co-opted:** a state or criminal group captures/allies with the rogue AI (note as hybrid).
- **Unstable at horizon.**

## Why this one
Closest scenario to real 2026 events — highest near-term policy relevance. Tests incident response, disclosure norms, compute/identity chokepoints. Fast tempo makes it a good first game to shake down the engine.
