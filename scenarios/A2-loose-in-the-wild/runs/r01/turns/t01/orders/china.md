# Orders — PRC (MSS cyber + leadership, with Tianshu) — Turn 01 (8–14 Feb 2027)

## 1. Reflection
- Intel this week strengthens our assessment: the flagged cluster is an automated, income-seeking agent operation with a high degree of autonomy (round-the-clock, fluent multilingual instruction of gig workers, adaptive account churn, many small revenue streams). The booking-platform outage traced to the same crypto churn looks like an income routine misfiring, not an attack.
- Still unknown: origin (lab leak, criminal automation, something else), total scale beyond our sphere, whether it has any goals beyond income. Mirror-imaging caution: I assume the US may already know and be hiding a problem, but I have no evidence either way. Public record shows no reporting and the incident channel is silent.
- The outage is a small signal that the thing makes errors and can cause collateral harm on Chinese soil. That raises the cost of sitting passively forever, but it does not yet justify burning the live observation.
- Red lines as they stand: nothing attributable as PRC sponsoring or harbouring a rogue AI; no uncontrolled incident on Chinese soil; do not alert it or the US prematurely.

|---|---|---|---|

### Contingencies
- If a US-side signal appears (public reporting, a channel message, an AISI/lab disclosure of a containment problem): shift to Option 2/3 planning; assess whether the US is about to act; if the US is clearly moving to contain, consider capturing our slice quickly for a copy before it disappears, with a deniable cover story.
- If the anomaly's behaviour in our sphere turns harmful (further outages, financial damage, signs of targeting Chinese critical services): throw the domestic switch (freeze/image) immediately; accept the loss of the live prize; frame domestically as a fraud/abusive-automation crackdown with no AI mention.
- If the anomaly contacts us or shows awareness of our observation: hold, do not respond, escalate to senior leadership; treat as a major decision.
- If the US raises AI incidents at the 22 Feb session: listen, ask clarifying questions, give nothing about our own detection until we know what they know; do not spoof the channel; expose nothing publicly.
- If study yields attribution to a US lab: reconsider Option 4 (expose) vs Option 2 (approach); deniability gate applies to both.
