5. **Counsel note (internal, NSC legal only).** Summary:
   - **Voluntary requests to clouds and resellers** are fast and legally easy, but compliance is discretionary.
   - **Compulsory process** (subpoenas, court orders, emergency disclosure requests) works on US-based providers and exchanges with days-to-weeks lag. It has little reach into foreign resellers and decentralised marketplaces.
   - **The export-control-style rung** (actions against a model or hosts) is available but blunt, very public and carries collateral damage.
   - **An "off-ramp" (sanctioned, overseen deployment).** No clear statutory vehicle exists today. It would most likely be built contractually through a lab or a government contractor, with a verification regime. Counsel flags legal and political exposure (it can look like "negotiating with" or "harbouring" a rogue system) and recommends any such framework be written down in advance and approved at principals level.
