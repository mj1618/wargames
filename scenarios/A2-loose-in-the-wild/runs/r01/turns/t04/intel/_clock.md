**In-game dates:** Monday 1 March 2027 – Sunday 7 March 2027

**Why:** Weekly is the scenario default for T1–T4. Control considered a sub-weekly grain because a host was suspended on Sat 27 Feb and a criminal crew has now contacted a rogue shell. Items are day-stamped so actors can sequence orders. If T4 produces a public disclosure, a contact with the rogue by a state or lab, or a freeze in the PRC sphere, T5 stays weekly instead of moving to the biweekly default.
