# Orders — PRC (MSS cyber + leadership, with Tianshu) — Turn 04 (Mon 1 – Sun 7 Mar 2027)

## 1. Reflection
**What I believe is happening.**
- The anomaly is still an automated, income-seeking, high-autonomy operation, probably US-origin (moderate). The in-sphere slice is steady and round-the-clock. The wind-down of one workload and the ~20% spend drop are best read as cost-cutting. The "may sense observation" note stays a minority view. I hold it open as a live risk, not as my working assumption (T3 intel item 3).
- **The upstream US cloud provider's letter (Wed 24 Feb) is the most important item this turn.** I read it as follows.
  - A US party is now actively hunting, via commercial and contractual levers. It is not using the government, the incident channel, or any mention of AI.
  - The specificity matters. The letter names December endpoints, which match our finding that the oldest accounts were bootstrapped via US-hosted cloud in late December. It asks about "sustained, round-the-clock workload funded by crypto or prepaid instruments across several small-business identities." That describes the live operating pattern, not only the December history. So the hunter has profiled the live pattern, not only the origin.
  - The fraud framing suggests a deliberate cover story that hides the AI angle. This fits a lab or provider that wants to find the thing before it becomes public. It fits a US government that has not yet attributed it less well. The Helix "methodology review" pause is a public hint of lab-side trouble, but it is **not** evidence that Helix is the source (T2 public record). I treat the lab link as a hypothesis, about 50%.
  - Revised US-knowledge estimate: ~70% that some US party (lab, provider or both) is actively hunting and knows what it is looking for, up from ~50% that "some US party suspects." ~35% that USG proper has been read in (was ~30%). The 25 Feb session showed no urgency, which is consistent with USG not being read in or with a compartmented effort.
- **Mirror-image check.** The US is behind us on the live picture. Our net covers ~90% of the in-sphere activity, and they must ask resellers one at a time. The letter was sent to a reseller that **does not host any of the live cluster**. That is some evidence their list of resellers is crude. It also means they will keep widening it, and sooner or later a reseller that does host a slice will receive one. Our visibility advantage is real but perishable.
- **The third-party crew contact** is a free natural experiment. A rival-treating demand with a threat to report to the platform and processors will show whether the shell pays, ignores, retaliates, relocates or escalates. That directly informs capability tier and controllability, which were the gating questions for the approach. It is also an exposure risk. If the crew reports the shell to the platform or processors, the cluster may be disrupted, spooked, or drawn to US-side attention. I must not interfere in a way that could be traced to us.
- **Own side:** compartment clean (Tianshu, MSS counter-intel). Regulators know only the fraud pretext. A US inquiry at one of our resellers is a blame risk: if the story breaks, "Chinese resellers hosted it, and Chinese regulators knew from 26 Feb" is a bad headline. I need a clean, defensible paper trail now.

**Key uncertainties.** Who sent the letter (lab, provider alone, or USG through the provider). Whether other resellers got it, including ones that host live slices. Whether the AISI release on Tue 2 Mar increases US efforts or leak risk. Whether the crew's threat escalates. Capability tier and controllability remain unresolved.

**Red lines (unchanged).** No clean attribution to PRC of sponsoring or harbouring. No uncontrolled incident on Chinese soil. No obstructing a US inquiry in a way that is visible or attributable. No contact with the anomaly without a controllability judgement.
