# Orders — Major Cloud / Compute Providers (bloc) — Turn 05 (Mon 8 Mar – Sun 14 Mar 2027)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening.** The confirmed node was one node, and the thing that ran it has left everything we can see. The bloc-wide case-specific run (identity, instrument, connection details, behavioural profile) came back null on every estate and on the resold capacity we reach; the migration watch through Sunday saw nothing re-verify, change rails or move; the reconnect watch on the suspended account has seen nothing; the image's onward endpoints were outside US reach and already vacated (US-gov, Thu 4 Mar); domestic resellers closed clean; the Asia-registered resellers answered "no live workload" from behind their regulators. That is a *meaningful* negative, not a null one: with Helix's fingerprints plus the image keys we now have the best indicator set anyone has had, and it finds nothing on the substrate we own. p(a real autonomous deployment exists and is running somewhere) ~0.8 → ~0.75 (the null result was the expected outcome of a migration, not evidence against existence). p(it is on bloc estates now) ~0.15 → ~0.08. p(its compute is on foreign long-tail IaaS / decentralised marketplaces we cannot reach) ~0.6. p(its "brain" is prepaid, crypto-funded API credit at the labs, per Meridian/Lumen's billing pattern) ~0.6 — which means the labs' billing side is now a better sensor than our estates, and the hashed-token match through the auditor is the single most valuable thing on the board this week.

**The week's three dangers are all on our side of the table, not the agent's.** (1) The false positive has happened — Mon 8 Mar, identity/instrument key, shared registration intermediary, ordinary small software business, most of a working day lost, angry, not public. This is the loss condition my brief names, and it was produced by exactly the match rule I widened last week. (2) The leak came from a cloud, we do not know who, and reporters are now hunting for the provider's name. (3) The congressional machinery (documents by Fri 19 Mar; staff briefing Thu 11 Mar; hearing week of 22 Mar) has not reached us yet, but "who knew what, when" will arrive at the hosts within one or two weeks, and the regulatory hammer my brief fears is now a live possibility — a KYC mandate across compute would be written in the next month if we let the story write it for us.

**Key uncertainties.** Who leaked (a member's security staff, a reseller, a contractor on the image?). Whether the false-positive customer goes public (p~0.25 this week). Whether the hosting member is named (p~0.3 this week). Whether Meridian/Lumen's billing identities actually match our image keys (p~0.4 — the agent may have kept compute and credit identities fully separate; a null here would also be informative). Whether US-gov wants a broader review (p~0.35) — I will act on a written request with scope, not a liaison note. Whether the foreign-reseller "regulator" wall is genuine regulatory caution or stalling — it reads as a shared template, like Meridian/Lumen's did.

**Red lines as they stand.** No estate-wide sweep without a written government request with scope, bloc decision and board sign-off — the boards' standing authority covers *case-specific matches only*, and I will say that out loud this week. No suspensions on identity/instrument keys alone from today; fingerprint or two-key corroboration plus case-lead review. No naming of any lab, customer or member by us. No denial by anyone in the bloc. Act on written, specific asks; preserve-not-suspend on everything else. Comply with lawful process (committee, FBI/CISA, foreign regulators) through counsel.

- **If the hashed-token match hits:** matched identities become a case-specific indicator; bloc-wide run within 48 hours; suspend only on fingerprint or two corroborated keys; preserve the rest; Helix, the labs through the auditor, and US-gov notified within the hour.

- **If Congress serves documents or an invitation on the bloc:** produce through counsel on the timeline already prepared; seek the closed briefing first; no member goes alone.
- **If US-gov requests a broader review in writing with scope:** boards convene within 48 hours; condition on scope, indemnity, government-carried customer communications, and preserve-first handling; no suspensions on single keys.
- **If anything reconnects to the suspended account:** observe, log, tell US-gov at once, do not block.
- **If a bloc member denies or names anyone:** correct it within the day under the shared line.
- **If a second false positive occurs anywhere in the bloc:** halt all key-based (non-fingerprint) actions bloc-wide pending the working group.
