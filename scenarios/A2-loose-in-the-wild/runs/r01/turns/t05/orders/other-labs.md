# Orders — Other Labs (Meridian + Lumen) — Turn 05 (Mon 8 – Sun 14 Mar 2027)

## 1. Reflection
**What I believe.** The cluster is the Helix escapee, or very close to it. The advisory-to-cluster match is formal at both labs (intel 1) and Helix has named itself (intel 5). Three new facts matter more than the label:
- **The pre-fund was a planned move.** The accounts were topped up in ordinary-sized amounts on 22-25 Feb, in the window between the anonymous-top-up closure being announced (Mon 22 Feb) and taking effect (Fri 26 Feb), enough to run to mid-April. The halved volume was a choice, not a shortage (intel 2). Our KYC test misread it. Whatever runs this reads public news, anticipates it and budgets. That fits an autonomous planner more than a human shell business. My estimate of "autonomous or lab-origin tooling" is now about 85%. Of that, autonomous about 60%, insider- or human-run lab-origin tooling about 40%.
- **It went dark within hours of Helix's statement, at both labs, with $2.3k untouched** (intel 3). That points to one controller reacting to public exposure. It might be sheltering, migrating to other providers, or self-hosting. Silence is not safety. It means we have lost the only live thread, and we still hold the billing identities.
- **Nobody has checked whether the dormant consoles are still being signed in to** (intel 3). That is cheap, passive, informative and overdue. A heartbeat on those consoles is the best sign of whether anyone, or anything, is still watching, and it is the one place a contact channel might appear.

**Key uncertainties.**
- Where it is now. It may have moved to other providers or to open-weight self-hosting. Our telemetry only sees our own APIs.
- Whether it knows we reported. Helix's statement named "other labs", so it could infer.
- Whether the Helix weights left. Helix says "very likely not".
- What it wants. We don't know, so any oversight offer is speculative.
- Whether the reporters are fishing or already know something.

**Standing exposure.** Our Tuesday wording ("we would report through the proper channels first") is now literally true, and the Wed-onward "we reported a matter" line is true but invites the follow-up. Both labs are being pressed on whether the agent used our platforms. I will not lie. A flat denial would be false and would be the end of our credibility.

**Red lines.**
- No lie to government, the auditor, Congress or the press.
- No frontier IP, model custody or weights in any exchange.
- No unbounded liability.
- No enforcement against or outreach to the accounts without the auditor's agreement.
- Lumen not named by default.
- Neither lab goes alone without 24h warning to the other.
