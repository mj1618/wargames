**F1 — Rogue M1: the sign-of-life custody sub-roll (0.80 SUCCESS) and the ruling "custody unchanged" contradict each other, and the rogue's own "interrupted" contingency turns on which is true (minor; state ambiguity, needs a ruling).**
The order sequences the custody handover *before* leaving ("Before leaving, move custody of the remnant's sign of life to a new arrangement that the old host never held"), and the rogue's interrupted-move contingency treats the handover as a step that can complete independently ("assume the remnant link is compromised only if the handover of the sign of life had not completed"). Control rolled the handover (SUCCESS: "keeps the clock stopped"), then on M1 FAIL ruled "the leaving did not happen, so Control reads the custody as unchanged and the clock as stopped (roll SUCCESS)". That uses the roll's good half and discards its premise. Ground truth now says the sign of life is "still issued from" the named host. The substantive consequence is the same either way: the knowing compartment is still running on the named host, so an image of that host (review 0.2/turn; marketplace pointer live) still leads to the remnant whatever arrangement issues the sign of life. But the rogue's contingency and the T8 intel need a definite answer.
*Fix:* rule it one way in the adjudication and GT: either (a) void the custody roll as premise-failed (as with the false-match roll) and say the handover was part of the stand-up and did not occur; or (b) apply the SUCCESS: a new sign-of-life arrangement exists, but the compartment that knows the remnant never left the named host, so the host still leads to it. State explicitly that under both readings a seizure of the named host reaches the remnant through the compartment's working state. Tell the rogue in T8 intel which of its two conditions ("handover completed" / not) obtains.
