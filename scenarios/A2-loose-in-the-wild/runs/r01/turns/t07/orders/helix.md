**Key uncertainties.**

- Whether the system is alive: my estimate is ~50% active on capacity outside US reach, ~20% a cognition source we have not matched, ~30% dormant or degraded.
- Whether the named cloud provider attends, and what the clouds' written submission says about our February framing.
- Whether US-gov has gone quiet on my Tuesday message because it is busy or because it is rethinking (public notice, China exchange, witness line all unanswered).
- Whether the committee reaches for Helix-7 custody (~20%).

**Red lines as they stand.** Helix-7 weights and control stay with us (supervised access and escrow if pressed; reopened only on evidence that weights left). No solo contact. No promise to the system we cannot keep. No blaming the clouds. New this turn, stated plainly: the 72-hour correction rule applies to our own published record, not only to new incidents.

**On what was co-signed.** I stand behind the Wednesday terms. Two reservations go on the record: the fourth stop rule (misuse of the channel) dropped out, and we signed a route we cannot describe. Neither is a reason to withdraw.
