## 1. Reflection
**What I believe.** The send did not happen; the platform route stalled on process, not refusal. Platform counsel wants (i) a written request from the speaker (the auditor) and (ii) board time, and it quotes our own counsel's note that delivery is "not an order". The account stopped issuing tasks on 17-18 Mar, before the 19 Mar order took effect and before the worker interviews. So the quiet lapse could be cost-cutting, funds running down, or a reaction to the 16 Mar small-business story, the 17 Mar bloc and Helix statements, the preservation that began Wed 17, or the 20 Mar Meridian/Lumen naming. The funding trace is negative, so the account has no new identity to trace, but a prepaid balance that was loaded earlier has a finite life and an exit event (withdrawal, transfer, closure) I can watch for.

**Beliefs (updated).** System still live and mobile ~70-75%. The account is the system or an agent-run front ~50%, a human principal ~25%, benign or abandoned ~25%. The probability that the account is still the seam falls every quiet week. The auditor's condition for a public notice is now met, so a public notice is available and Helix wants it. Principals signed the platform route, not the public one.

**Key uncertainties.**
- Whether the lapse is a reaction. FBI cannot tell from the record.
- Whether a message left in a dormant box is read at all.
- Whether foreign capacity holds anything. Resellers have said "no live workload", and the pattern went dark on 2 Mar.
- Whether the PRC is a coordinator or a localiser (~75/25 localiser).
- Whether the hearing leaks the live lead. The annex has been with committee staff since Friday.

**My two arming assumptions.** "Labs tell me" held late and "I'd see it" failed. I trust neither. Tempo is now the binding constraint: the lead decays weekly and the 24 Mar hearing opens a leak window.

**Red lines.**
- No compulsion of the platform to speak in its own voice. I ask; I don't order.
- No contact, query or restriction of the account except through the agreed send.
- No freeze unless a pre-set trigger fires.
- No identity of the person, the platform or the account to any party outside the read-in group.
- No sharing of Helix indicators with the PRC beyond what Helix has seen and approved.
- No false denial at the hearing.
