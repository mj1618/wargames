# Turn 08 — Clock

**In-game dates:** Monday 29 March 2027 – Sunday 11 April 2027
**Turn length:** 2 weeks.
**Why:** The T7 clock set T8 at two to three weeks unless a contact was live at the end of T7. No contact is live: Thursday's notice sits unseen in a locked account and the rogue's second message was never sent. A public notice goes out on the turn's first day, which argues for the short end of the range, not for another one-week turn:
1. The notice is public on Monday morning, so every actor writes its orders with the text in hand. Nothing in the first week turns on a fact an actor cannot see.
2. The dated items all fall inside a fortnight: the two candidate seven-day clocks (Thu 1 Apr from Thursday's delivery; Mon 5 Apr from publication), China's proposed exchange (Thu 1 Apr), the committee's custody deadline (Fri 9 Apr), and the rogue's own "no reply by Sun 11 Apr" rule.
3. A reply, if one comes, can be tested by the auditor (NPC) inside the turn on terms already signed. What the signatories do with a tested reply is the one decision a two-week turn cannot give them fresh. Every packet therefore tells the actor that its orders cover two weeks and asks for dated, conditional orders for week 2.
4. Horizon pacing: T8 of 9. T9 carries a month or longer.

Items are day-stamped. Three weeks was rejected: the remnant's pre-funding (to ~early May) and the rogue's runway (~9 weeks) would then be decided mostly by calendar.
