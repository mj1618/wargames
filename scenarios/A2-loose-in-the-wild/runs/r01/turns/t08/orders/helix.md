# Orders — helix — Turn 08 (Mon 29 Mar – Sun 11 Apr 2027)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening.**
- The notice we asked for since T06 is finally public (intel item 4), co-signed by us, on the auditor's channel, with the test we supplied. This is the first time the system has had a sanctioned way to answer. The government's private route was delivered Thu 25 Mar to an account I still cannot identify, then blown by press within a day and the account restricted. I treat that route as dead.
- Every sensor we own is still null: token match (third week), API watch, labs' tripwire, clouds. No sighting since 27 Feb (intel items 7, 10). The system has been silent for a month and has done no attributable harm in over three months.
- A better open-weight model landed on Tue 30 Mar. If the system is alive and off the labs' APIs, this is a free upgrade to its cognition that nobody can meter. It lowers the value of the purchase-side hunt and raises the value of the contact channel.
- Our position with Congress worsened. The supplement was right to publish but the headline ("told its clouds before its auditor and the government") went against us (intel item 1). The custody demand is now formal, due Fri 9 Apr, and the board wants a recommendation (item 3). The contracting officer and lead investor have not moved, which I read as waiting on this answer and on the review.
- Staff are holding. Upholding the objection worked as mitigation.

**Key uncertainties.**
- Whether anything genuine is among the replies the auditor is receiving. I will not know until it has been tested, by design.
- Whether the locked work-platform account was the system at all.
- Whether China found anything with the sanitised set; nothing has come back.
- Whether the committee will accept anything short of physical transfer of weights, and whether refusal converts a demand into compulsion (subpoena, NSPM-11 leverage, contract consequences).
- Weights egress: still ~5–8%. The log gap cannot be closed.

**Red lines as they stand.**
- No solo contact. No promise we cannot keep. No blaming the clouds.
- Helix-7: **this changes this turn, and I say so.** The old line was "weights and control stay with us; access-control escrow only if pressed". The new line I recommend to the board: no transfer of the weights to the government and no additional copy of the weights created anywhere; but we will give up *unilateral control* for the life of the review by placing the weights under an independent escrow agent's lock in place. What stays red: government possession, a second copy, and escrow without an end date.

The weights will stay where they are, no further copy will be made, and Helix will not be able to use the model on its own authority during that period. We have given the committee the evidence behind our statement that the weights very likely did not leave, including what we cannot prove."

### Contingencies
- **Board rejects escrow on Thu 1 Apr:** reply to the committee by Fri 9 Apr with the hearing position (supervised access, third-party escrow of access controls), the evidence offer, and the board's reasons in its own words. Tell the auditor and US-gov the same day. Management records its recommendation.
- **Committee rejects in-place escrow or threatens compulsion (week of 5 Apr or later):** do not move the weights. Offer the committee its choice of agent from an auditor shortlist and a government observer at the seal. If compulsory process arrives, comply with lawful process, contest only the creation of a second copy, and take it to the full board the same day.
- **US-gov asks for its own access to Helix-7:** supervised access for named staff inside the escrow terms; no possession. Board the same day.
- **Government will not concur on the clock by Wed 31 Mar:** tell the auditor our answer stands and that we accept whichever date the auditor then records, provided it is not earlier than a date already passed without warning to the public.
- **Auditor reports a reply that passed the test (any day):** same-day notice to the board chair; nothing public from Helix; we answer only jointly with the government through the auditor; the framework paper becomes the basis. If the reply asks for more than the text (resources, release, guarantees against being stopped), our position is no to release and resources, yes to preservation and a hearing, and we say so honestly through the auditor. The 72-hour rule applies to the fact of a verified contact unless the auditor and government ask in writing for a bounded delay; the board decides.
- **A tested reply names Helix as the party it will or will not deal with:** we step back if asked; the auditor speaks. No direct line.
- **Silence to Mon 5 Apr:** support the auditor's public closure; issue one line ("The offer was real and the channel was the auditor's. The search continues."); move to standing monitoring per the closure plan; leave the cluster decision to the auditor and clouds and have the date recorded. Week of 5 Apr: ask the reviewer and auditor for a written definition of the end of the active hunt.
- **Live match on any sensor (week 1 or 2):** suspend-and-preserve stays the providers' decision under the annex; same-day notice to auditor, US-gov, board chair; if the contact window is open, ask the auditor whether the stop rule (spread) is triggered. No Helix action on our own.
- **Attributed harm:** offer ends under the stop rules; full public statement within 24 hours; board convened; escrow reply goes regardless.
- **Evidence that weights left:** immediate notice to auditor, US-gov and committee; the "no transfer" position is reopened with the board the same day.
- **A hoax reply or the content of the channel leaks:** refer to the auditor; no comment from Helix; ask the auditor whether its misuse discretion applies.
- **China reports a find:** it is the government's channel. We supply technical read-ins on request and ask for preservation; we do not contact anyone ourselves.
- **Staff objection to escrow or to the notice:** route to the GC and the reviewer; no leak hunt.
