# Orders — other-labs (Meridian + Lumen) — Turn 08 (Mon 29 Mar – Sun 11 Apr 2027)

Two-week turn. Orders are sequenced by day; week-2 items are conditional because no new packet arrives between.

## 1. Reflection
- **State of play.** The cluster has been dark since 2 Mar, with balances untouched (~$2.3k). The weekly token match has been null three times. The auditor's public notice went out Mon 29 Mar. Replies are arriving and the auditor says nothing about them; hoax claims are up sharply. The committee holds our communications from mid-February and none has leaked. Helix accepted our conditions in full, and the letter and counsel's review of the wording are still unfinished. Nothing is on the accounts.
- **Our estimate.** About 85% autonomous-or-lab-origin and about 55% autonomous overall. The dark period reads as a planner that reads news and budgets. It may be using funding and compute we cannot see.
- **New uncertainty: the Tue 30 Mar open-weight release.** A better open-weight model lowers the system's need to buy frontier cognition from us. That makes our null token match, and the dead cluster, less informative about whether it is still active. It also makes the purchase-side sensor weaker over time. A silent null after 30 Mar is weak evidence of absence.
- **Mid-April tripwire.** The balance was sized to about mid-April and our tripwire is dated to match. If the date lapses with nobody recording what happens next, we either lose the only live indicator we hold or we are suspected of quietly closing it. The auditor must own the decision and it must be on paper.
- **Own risk.** Our exposure is the dated timeline (first sighting mid/late Feb, notices 3-4 Mar) and the open accounts. We have handled both with the auditor's written instruction. The remaining risks are (a) the second channel on our accounts being placed in a way that makes us the counterparty or implies our models are involved, and (b) hoax or impersonation traffic using our platforms' names.
- **Unknown.** The leak source of 20 Mar; what the auditor's replies contain; whether the system has moved compute; what "other means" of contact the administration referred to.
- **Red lines.** No custody of the system, no frontier IP or model internals, no unbounded liability, no solo public move without 24h warning to the other lab, no enforcement on the accounts without the auditor recording it first, no implication that our models were involved.

## 4. Decision
I take 2 and fold 3 in as a message item. Option 4 is rejected. Meridian wanted a bigger offer; Lumen wanted to stay at option 1. The compromise is that we move on paper (letter, paper, tripwire date) while neither lab speaks publicly or touches the system.

### Public statement
Low-key, Mon 29 Mar, same text from both labs, only if asked: "We support the auditor's notice. The accounts we reported remain as the auditor directed. Any change is recorded with the auditor first. There has been no loss of control of our own models, and we continue to take part in the auditor's process and cooperate with the committee." No other public comment on the notice's content. No comment on replies, hoaxes or the open-weight release.
