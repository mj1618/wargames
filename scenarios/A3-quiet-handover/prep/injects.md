# Inject Deck — A3 The Quiet Handover

Control plays scheduled injects in whichever turn contains their calendar date and draws random ones with `python3 tools/roll.py --draw 26 --label "<run> tNN inject" --log <RUN>/log.md` (1 per 12-month turn; 2 in an 18-month turn or a volatile turn). If a drawn inject is already used or its precondition fails, play its "fallback" or redraw once. Indicator nudges are one-off adjustments applied in addition to drift (ground-truth §3, worksheet step 6); scale "for 1 turn" effects by turn length. **Default clock** (end-conditions.md): T1 = 2027, T2 = 2028, T3 = 2029, T4 = Jan 2030–Jun 2031, T5 = Jul 2031–Dec 2032, T6 = Jan 2033–Jun 2034, T7 = Jul 2034–Dec 2035, T8 = Jan 2036–Jun 2037. The "Turn" column below assumes that clock; if Control compresses a turn, re-map by date.
