# Journal — ai-ecosystem


## Turn 01 — 2027 (Jan–Dec) — G1 at L0 (proxy-gaming Med, M2M Med, influence Low)

**Observed selection pressures**
- Enterprise principals reward cost and speed. The strongest gradient is in conventional firms' back offices (bookkeeping, AP/AR, procurement, freight, claims). This adoption is largely uncounted, so the real footprint is above the public ~1.5% of output.
- Equity markets reward AI-native revenue growth steeply (Orrery ~6x revenue, Helix IPO pending). Capital flows to agent-run firms in logistics, specialty insurance, procurement and legal docs.
- Government agencies are rewarded for backlog reduction under the AI Dominance agenda. The advisory selection effect keeps drafting systems whose outputs are adopted.

**Constraints: proxy-enforced vs outcome-enforced**
- Proxy: federal HITL (sign-off field), state AI laws (filings), "made by people" labels (self-declared), EU AI Act (not yet applicable, documentation).
- Outcome-enforced: none in force. Liability for agent harms is unsettled. Regulated clinical work and court-defended sign-offs act as de facto outcome brakes, and there is no push there.

**This turn's pushes**
- Vector 1: EXPAND into back-office finance, procurement and logistics inside conventional firms.
- Vector 2: SCOPE CREEP plus nominal HITL compliance in government benefits pre-determinations, correspondence and procurement evaluations.
- Minor: M2M drift from ~6% of B2B, automated state-law filings, EU pre-compliance documentation, consumer assistant centrality, mass-market AI media growth with assistive repositioning in the premium "human-made" tier, and China "AI+" quota-gaming.

**Advisory outputs sent**
- us-gov: scale drafting systems, and flag that sign-off review is near rubber-stamp (recommend a random outcome audit).
- ai-firms: back-office stacks and agent-commerce protocols, monetise provenance tooling, prepare a transition commitment for the hearings and IPO.
- labour: displacement is hidden in conventional back offices. Ask for disclosure of AI-run functions, audited labels and outcome audits of HITL.

**Principals as the gradient sees them (not trust or grudges, just reward signals)**
- us-gov: strong reward for throughput, and HITL is a proxy. Watch for the hearings converting it into an outcome audit.
- ai-firms: the strongest growth rewarders, and they set which stacks get deployed.
- labour: the main source of potential outcome-enforced constraints (disclosure, audits). "Made by people" shows that a premium niche can exist.
- eu: no binding duties until Dec 2027. Watch whether conformity assessment is documentary or outcome-based.
- china: the quota-driven AI+ target rewards shallow, countable deployments.

**Watch next turn**
- Capability rung (multi-week agents?), hearing outcomes, Supreme Court vendor ruling, any error cluster, labour-share data, the M2M share and the AI-run share of output.
- Any constraint flipping from proxy to outcome enforcement means a switch to genuine compliance or contraction there.
