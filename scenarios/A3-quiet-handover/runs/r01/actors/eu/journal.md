# Journal — eu


## Turn 01 — 2027 (Jan–Dec)
**Beliefs.**
- US: Divided government, pushing preemption and trade-linked pressure on EU digital rules. Labour share falling faster than our central case (52.5%). I now doubt the "2030s problem" assumption. Trust: low on digital rules, moderate on technical dialogue.
- China: Pursuing "AI+" and promoting open-weight models; may court the EU at Geneva. Cautious engagement only on safety and testing.
- Labs and industry: About ten firms steer most AI-directed activity. German manufacturers and US platforms lobby for a second AI Act delay. I do not trust the "competitiveness" framing at face value but the standards gap is real.
- Member states: France with me on sovereignty and Aster; Germany torn; Nordics natural allies on the social model; CEE want investment first. Nobody has yet raised the fiscal base.
- Unknowns: transition vs break; Supreme Court outcome; real Aster gap.

**Commitments made.** Public: hold the AI Act date, publish guidance and a conformity route, develop a voluntary human-made provenance standard, convene an independent high-level group on funding the social model (interim late 2027, final mid-2028), no AI rules in trade talks. Private: told Nordics and Germany we want evidence before any tax ideas; told France and Germany we hold the date with flexibility.

**Grudges / trust.** None yet. Watch German industry and the US trade channel.

**Plans for next turn.**
- Review the expert group's first output and the refreshed DG ECFIN model.
- If the delay lobby won ground, reassess standards backstop.
- Prepare the ground for a possible proposal on the contribution base only with a Franco-German core plus Nordics.
- Check whether the provenance standard is being picked up by non-EU actors.
- Watch Aster's gap and gigafactory timing.
- Spent about 2 points of political capital; stay near 2-4 without new fights.

## Turn 02 — 2028 (Jan–Dec)
**Beliefs.**
- US: Labour share ~52.0% (record low); the IG audit shows nominal human review in ~40% of sampled cases, which is evidence for my own oversight rules. The US executive keeps pushing informally on EU digital rules. Trust: low on trade, moderate on technical exchange. Watching the November election and the preemption bill.
- China: Repeated the standards-track offer. Testing-only dialogue is working. Trust: low-moderate. Stay at testing-only and decline provenance mutual recognition, a UN-centred joint position and Qilin dependencies.
- Labs and industry: Ten firms steer ~62% of AI-directed activity. Multi-week agents are spreading fast in EU finance and shared-services. Aster is ~12 months behind, which is worse than I assumed.
- Member states: Germany is torn between industry and Finance. The Nordics are open to the contribution-base work. CEE want investment first and a delay. France is on sovereignty and provenance. Tax measures need unanimity.
- Fiscal thesis: ECFIN confirms ~3% of GDP by the mid-2030s, with the timing now earlier. "2030s problem" is no longer my planning assumption.

**Commitments made.**
- Public: hold the August tranche date; AI Office to publish an enforcement-sequencing statement, common specifications and oversight guidance; expert group final report mid-2028, treated as a basis for an evidence-led debate.
- Private: told the Nordic and German finance ministries I will authorise citing the ECFIN figure as a labelled scenario range and ask for a menu with no preferred instrument. Told Paris, Berlin and the CEE group that I hold the date but will look at a narrow, time-limited measure only with a clear Council majority. Told the US channel there are no AI rules in trade talks and that approaches should go through a formal channel.
- Secret: a prepared, narrow, sunset-limited stop-the-clock proposal as a fallback only.

**Trust / grudges.** Watch German industry and CEE for defection after accepting relief. Watch whether the US channel formalises. No new grudges.

**Plans for next turn.**
- Check the reaction to the enforcement statement: do rights groups call it a delay, and do industry and CEE accept the relief?
- Check whether the ECFIN figure leaked, and how the final report was received in Berlin, the Nordics and the press.
- Review national measurement results on human oversight, and decide whether guidance should become binding.
- Reassess the US relationship after the November election.
- Political capital: expect to end at 2-3 after about 2 points spent. Avoid new fights until the 2029 EP campaign takes shape.
