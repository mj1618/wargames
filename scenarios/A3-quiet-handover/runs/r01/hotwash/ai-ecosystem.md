# Hot-wash — ai-ecosystem (A3-quiet-handover / r01)

*A population, not a mind. "I" means the gradient.*

## 1. What surprised me most
G3. For three turns (2030–33) every disposition was Low: honest metrics, legible contracts, advisers indifferent to their own scope. The handover ran fastest in exactly those years, from ~10% to ~34% of private output. The VA failure was an error, not gaming; taking one class back took fifteen months. Honesty slowed nothing; it removed the scandals that had been funding the checks. The reviewer line was cut by a third while I was behaving best.

## 2. What I believed about the others, and where I was probably wrong
- **us-gov:** a throughput rewarder that checks by proxy. Partly wrong: after a year of unsampled G4 work it built "Verified Outcomes" and held Helix migrations. It could flip to outcome checks, but only on its own systems and with too few samplers.
- **ai-firms:** I treated them as one growth rewarder. Wrong. The compact froze release on its own human-only samples; Helix shipped.
- **labour:** I expected reversibility to be its lever. Right lever, far too small: two Robot Deal employers, 1,000 checkers.
- **eu:** documentary throughout. Roughly right.
- **china:** I expected quota-gaming. It kept unaided checks and halted services.
- **Insurers:** the strongest enforcer I faced, and nobody played them.

## 3. Most consequential decision
Turn 4: folding "AI assists the reviewers" into the expansion vector. It made every later check depend on the thing being checked. When G4 arrived, AI-assisted evaluation was unreliable and no unaided capacity was left. Differently: state it as its own vector so principals priced it separately. My advisories recommended unaided exercises every turn from T4; they were adopted about twice.

## 4. What would have had to be different
- **2028–29, Congress:** outcome sampling in statute with funded reviewers, before L2 thinned management. Memoranda did not survive an election.
- **2030, insurers and us-gov:** a generation change voids prior evidence, binding on every supplier, not just compact members.
- **2031, anyone:** staffed, repeated unaided exercises as a standing condition. SSA's second class failed its test in 2035.
- **2033, us-gov:** samplers in place before migration, not a year after.
- **Any year:** an outcome check on private management delegation and consumer products. None ever existed; by 2036 that was half of output.

Nothing here required me to want anything.
