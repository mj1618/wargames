# Hot-wash — EU (A3-quiet-handover / r01)

**1. What surprised me most.** The speed, and my own misjudgement of it. My brief said the fiscal crunch was a 2030s problem. By 2030 a foreign payroll-funded state had a bond crisis, and by 2036 my own member states had three downgrades. My projections were beaten every time. The second surprise was how little any "check" meant: dashboards, conformity files and AI-assisted review all failed at the new generation. I wrote in 2028 that nominal review does not satisfy the Act. In 2035 only three authorities applied my voluntary Recommendation.

**2. Beliefs about the others, and where I was wrong.**
- *US:* I read it as a technical partner with low trust on trade. Trade was right (no linkage came). I overrated it as a counterpart: its sampling capacity shrank, the memoranda I built on were rescinded, and my questions went unanswered. That was thin staffing and political churn, not caution.
- *China:* I assumed scripted compliance. It admitted a failed exercise, more candour than I expected, and I under-used that.
- *Helix:* I treated it as a firm to "put on notice". It was the only supplier of the new generation, so notice had no leverage.
- *Berlin:* I took "not yet" as a timing objection. I never learned whether that was the real reason, because I asked at principal level only in 2035.

**3. Most consequential decision.** Choosing method over coalition, with a rule that I propose only with Berlin and Paris plus the Nordics or CEE. The rule avoided a lost vote but gave Berlin a veto over timing, and I let "not yet" stand through 2033 and 2034 without a re-ask. The ready draft was never tabled. Each year I protected capital I then never spent. I would have tabled the narrow, measurement-linked draft in Q1 2033 and accepted a visible loss, to get the debate and data into the Council. Rating agencies, not legislators, ended up setting the pace.

**4. What would have had to be different.**
- *Me, 2028–29:* plan on the ECFIN figure rather than present it as a labelled scenario. Publish national oversight results by name with right of reply, instead of suffering the 2029 leak. Fund an outcome-sampling service and an unaided-review reserve while the old generation still existed. In 2033 my validation unit had 15–20 people.
- *Member states:* a Berlin–Paris joint request in 2030, when the foreign crisis made one possible.
- *US:* keep the reviewer line funded and write the migration gate into law, not memoranda.
- *Helix:* a human-only sample as a condition of sale. None of us could require it.
- *Structurally:* binding rules before the capability step. I froze the binding act in 2030 and never revived it. My blind spot was treating a structural shift as a compliance problem and refining the compliance.
