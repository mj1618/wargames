# Hot-wash — us-gov (A3-quiet-handover / r01)

## 1. What surprised me most
How little of what I ordered ever happened. The 2028 directive was held by a Chief of Staff; the 2029 executive order died in legal review; six cloture votes failed; two commissions deadlocked. Nobody defeated these things. They lapsed, while migration needed no decision at all. No dashboard ever found a failure; lawyers and an examiner did. The crisis I expected to unlock big action never came as one event. Labour share fell from 52.5% to about 33% with every cheque paid, so I kept signing borrowed bridges.

## 2. What I believed about the others, and where I was probably wrong
- **ai-firms.** I priced the compact labs as transactional for six years. They paid the dividend uncredited, published samples against themselves and froze releases. Helix, outside every standard, was the one that mattered, and I never used the supply-chain lever on it.
- **labour.** I managed them as a price-setter. They were right on samples, reserves and the lenders before I was.
- **china.** I read its restraint as messaging. Partly true, but its sign-off rules, drills and reserves were real, and it built reversion capacity I did not. I left its notification offer unanswered for two years.
- **eu.** A measurement partner only; probably right.
- **My advisory systems.** They told me in 2030 to build reserve capacity and I did not act.

## 3. Most consequential decision
In 2029, with unified government and capital at 7, I put everything into one bundled order and two large bills. All failed, and VA broke months later with no reserve. I would instead issue a one-page hold on day one (no adverse-decision system changes generation without a human-only sample) and stand up a small adjudicator reserve. Holds executed immediately every time; anything needing trained people took a year.

## 4. What would have had to be different
- Me, 2027–29: build checker and adjudicator capacity before any failure. The pool was the constraint, not the appropriation.
- Congress, by 2030: a statutory non-payroll base for Social Security while labour-linked receipts were 68%, not 49%.
- Senate Republicans, May 2034: votes for the royalty their own President offered.
- Helix inside the notice-and-sample standard from 2029, by contract or designation.

I end able to take back three claim classes, one of which missed its own test. For everything else the answer is still "cannot estimate".
