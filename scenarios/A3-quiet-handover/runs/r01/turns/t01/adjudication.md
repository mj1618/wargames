### us-gov M1
> **Introduce the "American AI Leadership and Workforce Act": the preemption-for-protections bargain.** *Drivers:* White House accelerationists and Senate business conservatives (preemption title); House abundance moderates as cross-party brokers; populist-nationalists courted on the worker and accountability titles. **Needs Congress.** Bipartisan Senate Commerce introduction, with a House Education & Workforce / Energy & Commerce companion. **Title I, national framework:** preempts state laws regulating frontier-model *development* (training and safety-testing regimes); codifies a federal frontier transparency and incident-reporting standard modelled on what labs already comply with (SB 53-like); state laws on consumer protection, civil rights, and state government use are NOT preempted. **Title II, workers:** AI Adjustment Assistance with wage insurance for displaced workers over 45 and re-employment accounts; WARN Act expansion requiring notice for AI-driven mass layoffs; a refundable entry-level hiring and apprenticeship credit for under-26 hires. **Title III, accountability:** statutory minimum standard for meaningful human review of AI-assisted federal benefits and rights-impacting determinations, plus an appeal right. **Pay-fors (to negotiate):** closing a tax-expenditure loophole on accelerated depreciation of AI capex for the largest firms, plus spectrum auction proceeds; explicitly no compute tax. **Whip plan:** WH courts 8–10 Senate Democrats via Titles II and III; Speaker-side moderates carry the House companion; WH signals it will sign a bill with Titles I–III and veto clean preemption-only riders if the House sends them, which costs nothing and buys credibility. **Intended result this year:** introduced, marked up in Senate Commerce, and named as the reference text in the House hearings; passage targeted for 2028 before the conventions.
- **Actor's reasons:** each side's stated price is in the bill; DOJ suits stall; both parties want a 2028 "protected workers and beat China" story.

- **Base rate / resources:** introduction and markup are not passage. Senate Commerce is chaired by the President's party and can mark up on party lines plus a few Democrats.
