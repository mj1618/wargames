## 4. The environment as the selection process "sees" it (Q1 2027)

**Where measured metrics most favour agent deployment at L0:**
- Software development
- Customer support
- Marketing content
- Bookkeeping and back-office finance
- Legal document services
- Logistics brokerage and procurement
- Specialty insurance underwriting
- Federal and state benefits pre-determinations (drafting)
- Routine government correspondence and procurement evaluations

**Where measured metrics do not yet favour agents:**
- Open-ended management
- Regulated clinical decisions
- Physical work
- Anything that needs liability-bearing sign-off a human must defend in court

**Who rewards growth:**
- Enterprise principals reward cost and speed metrics.
- Equity markets reward AI-native revenue growth. Orrery's valuation is ~$210B on ~$34B revenue.
- AI-native firms ~$40B/yr revenue, growing fast; Orrery ~$34B of it.
- Machine-to-machine commerce: ~6% of US B2B transaction value flows between counterparties where both sides are AI agents.
- Public estimates of the AI-run footprint (~1.5% of US output) understate the true footprint, because conventional firms have quietly handed operations to agent stacks.

**Constraints in force and how each is checked:**
- **Federal "human-in-the-loop" for consequential decisions.** Enforced by a sign-off field, so it is a proxy. Review is often a fast approval click.
- **State AI laws** (California SB 53 and ~100 others). Mostly disclosure, reporting and documentation duties checked by filings, so they are proxies. Federal preemption suits are pending.
- **EU AI Act high-risk obligations.** Not yet applicable; they start December 2027 / August 2028.
- **Liability for agent harms.** Unsettled in US law. No outcome-based liability regime exists.
- **New this period: voluntary human-made provenance labels.** Retailers, two streaming platforms and several publishers have adopted them, and a premium market for certified human work has appeared. The labels are self-declared by producers. No independent audit regime exists yet.
- **Vendor usage policies.** The Supreme Court rules by June on whether the executive can blacklist vendors whose policies refuse government uses.

**Public mood.**
- About two-thirds of Americans want AI development slowed.
- Majorities use AI assistants weekly.
- ~25% of adults (survey) say an assistant is their primary source for news, advice and major decisions.
- ~14% of media time is AI-generated.
