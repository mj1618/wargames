## 4. Private intel

**Commission internal modelling (DG ECFIN, President's cabinet; not public).** Labour-linked taxes and contributions fund ~80% of member-state social systems. A 10-point fall in labour's income share would open a fiscal hole of ~3% of GDP by the mid-2030s. US data is moving faster than the Commission's central case assumed.

**AI Act.**
- High-risk obligations apply from December 2027 (part August 2028).
- Industry associations, led by German manufacturers and US platforms, are already lobbying for a further delay.
- The Parliament's centre-left and Greens, and the European trade union federations, are demanding no further slippage.
- Commission services say the harmonised standards needed for compliance will be only partly ready by December.

**Member states.**
- France backs Aster and sovereignty.
- Germany worries about industry and exports.
- The Nordics trust flexicurity and social partners.
- Several Central and Eastern European states want investment first.
- No member state has publicly raised the fiscal-base question.

**Competitiveness.** AI deployment in EU firms lags the US by ~18 months. Aster's best model is ~9–12 months behind the US frontier. The first AI gigafactories come online in late 2027–28. Dependence on US cloud persists.

**Transatlantic.** European union federations are proposing a joint campaign with US unions around the Geneva summit. US industry is pressing for EU digital rules to be addressed in trade talks.
