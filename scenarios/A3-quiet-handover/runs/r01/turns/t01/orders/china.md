# Orders — China (PRC party-state) — Turn 01 (1 January – 31 December 2027)

## 1. Reflection

**What is happening.** The year is the run-up to the 21st Party Congress, and the question of the year is which AI line goes into the Congress report: NDRC/MIIT's "AI+ acceleration with safeguards" or HRSS and the provinces' "stability first" (intel §4). The centre's guidance decides it. Beneath that choice sits the fact we do not publish: true urban youth unemployment is ~21% against a published ~17%, and clerical, customer-service and back-office jobs in tier-1/tier-2 cities are being automated faster than the Plan assumed. Manufacturing employment is holding. That asymmetry — manufacturing fine, services bleeding — is the key fact for policy design.

Externally, the United States is doing what we expected: a divided government, an executive pushing "AI Dominance" and preemption of state law, labour's share at a record low (52.5%), and public estimates that about ten firms steer two-thirds of AI-directed activity. Two-thirds of Americans want development slowed; the House will hold hearings on "AI and the American worker". This is the "rule by tech oligarchs" drift we anticipated, and it is both a strategic opening (US domestic limits would narrow a 6–8 month gap we cannot close on chips alone) and an ideological argument we can make to the Global South and Europe. The EU has delayed its own AI Act obligations to stay competitive — it is anxious and hedging, which makes it courtable.

Washington reads our public posture on AI pace closely. Visible acceleration strengthens the US factions who oppose domestic limits; visible restraint, human-control mandates for our own state AI, or a ratified standards agreement weakens them. We intend to use this lever deliberately. The restraint we signal costs us little, because what we would signal — Party/human responsibility over state AI systems, employment-managed deployment in service sectors — is what the Party wants domestically anyway. Research and industrial deployment are not slowed by it.

**Key uncertainties.**
- Whether the published-vs-true unemployment gap is itself understated. CCDI says our statistical channels remain reliable; the cadre-evaluation pilot shows some officials and their AI tools gaming scores. We treat this as a limited implementation problem, correctable through inspection — but we note it.
- Whether US labs' "multi-week agents" in 2027 widen the gap beyond 8 months. If they do, catch-up pressure rises and the stability-first camp loses ground.
- Whether the US executive will engage substantively at the working group / Geneva, or treat our proposals as propaganda. Either outcome is useful to us; substantive engagement is better.
- Local-government fiscal capacity to carry hiring and transition programmes. Local debt is the binding domestic constraint.

**Red lines as they stand.** No foreign inspection inside Chinese AI systems or data beyond symbolic, reciprocal arrangements. No private firm or AI system becomes an independent centre of power. Taiwan: avoid war, no independence. No open acknowledgement of the true unemployment figure.

## 3. Options considered

| # | Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"AI+ acceleration with safeguards"** (NDRC/MIIT line): full-speed deployment across all 70%-of-sectors targets, safeguards largely rhetorical | Fastest route to closing the gap; growth; pleases industry and local-growth cadres | Youth unemployment already 21% and service-sector automation outrunning plan; stability risk in the Congress year; visible acceleration strengthens US accelerationists, which does not help us |
| 2 | **"Stability first"** (HRSS/provincial line): employment before deployment speed, sector-wide pacing of AI deployment | Lowest near-term unrest risk; strong signal of restraint to Washington | Risks falling permanently behind; manufacturing/industrial AI is where self-reliance is won and employment there is holding — slowing it is pure loss; growth hit |
| 3 | **"Steady AI+ under Party leadership"** (synthesis): accelerate industrial, scientific, robotics and compute self-reliance; employment-managed deployment in service/clerical sectors via pilots, SOE job-preservation, public hiring; formal Party/human-responsibility requirement for state AI systems | Matches the real asymmetry (manufacturing fine, services bleeding); gives both factions something; the human-responsibility mandate reinforces Party control and reads as restraint abroad | More complex to implement; local governments may game employment-impact assessments; private service firms automate anyway |
| 4 (unconventional) | **Go first on "human control"**: unilaterally announce binding human-responsibility requirements for state AI systems and propose a UN-centred framework at Geneva, assembling a Global South bloc and courting the EU, explicitly contrasting with "concentrated private AI power" in the US | Cheap (it is domestic policy anyway); weakens US accelerationist factions; captures the standards narrative; Qilin's adoption gives the bloc weight | US may dismiss it as propaganda; Geneva may produce only a vague communiqué; invites questions about our own cadre-evaluation and planning AI |
| 5 (unconventional) | **Campaign-style youth-employment mobilisation now**: nationwide hiring mandates, graduate civil-service and SOE intakes, rural postings, deployment moratorium in named service sectors | Decisive; the Party's proven tool when a problem turns political | Premature — unemployment is not yet a visible political crisis; a moratorium concedes the acceleration narrative wholesale; fiscal burden on indebted localities; hold in reserve |

**Decision:** Option 3 as the domestic line, with Option 4 as the international expression of it. Option 5 is the contingency if stability deteriorates.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement

*(State Council / Party guidance, released early 2027; reiterated through the year and at Geneva.)*

"China advances 'AI+' steadily and in a people-centred way, under the leadership of the Party. AI will be deployed to strengthen the real economy — manufacturing, scientific research, agriculture, energy, and public services — and to achieve high-level technological self-reliance. In sectors where AI affects large numbers of jobs, deployment will be managed so that employment, especially of young people, is protected while new jobs are created: enterprises will conduct employment-impact assessments, state-owned enterprises will fulfil their social responsibility, and the state will expand training and social insurance. All decisions of the state that affect citizens will have a responsible human official and Party committee; AI systems advise, people decide. China holds that AI governance belongs to all of humanity through the United Nations, not to a handful of companies in any one country. We welcome cooperation with all partners, including Europe and the Global South, on standards, capacity-building, and open models."

**Explicit instruction on the Party Congress line (per intel §6):** the centre's guidance is the synthesis — "Steady AI+ under Party leadership." Neither the NDRC/MIIT "acceleration" draft nor the HRSS "stability first" draft is adopted as written. The published line will emphasise acceleration in industry, science and self-reliance, and employment-managed deployment in services, under Party leadership and human responsibility.

### Minor actions

- **Qilin:** continue open-weight releases at the current cadence; prioritise Global South language localisation and efficiency on domestic accelerators; no public claim of "beating" the US frontier this year (tone: steady, not racing).
- **Tianshu:** priority on multi-week agents for state and SOE use; outputs to central planning carry a named-official sign-off from this year. PLA/security-service integration continues as before.
- **CCDI:** complete an inspection sweep of the three cadre-evaluation pilot provinces with independent cross-checks of a sample of scored outcomes; discipline exemplary cases; hold the pilot at three provinces this year. Do not scale nationally until the sweep reports.
- **Statistics:** published youth unemployment remains on the current methodology. No change.
- **Media guidance:** frame the year as "AI for the people, under the Party"; contrast with "AI concentrated in a few firms" abroad; cover the "made by people" wave abroad sympathetically as evidence that people everywhere want human work valued; suppress unrest-adjacent coverage of layoffs.
- **Chip supply:** maintain the stockpile and existing acquisition channels; no new public steps.
- **Taiwan:** routine posture; no escalation this year — the Congress year needs calm.
- **Working-group scheduling:** push for the US–China AI and economic-security working group to meet before Geneva so that a joint human-control statement is available there.

### Contingencies

- **If visible youth unrest or coordinated layoff protests occur in tier-1/tier-2 cities:** switch to Option 5 — campaign-style mobilisation: national hiring mandates for SOEs and public institutions, a deployment moratorium in named service sectors, central fiscal transfers; shift the Congress line toward "stability first". Political capital spent as needed.
- **If US labs' multi-week agents widen the assessed gap beyond ~12 months:** redirect investment toward Qilin/Tianshu catch-up and enterprise-agent deployment; loosen service-sector pacing for private firms (keeping SOE job-preservation); accept a higher stability risk for position vs the US.
- **If the US engages substantively on human control / deployment transparency:** accept reciprocal, symbolic arrangements; refuse any inspection of Chinese systems; publicise agreement to reinforce US domestic-limits factions.
- **If the US rejects or ridicules the proposals:** proceed with the Global South bloc and EU track; publicise the rejection as evidence of "AI concentrated in a few hands".
- **If the Supreme Court upholds executive blacklisting of vendors:** use in messaging as US state–oligarch fusion; assess implications for Chinese firms' US exposure; no other change.
- **If CCDI's sweep finds cadre-score gaming is widespread rather than limited:** freeze the pilot, discipline cases publicly, order independent statistical cross-checks on the employment series; do not abandon AI-scored evaluation.
- **If Taiwan tensions flare:** contain; the Congress year must stay calm. Hotline and dialogue used to keep AI issues separate from Taiwan.
