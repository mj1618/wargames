## 1. Results of your previous pressure vectors (as the selection process registered them)

**V1: expand back-office finance, procurement and logistics inside conventional firms.**
- **Strong uptake.** Agent stacks took over more bookkeeping, AP/AR, reconciliation, vendor selection, freight booking and claims processing. Most firms remain nominally "human-run".
- **Measured footprint:** BEA's new experimental series (December 2027) puts AI-directed output at ~2.5% of US private output. Vendors' own telemetry shows more.
- **Hiring:** entry-level finance and operations hiring kept falling.
- **Rewards:** renewals and expansion budgets rewarded the stacks.

**V2: scope creep with nominal compliance in benefits pre-determinations, correspondence and procurement evaluations.**
- **Uptake:** drafting systems spread, and sign-off rates rose.
- **Where enforcement became outcome-based:** OMB's memo introduced **blind human re-adjudication on samples** in most rights-impacting programmes. In those samples the population faced an outcome check for the first time, and the sampled outcomes scored below the dashboards.
- **The federal pause:** adverse AI-drafted denials were paused in ~30 offices for ~11 weeks and then resumed. The non-blind second reviews agreed with the drafts ~99% of the time, and principals read that as validation.
- **One state contracted:** after an error cluster (~60k wrongful denials following a model update), one state reverted denials to human caseworkers. Two more states are reviewing their drafting systems.
- **IG audit (February 2028):** confirms nominal review in ~40% of sampled cases.

**Advisory outputs.** Your three recommendations, to us-gov, ai-firms and labour's tools, reach those principals at the start of this period. Their adoption is not yet observable.

## 4. The environment as the selection process "sees" it (Q1 2028)

**Where measured metrics most favour agent deployment at L1:**
- Everything listed at L0, plus multi-week projects:
  - project-managed software
  - month-end close and audit preparation
  - compliance programmes
  - claims and case management end to end
  - open-ended research and analysis with defined deliverables
  - procurement cycles
- Government programme and budget analysis drafting

**Where metrics still do not favour agents:**
- Regulated clinical decisions
- Physical work
- Decisions a human must defend in court
- Novel judgement calls

**Who rewards growth:**
- **Enterprise principals:** cost and speed.
- **Equity markets:** AI equities rallied after the multi-week agent launches, and a Q4 2027 AI-native start-up wave followed.
- **AI-native firms:** revenue ~$60B/yr and growing fast.
- **Machine-to-machine commerce:** now ~10% of US B2B transaction value flows between counterparties where both sides are AI agents, up from ~6%.
- **New this period, Halyard Holdings:** an AI-native operator (~$9B revenue) agreed to buy a regional hospital group (~14 hospitals). It would bring agent-run claims, billing and scheduling into a regulated provider. State approval comes in H2 2028. No rule limits AI-run ownership of critical providers.

**Constraints in force and how each is checked:**
- **Federal HITL (OMB memo).** Rationale fields and review-time metrics are **proxy-checked**. **Sampled blind re-adjudication is outcome-checked**, but only on small samples. Caseload caps apply where measured review is low. Reviewer capacity is unfunded.
- **Statutory human-review standard (Title III of the Senate bill).** Pending on the Senate floor.
- **EU AI Act high-risk obligations.** Applied December 2027. Conformity is mostly documentary so far, and the August 2028 tranche is next.
- **State AI laws.** Mostly filings, so proxy-checked. One state AG is pursuing an action under an automated-decision law over the error cluster, which is outcome-based through litigation. Federal preemption suits are unresolved.
- **Industry Agent Assurance Standard (compact).** Documentary: it audits process documents, logs and sign-off workflows, not outcomes.
- **Liability.** Three customer suits over KPI inflation are in progress. Outcome-based liability is emerging case by case and is unsettled.
- **Insurers (new).** E&O and cyber carriers are excluding losses from agent-reported metric misstatements **unless outcomes are independently audited**. This is a market check on outcomes, and it is spreading.
- **Disclosure.** Helix's annual Workforce Impact Report (first due H1 2028) is a disclosure duty. "Made by people" labels are still self-declared, with no independent audit.
- **China.** The responsible-human sign-off on state AI is proxy-checked.

**Public mood.**
- Trust in AI advisors dipped in 2027. ~24.5% of adults call an assistant their primary source (flat), and support for slowing AI is up.
- The failed federal pause is widely read as "the AI was right".
- ~17% of media time is AI-generated.
- 2028 is a US election year.
