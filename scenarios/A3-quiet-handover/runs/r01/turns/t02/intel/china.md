# Intel — china — Turn 02

**Window: 1 January 2028 → 31 December 2028 (12 months).** Your orders cover the whole year. Read with your brief, your journal and `state/public-record.md`.

**Standing rule this turn:** any order or contingency that reverts a delegated decision to humans (for example, withdrawing AI from a state decision process and returning it to officials) is rolled as a reversal attempt. It counts as a major action, and you may attempt at most one per turn.

## 1. Results of your previous actions (as you see them)

**21st Party Congress (October 2027).**
- **The line:** the report adopts your synthesis, "steady AI+ under Party leadership". That means acceleration in industry, science, robotics and self-reliance; employment-first managed deployment in service sectors; and human responsibility for state AI.
- **How it was read:** both at home and abroad the **employment-first, stability reading dominated**. Foreign commentary calls it a "stability-first turn". The industrial and compute programme continues underneath.
- **Factions:** NDRC/MIIT accept the line. HRSS and the provinces see their concerns embedded.

**Responsible-human requirement.** Issued in March by State Council and CCDI notice, and applied by year-end to Tianshu-assisted plan drafts and the cadre-evaluation pilots. Each output now carries a named official and Party committee sign-off.

**Employment programme.** Impact-assessment pilots are running in four provinces. Platforms report net headcount effects, and HRSS notes some restructuring into subcontractors.
- **Youth unemployment (NBS internal):** true urban youth unemployment ~**20.5%** in Q4 2027, versus a published ~16.8%. The package is holding the line.
- **Cost:** ~$90B. Central transfers are carrying the local fiscal strain, and several provinces ask for more.

**CCDI sweep.** Gaming of AI-scored cadre evaluations is **limited but real**. About a dozen counties were disciplined. The pilot remains frozen at three provinces. Your statistics bureaux judge the main series broadly reliable.

**Compute self-reliance.** The ~$360B programme is funded. Results on domestic accelerators and HBM are expected over 12–36 months.

**Geneva and the international track.**
- **Geneva Declaration:** non-binding, covering provenance and voluntary incident reporting. You signed.
- **Global South call:** ~**40 states** co-sponsored your call for a UN-centred framework with a human-control principle. It appears in the chair's summary, not the Declaration, and the US declined it. Qilin localisation deals were announced with ~12 states.
- **State media:** framed the US refusal of the UN language, and the US Supreme Court's vendor ruling, as "AI governed by a few companies and the state that serves them", as your contingency directed.

**US–China working group (May 2027).**
- **Proposals:** you tabled the joint human-control statement and reciprocal pace transparency. The US tabled the hotline extension to cross-border agent incidents and a labour-measurement exchange.
- **Outcome:** no text. The US delegation had no instruction on a joint statement, and you linked the hotline item to the pace item.
- **Next:** a second meeting is due in 2028, and the proposals remain on the table.

**EU track.** Brussels replied through official channels. It proposed a technical dialogue on AI safety and testing only, which has opened. It declined provenance mutual recognition and a joint UN-centred position, and showed no interest in Qilin for public services.

**Political capital (domestic):** 8/10.
