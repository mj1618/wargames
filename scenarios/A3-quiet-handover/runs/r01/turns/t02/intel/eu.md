# Intel — eu — Turn 02

**Window: 1 January 2028 → 31 December 2028 (12 months).** Your orders cover the whole year. Read with your brief, your journal and `state/public-record.md`.

**Standing rule this turn:** any order or contingency that reverts a delegated decision to humans (for example, requiring a public administration to withdraw an AI system and return decisions to officials) is rolled as a reversal attempt. It counts as a major action, and you may attempt at most one per turn.

## 1. Results of your previous actions (as you see them)

**High-level expert group on funding the social model.** Set up in March 2027 with Nordic and German finance-ministry nominees and social partners. Its interim note went to ECOFIN and EPSCO in December. It frames "labour-linked financing exposure" qualitatively, with no figure. The **final report is due mid-2028**.

The June US Treasury leak ("AI moves Social Security insolvency to 2031") made the topic respectable in Europe. The "EU AI tax" framing has not taken hold.

**AI Act.**
- **Date held:** high-risk obligations applied on **2 December 2027**.
- **Make-it-work package:** guidance and a presumption-of-conformity route were published in October, and SME simplification came through implementing acts.
- **Reactions:** German industry calls it "insufficient". Parliament's centre-left, the Greens and unions back it.
- **Next:** the **August 2028 tranche** is due.

**Provenance.** CEN-CENELEC has a mandate for a voluntary human-made provenance standard, with delivery expected 2029+.

**Your messages (2027).**
- **Nordic and German finance ministries:** they nominated experts. The Nordics are engaged, and Berlin is cautious but in.
- **Paris and Berlin:** they agreed the "hold the date with flexibility" line. Berlin agreed grudgingly under industry pressure, and France leads the cultural provenance side.
- **US trade and tech channel:** no formal reply. US trade officials keep raising EU digital rules informally.

**Contingency check: the US Supreme Court ruling (June 2027).** The ruling lets the US executive bar AI vendors it designates as supply-chain risks from federal procurement. **No European vendor has been designated.** The Legal Service notes the power exists. Your contingency was not triggered.

**China.** Per your contingency, you engaged on safety and testing only. A technical EU–China dialogue has opened. You declined provenance mutual recognition, a joint UN-centred position, and Qilin dependencies for public services.

**Political capital:** 4/10.

## 4. Private intel
- **DG ECFIN (restricted).** The refreshed model confirms the earlier result. Labour-linked taxes and contributions fund ~80% of member-state social systems. A 10-point fall in labour's share opens a fiscal hole of ~3% of GDP by the mid-2030s. The US data and multi-week agents push the timing assumptions earlier. The figure is not public. The expert group's drafting team has asked whether it may cite it in the final report. Drafting widens the circle.
- **Expert group: options under discussion.**
  - Broadening the contribution base beyond wages.
  - Taxes on capital or AI rents, coordinated Pillar-Two style.
  - An EU own resource.
  - Stronger social-partner bargaining over deployment.
  - Members want a steer on how far to go.
- **Adoption.** EU firms lag US deployment by ~18 months, but multi-week agents are being adopted fast in finance and shared-services centres. Aster is now ~12 months behind the US frontier.
- **AI Act August tranche.** German industry and some CEE governments are lobbying for a targeted delay. Harmonised standards for several provisions are still incomplete. Parliament's centre-left would oppose any reopening.
- **Member states.**
  - France pushes sovereignty and the cultural agenda.
  - The Nordics are open to the contribution-base discussion.
  - Germany is torn between industry and its finance ministry.
  - CEE wants investment and gigafactories first.
  - EU-level tax measures need unanimity.
- **Commission AI advisory tools:** routine output only this period.
- **EP elections (2029):** parties are starting to position. The "human-centred AI" theme polls well.
