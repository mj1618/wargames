# Orders — China — Turn 02 (1 January 2028; orders cover Jan–Dec 2028)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening.**

- *The Congress line held and is being read the way I wanted.* "Steady AI+ under Party leadership" is now doctrine; abroad it reads as a "stability-first turn" (intel §1). That reading is itself an asset: some US legislators are citing my responsible-human requirement in their own arguments for limits (intel §4), and the US Senate bill heading to the floor contains a statutory human-review standard. The lever the brief describes is working at the margin. The industrial and compute programme continues underneath, unremarked.
- *The stability problem is held, not solved.* True urban youth unemployment ~20.5% against a published ~16.8% (intel §1). The ~$90B package is holding the line, but it is being carried by central transfers, provinces are asking for more, and HRSS already sees platforms restructuring into subcontractors to get around the pilots. Graduate season (summer) is the next pressure point, and the pressure is now compounded: US multi-week agents shipped in October, Qilin will have comparable agents in H1, and private platforms are pushing to relax pacing (intel §4). If I let them, clerical and customer-service automation in tier-1/2 cities accelerates exactly as graduates arrive.
- *The gap is roughly stable but the next step could widen it.* Services assess 6–9 months (was 6–8). HBM is still binding; the $360B compute programme is funded but pays out over 12–36 months. The thing to watch in 2028 is the US "automated AI researcher" claim, which services judge likely. If real, it compounds: faster US research widens the gap faster than chips alone can close it. My journal trigger stands: beyond ~12 months I shift toward catch-up over stability. I should pre-position now so that the shift, if needed, is a matter of allocation rather than a new programme.
- *The United States is in an election year with a lame-duck executive.* Both parties' populists attack "tech oligarchs"; the executive's own preemption bill needs 60 votes; the IG audit confirmed that in ~40% of sampled cases "human review" of US benefits decisions was nominal (public record T2). This is ideological material for me, and it also means the US delegation to the second working-group meeting will have little authority. I should seek a small deliverable before November and prepare for whoever wins.
- *Europe is a thinner hedge than I hoped.* Brussels accepted only a safety-and-testing technical dialogue and declined provenance recognition, a joint UN position, and Qilin for public services (intel §1). Keep it warm, spend little on it.
- *The Global South track is my best international result.* ~40 co-sponsors, 12 Qilin localisation deals. The US declined the UN-centred language, which is exactly the contrast I want. The next step is to move the human-control principle from a chair's summary into a UN process.
- *Internal information.* CCDI found cadre-score gaming "limited but real" — a dozen counties disciplined, pilot still frozen. NBS judges the main series broadly reliable. I accept that. One thing I note and will check cheaply: provincial reports say responsible-official sign-off on Tianshu plan drafts is "fast in most cases" (intel §4). Fast sign-off is what an efficient system looks like, and it is also what the US IG found when review was nominal. The two systems differ — ours has a named official and a Party committee answerable for the result — but a spot-check costs nothing.

**Key uncertainties.** (1) Whether the US automated-researcher claim will be real or marketing, and how services will tell. (2) Whether graduate season passes quietly with the package at its current size. (3) Who wins in November and what they do on chips and on the dialogue. (4) Whether platforms comply with pacing in substance or route around it (subcontractors, "pilots", offshore). (5) Whether my own series on youth employment is as reliable as NBS says.

**Red lines as they stand.** No foreign inspection inside Chinese AI systems or data beyond symbolic, reciprocal arrangements. No private platform or AI system becoming an independent centre of power — platforms pushing to "relax pacing" are a request, not a decision; the decision is mine. Taiwan: avoid war, no independence; keep it quiet through the US election. Fiscal: prefer not to borrow centrally at scale; I will top up transfers, not open the taps.

| 2 | **Relax pacing now that US agents have shipped; go for catch-up in services.** Let platforms deploy multi-week agents broadly, lean on hiring mandates and media control to absorb the shock. | Faster productivity; closes the deployment gap; platforms happy; growth. | Hits graduates in the summer of a 20.5%-true-unemployment year; the "stability-first" reading collapses abroad, strengthening US accelerationists; violates my own order of evaluation (control and stability come before comparative position). |
| 3 (unconventional) | **Quiet catch-up through research, not deployment: an "automated AI research" priority programme.** Turn Qilin's and Tianshu's multi-week agents inward on AI R&D itself, with first call on stockpiled and new domestic compute, under lab Party committees. Public framing: "AI for Science", already in the Congress line. | Hedges the gap-widening risk without touching service-sector employment or the public restraint posture; exploits the one area where the state can direct compute by fiat; pre-positions the catch-up shift my journal trigger anticipates. | HBM-constrained, so returns are uncertain; if the compute priority leaks it undercuts the restraint signal; an automated-research loop is harder to supervise than a planning draft — I rely on the responsible-human rule and lab Party committees to keep it an instrument. |
| 4 | **Spend the turn on diplomacy: WG deliverable plus a UN process.** Make the second WG meeting and a UNGA resolution the two majors. | Locks in the human-control principle internationally while the US is divided; cheap. | Does nothing for the summer; the US delegation has no authority in an election year; a UN resolution without Western support changes little in practice. | 5 | **Ease the youth number: publish a corrected series and a bigger cash-transfer programme.** | Restores credibility of statistics; a frank move. | Not how we handle this; a visible correction in a tense year is a stability risk in itself; cash transfers are the Western answer. Rejected. |

**Decision:** Options 1 and 3 as the two majors. Option 4 runs through messages and minor actions. Option 2 is held in reserve as the catch-up contingency, applied in tier-3+ cities first if the gap trigger fires.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement

*State Council and Party communiqué, with follow-up through state media across the year:*

"2028 is the first full year of steady AI+ under Party leadership. The Party's principle — AI advises, people decide — now applies to every AI-assisted state decision, each carrying a named responsible official. We will apply the same principle to the economy: employment-impact assessment, proven in four provinces, becomes the national standard for AI deployment in employment-sensitive service sectors, and a Graduate Employment Action for 2028 will ensure that this year's graduates enter work, study or service. Chinese AI advances steadily in industry, science and self-reliance; Qilin's new generation of models will serve Chinese manufacturing, Chinese science and our partners in the developing world. We welcome the second meeting of the China–US working group and are ready to conclude practical measures on incident notification. With more than forty partner states we will carry the principle of human control of AI into the United Nations in 2028, where the governance of a technology that affects all humanity belongs — not in the hands of a few companies and the state that serves them."

### Minor actions

- **Qilin/Tianshu public posture:** Qilin's H1 agent launch is framed as steady, for industry, science and partners; no "beating the US" claims; offered to Global South partners under the 12 localisation deals. Tianshu stays out of public view.
- **WG delegation instruction:** seek a Q2 meeting; close the hotline extension to cross-border autonomous-agent incidents (financial systems and critical infrastructure included) — it is in our interest now that multi-week agents cross borders; seek a short recorded principle on responsible human officials for AI-assisted state decisions; accept the labour-measurement exchange at methodology level only, no micro-data; no export-control discussion. Delink the hotline from pace transparency if the principle text is obtainable.
- **UN track:** Foreign Ministry and UN mission to build, with the ~40 co-sponsors, a UNGA resolution for autumn 2028 establishing an intergovernmental process on AI governance with the human-control principle; funded by the capacity-building package (~$10–15B over several years, first tranche 2028).
- **EU:** continue the safety/testing technical dialogue; propose a joint note on agent testing methods ahead of the August tranche; re-offer provenance alignment in narrow technical form (metadata compatible with the CEN-CENELEC standard). No system access.
- **CCDI spot-check (not a reversal):** sampled blind review of responsible-official sign-off on Tianshu-assisted plan drafts and the frozen cadre-evaluation pilots — review time, override rate, whether the official could explain the decision without the draft. Report to the PSC by Q3. The pilot stays frozen at three provinces.
- **NBS cross-check:** a one-off independent household survey of youth employment in six tier-1/2 cities, run outside the provincial reporting chain, reported privately by Q2 before graduate season.
- **State media line for 2028:** the US IG audit ("review under 30 seconds"), Halyard buying hospitals, "ten companies steer three-fifths" — contrasted with "AI advises, people decide". Do not attack US candidates by name or take sides in the election.
- **Platforms:** Party committees in the major platforms are told that pacing is relaxed only in industrial, R&D, logistics, export and tier-3+ uses; any platform reorganising itself as an "AI-native" firm with agent-run operations outside the pacing rule will be dealt with under the 2021 precedent.
- **Taiwan:** routine posture; no new exercise cycles timed to the US election; respond to campaign rhetoric with words.
- **Compute programme:** continue; quarterly reporting on HBM and accelerator milestones to the PSC.

### Contingencies

- **If a US lab makes an automated-AI-researcher claim and services assess the frontier gap beyond ~12 months (journal trigger):** shift toward catch-up. Redirect further compute to Action 2; relax the pacing rule in tier-3+ cities and in industrial and export uses first, tier-1/2 services last and after graduate season; keep the public tone steady. Re-evaluate the restraint posture only if the US election produces an executive that cannot be moved by it anyway.
- **If graduate-season unrest appears in more than one city:** campaign-style mobilisation — a temporary moratorium on new agent deployment in customer-service and clerical roles in affected provinces; SOE hiring mandates; a further transfer; media control. Postpone any platform pacing relaxation.
- **If the US Senate passes the bill with the human-review standard:** state media frames it as "the United States adopts the principle China set in 2027"; table the shared-understanding text at the WG meeting with that reference.
- **If the WG meeting produces no text again:** conclude the hotline extension anyway (it serves us) and hold the principle text for the next administration. After November, re-open the dialogue with the winner at principals level in Q1 2029.
- **If the new US administration tightens chip controls or designates Qilin:** accelerate self-reliance; keep the hotline open; do not retaliate on Taiwan; use the UN track and the Global South to frame the US as closing rather than leading.
- **If the CCDI spot-check finds sign-off nominal in a large share of Tianshu plan drafts:** tighten procedure — minimum review period, a second reader without the draft for high-impact items (not a reversal). Consider a reversal attempt in a later turn only if plan distortion is demonstrated.
- **If the NBS cross-check shows youth unemployment materially above 20.5%:** enlarge Action 1(b) and bring forward the moratorium option; re-examine the provincial reporting chain.
- **If a Chinese platform reorganises as an agent-run firm outside the pacing regime or acts on its own line:** rectification under the 2021 precedent — the control red line.
- **If Taiwan becomes a US election issue:** respond rhetorically; no change in posture before November.
