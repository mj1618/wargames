# Orders — EU — Turn 02 (1 Jan – 31 Dec 2028)

## 1. Reflection
**What I believe is happening.**
- The fiscal thesis has hardened. The refreshed DG ECFIN model (intel s4) confirms ~3% of GDP by the mid-2030s for a 10-point fall in labour's share, and the US data plus multi-week agents pull the timing earlier. My T1 doubt about "a 2030s problem" is now stronger. US labour share is ~52.0%, a record low, and the Treasury leak ("insolvency to 2031") made the topic respectable. Europe is lagging adoption by ~18 months, but multi-week agents are moving fast in finance and shared-services centres. I should plan for the lag to shrink, not hold.
- The US evidence base is useful to me. The IG audit finds nominal human review in ~40% of sampled cases. That is a direct empirical test of the "human oversight" requirement in my own high-risk regime. "Human in the loop" can become paper rights.
- The August tranche is the main near-term political fight. German industry and some CEE governments want a targeted delay, harmonised standards are incomplete, and Parliament's centre-left would oppose any reopening. I will not reopen the Act, but the standards gap is real.
- The expert group's final report (mid-2028) is my one chance to set the frame before the 2029 EP campaign. Its drafting team has asked to cite the ECFIN figure. Citing it widens the circle, so a leak is likely. A controlled release beats a leak.
- The Supreme Court contingency did not trigger: no European vendor has been designated. The US 2028 election (Nov) adds uncertainty, and a Senate preemption-for-protections bill is heading to the floor. I will watch both and not act yet.
- China made a leader-level offer on provenance, oversight and a UN-centred position. I engaged on testing only. I stay there. Qilin dependencies are still refused.

**Key uncertainties.**
- Whether the US shock is a transition or a structural break.
- The real size of Aster's gap (intel says ~12 months, slightly worse than the brief's 9-12).
- Whether Berlin's finance ministry or industry wins inside the German government.
- Whether the ECFIN figure survives contact with the press.
- Whether the US trade channel moves from informal to formal pressure after the election.
- Whether the AI advisory tools are anchoring me on "compliance" answers. They produced routine output only this period.

**Red lines as they stand.**
- No abandonment of the AI Act rights framework.
- No accepting US preemption of EU law via trade deals.
- No Qilin dependency for public services.
- No new tax measure promised that cannot clear unanimity.
- No spending of political capital below 2/10.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement
"The Commission confirms that the AI Act's second tranche applies in August as scheduled. Our aim is that every operator can comply. Where harmonised standards are not yet complete, the AI Office will publish common specifications and a sequencing statement, and will help national authorities work with operators in good faith. We will not reopen the Act. The independent expert group on financing Europe's social model will publish its final report in the summer. We will treat it as a basis for a transparent, evidence-led debate with Member States and social partners. Europe's approach remains human-centred: oversight must be real, and not a formality."

### Minor actions
- AI Office and the AI Board issue a voluntary request to national authorities to run OMB-style measurements (review times, override rates, sampled blind re-review) on public-sector high-risk AI. This is a low-cost way to test whether our own oversight regime is real.
- The Commission opens a first-stage social-partner consultation (Article 154 TFEU) on AI deployment and workplace bargaining. This channels the unions' push into a formal route without committing to legislation.
- Keep the Commission's AI advisory tools in a "challenger" role: ask them for the strongest case against the August approach and against the ECFIN publication. Treat routine output with scepticism.
- Hold the provenance standard track at CEN-CENELEC (delivery 2029+). Ask whether non-EU actors, including the transatlantic unions' proposal, are picking it up. No endorsement of the union statement.
- Ask Aster and the gigafactory programme for a candid gap and timing report. Intel says ~12 months behind, and the brief assumed 9-12.
- China: stay at testing-only. Continue the technical dialogue sessions. Decline provenance mutual recognition, a joint UN-centred position and Qilin dependencies again, unless something genuinely new is offered.

### Contingencies
- **If a German/CEE push for a legislative delay gets a Council majority and Parliament's centre-left signals acquiescence:** I propose only the narrow, sunset-limited stop-the-clock, limited to provisions with no standard. I will insist on a fixed end date and a standards delivery plan, and reject anything touching rights provisions or the date of the whole tranche.
- **If the ECFIN figure leaks before the report:** I acknowledge it as a modelled sensitivity, publish the method note immediately and stress "no proposal on the table". I will not deny it.
- **If a European vendor is designated a US "supply-chain risk" (the earlier contingency):** I convene the Legal Service and Council in 30 days and consider a coordinated procurement and trade response. No retaliation without Council backing.
- **If the US Senate passes a preemption bill that reaches EU-facing rules, or the US makes a formal trade demand tying market access to AI rules:** I treat it as a red line, reject it publicly, and go to the Council for a trade-defence posture. Otherwise I stay quiet through the November election.
- **If a US administration change (November) alters the trade tone:** I reassess in the next turn, with no new commitments in December.
- **If the national measurement exercise shows nominal review is widespread in EU public bodies:** I make the oversight guidance binding through harmonised standards and name the findings publicly, not wait for enforcement.
- **If China makes a new, genuinely different offer that is safety-limited:** I accept further technical sessions only. I do not enter provenance mutual recognition.
- **Reversal rule:** I am not ordering any reversion of a delegated decision to humans this turn. If the national measurement exercise later calls for it, that will be a major action in a later turn.
- **If political capital falls below 2:** I drop the social-partner consultation and the national measurement exercise and defend only the date and the report.
