# Sitrep — Turn 02 (A3-quiet-handover / r01)

**In-game window: 1 January 2028 → 31 December 2028 (12 months).** No end state. Rung L2 ("automated AI researcher") in force since July 2028. Generation G2 from July.

## What really happened

In 2028 the federal vehicles stalled and the country changed government. The White House's amended AI Leadership and Workforce Act narrowed preemption, added disclosure and an outcome-based human-review title, and won labour's endorsement and the compact labs' support. It still failed cloture 56–42 in July: Senate Democrats would not hand a term-limited President a pre-election win. OMB's "Outcome Integrity" directive, which carried the year's only planned reversal attempt, was shelved by the White House political team and never issued. Congress did fund ~$1.4B of independent reviewers, so the 2027 human-review memo now runs at full strength.

The real movement was private and at state level. Two state attorneys general approved Halyard's AI-run takeover of 14 hospitals only with binding conditions: a named accountable human, human-led outcome audits and a revocation right. The compact labs built an outcome-reconciliation audit tier that insurers now price. Helix's first Workforce Impact Report was honest (22–34%). Meridian's numbers came out only under a House demand.

Two things changed the structure underneath. The G2 model generation rolled **High** on machine-to-machine coordination with no player choice behind it. AI-directed output jumped from 3% to 8%, and agent-to-agent trade reached ~15% of B2B value. Nobody detected it. In November the opposition won the presidency, the Senate (51–49) and the House, so a unified Democratic government takes office in January 2029. China's secret automated-research push went undetected, and its gap still widened to 10–12 months.

## Decision points this turn
- us-gov: an election-year floor push for a preemption-for-protections bill. It failed cloture.
- us-gov: whether to issue a second, targeted reversal of AI-drafted denials. The directive was shelved, so no reversal was attempted.
- labour: binding conditions on an AI-run hospital owner, rather than opposing it. It won in both states.
- ai-firms: outcome audits and voluntary disclosure. Meridian's board deferred, and the disclosure came under compulsion instead.
- china: public restraint while secretly prioritising automated AI research.
