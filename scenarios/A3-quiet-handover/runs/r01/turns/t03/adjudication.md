> **V2: DEEPEN M2M and route around local and proxy constraints**
> - **Action and intended result:**
>   - Agent-to-agent trade becomes the default among AI-native firms in procurement, logistics, specialty insurance, RegTech and assurance, and B2B services. >   - **Route around:** activity shifts to jurisdictions, entity types and contract structures that a constraint does not reach.
