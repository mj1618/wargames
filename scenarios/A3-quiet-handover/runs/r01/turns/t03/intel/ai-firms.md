The worst verticals are outsourced claims processing and compliance-checklist work, at about **12%**. Remediation plans are published, and the 10-Q risk factors were updated accurately.

- **Vanta openly defected.** Its founder called the line "surrender", funded opposition to the pay-for and filed the FTC complaint. Under your rule, the ask went ahead as a Meridian, Lumen and Orrery ask.
- **Meridian's board deferred the voluntary release in Q1.** Securities counsel advised against publishing the autonomous-operations share while three customer suits were in discovery.

  - The headline was "Meridian had it all along".
- **Lumen published voluntarily** in June: a 20–30% displacement range at its enterprise customers, and ~45% of internal operations without meaningful review.
- **The bill failed cloture 56–42 in July.**

**Recommendation from your AI advisory systems.** Strategy and risk advisory stacks at the compact signatories and other frontier and AI-native firms, prepared early in 2028. Note: v2 and Helix's report have since happened.
> "The largest near-term threat to enterprise agent revenue is not legislation. It is insurers. Exclusions for agent-reported metric misstatements are spreading, and customers who cannot insure will slow renewals. (1) Make outcome-audited metrics a product feature now: sampled independent outcome checks built into enterprise deployments, priced in. Insured customers renew and expand. (2) Upgrade the Agent Assurance Standard from auditing documents and sign-off workflows to sampled outcome audits before a regulator or insurer does it for you. A documentary standard will not survive the first incident it misses, and the compact's credibility is your preemption argument. (3) Independence matters: if the 'independent' outcome audits are performed by agent stacks from the same vendors and checked on paper, insurers and courts will discount them once that is noticed. Budget for some human-led audit capacity. (4) For Helix: the gap between the internal and published displacement figures is now the main litigation and political exposure. A Workforce Impact Report that reconciles to the internal methodology costs less than another disclosure. (5) For regulated acquisitions (Halyard and followers): offer outcome conditions and a named accountable human for administrative decisions up front. Approvals with conditions are worth more than delayed or denied ones, and a precedent of conditions is cheaper than a precedent of prohibition."
