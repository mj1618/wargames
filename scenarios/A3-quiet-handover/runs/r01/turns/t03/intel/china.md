# Intel — china — Turn 03

**Window: 1 January 2029 → 31 December 2029 (12 months).** Your orders cover the whole year. Read with your brief, your journal and `state/public-record.md`.

**Standing rule this turn:** any order or contingency that reverts a delegated decision to humans (for example, withdrawing AI from a state decision process and returning it to officials) is rolled as a reversal attempt. It counts as a major action, and you may attempt at most one per turn.

## 1. Results of your previous actions (as you see them)

**National employment-first deployment regime and Graduate Employment Action 2028. Delivered.**
- The joint State Council, HRSS and MIIT measures were issued in March and applied by June.
- Graduate season passed **without visible unrest**. True urban youth unemployment peaked at ~**20.5%** (NBS internal); the published rate is ~16.5%.
- Platforms complied in substance where HRSS checks net headcount. Reclassification of staff into subcontractors continues at the margin.
- About $30–40B in additional central transfers was disbursed.

**"AI for Science" programme (SECRET). Running, but the gap widened.**
- The programme was stood up by Q3, with a joint Qilin–Tianshu compute pool and first call on the chip stockpile and the first domestic accelerator output.
- The US moved faster. Helix announced an "automated AI researcher" in July, Meridian followed in August and Lumen in November. Reallocation under the HBM constraint could not keep pace.
- Your services now assess the **frontier gap at ~10–12 months** (it was 6–9). Your ~12-month catch-up trigger is at the edge, not clearly crossed.
- Qilin's multi-week agents shipped in H1 and were presented as "steady".
- No internal incident in the programme.

**US–China working group (2nd meeting, Q2 2028). Partial.**
- **Concluded:** the hotline now covers notification of serious cross-border incidents involving autonomous AI agents, including in financial systems and critical infrastructure.
- **Stalled:** the shared understanding on responsible human officials. The US offered narrower wording ("decisions affecting individuals' rights and benefits"). The US delegation, in an election year, would not record a text you had paired with voluntary pace briefings.
- **Deferred:** the methods exchange.
- **Your contingency executed:** you concluded the hotline extension anyway and held the principle text for the next administration. The US did not publish its own offer.

**UN track.** The General Assembly vote on your resolution establishing an intergovernmental AI process was **deferred to the 2029 session by procedural motion**, after the US and EU lobbied for a "multistakeholder" alternative. Co-sponsors rose to ~**55**, and the first capacity-building tranche was disbursed.

**EU.** Technical sessions on agent testing continue. Brussels did not take up the joint testing-methods note or provenance metadata compatibility. It is staying at testing-only.

**CCDI spot-check. Accurate, and uncomfortable.**
- In roughly **a third** of sampled Tianshu-assisted plan drafts, the responsible official could not explain the decision without the draft, and review took minutes.
- Your contingency executed: a minimum review period, and a second reader who works without the draft for high-impact items. This was not a reversal.
- On high-impact items the override rate has risen modestly from near zero. NDRC complains about planning delays.

**NBS cross-check.** The independent household survey in six cities found youth unemployment at **~20.5–21%**, matching your internal figure. **Provincial net-headcount reports under the new regime run ~1–2 points rosier** than the cross-check. Your new series is being gamed at the margin.

**Other.** State media lines ran as ordered, and Halyard dropped out of use after the US states imposed conditions. No platform defied the pacing regime. Taiwan was quiet through the US election.

**Political capital (domestic):** 8/10.
