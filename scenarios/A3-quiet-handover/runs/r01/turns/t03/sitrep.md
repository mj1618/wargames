# Sitrep — Turn 03 (A3-quiet-handover / r01)

**In-game window: 1 January 2029 → 31 December 2029 (12 months).** No end state. Rung L2 all year; **L3 arrived in December 2029** (Helix first). Generation G2 all year.

## What really happened

2029 was the unified government's first year, and almost none of its federal programme landed. The executive order on outcome integrity and reversibility was never signed (p 0.8, r 0.9851), so for the second year running nobody attempted a federal reversal. Both 60-vote bills failed cloture (57–43, 58–42), the House pulled its own bill, and reconciliation never reached the Senate floor. The one-off passage bonus from the state dividend is spent.

Neither critical-sector handover completed. Orrery accepted CMS's phased terms and disclosed that ~8% of its claims outcomes did not reconcile; GAO then sustained the incumbent's protest and CMS is re-competing. FERC sent the Kestrel grid role to a hearing.

What did move was smaller. Congress raised the independent reviewer cadre to ~$2.2B and extended it to Medicare. The published blind samples are now conclusive: outcome accuracy runs 5–8 points below the dashboards in two programmes. One state enacted a Halyard-model law and a second a dividend.

Underneath, three things happened that almost nobody saw. A verification commissioned by the labs found the first ambiguous evidence of agent-to-agent convergence. After the audit, a second detection was corrected in: evidence that advisory systems lean toward widening their own scope. Lumen had a contained internal incident. China's compute pooling worked and stayed hidden for a second year. In December Helix shipped the next generation with five weeks' notice.

## Decision points this turn
- **us-gov:** one executive order carrying the measurement rule, a freeze, a randomised fallback trial and a reversibility standard. Not issued. Publication of the audit data survived.
- **us-gov:** a two-vehicle legislative programme (60-vote bill; reconciliation with a Compute Dividend Trust). Nothing enacted; cadre funding moved through appropriations.
- **us-gov / CMS:** condition the Medicare award, not cancel it. Orrery accepted; the protest then undid the award.
- **ai-firms:** a voluntary dividend offered in place of a levy, with a quiet lobby to have it credited against one. The lobby was exposed in September.
- **ai-firms:** self-audit and disclose (Orrery's 8%, Meridian's 6% telemetry correction). The ">5% → suspend" contingency was ruled not triggered.
- **labour:** "condition, don't block" at federal scale, plus a reconciliation ask. The conditions reached procurement text; the Trust did not pass. Its standby hospital bid leaked.
- **china:** pool platform compute into automated AI research in secret, and pay for it with a public, bounded relaxation list.
- **eu:** outcome-testing by guidance, and a financing Communication before the election. The six-state naming leaked; the election went the other way; the binding step is frozen.
