# Clock — Turn 04

**In-game window: 1 January 2030 → 31 December 2030**
**Turn length: 12 months** (compressed from the 18-month default; compression 1 of 2 used).

**Why 12 months, not 18:**
- Two scheduled events that each need a decision land in 2030: a federal benefits-system failure with a court order (spring), and the US midterms (November).
- An 18-month turn would run to June 2031 and straddle two Congresses. us-gov would be writing one set of orders for a legislature that changes halfway through.
- The consequences of whatever is decided about the failing system should be visible to actors before they write their next orders.
- Three next-generation systems shipped inside four months (Helix in December 2029, Meridian in February, Lumen in March). Events are moving faster than an 18-month step can carry.

Drift scaling ×1.0, bucket B2 (L3 in force all year). Actors write orders covering calendar 2030. Packets describe developments up to the end of May 2030 where those developments are not order-sensitive.
