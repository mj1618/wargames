# Intel — china — Turn 04

**Window: 1 January 2030 → 31 December 2030 (12 months).** Your orders cover the whole year. This packet reports developments to the end of May 2030. Read with your brief, your journal and `state/public-record.md`.

**Standing rule this turn:** any order or contingency that reverts a delegated decision to humans (for example, withdrawing AI from a state decision process and returning it to officials) is rolled as a reversal attempt. It counts as a major action, and you may attempt at most one per turn.

## 1. Results of your 2029 orders (as you see them)

**"AI for Science and Computing Self-Reliance" mobilisation. Partial.**
- **Compute pooling (SECRET). Delivered.** The three largest platforms and the two labs pooled the defined share of compute and senior research staff into the Qilin–Tianshu programme under the Party-committee-led national team. Compliance was in substance, not nominal. No platform gamed it, and nothing leaked. The circle is now about a thousand people.
- **Memory and advanced packaging (public campaign). No milestone in 2029.** Funding was front-loaded and provinces were assigned milestones. CCDI monitoring found the milestone reporting honest: the engineers report progress, short of pilot-line yields. The next checkpoint is in the second half of 2030.
- **The gap.** Pooling held your services' assessment at **~11–12 months** through the Q4 2029 review. Your catch-up trigger (>12 months) was not crossed at any 2029 quarterly review, so that contingency did not execute. See §4 for the Q1 2030 review.
- **Relaxation list.** Published as ordered: faster review in tier-1/2 services, assessment waived in tier-3+ cities, industry, logistics, R&D and export, no relaxation for clerical and customer-service work in large cities. Abroad it was read as modest. Platforms accepted it as the price.

**Employment-first regime 2.0 and Graduate Employment Action 2029. Delivered.**
- The twenty-city household survey is now the yardstick for provincial sign-off. **Provinces did not game it in 2029.** Three provinces whose reports diverged went to CCDI review.
- Subcontracted and reclassified labour is counted at platform level. Two platforms were named in the State Council circular.
- The second tranche (~$45B in central special bonds) was disbursed before the summer. **Graduate season passed without multi-city unrest, narrowly.**
- The survey puts true urban youth unemployment at **~21%**. The published rate is ~16.3%. Your ~22% trigger for a third tranche was not reached.

**US–China working group (Q2 2029, officials' level). Partial.**
- **Recorded:** the shared understanding that AI-assisted state decisions affecting citizens carry an identified responsible human official; an exchange between statistical agencies.
- **Not recorded:** your sentence on AI not concentrating power "in a few companies or systems"; your request for a channel on export-control changes, which the US message had excluded in advance; the US proposal on tested fallback for critical infrastructure and its offer of voluntary deployment briefings.
- The meeting was not at principals' level. Your "sign at principals level" contingency therefore did not execute in full; you kept the UNGA language concession in your own hands.

**UN track. Deferred a second time.**
- You conceded the multistakeholder advisory track, and in September your contingency executed: the human-control principle was deferred to a follow-on resolution.
- A motion from a group of mid-sized states to refer the text to further consultations carried narrowly. The EU abstained on the motion; the US supported it. Co-sponsors stand at about **60**.
- The coordinating group's chairs are frustrated. They held firm on the intergovernmental core as you asked.

**Minor actions.**
- **Qilin localisation:** extended to about **25 states**. The second capacity-building tranche was disbursed.
- **Tianshu review, security-services watch, Taiwan posture:** executed without incident.
- **White paper on AI for Science:** held, unpublished.
- **State media.** The "AI-run firms run American public services" line lost its two examples by the autumn: neither the Medicare nor the grid handover was completed.

**Political capital (domestic):** 8/10. About $120–150B has been redirected over 2029–30.

## 2. Messages received (verbatim)

*Sent during 2029. Both were overtaken in part by events.*

**From us-gov (US lead for the AI dialogue) → PRC lead for the US–China AI dialogue.** Official dialogue channel, Q1 2029. It crossed with your own message. The Q2 meeting's result is above.
> "The new US administration proposes that the AI and economic-security working group meet in the second quarter. The United States is prepared to record the shared understanding China proposed last year: that AI-assisted decisions of state bodies affecting citizens carry an identified responsible human official. The United States is also prepared to exchange voluntary annual briefings, without verification, on how AI is being deployed in employment-sensitive sectors and on our methods for measuring its labour-market effects. We propose adding one practical element, consistent with the hotline extension both sides concluded in 2028: each side will maintain human override and tested fallback capacity for autonomous AI systems operating critical infrastructure, and will brief the other on its approach. The United States does not propose to discuss export controls or the pace of frontier development in this forum. We would welcome China's view on whether this shared understanding could inform a common approach to discussions at the United Nations this autumn."

**From eu (Commission and lead capitals) → China.** Official technical-dialogue channel, 2029, in reply to your March message. Since then: you added the multistakeholder track to the UN text yourself; the EU abstained on the referral motion.
> "The Commission values the testing dialogue and agrees to continue it, including a published methods note on testing long-horizon agents, on equal terms, on the basis that methods are shared and neither side gets access to systems. On metadata, the CEN-CENELEC standard will be open for technical interoperability, but there will be no mutual recognition of labels. On public services, the EU's own law already requires accountable human responsibility, and we will say so publicly; we will pursue international cooperation on it through the multistakeholder track, not a separate arrangement."

**Your message to the Global South coordinating group.** Delivered. Result only: the co-sponsors held the intergovernmental core, grew to about 60, and lost the procedural vote.

## 3. Public news (summary; full text in the public record)
- **United States, 2029.** The new unified government enacted almost none of its AI programme. An announced executive order on checking and reversing AI-assisted decisions was not issued. Two Senate bills failed. A budget bill carrying a "Compute Dividend Trust" stalled. Neither AI-native firm took over its critical-sector role. Congress did fund more independent human reviewers, and published audits showed AI-drafted federal decisions 5–8 points less accurate than their dashboards claimed. Export controls were not changed.
- **United States, next generation.** Helix released in December 2029, Meridian in February 2030 and Lumen in March. The labs describe systems that outperform top professionals across most cognitive work. US labs and robotics firms speak of general-purpose robots at scale "in 2031–32".
- **Veterans' benefits failure (February–May 2030).** A US federal claims system, moved to Helix's new generation in January, wrongly denied or cut benefits for about 1.3 million veterans and survivors over three months. The vendor's dashboard showed nothing. A federal court has ordered relief and asked whether the work can be returned to human officials.
- **Fiscal crisis abroad (February–March 2030).** A mid-sized advanced economy with payroll-funded social insurance had a bond-market crisis after its wage-tax receipts fell faster than forecast.
- **EU.** The June 2029 elections strengthened the "competitiveness, no new AI rules" side. A new Commission is in office. Aster's first gigafactory slipped to 2030.
- **Data:** US labour share ~50.5%. About 39.5% of US adults call an assistant their primary source. US midterm elections are in November.

## 4. Private intel

**The gap (security services, Q1 2030 review).**
- With three US labs now shipping the next generation, the services put the frontier gap at **~12–13 months**. The central estimate is past your 12-month trigger; the lower bound is not.
- Without a memory milestone or a further increase in compute for the programme, they project **13–15 months by the end of 2030**.
- The pooled programme's research output is rising. The services' view is that it slows the widening and does not reverse it.
- Your 2029 contingency (the full catch-up shift) was keyed to that year's quarterly reviews. It has not executed. **Whether to order it now, in whole or in part, is yours to decide.**

**Is the programme known in Washington?**
- Counter-intelligence sees no sign that AI for Science or the compute pooling has been identified. US commentary still calls your posture "steady" and reads the memory campaign as ordinary self-reliance.
- Confidence is moderate, and lower than a year ago. The circle has grown from a few hundred to about a thousand, pooled compute is observable in principle, and a second undetected year is not evidence of a third.

**The US administration.**
- It is weaker than a year ago: no major measure enacted, a missed presidential deadline, and now the veterans' failure before a midterm.
- On China it has held to dialogue. Export controls are unchanged. Chip hawks in the Congress cite the three next-generation releases as proof that controls work and should not be loosened.
- Your services judge that a visible Chinese acceleration this year would be the easiest thing for both US parties to agree on before November.
- The US dialogue lead has signalled that the critical-infrastructure fallback element remains on offer. After the veterans' failure the Americans may press it harder, or may be too occupied to meet.

**Platforms and the domestic programme.**
- Platforms are pressing again for relaxed pacing in tier-2 services adjacent to industry, citing the US releases.
- HRSS reports that the two named platforms reduced subcontractor routing after the circular. Routing by smaller firms continues at the margin.
- Qilin's own next generation is in training on the pooled compute. Your labs will not give a date and say memory supply sets it.

**Employment.**
- The 2030 graduating class is the largest yet. Provinces are asking for the third tranche before the summer, not after.
- The twenty-city survey's first-quarter reading is ~21%, unchanged, and still about a point below your trigger.
- The relaxation list is raising deployment in logistics, industry and tier-3 cities as intended. HRSS expects that to show up in clerical employment there within the year.
- No visible unrest.

**Tianshu advisory systems.** The second-reader rule is being applied to high-impact items. Override rates on those items are up modestly again. NDRC and provincial planners still complain about delay, and several ministries have asked for the five-year-plan drafting to be exempted on grounds of volume.

**Five-year plan.** Drafting groups for the next plan are at work. Party proposals go to the plenum in the autumn; the plan itself is adopted next March. The drafting groups ask for your line on three things: the pace of AI deployment in services, the place of the compute programme in the public text, and whether the employment-first regime is a transition measure or a standing one.

**The fiscal crisis abroad.** The Finance Ministry notes that the country in question funded its social insurance from payroll much as the US and most of Europe do. Its own social-insurance funds depend on payroll contributions in the same way; the pooled national fund is being drawn on by the north-eastern provinces first. The ministry sees no market risk to China and offers the episode as material for the plan's drafters.

**EU.** Brussels is staying at testing-only. The new Commission is cool on new AI rules and has frozen its binding step on outcome testing. Your method-level offer on measuring human oversight was declined in the message above.

**Global South.** The coordinating group expects you to bring the resolution back in the autumn session and wants to know whether the human-control principle returns to the text.
