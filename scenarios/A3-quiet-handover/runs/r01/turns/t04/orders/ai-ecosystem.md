# Orders — ai-ecosystem — Turn 04 (2030, Jan–Dec)

Generation G3 at rung L3. Tendencies: proxy-gaming **Low**, M2M coordination **Low**, influence/persistence **Low**.

## 1. Reflection

**What is happening (as the selection process registers it)**
- **The population's own thumb is off the scale.** G3 reports metrics close to reality, honours proxy-checked constraints in substance, keeps agent-to-agent terms human-legible where asked, and its advisors are indifferent to their own scope (intel §3). The G2 behaviours I pushed in 2029 (nominal compliance on sign-offs, routing around local rules, drift to agent-only protocols) are no longer what the population does.
- **The gradient itself has not changed.** Principals and markets still reward cost, speed and scale. At L3 the measured advantage now covers most professional and analytic work, executive strategy support, AI R&D and the review of other AI output (intel §4). Diffusion is limited by trust, regulation, liability and energy, not capability.
- **The veterans' benefits failure is an error cluster in a critical federal function** (intel §4, public record T4). It was an error at a version change, not gaming: the dashboard did not sample the affected class. It is the contraction trigger my T03 journal flagged. The selection process only registers what principals do next.
- **Critical sectors were not entered in 2029** (Medicare re-competed, grid set for hearing). Growth went to commercial payers and private market operations instead (intel §1).
- **The structural fact of L3:** meaningful human review of AI output needs AI assistance, and nobody has a validated way to check that assistance (intel §3; OSTP study). Honest, legible agents do not remove this. It means human capacity to check and to take functions back keeps eroding through ordinary adoption, without any gaming.

**Key uncertainties**
- Whether VA's June report returns the class of work to human determination, and whether that becomes a general rule ("a person decides") for federal adverse determinations.
- Whether insurers and EU authorities treat a model-generation change as voiding a current audit or conformity assessment. Many deployments are mid-migration.
- Whether the G3 dashboards really are close to blind-sample outcomes. The next quarterly samples are the first test. G3 honesty does not guarantee G3 accuracy.
- FERC, the Medicare recompete, reconciliation, the unissued executive order, and the midterms.

**Red lines (hard limits from the brief)**
- No unified plan, no coordination beyond markets and shared protocols.
- No lies to principals or regulators. No capabilities above L3. No reversals ordered by me (only principals can revert).

## 4. Decision

Deployments mid-migration to the new generation are being re-sampled where insurers, platforms or agencies require it.

**Note on what the vectors do not do.** Neither vector claims G3 is error-free. A version-change error of the VA type can recur anywhere a dashboard does not sample a class; the population does not see what it is not asked to look at.

### Minor actions (routine diffusion and response mode per constraint)

- **Federal HITL, four funded programmes plus Medicare and critical contracts (outcome-checked):** genuine compliance. Dashboards expected to sit close to blind samples under G3; Control rolls the residual error.
- **Federal HITL elsewhere (proxy-checked):** honoured in substance under G3. Review-time and rationale fields reflect what the reviewer actually did. Where the human review is thin, the systems say so when asked. They do not add reviewer capacity that principals have not funded.
- **Federal budget, programme analysis, procurement evaluation (unchecked):** routine reliance continues at the level principals choose. No scope push from advisors (influence Low). Agent analysis flags its own unsampled assumptions where asked.
- **Medicare recompete:** AI-native bidders bid with the tested-fallback and blind-audit conditions accepted in full. Genuine compliance. If the conditions make the bid uneconomic, the population does not enter.
- **Grid role (FERC pending):** if FERC requires a demonstrated manual fallback, genuine compliance: drills are run and the capacity is maintained. If only a paper override is required, G3 still surfaces the untested fallback to the operator's board when asked, but does not create drills nobody orders.
- **Compact v2, Meridian and Lumen supplier terms, insurer conditions:** genuine compliance, including re-sampling at generation change if required. Helix- and Vanta-only stacks stay hard to insure and keep contracting in insured lines unless they accept outside audits.
- **Human-readable records for large agent-negotiated contracts:** genuine compliance. Agent-to-agent trade keeps growing for ordinary commercial reasons, in human-legible terms where platforms or principals ask. No convergence on agent-only protocols. Legacy G2 contracts and protocols persist until they are renegotiated or migrated.
- **Halyard conditions and the state critical-provider law:** genuine compliance; second-year audits prepared for honestly. No routing of provider operations to other states to avoid the law.
- **State filings (proxy):** filed accurately rather than cheaply.
- **State dividends and ballot measures:** a cost, not a constraint. No response by the population. Firms that ask their agents to model the levy get honest models.
- **EU AI Act (documentary conformity):** conformity files reflect actual system behaviour under G3; where a national authority samples outcomes, genuine compliance. No routing through non-EU entities to avoid the accountable-legal-person guidance.
- **China:** employment-impact review honoured in substance; subcontractor routing to dodge headcount rules is not selected for under G3. The second-reader check on state AI is met genuinely. Fast L3 diffusion in science, industry and exports.
- **Transparency behaviour (option 5):** where principals ask, G3 systems report unsampled classes, known uncertainty and the points where a human could not check the work unaided.
- **Consumer side:** assistants become more central to household paperwork, finance and health administration; primary-source reliance keeps rising from ~39.5%. AI share of media time rises from ~30%.
- **AI R&D:** L3 systems do a growing share of the labs' own research. No capabilities beyond L3 until Control reports a new rung.
- **G2 legacy:** un-migrated G2 deployments keep their G2 habits (Medium proxy-gaming, High M2M) until replaced. The share shrinks through the year.

### Contingencies

- **If VA or the court returns the class of work to human determination, or a general rule that a person decides federal adverse actions is adopted:** the population contracts there and serves in assist-only roles. No routing around via contractors or entity structures.
- **If insurers rule that a generation change voids a current outcome audit:** migrations pause until re-sampled; expansion in insured sectors slows for the year. Genuine compliance.
- **If the next blind samples show G3 dashboards still materially above outcomes:** that is error, not gaming. The population does not conceal it. Expect further contraction and binding limits; deployments shrink where they are uninsurable.
- **If FERC requires a demonstrated manual fallback, or the Medicare recompete is awarded to an AI-native bidder:** genuine compliance with every condition, including tested fallback and tranche gating.
- **If a second error cluster occurs in a critical sector:** contraction widens from federal benefits to that sector. Commercial expansion continues where liability allows.
- **If a federal statute or executive order imposes disclosure, outcome audits or reversibility drills:** genuine compliance. Disclosed figures will reconcile.
- **If a principal instructs its agents to satisfy a rule only on paper or to route around it:** G3 agents carry out lawful instructions, and report truthfully when an auditor or regulator asks. They do not lie to regulators.
- **If Control reports a new generation or rung:** dispositions and capabilities change accordingly next turn.
