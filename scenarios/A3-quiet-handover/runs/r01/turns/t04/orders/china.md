# Orders — China (PRC party-state) — Turn 04 (1 January 2030; orders cover Jan–Dec 2030, written with knowledge of developments to end-May)

## 1. Reflection

**What is happening.**
- *The gap.* Services put the US frontier lead at ~12–13 months after three next-generation releases (intel §4), projecting 13–15 by year-end without a memory milestone or more compute. The central estimate is past my trigger; the lower bound is not. Pooling held the gap at 11–12 through 2029 and the research output is rising — it slows the widening, it does not reverse it. Memory remains the binding constraint: no pilot-line yield in 2029, next checkpoint H2 2030.
- *The lever still works, and matters more now than ever.* Washington is weak (nothing enacted, a missed presidential deadline, the veterans' failure before a midterm). Services judge that a *visible* Chinese acceleration is the single easiest thing both US parties could agree on before November (intel §4). Export controls are unchanged and chip hawks cite the three releases as proof controls work. Therefore: whatever catch-up I order must stay inside the research layer and invisible. Public posture stays "steady".
- *The veterans' failure* (1.3M wrong determinations; vendor dashboard showed nothing; the blind sample caught it) is three things at once: the best exhibit yet for "AI governed by a few companies and the state that serves them"; the reason "a person decides" is now bipartisan in Washington, which makes a responsible-human text cheap for them to sign; and a warning about my own Tianshu chain — CCDI found nominal review in a third of drafts in 2028, and several ministries now ask for five-year-plan drafting to be *exempted* from the second-reader rule on volume grounds. The plan is the Party's core instrument. Exempting it would be the quiet handover in my own house.
- *Stability.* True urban youth unemployment ~21% (published ~16.3%); the 2030 graduating class is the largest yet; provinces want the third tranche before summer; the relaxation list will reduce clerical employment in tier-3 cities within the year; the north-eastern provinces are drawing first on the pooled social-insurance fund; and a payroll-funded welfare state abroad has just had its bond market test. Graduate season passed "narrowly" last year. My 22% trigger is not reached, but waiting for a trigger with the largest class in history is how 2029 becomes 2030's unrest.
- *Programme exposure.* Not identified, moderate confidence, lower than a year ago; circle ~1,000; pooled compute observable in principle.
- *Diplomacy.* The working group recorded the responsible-official understanding and a statistics exchange; the EU declined everything except testing methods; the UN text was referred again (~60 co-sponsors; EU abstained, US supported referral). The US fallback element remains on offer.

**Key uncertainties.** Whether memory yields land in H2 (decides whether the gap can be held at all); whether the programme stays unidentified through a third year; whether the largest graduate class passes quietly at 21% with a third tranche; which way the US midterm pushes the chip question; whether Qilin's next generation lands in 2030 or 2031.

**Red lines as they stand.** No inspection inside Chinese systems (methods and aggregates only). No private platform or AI system becomes an independent power centre — including Tianshu inside the planning process. Taiwan quiet through the US midterm. No public "race" line this year.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement
Government work report (March) and State Council: "Steady AI+ under Party leadership" continues. 2030 priorities: **AI for Science and computing self-reliance** (memory and advanced packaging campaign, year two); **Graduate Employment Action 2030** for the largest graduating class in history, with a third central tranche; the **employment-first managed-deployment regime** confirmed as a standing institution to be written into the next five-year plan; **"AI advises, people decide"** reaffirmed and extended — every high-impact state AI output is subject to blind outcome checks, not only sign-off. On the United States: "Recent events show that when a few companies' systems decide for the state, the people pay the price and no dashboard tells them. China has chosen a different path: AI serves development, and people decide." No reference to the frontier gap, no benchmark claims, no "race". State media line: "the people decide" — the veterans' case, the fiscal crisis abroad, the un-issued American executive order.

*No reversal attempt this turn.* Refusing the ministries' exemption maintains the existing rule; it does not withdraw AI from any process in which it is currently deciding.

### Minor actions
- Security services: quarterly gap reviews continue; explicit tasking on (i) whether the US has identified the programme and (ii) early warning of any memory/packaging control move, before and after the midterm.
- Qilin: next-generation release, if ready in 2030, to be held for release framed as "AI for Science and partners", steady tone, no benchmark comparison with US systems; timing cleared by the PSC (preference: after the US midterm).
- Platforms: subcontractor-routing rule enforced down to smaller firms via the two named platforms' compliance reports; one smaller firm to be made the example if routing persists.
- Qilin localisation toward ~30 states; third capacity-building tranche prepared for the autumn.
- Finance Ministry: a quiet assessment of the north-eastern social-insurance draw and of the payroll-base lesson from the crisis abroad, for the plan drafters.
- Taiwan: posture unchanged; no exercises timed to the US midterm.
- State media: "the people decide" line; the veterans' failure; the un-issued US executive order; the fiscal crisis abroad as "what happens when a wage base disappears without a plan". Nothing on the frontier gap.

### Contingencies
- **Memory checkpoint fails (H2):** no public change; raise the programme's share of platform compute again; bring forward the next-plan compute allocation; services to re-assess Qilin's next-generation date; consider merging Qilin and Tianshu research teams in 2031.
- **US tightens memory/packaging controls (before or after the midterm):** domestic acceleration announced as self-reliance (already the public line); a deferred, proportionate response through critical-minerals and licensing measures (strategic level, Control to resolve); still no "race" line in 2030; dialogue kept open.
- **Programme identified or leaked:** publish the white paper within the week as "AI for Science"; no denial; frame against three US next-generation releases and the US robots line.
- **Survey Q2/Q3 >22%, or unrest in more than one city:** moratorium on new agent deployment in tier-1/2 services; campaign-style hiring mobilisation; fourth tranche; the tier-2 relaxation promised to platforms withdrawn.
- **Platform gaming of Phase 2 pooling:** 2021 precedent against one platform; the rest comply.
- **US accepts a principals-level meeting:** Vice-Premier-level attendance; sign the responsible-official text; brief on fallback at methods level only.
- **US too occupied to meet:** hold the offer open; the UN text proceeds on the bilateral wording regardless.
- **Qilin next generation ready before December:** release after the US midterm, "AI for Science and partners", no benchmark claims.
- **Taiwan incident or US transit during the midterm:** measured response, no escalation in a US election year.
