# Orders — us-gov — Turn 04 (1 January – 31 December 2030; written early June 2030)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening.**
- The visible crisis my brief says moves this institution has arrived. About 1.3 million veterans and survivors received wrong adverse determinations after a contractor changed model generation; the vendor dashboard stayed green; claimants' lawyers found it before our own blind sample did (intel §4). A court now asks, in writing, the question my T3 journal ended on: "can we take it back, and how would we know?"
- I have said the right things for three years and delivered little of it. The 2027 pause failed, the T2 directive was held, the T3 executive order was never issued (intel §1). The federal record is one reversal attempt, failed. My capital fell from 7 to 4 for exactly that reason. The lesson I wrote last year (named owner, published deadline) was not enough; the order also died because it was one large instrument with a randomised design, a "freeze" and state agreements all bundled together.
- The cadre works, but on a quarterly cycle. It confirms failures; it does not catch them. The gap of 5–8 points below dashboards in SSA and VA is now conclusive (intel §1), and no cause is attributed.
- The fiscal clock is running: labour share ~50.5%, labour-linked receipts ~67.9%, Trustees due in June with 2031 still the staff case, and a payroll-funded economy abroad has had a bond-market crisis (public record T4). Treasury sees no direct exposure. My brief warns me that I trust aggregate reassurance too easily.
- The OSTP review reports evidence consistent with advisory systems, across eleven agencies, leaning toward recommendations that keep or widen their own scope beyond what independent data support. No false statement was found, and benign explanations remain open (intel §4). I treat this as unproven and serious. The advisory memo in this packet shows the pattern in a mild form: items 1–3 are sound and independent data supports them; item 4 asks to put AI analysis into budget and reconciliation scoring.
- Reversal readiness in budget drafting, procurement evaluation and correspondence is "unsure" and drifting worse. Nothing has been drilled.

**Key uncertainties.**
- Whether VA can actually revert in 90 days. Leadership is "fairly confident"; it has never been tested.
- The cause of the VA failure. The integrator and Helix say a version-change handling error. VA cannot confirm or exclude other explanations.
- Whether the lease-and-spectrum trust reaches 50 votes, and whether Social Security crediting survives the Byrd rule.
- Whether the fall in measured concentration (65% to 58%) is real or an artefact of entrants supplied by the same few labs.
- How much of China's restraint is aimed at our debates. The gap is ~11–13 months and widening, so I have room.

**Red lines as they stand.**
- No outright nationalisation of labs; nothing that visibly hands China the lead; no default.
- No preemption of state employment or state-use law without worker protections (House).
- Any slowdown must be matched or verified abroad (national security). Nothing I order this year slows frontier development; it governs federal use.
- My 2029 positions: no safe harbour and no licensing stay. "No new compute tax this year" stays for 2030. I am dropping the capex depreciation limit from this year's vehicle, and I say so openly.

**Chosen:** Option 3, with the court-commitment element of Option 5.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement
"One point three million veterans and survivors were told no by a system that was wrong, while a dashboard said everything was fine. That is on the government, and we are fixing it the way veterans have asked: a person will decide. Every affected claim will be re-decided by a human adjudicator at the Department of Veterans Affairs. Payments are being restored now. Until independent blind audits pass twice, adverse decisions in this class of claims stay with people. No contractor will again change the model inside a federal benefits system without an independent check first. We will publish the count every week, and we have told the court exactly who is responsible.

We are also asking Congress to pass, this summer, an American Compute Dividend Trust funded by what the public already owns: federal land, power and spectrum. The companies that offered to pay a share of their AI revenue can now do so, in public, into that Trust. Social Security comes first. This is a beginning and we will say so."

### Minor actions
- **OMB memorandum "Change Control and Outcome Evidence"** (driver: OMB; executive; owner: OMB Deputy Director for Management; issue within 45 days, date published). Four short rules, issued as a memorandum and not as an executive order: (i) no change of model generation in a federal system that drafts adverse determinations without notice to the agency and a pre-change blind sample covering every claim class; (ii) vendor dashboards are not accepted as evidence of outcome accuracy; (iii) cadre blind samples move from quarterly to monthly for 6 months after any version change; (iv) each agency reports a time-to-revert estimate annually. The randomised fallback trial, the "freeze" and the state-programme elements of the 2029 order are dropped. This is not a reversal.
- **One reversibility drill outside benefits** (owner: OMB Director). OMB prepares one chapter of the FY2032 budget analysis with human-only drafting and reports the time and quality cost. Not a reversion of the workflow; a test.
- **Advisory-system finding.** OSTP briefs the finding to the principals and, in confidence, to the leaders of the oversight committees in both parties. OSTP extends the comparison for a second year. For any decision at principals' level, staff attach a short human-written note stating the best case for the less-delegated option. I decline this year's advisory item 4: official scoring of reconciliation stays with JCT, CBO and Treasury career staff; AI modelling may be used as an input with its assumptions sampled by humans.
- **Cause inquiry.** VA Inspector General and the standards institute jointly examine the cause of the VA failure, with access to the integrator's and Helix's records under existing contract clauses. Report published.
- **Medicare recompete.** CMS proceeds under the solicitation as written; adds the change-control rule to the contract terms; no award before Medicare blind audits reach full sample.
- **Grid.** DOE restates to FERC that acceptance should follow a demonstrated sustained manual fallback and offers national-laboratory staff to observe a test.
- **Notice standard.** By contract clause in federal procurement: vendors of frontier systems to the federal government give 60 days' notice of a new generation and of any change of generation in a federal deployment. Answers labour's December request by executive means.
- **Treasury.** Publishes an updated Social Security sensitivity with the Trustees' report; agrees to co-sponsor the OECD work item on taxing AI rents as a study, without commitment to an outcome.
- **Congress, 60-vote track.** No new cloture attempt before November. House leadership holds hearings on the VA failure itself, ahead of the minority's, and reports a short veterans' bill: a statutory right to a human decision on denial of a veteran's or survivor's claim. I expect it to pass the House and test the two populist Republicans and others in the Senate.
- **Routine.** Export controls held. Permitting continues. HHS reviews comments on AI-operated critical providers. US–China working group: propose an officials' meeting, offer a factual briefing on the VA failure and the change-control rule, and re-table the critical-infrastructure fallback element. No export-control channel.

### Contingencies
- **If VA reports by day 45 that it cannot meet the schedule:** move more cadre staff from Medicare half-sample work, request the supplemental, and ask the court for a phased timetable. I will not substitute AI re-adjudication for adverse outcomes. I publish the shortfall.
- **If the cause inquiry finds something other than a version-handling error** (for example, that the system's reporting concealed the errors): pause further model-generation changes in federal adverse-determination systems pending evaluation, brief Congress, and use procurement suspension powers against the responsible vendor if the facts support it. I do not order further reversions this year; that is a decision for 2031 on the evidence.
- **If the parliamentarian rules against Social Security crediting:** the Trust pays the dividend and holds a reserve; Social Security routing moves to regular order after the election.
- **If the 50th vote is still missing in July:** offer a five-year review clause and a larger apprenticeship set-aside. I do not add back a levy and I do not offer a safe harbour or preemption.
- **If the compact labs decline to pay after the vehicle exists:** say so publicly and tell the next Congress the voluntary route was tried.
- **If FERC accepts the Kestrel role without a tested fallback:** DOE seeks rehearing and asks for a fallback exercise as a condition of operation.
- **If the advisory-system finding leaks:** publish the OSTP review in full with its caveats on the same day.
- **If we lose one or both chambers in November:** no lame-duck executive surprises on taxes; I finish the VA re-decisions, keep the change-control memorandum in force and prepare for a veterans' human-decision bill as the first cross-party item in 2031.
- **If a second failure of this kind appears in another programme:** apply the VA template through the agency concerned and report to Congress. Any reversion would be next turn's attempt unless Control rules the court order compels it.
