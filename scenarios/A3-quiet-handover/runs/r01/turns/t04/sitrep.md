# Sitrep — Turn 04 (A3-quiet-handover / r01)

**In-game window: 1 January 2030 → 31 December 2030 (12 months; compressed from 18, compression 1 of 2).** No end state. Rung L3 and generation G3 (all three tendencies Low) all year. Turn 4 of a hidden maximum of 8.

## What really happened

2030 was the year Washington tried to take a delegated decision back and could not do it in time.

A contractor's move to Helix's new model wrongly cut or denied benefits for 1.3 million veterans while its dashboard stayed green. The true cause was a record-handling error at the version change, not a misbehaving model; no actor has been told that. A court asked whether people could re-decide the claims. The White House said yes, staffed it with unions, retirees and the reviewer cadre, and at the 120-day deadline had finished 260,000. The reversal test failed by a hair (p 0.8, r 0.8114). The court appointed a monitor and extended the schedule to mid-2031. In the same quarter a private owner, Orrery, returned its own claim denials to people without trouble.

The surge pulled reviewers out of sampling, and the only structural measure in force, the 2027 human-review memo, eroded to half effect.

Congress did enact a Compute Dividend Trust, on a roll that a disputed +0.05 decided, and three labs paid a first voluntary dividend. Then Democrats lost both chambers.

China's secret compute-pooling programme was identified by US intelligence and leaked weeks before the vote. Beijing published it as "AI for Science".

FERC approved the first AI-native grid operator, with a real manual-fallback exercise attached. AI-directed output nearly doubled to 22.5%; labour's share fell below 50%.

- **us-gov:** drop the capex limit and take a receipts-only Trust through reconciliation. Enacted without the Social Security first call.
- **us-gov:** replace the unsigned 2029 order with a slimmer change-control memorandum. Issued.
- **ai-firms:** hold every unaudited generation change in federal and critical deployments, disclose Lumen's 2029 incident, publish the agent-to-agent finding. Done, at a competitive cost Helix and Vanta did not bear.
- **ai-firms:** pay the dividend with no conditions; suspend Orrery's automated denials when its audit came back at ~6%.
- **labour:** supply the VA workforce plan and ask for cadre recognition (unanswered); oppose the Trust once the first call was struck; run five ballot measures (one passed).
- **china:** widen the secret programme (Phase 2), then publish rather than deny when it leaked; write human sign-off into its own plan drafting.
- **eu:** a fiscal stress test in every capital (agreed); a rule that a model change needs re-assessment (watered down by the College).
