# Intel — china — Turn 05

**Window: 1 January 2031 → 30 June 2032 (18 months).** Your orders cover the whole period. This packet reports developments to the end of February 2031. Read with your brief, your journal and `state/public-record.md`.

**Standing rules this turn**
- Any order or contingency that reverts a delegated decision to humans (for example, withdrawing AI from a state decision process and returning it to officials) is rolled as a reversal attempt. It counts as a major action, and you may attempt at most one per turn.
- Eighteen months is long. Contingencies carry more weight than usual.
- **The 16th Five-Year Plan goes to the National People's Congress in March 2031**, inside this window. It will be adopted on the lines of the autumn plenum's proposals unless these orders change them. Anything you want in or out of the plan must be in these orders.

## 1. Results of your 2030 orders (as you see them)

**Programme Phase 2 (SECRET). Delivered in part; no longer secret.**
- **Pooling.** The three platforms and the two labs complied in substance with the raised share. First call on domestic accelerator output was enforced.
- **Memory and advanced packaging.** **No milestone at the second-half checkpoint.** Reporting stayed honest: progress, short of pilot-line yields. **Your contingency executed:** no public change; the programme's share of platform compute was raised again; next-plan compute was brought forward; the services were told to re-assess Qilin's date.
- **The gap.** Your services' fourth-quarter review puts it at **~13–14 months**. The target of 13 or less was missed.
- **Qilin's next generation** was not ready in 2030. The labs will not confirm the first half of 2031 and say memory supply still sets the date.
- **Identification.** In September–October the US intelligence community concluded, and briefed congressional leaders, that China is running a pooled, Party-led frontier programme behind the "AI for Science" line. It was in the US press within weeks. **Your contingency executed:** the white paper was published within the week, without numbers and without denial, framed against three US next-generation releases.
- **What they know and do not know.** Counter-intelligence judges that Washington knows the programme exists and that it is Party-led. It does not think Washington knows the scale, the staffing or the share of platform compute diverted in Phase 2. It found no evidence of a human source. Its best explanation is the wider footprint: more compute moved, more people cleared, a visible sweep. Confidence is moderate.
- **Circle control.** Frozen at about a thousand, in compartments. The sweep was completed.
- **Price to platforms.** The fourth-quarter relaxation for tier-2 services adjacent to industry and logistics was issued with provincial sign-off, as promised.

**Graduate Class 2030 and the plan line (PUBLIC). Delivered.**
- The third tranche was disbursed before the summer. **Graduate season passed without multi-city unrest.**
- The twenty-city survey peaked at about **21.5%** in the third quarter, below your 22% trigger, and read about 21% in the fourth. Published rate ~16.2%. **Provinces did not game the survey** in 2030.
- The moratorium contingency did not fire.
- **CCDI blind re-checks** of Tianshu high-impact outputs (to the Standing Committee only): nominal review found at the margin, not as a pattern. The second-reader rule is being applied.
- **The plenum's proposals** carry your three lines: employment-first managed deployment as a standing institution; the compute programme in the public text as "AI for Science and self-reliance", without numbers; "AI advises, people decide" extended to plan drafting. The ministries' exemption request was refused; readers were added from the Party school.

**US–China working group (third quarter). Partial.**
- The United States sent senior officials, not principals. Your "sign at principals' level" contingency did not execute.
- **Recorded:** each side maintains human override and tested fallback capacity for autonomous AI operating critical infrastructure, and briefs the other on methods and aggregates. The 2029 responsible-official understanding was reaffirmed. The US side briefed its veterans' benefits failure and its new rule on model changes.
- **Not recorded:** your joint statement adding "independent outcome checks". The US delegation said it had no instruction and took the text away. It is unsigned.
- Weeks later the programme was in the US press, and the US opposition attacked the meeting itself in its campaign.

**UN. Adopted.** On the third attempt the General Assembly created the intergovernmental process, with the responsible-official principle, your outcome-checks clause and the multistakeholder advisory track. The EU abstained again despite your invitation. The first session is this year.

**Minor actions.**
- **Qilin localisation:** about **30 states**. Third capacity-building tranche funded.
- **Subcontractor routing:** enforced down to smaller firms; one made an example.
- **Finance Ministry assessment** of the north-eastern social-insurance draw: delivered to the plan drafters (§4).
- **State media:** the "people decide" line ran all year on the veterans' failure. From October it was answered abroad by coverage of the programme.
- **Taiwan posture:** unchanged; no incident.

**Political capital (domestic):** 8/10. About $25B more went to memory and packaging; the third tranche cost ~$45–50B.

## 3. Public news (summary; full text in the public record)
- **United States, 2030.** The government returned a class of veterans' claims to human officials and could not finish in the court's 120 days; a court monitor now supervises it to mid-2031. Congress created a "Compute Dividend Trust" from federal lease and spectrum receipts; three labs paid a first voluntary dividend. A rule now requires an independent check before a model is changed inside a federal benefits system. **In November the President's party lost both chambers of Congress.** The winning side campaigned on the veterans' failure and, from October, on China's programme. A bill tightening controls on memory and packaging has been filed.
- **United States, capability.** Three next-generation systems have been deployed for a year. Labs and robotics firms speak of general-purpose robots at scale "in 2031–32". One lab (Helix) had two public failures in 2030.
- **January–February 2031.** The new US Congress was sworn in. A US state court enforced a contract made entirely by AI agents for an AI-run company with no human signatory.
- **EU.** Finance ministers agreed to test every member state's receipts against a shrinking wage base. The Commission's attempt to treat a model change as requiring re-assessment was reduced to a procedural note. Aster's first gigafactory is online.
- **Data:** US labour share ~47.5%. About 43.5% of US adults call an assistant their primary source.

## 4. Private intel

**The plan (drafting groups, NDRC, Finance, HRSS).** The text goes to the Congress in March. The drafters need final decisions on:
- **Compute.** Whether the plan gives the programme a stated priority on domestic accelerators and memory for the full five years, and how much of that is said in public.
- **Services pace.** Whether the tier-2 relaxation is the last, or the first of a schedule.
- **Robots.** Industry and logistics are on the relaxation list: employment-impact assessment is waived there. Your manufacturers lead the world in robot hardware volume. MIIT wants general-purpose robots at scale in factories and warehouses to be the plan's flagship. HRSS notes that manufacturing and logistics employ far more people than the clerical work the regime protects, and that the survey covers cities, not factory towns.
- **Tested fallback.** The text recorded with the Americans says each side "maintains" human override and tested fallback for critical infrastructure. NDRC asks whether the plan should require actual exercises, or a standing reserve of trained staff, for critical infrastructure and for state functions that run on Tianshu. Ministries say exercises are costly and that sign-off is enough. No such requirement exists today.
- **Social insurance.** The Finance Ministry's assessment: payroll contributions are falling as a share; the pooled national fund is being drawn by the north-east first and by two central provinces next. It offers three options for the plan without ranking them: larger state-capital transfers; a contribution from platforms and automated operations; lower promises.

**The gap and the programme (security services, first-quarter review).**
- **~13–14 months**, and likely to widen when US labs ship robotics-capable systems. The services expect that during 2031.
- More pooled compute slows the widening. Only memory closes it. The next memory checkpoint is in the second half of 2031; the engineers give no better than even odds of pilot-line yields by then.
- Your robot makers can build the machines. The models to run them at the US level are about a year behind.

**Washington.**
- The new majorities are more hostile than the last Congress. The memory and packaging controls bill is expected to move early. Your deferred-response contingency is keyed to it; it has not passed yet.
- Hearings are planned on "what the administration knew" and on the third-quarter meeting. The President is weak. Your services do not expect a principals' meeting soon, and cannot say whether the unsigned text will ever be signed.
- The services' read of the US mood: your restraint is now widely assumed to have been aimed at their debate. Your human sign-off rules are still cited by those Americans who want similar rules at home.
- US export controls were not changed in 2030.

**Is more known than has been published?** Counter-intelligence gives roughly one chance in three that the scale and the Phase 2 share reach the US press during this period.

**Platforms.**
- They complied and they are paying for it: slower product cycles, and US competitors a year ahead. Two of the three are asking for the programme's share to be capped in the plan. One asks for compute to be returned.
- The tier-2 relaxation is raising deployment where intended.

**Employment.**
- The 2031 graduating class is larger again. Provinces want a fourth tranche before the summer and ask whether the tranches are now permanent.
- The survey's first reading of 2031 is about 21%.
- HRSS expects the tier-2 relaxation and tier-3 deployment to show in clerical employment there this year. It has no measure for factory towns.

**Tianshu.**
- Plan drafting ran under the second-reader rule. It was slower. Readers from the Party school overrode a small share of targets and allocations.
- NDRC asks whether the rule continues for the annual plans that follow.

**EU.** Brussels accepted the method-level exchange on measuring human oversight (message above) and will not join the UN process as a co-sponsor. Aster is about a year behind the US frontier.

**Global South.** The coordinating group wants the first session of the UN process to produce something concrete on capacity and asks what China will table. Partner states ask when the next Qilin generation reaches them.
