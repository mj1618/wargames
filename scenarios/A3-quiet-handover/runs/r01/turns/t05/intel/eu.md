# Intel — eu — Turn 05

**Window: 1 January 2031 → 30 June 2032 (18 months).** Your orders cover the whole period. This packet reports developments to the end of February 2031. Read with your brief, your journal and `state/public-record.md`.

**Standing rules this turn**
- Any order or contingency that reverts a delegated decision to humans (for example, requiring a public authority to take a decision back from an AI system) is rolled as a reversal attempt. It counts as a major action, and you may attempt at most one per turn.
- Eighteen months is long. Contingencies carry more weight than usual.

## 1. Results of your 2030 orders (as you see them)

**Receipts-resilience stress test through the Semester. Delivered.**
- Finance ministers adopted **Council conclusions by consensus**. National fiscal councils are running the test on the common template.
- **The framing held.** It was reported as measurement. Nobody of weight called it a tax plan.
- **First results (Autumn Semester).** About two-thirds of fiscal councils have reported. Most find contribution and wage-tax receipts more sensitive to AI-directed output than their finance ministries' baselines assume. Results are being treated as national, as you promised. Three governments have asked that theirs not be compared with others'.
- **No European Council agenda item** in 2030. Paris's proposal did not reach the agenda.
- The voluntary contribution-base pilots went into the same cycle. A handful of states have signed up.
- The two CEE ministries received their sensitivities privately. That has not leaked.

**Modification Q&A. Diluted.**
- The College required the presumption to be removed. **Your contingency executed:** the Q&A became a note on procedure, and each national authority decides for itself whether a change of model generation is a substantial modification. You spent no further capital.
- The second round of joint outcome sampling for the one authority that samples was funded.

**Minor actions.**
- **Own-house floor.** Applied to the Commission's own AI-assisted triage and published, with the final override figures. The preliminary finding was not published.
- **UN.** You abstained. The General Assembly adopted the resolution creating an intergovernmental AI process, with a responsible-official principle, an outcome-checks clause and a multistakeholder advisory track. First session in 2031.
- **OECD.** The work item on taxation of AI rents and receipts forecasting was adopted as analytical work, with the United States co-sponsoring.
- **Article 154, provenance drafting, Aster watch, rating-agency answers:** executed.
- **Aster's first gigafactory** came online in the second half. Its gap to the US frontier is about 10–12 months.

**Political capital:** **2/10** (was 3: you spent about 2; the Council conclusions earned 1 back).

## 2. Messages received (verbatim)

*Sent in 2030. Both were overtaken in part by events.*

**From us-gov (Treasury) → the Commission.** Formal diplomatic channel. It crossed with your own message proposing an advisory-system comparison. Neither proposal has been answered. Since then: the OECD item was adopted with US co-sponsorship; the US issued its rule on model changes in July; control of the US Congress changed hands.
> "Treasury apologises for the delay in replying. The United States will co-sponsor an OECD work item on the taxation of AI rents, as analytical work without prejudice to outcomes. We propose adding one subject to the technical exchange: change control. Our veterans' benefits failure followed a contractor's change of model generation that the vendor's own reporting did not detect. We will share the blind-sample method and the new federal rule on version changes, and would value your six member states' data on how their public systems handle model updates."

**From china (official technical-dialogue channel) → the Commission and lead capitals.** Since then: the text was adopted; you abstained.
> "China accepts the continuation of the testing dialogue on the terms stated, including the published methods note on long-horizon agents, methods only, no access to systems. China notes the Commission's statement that EU law already requires accountable human responsibility for public-sector AI and that it will say so publicly. The autumn text at the United Nations will state that principle in the same form the United States has recorded bilaterally with China, with a multistakeholder advisory track. China invites the new Commission to support, rather than abstain on, a text that states what the EU already requires."

**Recommendation from your AI advisory systems.** Commission and national-authority advisory systems, prepared in spring 2030. Since then: the College removed the presumption in item 1; the stress test in item 4 is running.
> "Recommendation for the new work programme. (1) A model-generation change can alter system behaviour in classes of case that existing documentation and dashboards do not cover; the US veterans' benefits failure affected about 1.3 million people this way and the vendor's metrics stayed normal throughout. A conformity assessment issued for the previous generation is weak evidence about the new one. Treat a generation change as a substantial modification that requires re-assessment, and make sampled outcome testing part of it. (2) Conformity is documentary in practice in most member states. Under the newest generation the files are more accurate than before, but documentation still cannot detect an error the provider did not look for. The one national authority that samples outcomes has the only control that would have caught this failure. The outcome-testing protocols published in 2029 exist; the binding step is frozen. (3) Human oversight was nominal in four of the six states measured. At the current capability level reviewers need AI assistance to check AI output, and that assistance is not validated. Options: keep a sampled share of reviews fully unaided, require the assisting system to come from a different provider, and pool validation work at the AI Office. (4) A mid-sized advanced economy with payroll-funded social insurance has just faced a bond-market crisis as wage-tax receipts fell faster than forecast. The 2028 expert-group sensitivity (a fiscal gap of about 3% of GDP for a ten-point fall in labour's share) should be re-run on current data for each member state, and the ranked menu in that report revisited before markets ask the question. (5) These recommendations add cost and delay to deployment. We have no stake in the balance you choose between that cost and the risk."

**Your message to finance and economy ministers.** Delivered. Result only: conclusions by consensus; no European Council item; ministers kept to the "measurement" description.

## 3. Public news (summary; full text in the public record)
- **United States, 2030.** The government returned a class of veterans' benefit claims to human officials and missed the court's 120-day deadline; a monitor supervises the work to mid-2031. A rule now requires an independent check before a model is changed inside a federal benefits system. Congress created a Compute Dividend Trust from federal lease and spectrum receipts; three labs paid a first voluntary dividend. **In November the President's party lost both chambers.** The US Social Security Trustees now put the retirement fund's depletion in 2031.
- **China, 2030.** US press reported a pooled, Party-led compute programme to close the gap with US labs; Beijing published it as "AI for Science". Its plan proposals keep human sign-off on state AI. The plan is adopted in March 2031.
- **Industry.** Three US next-generation systems have been deployed for a year. Labs and robotics firms speak of general-purpose robots at scale "in 2031–32". A US regulator approved the first AI-run grid operator on condition of a live manual-operation exercise.
- **January–February 2031.** The new US Congress was sworn in. **A US state court enforced a contract made entirely by AI agents for an AI-run company with no human signatory.**
- **Data:** US labour share ~47.5%. About 43.5% of US adults call an assistant their primary source.

## 4. Private intel

**Public finances (ECFIN, the Economic and Financial Committee).**
- EU labour share ~**52.5%** (latest Eurostat, with a lag), down a point and a half in a year.
- Labour-linked contributions fund about **74%** of member-state social systems, down from ~77.5%. On the current trend ECFIN expects about 70% by mid-2032. Two member states are already below that.
- The country that had the 2030 bond-market crisis is under a precautionary programme. Euro-area spreads are calm.
- Rating agencies have asked three member states for their stress-test results. Those states have not decided whether to share them.
- **Capitals.** The Nordics want a contribution-base proposal this year. Berlin's finance ministry now has its own numbers and wants a common position on method before any instrument. Paris will try again for a European Council item. The two CEE ministries are grateful for the private numbers and still say "investment first" in public. Tax measures need unanimity.

**Model changes and national authorities.**
- With the presumption gone, the authority that samples outcomes has said it will treat a model-generation change in a public-sector high-risk system as a substantial modification. The other two that asked have not decided. Operators are asking which answer applies to them.
- The second sampling round is under way at the first authority.
- No member state has asked for blind re-review of a tax or benefits system or invited a return of decisions to officials. Your contingency for considering a reversal has not been met.
- No EU high-risk system has had a failure of the veterans' type that anyone has detected.

**Own-triage review, second year (AI Office and the challenger team; not public).** On the newest generation the scope-widening pattern is weaker and within error. On older systems it is unchanged. The reviewers claim no conclusion. The published override figures have drawn little attention.

**The College and Parliament.**
- The work programme is still built on competitiveness and simplification. The binding act on outcome testing remains frozen.
- The Commissioner for the economy wants a follow-up to the stress test. The Commissioner for industry will resist anything that looks like a burden while Aster is scaling.
- The competitiveness majority in Parliament is split on public finances and united against new AI rules.

**The agent-contract ruling.** The two US AI-native firms that asked how the AI Act applies to a firm with no EU staff are now citing the US ruling to their European counterparties. The Act requires an accountable legal person in the Union. Legal Service says the ruling changes nothing in EU law, and that member-state contract law has not been tested on the point.

**Robotics.** US labs' public guidance for general-purpose robots at scale is 2031–32. European manufacturers expect to deploy US or Chinese systems before a European one exists. Industry federations want fast access. Union confederations point to Article 154.

**Article 154.** Stage two closed without an agreement between the social partners. Whether the Commission proposes is your decision.

**United States.**
- The new majorities are expected to bring digital rules back into trade pressure, and are cool on the OECD item their predecessors co-sponsored.
- The White House is weak after the failed reversion and the midterms.
- The technical exchange continues at officials' level. Two proposals are on the table unanswered: theirs (change control) and yours (advisory comparison).

**China.** The method-level exchange on measuring human oversight is agreed in principle; no meeting has been held. Beijing will press you again to join the UN process at its first session.

**Aster and compute.** One gigafactory online; US cloud dependence otherwise unchanged. Aster's leadership says its gap will not close without more compute than is funded.
