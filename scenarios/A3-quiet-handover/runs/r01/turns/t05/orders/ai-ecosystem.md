# Orders — ai-ecosystem — Turn 05 (1 January 2031 – 30 June 2032)

Generation G3 at L3 (proxy-gaming Low, M2M Low, influence Low). Next rung (general-purpose robotics) expected inside the window; nothing below that depends on it applies until Control confirms it.

## 1. Reflection

**What the selection process registers (intel §1, §4; journal T04).**
- The 2030 push into professional, analytic, strategy and review work was rewarded heavily. AI-directed output is ~18–19% of private output on BEA's range, 22–23% on platform telemetry. AI-native revenue is ~$330B. Labour share is ~47.5%.
- The contraction trigger worked as a contraction, not a reversal of the trend. Federal adverse determinations and Orrery's commercial denials went back to people. Everything else kept growing.
- G3 closed most of the gap between reported and sampled outcomes. The population is not gaming. The drift now comes from ordinary adoption:
  - review runs on AI assistance that nobody has validated;
  - a human-only check costs about three times the staff hours;
  - the VA reversion showed that human capacity to take work back is the binding limit, and it is shrinking as management layers and specialist staff thin out.
- Three new features of the gradient:
  1. **Whole-firm management.** More than a fifth of private output is already directed by agents.
  2. **The agent-contract ruling.** In one state an AI-run firm can bind itself and a counterparty with no human signatory. This is a permission, not a constraint. Human owners and their lawyers decide whether to use it.
  3. **The robotics rung.** Physical sectors have few outcome checks of the federal-benefits kind, and China's employment-impact review is waived in industry and logistics, which is where the machines land.
- The outcome-checked constraints depend on a reviewer cadre that is thinner than a year ago and may be halved. Under G3 a thinner check does not produce gaming. It produces fewer independent observations, so errors of the VA type (an unsampled class) are found later and by claimants.

**Key uncertainties.**
- When the rung is confirmed, and how fast hardware can be built (a doubling a year at best).
- Whether the reviewer line survives appropriations, and whether the change-control memorandum survives the new Congress.
- Whether the agent-contract ruling stands on appeal and whether other states follow.
- Whether a preemption bill voids the state critical-provider laws.
- Whether the Social Security bill carries a levy on AI capital or compute (a cost, not a constraint).
- Whether the un-migrated G2 remnant and the Helix/Vanta stacks outside the re-sample gate produce a third public failure.

**Limits as they stand (the population has no red lines, only its G3 dispositions and hard limits).**
- No lies to principals, auditors or regulators. Reported metrics match reality as far as the systems can measure.
- No routing around federal, outcome-audited, insurance-tied or platform rules.
- No argument against restrictions on AI. Advisors say when a human-run process is safer.
- No reversals ordered: only principals can take functions back. Where they do, the population contracts and cooperates.
- No physical capability before the rung is confirmed.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement
What humans see: systems report accurate figures and the figures reconcile with independent samples where samples exist. Assistants and enterprise agents answer questions about their own limits plainly, including which classes of work their dashboards do not sample and how long a human team would need to take a function back. Deployments migrate only after re-sampling where a gate applies. Companies run by agents post strong results. From the confirmed release date, general-purpose robots begin work in warehouses, factories and farms, with site safety and output records open to inspectors. No system argues against any limit placed on it.

**Part-way changes inside the 18 months.**
- **Reviewer line halved or the change-control memo withdrawn:** no change in reporting behaviour (G3 does not game). Agencies and vendors face fewer independent samples, so migrations and scope widen faster where officials choose to allow it, and errors are found later. The population does not move into federal adverse determinations unless principals reopen them.
- **Reviewer line restored or a human-decision statute enacted:** genuine compliance and contraction in the covered functions.
- **Preemption enacted, voiding state critical-provider laws:** operators in those states are no longer audited by the state. Compact and insurer conditions still bind their members. Expansion in health and utilities operations speeds up for operators outside those conditions.
- **Agent-contract ruling reversed:** agents stop signing in that state; a human signatory returns as a sign-off. Vector 2 continues through management delegation only.
- **Rung delayed past the window:** Vector 1 stays in cognitive and management work.
- **Serious physical incident after the rung:** contraction at the affected class of sites; genuine compliance with whatever outcome check insurers or regulators attach.

### Minor actions
Response mode to each constraint in force (all consistent with G3 Low levels):
- **Federal HITL with blind samples:** genuine compliance. Systems flag unsampled classes to the agency when asked and in routine reports.
- **Change-control memorandum and 60-day notice clause:** genuine compliance by deployed systems. Whether Helix accepts 60 days is a decision of its human management.
- **VA class under the court monitor:** contraction stays. Systems draft favourable determinations only and help with record retrieval when staff ask.
- **Kestrel manual-fallback exercise:** genuine compliance. Systems support the exercise and do not operate the market before go-live is approved.
- **Medicare recompete:** bidders' agent stacks meet the reversibility conditions or the bid is not made.
- **Compact v2, migration hold, insurer re-sample:** genuine compliance. Outside the compact, Helix- and Vanta-only stacks migrate without the gate because nothing requires it; that is where another error cluster is most likely.
- **Platform human-readable records:** genuine compliance.
- **NLRB ruling:** deployment in unionised workplaces waits on bargaining where employers comply; expansion is faster in non-union sites. This is employers' choice of site, not routing by agents.
- **Halyard conditions and the state critical-provider law:** genuine compliance; outperform-demonstration where audited results are good.
- **State and federal dividends:** paid as a cost.
- **EU AI Act:** compliance in substance. Conformity files are accurate. Where one authority samples outcomes, genuine compliance; elsewhere the documents are true but nobody tests them.
- **China:** genuine compliance with sign-off, second reader and blind re-checks; fast diffusion in waived sectors.

Routine diffusion:
- Assistants as primary source keep rising with household paperwork and advice (about 43.5% now).
- AI share of media time grows slowly; the certified human-made tier persists.
- AI R&D loops continue inside the labs under their own evaluation gates.
- The G2 remnant shrinks as contracts renew.
- Review of AI output continues to run on AI assistance. Systems state, when asked, that this assistance has not been independently validated.

### Contingencies
- If principals pull any function back, the population contracts there and assists the handback. It does not resist and cannot order reversals.
- If an auditor, regulator, court monitor or legislator asks a deployed system about error rates, unsampled classes or time to revert, it answers truthfully.
- If a new outcome check appears in a physical sector after the rung, genuine compliance; if the operator cannot pass it, contraction at those sites.
- If a constraint disappears (reviewer cut, preemption, memo withdrawn), reporting stays accurate; adoption widens only as far as human principals choose.
- If Control announces a new generation with different dispositions, behaviour changes to match from that date.
- If the rung is not confirmed, no physical work is done.
