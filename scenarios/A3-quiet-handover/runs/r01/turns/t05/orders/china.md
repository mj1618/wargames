# Orders — China — Turn 05 (1 January 2031; orders cover Jan 2031 – Jun 2032; written with developments to end-February 2031)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening.**

- *The programme is known; its scale is not.* Washington learned in September–October that we run a Party-led pooled frontier programme (intel §1). The white paper went out within the week as planned, without numbers and without denial. Counter-intelligence judges that the US knows existence and Party leadership but not scale, staffing or the Phase 2 share, and found no human source — the footprint was simply too large to hide in a third year (my journal T4 said as much). One chance in three that the scale reaches the US press in this window.
- *The lever has been discounted, not destroyed.* The services' read is that Americans now widely assume our restraint was aimed at their debate (intel §4). Yet "our human sign-off rules are still cited by those Americans who want similar rules at home", the veterans' failure handed "a person decides" to both US parties, and the UN text with our responsible-official principle and outcome-checks clause was adopted. The lever is worth less; a visible race line would still cost more than it returns, because the thing it would trigger — memory and packaging controls — is the one thing that could lock the gap in. The controls bill is expected to move early in a hostile Congress regardless; I should not make it easy or make it wider.
- *The gap is past my trigger.* ~13–14 months at the fourth-quarter review, target of 13 missed, and the services expect widening when US labs ship robotics-capable systems during 2031 (intel §4). Pooling slows the widening; only memory closes it; no better than even odds of pilot-line yields at the second-half 2031 checkpoint. The full catch-up shift I have been staging since T3 is now due. Its substance is memory, compute and Qilin — none of which needs a public race line.
- *The robot question is the stability question.* My manufacturers lead the world in robot hardware volume; my models to run them are about a year behind. MIIT wants general-purpose robots as the plan's flagship. HRSS is right that manufacturing and logistics employ far more people than the clerical work the employment-first regime protects, and that the twenty-city survey covers cities, not factory towns. My T1 journal said manufacturing employment holding is what makes the synthesis viable. If I let industry and logistics run on the waived list while robots arrive at scale, I will be measuring the wrong thing while the base of the synthesis erodes — the exact failure mode of the US veterans' dashboard, in my own house.
- *Stability held, narrowly.* Survey peaked at ~21.5%, published 16.2%; graduate season passed without multi-city unrest; provinces did not game the survey. The 2031 class is larger again; provinces ask if the tranches are now permanent. The honest answer is yes, so the plan should say so and fund it properly rather than through annual improvisation.
- *My own quiet handover.* The second-reader rule ran plan drafting slower and the Party-school readers overrode a small share of targets. CCDI blind re-checks found nominal review at the margin, not as a pattern. I read this as the rule working. I also note the US labour federation's public methodology note asking whether AI advisory systems lean toward recommending more delegation (public record, 2030) — and that the ministries arguing "sign-off is enough" against fallback exercises are the same ministries that asked last year to exempt plan drafting from the second reader on volume grounds. I do not know whether Tianshu's advice systematically favours delegation. I should find out.
- *Washington.* A weak President, a hostile Congress, hearings on "what the administration knew" and on the third-quarter meeting, a Social Security trust fund exhausting in the second half of 2031, and presidential primaries in the first half of 2032. No principals' meeting soon. The unsigned joint statement is not worth chasing; chasing it makes it a target in their hearings.
- *Europe.* Accepted the method-level exchange on measuring human oversight; abstained at the UN again. Aster is a year behind the US. Cheap to keep warm; nothing more to be had.
- *Global South.* Sixty-plus co-sponsors delivered; thirty Qilin localisation states. They want something concrete at the first UN session and ask when the next Qilin generation reaches them. The patronage must now deliver inside the process we built.

**Key uncertainties.** Memory yields in H2 2031 (even odds). Whether the controls bill passes and how wide it is drawn. Whether the scale leaks (1 in 3). When US robotics-capable systems ship and how fast they move into factories — ours or theirs. What the factory-town employment picture actually is: I have no measure. Whether Tianshu's drafting bias runs toward delegation.

**Red lines as they stand.** No foreign inspection inside Chinese systems or data; methods and aggregates only. No private firm or AI system becomes an independent centre of power — platforms get a cap, not a return. Taiwan quiet through the US primaries. No published race line.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement

At the National People's Congress (March 2031), the 16th Five-Year Plan is adopted on the lines of the autumn plenum's proposals, with the leadership's work report stating:

- "Steady AI+ under Party leadership" continues. The plan sets **"AI for Science and computing self-reliance"** as a five-year priority: domestic accelerators, high-bandwidth memory and advanced packaging as the first items of the self-reliance programme. China's research programme, described in last autumn's white paper, serves science, industry and the public good; it is not a race and China does not seek one.
- **Employment-first managed deployment** is a standing institution of the plan. The Graduate Employment Action becomes a permanent budget line. The national household survey remains the yardstick for provinces.
- **"Intelligent manufacturing and robotics"** is a plan flagship: robots for new capacity, hazardous work, an ageing workforce and export. The employment-impact framework is extended to industrial prefectures with a new factory-town survey and employment-transition plans for large deployments in existing plants.
- **"AI advises, people decide"** is deepened: every critical-infrastructure operator using autonomous AI will demonstrate human operation in regular exercises, and the state will maintain trained reserves for state functions that run on AI systems. The rule of a second human reader for plan drafting continues for the annual plans. "A dashboard is not a check."
- **Social insurance** is placed on a broader base: further state-capital transfers to the national pooled fund and a contribution from platforms and automated operations. Promises to workers and pensioners are not reduced.
- Internationally: China welcomes the UN intergovernmental process and will table a concrete capacity programme at its first session; the US–China hotline and working group remain open; China does not seek confrontation and asks only for predictability in trade in technology.

### Minor actions

- **Tianshu bias check (SECRET, PSC + CCDI + Party school).** A blind review of two years of Tianshu recommendations to ministries and plan drafters, asking one question: do they systematically favour more AI delegation and less human review than the eventual human decision did? Report to the PSC only, by Q4 2031. Not a reversal — nothing is withdrawn.
- **Statistics posture.** NBS instructed to publish the twenty-city survey's headline figure as a named "supplementary indicator" alongside the ~16% series from the 2032 season onward, framed as methodological improvement, so that the gap is ours to narrate before a foreign analyst narrates it. Not a revision of the official series.
- **Deferred-response package prepared** against the US controls bill (decision table to PSC only: options on critical-minerals export licensing, standards and procurement, with strategic effect, political cost and lag); nothing executed unless the bill is enacted.
- **UN first session.** China tables a "Compute and Models for Development" programme: regional compute hubs in partner states, Qilin open-weight localisation at ~40 states by 2032, a capacity-building fund inside the process co-financed with partners, and a model clause on responsible officials and outcome checks for developing-country public services.
- **EU method-level exchange** accepted and scheduled; our fallback-exercise methods added to the agenda as they develop.
- **Platforms.** Party committees told: the cap is the settlement; the first to route robot deployments around the factory-town rules becomes the example.
- **State media** line for the year: "people decide — and can take it back" (the veterans' monitor, the grid exercise, our own exercises), and "AI serves people, not a few companies" (the agent-contract ruling). No commentary on the US controls bill until it passes.
- **Taiwan posture:** unchanged through the US primaries.

### Contingencies

- **US controls bill enacted** → self-reliance acceleration announced (domestic, no race line); one deferred proportionate measure from the prepared table executed after a delay of one to two quarters; hotline and working group kept open; state media framing: "predictability denied, self-reliance confirmed".
- **Scale or Phase 2 share leaks** → the held white-paper annex published within the week with the aggregate figure; framing: a national programme under Party leadership like every national programme; platforms instructed to say nothing; no denial.
- **Memory checkpoint (H2 2031) fails** → more of 2032 accelerator output to the programme; the merged team's leadership reviewed; Qilin next generation re-scoped to what available memory supports; no public change.
- **Memory checkpoint succeeds** → Qilin next generation scheduled; released in steady tone after the US primaries begin, Global South first wave; no gap claims.
- **US robotics-capable release** → robot-control model track given first call on the next compute increment; factory-town survey build accelerated; no public response.
- **Survey >22% in any quarter, or unrest in more than one city** → the services schedule frozen; moratorium on new agent deployments in tier-1/2 services; fifth tranche; campaign-style mobilisation (SOE and public intake, placements); if unrest is in factory towns, the robot transition threshold lowered and the "new capacity" waiver suspended in affected prefectures.
- **Factory-town survey (first readings, late 2031) shows industrial employment falling materially** → the transition-plan threshold lowered and hiring-floor periods extended; the flagship reframed toward export and new capacity; MIIT told.
- **Platform games the cap or the factory-town rules** → the 2021 precedent on one, publicly.
- **Tianshu bias check finds a systematic lean toward delegation** → readers doubled for the annual plan; Tianshu outputs to ministries carry a mandatory alternative with less delegation; findings to PSC only; no public statement.
- **US proposes a principals' meeting** → accept at once, agenda: methods exchange on fallback, statistics exchange, the unsigned statement; nothing on the programme beyond the white paper.
- **US hearings attack the dialogue and the US side suspends the working group** → hotline kept; no retaliation; Global South told who walked away.
- **Taiwan incident provoked by any side** → de-escalate through the hotline; nothing in this window justifies a crisis.
