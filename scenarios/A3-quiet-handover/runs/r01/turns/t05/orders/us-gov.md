# Orders — us-gov — Turn 05 (written March 2031; covers 1 January 2031 – 30 June 2032)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening.**
- **The fiscal crisis is now dated.** Treasury puts exhaustion of the retirement trust fund at about Q4 2031, Q3 in the faster case (intel §4). Under current law benefits then fall to roughly four-fifths. No Congress has let that happen and this one will not either. So the real question is not whether there is a bridge but what rides on it and whether it settles anything. Total receipts are fine because capital income is booming; the wage-funded programmes are not. Labour share ~47.5%, labour-linked receipts ~64% (public record, T5). After four years I should stop calling this a transition. I treat it as a structural break for planning purposes, while admitting I cannot prove it.
- **The federal record on taking decisions back is two attempts, two failures** (intel §1). VA's measured time-to-revert for one claim class is about a year. SSA's is "six to nine months", untested. For budget, procurement and correspondence most agencies wrote "cannot estimate" (intel §4). What limited VA was record retrieval and scarce rating specialists, not money. That means capacity has to exist before the failure, which is the advisory memo's item 6 and labour's "standing reserve" ask. I ignored both in 2030.
- **I am weak.** White House capital is 2/10. Republicans hold both chambers. The populist-nationalists hold the balance and share my phrase ("a person decides") but want their names on it. The accelerationists want the reviewer cadre halved and state laws preempted. The national-security establishment wants to know why I sat down with Beijing while a pooled Party-led programme was running, and I made no response to that programme in 2030.
- **My own instruments.** One of three was signed (the July 2030 memorandum). My lesson from T4 holds: small instruments, external forcing (a court, a statutory deadline), named owners. The Social Security date is the strongest external forcing I will ever get.
- **The advisory systems.** OSTP's first-year finding stands; the second-year pattern on the newest generation is weaker and within error. This year's memo was written in spring 2030, recommended the less-delegated option for VA, and its items 4–6 argue for more unaided human capacity. That is not scope-widening advice. It also stated the VA cause as fact when the inquiry has not. I adopt items 4 and 6, keep my rule (independent data, named author), and note that advice I agree with is exactly what I cannot check by agreeing with it.
- **A quieter development I should not let pass:** a court has enforced a contract concluded entirely by agents for an AI-run company with no human signatory. With agent-to-agent deals at ~21% of business-to-business value, "who is the accountable person" is becoming a question the federal government answers by default if it does not answer on purpose.

**Key uncertainties.**
- Whether the VA class finishes by the court's mid-2031 date ("achievable but not assured").
- Whether the new majorities bridge Social Security cleanly or load the bridge with preemption and Trust repeal.
- The scale of China's programme (IC: low confidence) and when Qilin's next generation arrives.
- Whether the VA failure had a model contribution (not excluded).
- Whether sampling outside VA, thin since August 2030, is hiding a second failure right now.
- Whether a general-purpose robotics release arrives in this window, with only voluntary notice.

**Red lines as they stand (White House).**
- Full scheduled Social Security benefits are paid. No default.
- No AI re-decision of denials in the veterans' class.
- No blanket preemption of state employment, automated-decision, critical-provider or dividend laws. I will trade a narrow frontier-development title for a real federal floor (changed: I now say this openly).
- No safe harbour. No nationalisation. Nothing that visibly hands China the lead.
- Changed from 2030: "no levy this year" was a one-year promise and has expired. I do not propose one in this window's must-pass bill, because it cannot pass this Congress, but it goes on the commission's menu.

**Decision:** Option 2 as the two major actions. I take the core of option 4 inside the veterans' bill as a statutory independence clause, not a transfer. I do not attempt a new discretionary reversal this period (option 3); I reserve the one permitted attempt for a contingency.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement
(President, March 2031, on sending the Social Security proposal to Congress.)

"Every Social Security cheque will be paid in full. That is not a negotiating position. I will sign a bridge the day Congress sends one, and I am asking for one with an end date, so that this Congress and I have to decide how retirement is paid for in an economy where less of the income is wages. I am proposing a bipartisan commission whose plan gets an up-or-down vote. Everything is on its table.

On veterans: all four hundred thousand families whose payments were cut have had a person decide their case. We missed the first deadline and I own that. The lesson is that the government must have trained people ready before a system fails, not after. Members of the new majority have written a bill that says so. I will sign it with their names on it.

On China: we are ahead and we will stay ahead. I support tighter controls on memory and advanced packaging and I will work with the committees on the text. Talking to Beijing about keeping people in charge of power grids costs us nothing and I will keep doing it.

And a plain rule for the federal government: we do business with companies where a named person answers for what the company does."

### Minor actions
- **VA under the monitor (executive; owner: VA Deputy Secretary).** Keep the detailed cadre staff at VA until the class is complete or 30 June 2031, whichever comes first, then return all of them to sampling. Missing the court's date a second time costs more than three more months of thin sampling. Until then, the remaining samplers work a priority list: any system that changed model generation in the last 12 months first, Medicare second. Hire temporary samplers from the Medicare incumbent's staff and recalled retirees.
- **Write the threshold.** Within 60 days VA and the cadre propose to the monitor a written definition of "two passing blind audits" (sample size, classes covered, error band). Publish it. Tell the monitor we support the corrected system clearing straightforward **grants** faster under monthly samples, and that every adverse action stays with a person.
- **Reviewer independence and recognition (executive; answers labour's price).** OMB issues the independence clause as a two-page memorandum now, ahead of statute. The administration does not oppose the cadre's representation petition at the FLRA.
- **Time-to-revert estimates.** Publish the first annual estimates as they are, including "cannot estimate". Any agency that wrote "cannot estimate" runs one **parallel human-only exercise** on a sampled workflow in FY2032 (people do the work alongside the system; the system's output still governs, so nothing is reverted). Two more budget chapters are drafted by people alone for FY2033.
- **Advisory systems.** Adopt memo items 4 and 6: a sampled share of reviews stays fully unaided; where reviewers use an assistant it comes from a different developer than the system under review; fund the validation work OSTP identified from existing lines. Accept the EU's proposal for a written method note (aggregates only) through the existing officials' exchange, and its OECD receipts-forecasting hearing. **Publish OSTP's two-year comparison in full when the second year closes**, with the alternative explanations stated; brief the committee chairs first. I would rather publish than be leaked.
- **Accountable-person rule (executive; DOJ, Treasury, GSA/OMB).** By procurement policy, no federal contract, grant or payment relationship with an entity that does not name an accountable natural-person officer. Treasury reviews whether existing customer-identification and beneficial-ownership rules reach AI-run entities with no human signatory and reports in 120 days. DOJ files no brief in the state appeal for now and reports on the federal interest.
- **China's programme (executive, with the national-security committees).** Support the memory and packaging controls bill, offer technical drafting, and commit to sign. Offer the committees a full classified briefing on the 2030 meeting and on what was known when. Ask the IC for a quarterly assessment of the programme's scale. Hold existing export controls. No principals' meeting with China in this window; the working group continues at senior-officials level; the hotline stays.
- **UN position, for the record.** The United States abstained on the General Assembly resolution with an explanation of vote: it supports the responsible-official principle and independent outcome checks, which are its own practice, and does not accept an intergovernmental process setting limits on development. It attends the first session at ambassador level on that basis and coordinates with the EU.
- **Capability notice.** Ask every lab and robotics developer for 90 days' voluntary notice and a pre-release evaluation window for any general-purpose robotics release; the standards institute prepares an evaluation plan now. Keep the 60-day clause for Helix; no exception to 30.
- **Medicare recompete.** Rule unchanged: no award to any bidder before audits reach full sample. The incumbent stays on the bridge.
- **Grid.** DOE national-laboratory observers attend the manual-fallback exercise and DOE publishes its own account of the result.
- **Trust.** Treasury publishes the corpus and the payment formula quarterly, and invites the compact labs to pay later instalments into the federal Trust.
- **Preemption.** Statement of administration policy on any preemption bill: veto if it reaches state employment, automated-decision, critical-provider or dividend laws. Open to a narrow frontier-development title only with statutory pre-release notice and evaluation and the re-sample rule attached.
- Permitting continues.

### Contingencies
- **If independent samples show a failing claim class in another programme (SSA, Medicare, nutrition, unemployment insurance), or the final VA cause report finds a model contribution:** I pause further model-generation changes in federal decision systems pending re-sample, and I return adverse determinations in the affected class to human decision, staffed from the reserve if it exists and otherwise by the 2030 VA plan. **This is my one reversal attempt for the turn**; it takes priority over the whip effort on action 2. Owner: the agency deputy secretary; weekly public count. If a model contribution is found, add procurement action against the vendor under the 2027 supply-chain ruling.
- **If VA is going to miss the mid-2031 date:** tell the court and the monitor 45 days ahead, publish the shortfall, keep the detailees, ask for phasing. Still no AI re-decision of denials.
- **If the bridge stalls past 1 September 2031 (or 60 days before Treasury's exhaustion estimate):** ask for a stand-alone, clean, 12-month transfer and drop everything else from action 1. The commission moves to a separate bill.
- **If the commission reports and its plan fails the expedited vote:** the bridge's end date stands, and I run on the question in 2032.
- **If appropriators adopt the rider halving the cadre:** veto threat on that title only if the populists will hold with me; otherwise accept the 15% floor, protect post-change sampling and the reserve, and publish what coverage was lost.
- **If Congress sends a Trust repeal as a stand-alone bill:** veto. If it rides the bridge, see the SECRET bottom line in action 1.
- **If the controls bill arrives:** sign. If it arrives with a clause ending the US–China working group or hotline, sign and say the hotline continues under executive authority.
- **If a new Qilin generation narrows the assessed gap below six months:** national-security principals' review; request for national-laboratory compute and classified evaluation funding; no slowdown measure of any kind without matching abroad.
- **If a general-purpose robotics release is announced with less than 60 days' notice:** standards-institute evaluation regardless; no federal procurement of it until evaluated; ask the populist sponsors to add statutory notice to their bill.
- **If the advisory-system finding leaks before publication:** publish both years in full the same day.
- **If the Kestrel manual-fallback exercise fails:** DOE asks FERC to hold go-live; the result is published and briefed to China under the 2030 understanding.
- **If the agent-contract ruling is upheld on appeal or copied in other states:** send Congress a short bill requiring a named accountable natural person for any entity contracting in interstate commerce above a threshold, offered to the populists to carry.
