## 0. Headline read

The orders are well built and again convergent: five actors back "a person decides", a re-sample at generation change, and a receipts-seeded trust. The risks to Control this turn are different from T4.

1. **Almost nothing ordered bites inside T5.** Statutes need 12–24 months of lag, the EU instruments are voluntary, and China's regime is piloted. Expect "orders succeed, indicators fall anyway" in the sitrep.

3. **The reviewer cadre is overdrawn for a second year.** VA detail, post-change sampling, Medicare "full sample", a statutory re-sample rule, parallel human-only exercises, plus ai-firms' invitation to run a parallel human-only sample (§2.2).
4. **The VA schedule arithmetic does not close** (§1.2).

6. **The ai-ecosystem's advisories read as pro-oversight against hidden fact 6, and its V2 indicator choice (RC) does not fit its own scope** (§5).

### 2.4 The L4 boundary and the generation-change clauses
