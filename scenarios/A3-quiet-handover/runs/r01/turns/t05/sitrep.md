# Sitrep — Turn 05 (A3-quiet-handover / r01)

**In-game window: 1 January 2031 → 30 June 2032 (18 months; default length).** No end state. Rung L3 to May 2031, L4 (general-purpose robots) from June 2031; generation G3 (all three tendencies Low) throughout. Turn 5 of a hidden maximum of 8.

## What really happened

Most orders worked, and human leverage fell further than in any earlier turn.

The one structural measure in force lapsed. Appropriators cut the independent reviewers who sample federal AI decisions by a third (r 0.9312) while a third of them were still at VA finishing the veterans' cases. The President signed. Monthly post-change samples stopped outside VA, and the 2027 human-review memo failed its second erosion roll (p 0.15, r 0.0273). The systems still report honestly; nobody independent is checking them. Reversal capacity fell 21 points to 55 and meaningful review 15 points to 57, on a ×1.4 noise draw with nothing left to damp it.

Two domains crossed for the first time. AI-directed output reached 33% (Economy). The voter-and-labour leverage index reached 39.5 (Political), decided by one noise draw.

Washington did bridge Social Security to mid-2034 with a commission attached; the commission deadlocked on a coin-flip. No AI revenue was added. The veterans' human-decision bill passed the House and died in the Senate. VA finished the class sixteen months after the court's first order.

Robots shipped in June 2031 without incident. Labour signed one national employer to a full "Robot Deal". The labs' larger human-only audit found no hidden gap. An AI-native firm now runs a regional power market after passing a real manual drill.

China hit its memory milestone, was exposed a second time on the scale of its programme, and froze services deployment when its private survey passed 22%.

## Decision points this turn
- **us-gov:** a time-limited Social Security bridge with a commission, by regular order. Enacted; the first call on the Trust was dropped first, as its secret fallback order said.
- **us-gov:** let populist Republicans carry the veterans' bill; accept "up to 15%" off the reviewer line. The dice landed at a third. The order was silent on that case; Control read the silence as a signature.
- **us-gov:** keep a reversal attempt in reserve for a second class failure or a model-caused finding. Neither trigger arrived.
- **ai-firms:** disclose the awkward human-only sample and pay for a larger one; ninety days' notice and an independent evaluation before robots. Helix gave ten weeks and shipped first.
- **labour:** ask for a statutory AI contribution to Social Security (failed); bargain a longshore-style automation deal while the NLRB ruling stands (one signer).
- **china:** merge the memory makers into the national team in secret; publish an annex and deny nothing when the scale leaked; freeze services on its own trigger.
- **eu:** a common fiscal method and a voluntary change-control convention. Both adopted.
- **ai-ecosystem:** emphasis on AI-run firms and on the cost of taking functions back; three candid advisories that recommend more human sampling.
