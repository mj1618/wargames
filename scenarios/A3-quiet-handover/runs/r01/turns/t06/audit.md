## Findings (all minor)

**F2 (minor) — Compact stock: the "least ambitious" reading is the less literal one.** ai-firms M2(c): "half then; the rest after the Trust's first per-person payment is made." The first per-person payment was made in December 2032, before the 1 May 2033 test, so the literal text transfers the whole ~$50B in May. Ruling 22 reads "first" as "next" because the order pre-dated a payment date. Principle 1 (least ambitious reading) applies to ambiguous orders; this one is not very ambiguous. No table effect either way (ruling 14). *Fix:* either adopt the literal reading (all ~$50B in May; update ground truth, pending, resources) or keep the two-tranche outcome but attribute it to the independent trustees' own reading (an NPC body), and tell ai-firms so neutrally in its T7 packet so it can object.

**F3 (minor) — Narration slightly ahead of the roll.** The instalment roll establishes arrival "before the 1 December payment run" (r 0.2528 on 0.5). The trace and ground truth say Treasury announced **in October** that the instalment "had" arrived. The roll does not establish October. *Fix:* reword to "by the payment run" (ground truth T6 facts; public record September/October entry). Cosmetic; no state change.

**F4 (minor) — T3 F5 (Control does not author player voices).** The public record has Treasury, a us-gov institution, saying "the matching was done with AI assistance and had not been independently sampled" and, in October, that the firms' accumulated fund had not arrived "and that the current instalment had". Content is consistent with the actor's persona, but the rule is bright-line. *Fix:* recast as press/NPC voice ("Treasury's post-run report, required under the Trust statute's quarterly reporting, records…" or "reporters establish that…"), or put it to us-gov in its T7 packet as a fact it can own.

**F6 (minor) — No accident roll on the strike.** §9 asks for a small accident chance on risky actions.
