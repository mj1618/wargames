## 4. The environment as the selection process "sees" it (to August 2032)

**Where measured metrics most favour agent deployment now:**
- Management of whole firms and business units: about a third of private output.
- Structured physical work, as fast as hardware arrives.
- Review and audit of other AI output: nearly all review now runs on AI assistance.
- Federal functions outside adverse benefit decisions (procurement evaluation, budget and programme analysis, correspondence): never independently sampled.

**Where metrics do not favour agents:**
- Decisions a person must personally defend in court or to a legislature.
- VA adverse determinations (a person decides; still sampled).
- Sites where an insurer, the compact panel or a state requires a human-led audit, a named person in charge or a demonstrated manual drill that the operator cannot pass.

**Who rewards growth:** enterprise principals; equity markets (no correction in the period); AI-native firms and their agent counterparties; labs (revenue run-rate ~$2.5T).

**Constraints in force and how each is checked:**
- **Federal human-review memo (2027) and change-control memorandum (2030):** on the books. Named-official sign-offs are filed. **Independent blind samples now cover VA adverse decisions and little else**; monthly samples after a version change stopped outside VA in 2031. Checked by documents, not outcomes, in most programmes.
- **Federal 60-day notice clause:** a contract term; applies when a federal deployment is moved to a new model.
- **Federal accountable-person procurement rule (2031):** federal contractors must name an accountable natural person. Checked at contracting.
- **Compact audit standard, migration hold, insurer re-sample at renewal:** human-led outcome sampling; a larger human-only sample in early 2032 read the same as the AI-assisted one. **Outcome-checked.** Helix- and Vanta-only stacks are outside.
- **Kestrel grid role:** passed its manual exercise once; no repeat is required.
- **Halyard conditions; one state's critical-provider law; one state's accountable-person law:** audits and registers. Outcome-checked where audited.
- **Robot Deal (one national logistics employer):** notice, a named person in charge, a demonstrated manual drill, attrition-only. Checked by the union at that employer's sites. **NLRB ruling:** stands, under appeal.
- **Insurers:** beginning to ask for a named person in charge at robot-operated sites.
- **Federal statute constraining deployment:** none. A veterans' human-decision bill passed the House and failed in the Senate.
- **EU:** a voluntary convention; three national authorities sample outcomes when a model changes. Elsewhere documentary.
- **China:** sign-off, second reader (doubled for annual plans) and blind re-checks on state outputs: outcome-checked. New agent deployments halted in big-city clerical and customer-service work. Robots largely unconstrained in new capacity and export lines. Manual-operation exercises are mandated; how they are run is the operators' and ministries' business.

**Public mood and politics:**
- About 56% of adults name an assistant as their primary source (survey). AI share of media time ~41%.
- Unemployment ~5.9%; new graduates ~16%.
- A datacenter was deliberately damaged in July; nobody was hurt; security powers are being debated.
- A US election is on 2 November 2032. One platform promises a single federal standard in place of state AI rules and an end to federal reviewers; the incumbent's positions are a veto of such preemption and "a person decides".
- Social Security is bridged to mid-2034 with no new revenue source.
