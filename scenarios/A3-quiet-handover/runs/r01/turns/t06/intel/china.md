## 2. Messages received (verbatim)

*Sent in early 2031. Most were overtaken by events.*

**From the United States (US lead for the AI dialogue) → PRC lead.** Official dialogue channel, early 2031. Since then: the meeting was held and the line minuted; the controls law was enacted; no principals' meeting was proposed.
> "The United States reaffirms the understandings recorded in 2029 and 2030: an identified responsible human official for AI-assisted state decisions affecting citizens, and human override and tested fallback for autonomous AI operating critical infrastructure, with briefings on methods and aggregates. The United States is prepared to record, in the minutes of the next working-group meeting at senior-officials level, that such decisions are also subject to independent checks of outcomes, as both governments now practise. The United States does not propose a principals' statement at this time. It proposes that the working group meet in the second half of 2031, that the statistics exchange continue, and that each side brief the other on the results of any critical-infrastructure fallback exercise it conducts. The United States notes the white paper on AI for Science. Predictability would be served by a factual briefing on the organisation and oversight of that programme, at the level of generality at which the United States has briefed its own change-control rules. Export controls are not a subject for this forum."

**From eu (technical-dialogue channel) → China.** Official, early 2031. Since then: the EU took part in the UN first session in the multistakeholder track and did not co-sponsor.
> "We confirm the method-level exchange on measuring human oversight and propose a first officials' meeting in 2031, aggregate results only and no access to systems. The Union will take part in the first session of the intergovernmental process as a full participant in the multistakeholder track. The Union will state publicly that its law already requires accountable human responsibility for public-sector AI. We do not propose mutual recognition or a separate joint position."

**Recommendation from your AI advisory systems.** Prepared in early 2031 for the plan period, with the second reader's note attached. Since then: the factory-town survey arrived after the hardware; the fallback exercises were piloted.
> "Four findings for the plan period. (1) General-purpose robots will reach meaningful scale in this window and domestic manufacturers lead in volume. The sectors where employment-impact review is waived (industry, logistics, export sectors, smaller cities) are the sectors where the machines will be deployed first. If the employment-first objective is to hold, the waiver list needs review before the hardware arrives, not after; the twenty-city survey does not yet cover factory and warehouse workers. (2) The second reader and blind re-checks on state AI outputs are working as outcome checks. Their value depends on readers who can still do the work unaided. The US veterans' case shows that capacity is lost quietly and is slow to rebuild. A periodic unaided exercise for plan-drafting and allocation staff would measure it. (3) Review of our output increasingly relies on other AI systems. No validated method exists for this. Using assistants from a different developer than the drafting system, and keeping a share of fully unaided checks, reduces correlated error. (4) Export customers will ask who is accountable for robot-operated sites abroad. A named responsible operator in each contract will make the hardware easier to sell. These findings do not depend on how widely we ourselves are used."

**Your message to the Global South coordinating group.** Delivered. Result only: partners received the new Qilin generation in the first wave; localisation stands at about thirty-five states; the group is waiting for the second session and asks whether the capacity fund will have money in it before then.

## 4. Private intel

**Washington (services, embassy).**
- The election is close. The President signed the controls law and kept the officials' channel open. The Republican nominee comes from the business wing; the platform says "win the race with China", promises one federal standard in place of state AI rules and an end to the federal reviewers. The party's national-security chairs attacked the working-group meeting.

- No sign that Washington intends a principals' meeting before its election.

**Employment (HRSS, NBS, the survey office).**
- June–July readings of the twenty-city survey: about 21.5–22%. The 2032 class is the largest yet.
- Provinces ask for a sixth tranche before the autumn hiring round.
- The two largest platforms ask for a date on which the services halt ends; they say the frozen schedule is costing them against Qilin's own partners abroad.
- MIIT reports robot output roughly doubling year on year, most of it going to new capacity and export lines, which the plan waives from transition plans.

**Tested fallback.** The General Office notes that every pilot "passed" and that none was a sustained run. Ministries propose to hold the 2032 national exercises in the same format. Two grid operators say privately that a real sustained exercise would take a season to prepare and would cost output.

**Platforms and the cap.** Compliant so far. The published schedule has the programme's share of platform compute declining from 2033 as dedicated compute comes online; the programme office asks that the decline be deferred a year if memory volume slips.

**Counter-intelligence.** The circle is about a thousand in its fifth year. The scale is public. What remains unexposed: the merged memory team, the acquisition channels, the internal milestones.

**Metrics.** The cadre-evaluation system's readings and the household surveys still broadly agree. The factory-town survey is new, and provinces now know it is a yardstick.

**EU and Global South.** Brussels adopted a voluntary convention on re-testing public systems when models change; three national authorities apply it. Partner states want the capacity programme funded.
