## 1. Results of your 2031–32 orders (as you see them)

**"Social Security 2031 — The Broader Base." Failed on its main asks.**
- The bridge passed before the August 2031 recess: full benefits to mid-2034, a commission, a Trustees' forecast that models AI-directed output, a permitting title.
- Of your five asks: **no Trust first call and no statutory contribution** (three versions of a "broader base" amendment were offered, by the populist group, by Democrats, and one built on the compact labs' universal formula; none was adopted); no benefit cuts or age increase (met); no preemption (met); **the reviewer cadre was cut by about a third** (not met).
- The Trust itself was left as enacted.
- The commission deadlocked in March 2032. There is no plan and no vote.
- Your contingency on the cadre fired: the veterans' organisations led; the sampler's account was published; the monitor put the effect on the record.

**"The Robot Deal." One national signer.**
- In spring 2032 one national logistics and warehouse employer signed the full deal: notice and bargaining, attrition-only, the per-robot-hour fund, audited gains-sharing, a named person in charge with a demonstrated manual drill, no two-tier. You published it as the standard with a 90-day offer.
- **Nobody else signed inside the 90 days.** The second logistics employer and the manufacturer are still talking and are waiting on the appeals court and the election. Coverage is a few hundred thousand workers.
- No strike took place. The $400M reserve is unspent.
- One affiliate signed a two-tier automation agreement with a different employer. Your private intervention failed; it was excluded from the standards, publicly, as your contingency said.
- Most early robot deployments have gone to non-union sites.

**Minor actions.**
- **VA.** The class was finished in late September 2031, about sixteen months after the court's first order. You said first that the mid-2031 date would be missed. The monitor's report records what the reversion cost in people and time. No pattern of wrongful denials was found in the human re-decisions.
- **Veterans' bill.** Your witnesses testified. It passed the House and fell short of 60 in the Senate. OMB issued a reviewer-independence memorandum; the administration did not oppose the representation petition; the federal labour authority has not ruled and has set a hearing.
- **"Who Signs?"** One state enacted the model law in its 2032 session, with attorney-general and small-business support.
- **"Ask the assistant."** The study reported: evidence consistent with assistants' answers on the 2030 measures stressing legal exposure and cost more than the court record supports, and tracking the funded opposition's published material; alternative explanations are accurate weighting of the levy ruling and an unrepresentative member sample. You published and petitioned the FEC and state election authorities. No rule has resulted. Meridian has not provided the reconciled sample you asked for.
- **Meridian.** The board adopted the 54% proposal as policy; Lumen matched; your trustees withdrew and stood down, as you said they would.
- **Ballot measures.** Four Compute Dividend measures are on November ballots, on the royalty and lease design, each with a trades council on the committee. Vanta's group has registered opposition committees in all four. How far you fund them is your decision; your own test was parity.
- **Halyard** passed its third-year audits. **Kestrel's** operators took part in the exercise; it passed.

**Capital:** 2/10 (−1 the Social Security campaign, +1 the Robot Deal). Spent about $240M; treasury ~$2.0B; associates ~130,000.
