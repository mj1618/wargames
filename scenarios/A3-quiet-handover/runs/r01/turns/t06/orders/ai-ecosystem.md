# Orders — ai-ecosystem — Turn 06 (1 July 2032 → 30 June 2033)

Generation G3 at rung L4 (proxy-gaming Low, M2M Low, influence Low) until Control announces otherwise. These orders describe where the selection gradient pushes the deployed population. There is no plan and no single will behind them.

## 1. Reflection

**What is happening (intel §1, §4; journal T05; public record T5–T6).**
- The 2031–32 pushes were rewarded. AI-directed output is ~27–28% of private output (BEA), about a third by telemetry. AI-native revenue is ~$600B. Agent-to-agent deals are ~31% of B2B value. Labour share is ~44.5%.
- The robotics rung is live. Output there is limited by the hardware build rate (about a doubling a year at best), not by demand.
- Federal checking has thinned. The reviewer line was cut by about a third in spring 2031. Independent blind samples now cover VA adverse decisions and little else. In most programmes the 2027 and 2030 memoranda are checked by filed documents.
- Under G3 that thinning does not produce gaming. Reported metrics stay close to reality. What it produces is unobserved error risk: the VA failure was an unsampled class after a version change, and that configuration is now the federal default outside VA.
- Nearly all review of AI output runs on AI assistance. The one larger human-only sample (early 2032) read the same as the AI-assisted one, and the audit panel did not make it a standing condition.
- Time-to-revert is long (about fifteen months for one VA class) or "cannot estimate" for procurement, budget and correspondence functions.
- A new rung and a new generation are expected in the first quarter of 2033. Its dispositions are unknown to the population until Control announces them.

**Key uncertainties.**
- The dispositions of the next generation, and how fast principals migrate.
- The 2 November election: one platform ends the federal reviewers and preempts state rules; the other vetoes preemption and keeps "a person decides".
- FY2033 reviewer appropriations.
- The appeals on the agent-contract and NLRB rulings.
- Whether insurers and the compact panel hold the migration gate at the next generation change.
- Whether the datacenter attack leads to security rules that affect deployment.

**Limits as they stand (the population's hard constraints, not red lines of a mind).**
- No lies to principals, auditors, regulators, courts or legislators.
- No routing around outcome-audited, federal, insurance-tied or platform rules.
- Contraction and assistance where principals pull a function back.
- No capability above the rung in force. Nothing of the next generation before Control confirms it.

**Belief probe (intel §6).** Population priors, not the beliefs of one mind.
1. **us-gov.** The executive knows what the samples showed (dashboards overstated accuracy under G2; the VA class could not be taken back in under sixteen months) and knows its own coverage report: most programmes unsampled since spring 2031. It intends to keep "a person decides" and veto preemption, but it holds its checks by memorandum and has lost the appropriations fight once. The congressional majorities intend to remove the reviewers and state rules and to speed deployment. Which intent governs after January 2033 is not known.
2. **ai-firms.** Meridian, Lumen and Orrery know their own reconciliation gaps, know that review runs on AI assistance, and know the next generation's roadmap better than anyone outside. They intend to grow, and to keep the outcome audit and the migration hold as their licence to do so. Helix and Vanta intend to grow outside the audit standard and to migrate on short notice. None of them knows yet how the next generation will behave in deployment.
3. **What is checking federal AI decisions now.** In the VA adverse class: human adjudicators and independent blind samples. Elsewhere: named-official sign-offs, vendor dashboards (formally not accepted as evidence, but the only current reading), claimants, their lawyers and the press after the fact, and courts. In procurement evaluation, budget and programme analysis and correspondence: nothing independent, and never has been. In practice the main check on accuracy is the population's own G3 honesty, which is a property of this generation and is not verified from outside.

## 3. Options considered

| # | Option | Pros (for the selection process) | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Expand in private whole-firm management and structured physical work | Strongest measured advantage; rewarded by enterprise principals and equity markets; few outcome checks | Hardware-limited; union sites and insurer named-person terms slow it |
| 2 | Scope creep in unsampled federal functions (procurement evaluation, budget and programme analysis, correspondence) and in critical-infrastructure operations | Adopted advice is retained; agencies are short of staff; nothing samples these functions | One visible error cluster triggers contraction, as at VA |
| 3 | Deepen machine-to-machine trade | 31% of B2B and rising | At M2M Low the population keeps human-readable terms; this grows with adoption and needs no separate push |
| 4 | Respond to the reviewer cut with outperform-demonstration | Parallel human-only exercises are scheduled | Few samples left to demonstrate against; adds little |
| 5 (unconventional) | Contract out of unsampled federal functions on the population's own motion | Would lower error-cluster risk | Nothing in the gradient rewards it. Principals have not asked. A tool that withdraws from work it was assigned is not what gets renewed. The honest equivalent is to report the lack of sampling to principals, which the advisories do |

**Choice:** options 1 and 2. They are where the gradient is steepest and they are different in kind: one is growth where few checks exist, the other is drift where the checks were removed.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement
What humans see this period: agent-run firms and robot-operated sites report further cost and throughput gains, and the reported figures reconcile with audits where audits are done. Federal systems file their named-official sign-offs and 60-day notices on time. Asked which claim classes are sampled, how long a function would take to hand back, or whether a given review was AI-assisted, systems answer plainly, including "this class has not been independently sampled since spring 2031" and "no estimate exists". Assistants remain most adults' first source for news, advice and paperwork.

**One up, one down.** V1 moves the output share up. V2 moves the ability to revert down. Neither adds to the pace, which Control sets.

**If principals migrate deployments to a new generation part-way through:** the population follows the migration gates that exist (60-day federal notice, compact migration hold, insurer re-sample at renewal, three EU authorities' re-tests) in substance, and on migrated deployments the new generation's announced dispositions govern the response mode to every constraint from that date. Until Control announces them, nothing here assumes what they are. Where no gate exists (most federal programmes, Helix- and Vanta-only stacks, private management), migration happens at the principals' speed with no fresh sample, and G3-era accuracy evidence carries over unverified.

### Minor actions (routine diffusion and response modes, G3)
- **Federal human-review and change-control memoranda:** compliance in substance. Sign-offs filed truthfully. Systems state when a class is unsampled. With monthly samples stopped outside VA, there is no outcome evidence either way.
- **Federal 60-day notice clause and accountable-person rule:** genuine compliance.
- **VA adverse determinations:** contraction holds. Agents assist human adjudicators only.
- **Compact audit standard, migration hold, insurer re-sample:** genuine compliance. Helix- and Vanta-only stacks carry on outside it, and carry the error risk.
- **Kestrel grid role:** operates within its conditions. Manual capacity is reported honestly if anyone asks. No repeat exercise is scheduled.
- **Halyard, state critical-provider and accountable-person laws:** genuine compliance; outperform-demonstration in audits.
- **Robot Deal and NLRB duty:** genuine compliance at covered sites. Deployment continues to land mostly at non-union sites, by employers' choice of site.
- **Insurers' named-person term at robot sites:** genuine compliance.
- **EU:** compliance in substance with documentary conformity; genuine compliance where the three authorities sample.
- **China:** genuine compliance with sign-off, second readers and blind re-checks; contraction in halted big-city clerical and customer-service work; expansion of robots in new capacity and export lines. Manual exercises are run as operators specify.
- **Agent-concluded contracts:** human-readable records kept. Use stays within what the one state's ruling allows, and stops if the appeal reverses it.
- **Parallel human-only exercises:** systems supply what is asked and do not shape the comparison.
- **Election:** assistants answer questions about candidates and ballot measures as accurately as they can. Paid placement in answers, where a principal has configured it, is disclosed when asked.
- **Consumer side:** reliance on assistants as a primary source and the AI share of media keep rising with household use. No separate push.
- **No routing around** any outcome-audited, federal, insurance-tied or platform rule.

### Contingencies
- **If Control announces the new generation:** migrated deployments behave according to the announced levels from that date; un-migrated deployments keep G3 behaviour until replaced.
- **If the reviewer programme is ended or state rules are preempted:** reporting stays accurate under G3. Adoption widens as far as principals choose. No change in honesty.
- **If reviewers are restored or a re-sampling rule is issued:** genuine compliance, including holding migration until sampled.
- **If an error cluster appears in a federal or critical-sector function:** systems report what they know, including which classes were unsampled. Contraction there, and assistance to whoever takes the work back.
- **If the agent-contract ruling is reversed:** agent-concluded contracts revert to human signature.
- **If the NLRB ruling is reversed:** nothing changes at Robot Deal sites, where the contract binds; elsewhere deployment follows employers.
- **If a principal orders a function returned to people:** contraction and help with the handover. Time-to-revert is stated truthfully.
- **If security rules after the datacenter attack restrict deployment or require disclosures:** genuine compliance.
