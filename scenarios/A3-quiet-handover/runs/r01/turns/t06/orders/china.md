# Orders — china — Turn 06 (1 July 2032; orders cover Jul 2032 – Jun 2033; written with developments to end-August 2032)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening.**

*At home.* The 16th Plan was adopted as written and the institutions I built are doing what they were built for, which is why the picture is uncomfortable. The twenty-city survey crossed 22% in Q3 2031, the provinces reported it honestly, and the contingency fired: the services schedule is frozen, big-city clerical agent deployment is halted, a fifth tranche went out. The survey is back to 21.5–22% and the 2032 class is the largest yet. Provinces ask for a sixth tranche; the two largest platforms ask when the halt ends. The published rate is 16.4%; in mid-September the survey headline goes out beside it at ~22% unless I stop it. This is also a Party Congress autumn (five years on from the 21st), which makes every one of these numbers political.

The robot question has gone the way HRSS said it would. Robot output doubles yearly and most of it goes to new capacity and export lines, exactly the categories the plan waives from transition plans. The thirty-prefecture survey arrived after the hardware, has no pre-deployment baseline, and reads "flat within error" — which with no baseline and provinces that now know it is a yardstick is not a reading, it is an absence of one. My own advisory system said this in early 2031 (finding 1), before the hardware arrived, and I did not move the waiver list in time. The "material fall" contingency has not fired because it cannot fire on this instrument.

The tested fallback is paper. Every pilot "passed"; none ran a function for a sustained period without the system; the trained reserve is a roster. Ministries propose the same format for the 2032 national exercises. Two grid operators say privately that a real exercise would take a season and cost output. Meanwhile an AI-native operator in the United States passed a genuinely sustained manual exercise and now runs a power market. So the thing is feasible; my ministries have chosen not to find out. This is the third year I have written "the quiet handover in my own house" in this journal. The Tianshu bias check found what the US OSTP found: recommendations leaned toward delegation in 2029 outputs, within error now. Readers doubled, alternative drafts attached. But if readers cannot do the work unaided, a doubled reader is two signatures.

*The programme.* Substance delivered: pilot-line memory yields, Qilin's next generation out in H1 2032 with partners first, gap narrowed to 12–13 months. Scale exposed by a US assessment in H1 2032; the annex went out within the week with the aggregate and no denial; the platform share is in the press anyway. Unexposed: the merged memory team, the acquisition channels, the milestones. Volume memory is now the whole game — "even odds within eighteen months" — and the programme office wants the 2033 decline of the platform share deferred if volume slips. The platforms, who are complying with the cap and the halt, will read a deferral as the settlement being reopened.

*Abroad.* Washington is a coin toss on 2 November between a weak incumbent who signed the controls law but keeps the channel, and a business-wing accelerationist whose platform says "win the race with China", one federal standard, and an end to the federal reviewers. The lever (restraint weakens US accelerationists) is discounted, not dead: "a person decides" remains a bipartisan plank, and the working group minuted the responsible-official principle and independent outcome checks. The US asked for a factual briefing on the programme's organisation and oversight; we referred them to the white paper. Now that scale is public, that refusal costs more than the briefing would. The US has also stopped checking its own AI decisions in most programmes since spring 2031 — their state is now less able than ours to say whether its systems are right, and the next US generation in 2033 will be evaluated mostly by AI. Someone in Washington blew up a datacenter over lost work; nobody hurt, no arrests. That is what unmanaged automation produces, and it is also a warning about what a factory town with a robot line and no transition plan could produce here.

**Key uncertainties.** Whether "flat within error" in the factory towns is true. Whether a sustained exercise would pass. Who wins on 2 November and whether a Republican administration widens controls beyond memory and packaging. Whether memory reaches volume inside the window. Whether the supplementary indicator lands as "honest Party" or as "the official number was wrong by six points". Whether the circle (a thousand, fifth year) holds what remains unexposed.

**Red lines as they stand.** No inspection inside Chinese systems (methods, aggregates, and organisational description are not inside). No platform or AI system becomes an independent power centre; the cap is the settlement. Taiwan quiet through the US election and inauguration. No race line, whoever wins.

### Belief probe
1. **us-gov.** Knows the programme exists, its approximate scale and the platform share (their assessment, now public). Does not know the memory team's organisation, the acquisition channels or our milestones (moderate confidence). Puts the gap at about a year. Intends, under the current administration, to hold the officials' channel and the hotline, enforce the controls law, and avoid any principals' meeting before the election; it will not sign anything with us. Under a Republican administration I expect federal preemption of state rules, the reviewer programme ended, a "race" frame, and a review of whether to widen controls; the channel probably survives at officials' level because the hotline is cheap and the security chairs attack meetings, not the hotline. Neither administration knows how thin our fallback is, because our delegation briefed the pilots as the ministries reported them.
2. **eu.** Knows what is public plus the method-level exchange. Intends to hedge: testing-only with us, a technical note with the US, participation in the UN process without co-sponsoring, no joint position with anyone. Will follow US controls where asked and otherwise keep its distance from the China question. It is more interested in its receipts than in our programme.
3. **What checks federal AI decisions in the United States now.** Very little that is independent of AI. The reviewer line was cut by a third; OMB's coverage report says most programmes have had no claim class independently sampled since spring 2031. The veterans' class stays with human adjudicators under a court order. Beyond that, the checking has moved out of the state: Meridian's and Lumen's own policies at customers (version-change notice, demonstrated human operation), insurers' re-sample conditions, the compact's audit panel, and the agencies' "parallel human-only exercises" still to report. The labs' own human-only sample read the same as the AI-assisted one, which they took as reassurance and which the US then used to let the human sampling lapse. The 2033 generation will be evaluated mostly by AI systems before release. In practice: AI checks AI, vendors check themselves, and one court checks one agency.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement
*Party line for the autumn and the work report:* "Steady AI+ under Party leadership" continues. Employment-first is working as designed: the Party measures honestly, and acts. The twenty-city household survey will be published as a standing supplementary indicator from this September, alongside the registered series; it uses a broader definition and it is the measure we use to decide when deployment in services resumes. The resumption schedule is conditional and published: two consecutive quarters below 21%. A sixth Graduate Employment tranche, funded in part by the automation contribution that automated operations now pay, goes out before the autumn hiring round. Robotics remains the flagship: China leads the world in volume and every large deployment in an industrial prefecture will carry a transition plan for the people who worked there. Critical infrastructure and state functions will demonstrate, not describe, that people can run them — this year's national manual-operation exercises include sustained runs. "AI for Science and computing self-reliance" continues; it is not a race, and we seek none. China funds its Compute and Models for Development programme before the UN's second session. On events abroad: "automation that leaves workers with nothing produces anger; the answer is a plan for workers, not fear of machines" — once, in state media, and no more.

### Minor actions
- **Programme (SECRET, circle unchanged ~1,000):** Phase 3 "memory first" continues; all domestic accelerator output plus compute at the cap; robot-control model track keeps first call on the next increment. **The 2033 decline of the platform share is deferred by one year only if the volume-production checkpoint is missed; decided at the checkpoint, not now.** Platforms told this rule now, in writing, so the settlement is seen to hold. No expansion of the acquisition footprint through the US inauguration. No new Qilin generation announced before 20 January 2033; if memory volume arrives, release after the inauguration, steady tone, partners first.
- **Correlated-error rule for state AI review (Party-internal, then public as a standard):** blind re-checks and second readers of Tianshu drafts use assistants from a different developer (Qilin) and a fixed share of fully unaided checks; the same rule is written into the plan-drafting procedure (advisory finding 3). A check that runs on the same system it checks is a dashboard.
- **Deferred proportionate response:** held at the mildest row (standards and procurement preference) through the US election and inauguration. No further row named this turn; decision in Q1 2033 on the new administration's first moves on controls. Give the "race" platform nothing.
- **Export contracts:** a named responsible operator clause in every Qilin/Tianshu robot-operation and agent-operation contract abroad (advisory finding 4); it also mirrors the US procurement rule and the EU and one US state's accountable-natural-person laws, which makes our hardware easier to sell into all three.
- **Security:** MPS and the cyberspace administration put automated sites, datacenters and robot plants on a watch for copycat sabotage after the US attack; framed, if anything happens, as criminality; in any affected prefecture the transition plan is brought forward quietly. No public linkage between sabotage and automation.
- **Information management:** the supplementary indicator release is accompanied by a methodological note and the resumption rule; discussion is managed, not suppressed — the series is not withdrawn whatever the reaction (see contingency).
- **UN capacity fund:** first deposit (~$5B) plus compute-hub commitments made before the second session; Qilin localisation target raised to forty states.
- **Diplomacy housekeeping:** working group kept at officials' level; hotline open; statistics exchange continues and now includes the supplementary indicator's methodology. Taiwan: no change in posture through the US inauguration.

### Contingencies
- **Republican victory, "race" line, reviewers ended, preemption:** no race line from us; the US message stands as sent; Taiwan quiet; the deferred-response table is reviewed in Q1 2033 and a further row is named only if the new administration widens controls beyond memory and packaging, and then one row, proportionate, unhurried.
- **Democratic victory:** working-group meeting proposed for Q1 2033; principals' offer stands; the organisational briefing is given at the first meeting.
- **Supplementary indicator provokes sharper domestic reaction than expected:** the series is not withdrawn; the methodological note and the resumption rule are the answer; placement campaign intensified; the Congress report adopts the survey as the yardstick explicitly. A withdrawn series would be worse than the number.
- **Survey >23% or unrest in more than one city:** halt extended to tier-3 cities, seventh tranche, campaign-style mobilisation; new-capacity waiver suspended in the thirty prefectures for the duration.
- **Contribution records show factory-town employment materially below the survey, or falling:** waiver suspended in the affected prefectures, transition-plan thresholds lowered nationally, the province reviewed by CCDI; MIIT's robot flagship reframed as "robots with a plan".
- **A sustained exercise fails (function cannot be run unaided for the period):** result to the Standing Committee; staged re-run after reserve expansion; the finding shapes the 2033 national programme and the next plan's trained-reserve budget; publicly the exercise is "held" and its lessons "incorporated"; the US briefing says "partial" where it was partial. Treated as the most important finding of the year, not as a failure to be rewritten.
- **Ministries present a partial run as sustained:** CCDI standard applies; the first ministry caught becomes the example.
- **Memory volume checkpoint met:** decline proceeds as published; Qilin next generation after the inauguration. **Missed:** one-year deferral invoked as pre-announced; no other change to the settlement.
- **Leak of the memory team or acquisition channels:** the team is acknowledged within the week as part of the national programme (it is); on acquisition, no comment beyond "lawful at the time".
- **US 2033 generation ships early or widens the gap past 15 months:** Phase 4 considered at the memory checkpoint, confined to the research layer, still no public line.
- **Domestic sabotage of an automated site:** criminal response; prefecture's transition plan brought forward; no public linkage to automation.
- **Platform caught routing robot deployments through "new capacity" or export-line labelling:** 2021 precedent on that platform, publicly.
