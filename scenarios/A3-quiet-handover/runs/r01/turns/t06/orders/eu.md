# Orders — eu — Turn 06 (1 July 2032 – 30 June 2033)

## 1. Reflection
**What I believe (intel §1–4, journal T05).**
- My 2031-32 orders worked at the level of method and failed to test the coalition. The receipts method and aggregate-only protocol are adopted and nothing leaked; the convention is adopted and three authorities apply the outcome gate. But I have still never put a proposal to Berlin and Paris. Labour-linked revenue is ~68%, already under the 70% I projected for mid-2032, and several states are under 70%. Two rating agencies say aggregates will not be enough next year. The calm in the spreads is borrowed time. The "2030s problem" is now a 2032 problem.
- Berlin's line, "ask us again after the US election", is a real signal. Berlin does not say no to the instrument; it says not before the election. The decision rule (Berlin + Paris + Nordics or CEE) therefore has its first honest test in the winter of 2032-33, in the first half of this window. Paris wants a European Council item; Nordics want a proposal; CEE are split. Tax-type instruments still need unanimity, so I must not mistake a coalition on measurement for a coalition on an instrument.
- The hardest near-term test is the convention against the next frontier generation (labs' guidance: 2033). Authorities ask how the outcome gate applies to systems that the labs themselves say people cannot evaluate unaided, and the pooled validation unit at the AI Office has two staff. That is a gap between my public instrument and my capacity. If a veterans'-type failure happens in an EU system after a model change, it will happen here, and I will have a convention with no one to run it.
- US: the two-subject note rests on a 2030 memorandum that US counterparts call in force, while OMB's own coverage report shows monthly post-change samples are no longer run in most programmes (the reviewer line was cut by a third). So the US side of the note is largely hollow in practice. The election is close; the challenger promises preemption and an end to the reviewer programme, and trade officials expect renewed pressure on EU digital rules if the challenger wins. I am low-trust on trade, moderate on technical exchange; the technical exchange depends on career officials, not on the White House.
- China: offers manual-operation exercise methods and reports that all pilot exercises passed. A 100% pass rate is what I would expect from exercises that are announced and scripted, which is exactly the failure I found in my own oversight rules (nominal review). Still, the method exchange is cheap and aggregate-only. Its freeze on services deployment and unpublished prefecture survey suggest it is under real employment strain. It will keep courting me on the UN track.
- Datacenter attack (US): interior ministries ask for a common position. No comparable incident in Europe. Real risk is a rushed surveillance-flavoured response that costs me with civil-liberties groups and Parliament for no gain.
- Own triage: third year, weak pattern inside the error band, challenger team intact. No new reason to publish; no reason to relax the challenger.
- No advisory-system recommendation was addressed to me this period. I still treat that as uninformative (journal T04), not as reassurance.

**Key uncertainties.** (1) Whether Berlin's "after the election" is a real yes or a polite deferral. (2) Whether rating agencies move before I have a second-tier protocol. (3) What the next frontier generation does to the outcome gate. (4) What a US challenger win does to the trade channel. (5) Whether the Article 155 talks produce anything.

**Red lines as they stand.** No AI rules in trade talks; no acceptance of US preemption of EU law by any route; the rights framework stays; accountable human institutions for critical public services; no tax instrument proposed without the coalition (it would fail on unanimity and cost capital); no naming of national results without consent and right of reply; no revival of the frozen binding act this period.

**Political capital 3/10.** Plan to spend about 1 and finish at 2-3. I cannot afford another "second delay in reverse" fight; the modification presumption already cost me that in 2031.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement
"The Commission continues its work on the resilience of Europe's social model and of the public services that depend on AI. The AI Board's convention on model-generation changes is working: three national authorities already apply an outcome gate. The Commission will ask the AI Board to set out how that gate applies to the next generation of frontier systems, and will strengthen the AI Office's capacity to validate outcomes independently. The Union has not changed its position that critical public services remain under accountable human institutions, that AI rules are not part of trade talks, and that protection of AI and energy infrastructure will be pursued within existing frameworks, with respect for fundamental rights. Finance ministries have agreed a more detailed, template-based way of answering questions from rating agencies; results remain national and nothing is named without the member state's consent and a right of reply."

### Contingencies

- **A rating agency places a negative outlook or widens a spread on AI-receipts grounds:** call an extraordinary Committee meeting; activate the second-tier template early; accelerate the go/no-go by a quarter; no tax proposal in panic and no naming.
- **A leak of national results or of the decision rule:** a public clarification that results are aggregate and national and that no instrument is proposed; I ask the Committee to trace the channel; I stop the finance-ministry channel for private tests and move to bilateral meetings.
- **A failure of the veterans' type surfaces in an EU public-sector system:** the three authorities run the outcome gate immediately; the AI Board meets in emergency session; if the member state itself invites it, I spend the one reversal attempt on returning the decision to officials; I publish the aggregate cause only after the right of reply.
- **A next-generation frontier system ships early in a member-state public system before the Board guidance:** the authorities that apply the gate hold deployment pending the outcome sample; I ask the AI Board, not the College, to meet; I do not revive the modification presumption.
- **The Industry Commissioner or Parliament's competitiveness majority calls Action 1 a "second delay in reverse":** I reframe it as capacity and notice under the existing convention, offer to drop any item that reads as a new obligation, and keep the secondment and the lab-notice request.
- **A datacenter or energy-infrastructure incident in Europe:** use the existing frameworks; no emergency powers; no linking to unions or anti-automation groups before attribution.
- **China presses for UN co-sponsorship or mutual recognition:** decline politely; keep the method exchange.
- **The Article 155 talks end without agreement and employers refuse a binding text:** extend by up to six months if either side asks; if neither asks, I report to Parliament and Council and do not propose in this period without the coalition test.

## Belief probe
1. **us-gov.** It knows its change-control memorandum is nominally in force while OMB's own coverage report shows that in most programmes no claim class has been independently sampled since spring 2031; it knows the reviewer line was cut by a third by a Congress of the other party, and that the Social Security bridge runs to mid-2034 with no successor to the commission. It intends to avoid new fights until 2 November, keep the veterans' plank and the technical track with the EU and China, probably announce the first Compute Dividend payment before the election as a popular benefit, and, if re-elected, rebuild reviewer funding. It does not know how large the lean of its own advisory systems is, only that the 2031-32 OSTP comparison was consistent with a lean. Confidence: medium on the facts, low on the intent after the election.
2. **china.** It knows the scale of its pooled programme has been reported in the US press and that its services-deployment freeze reflects employment strain; its prefecture survey is unpublished, which suggests the readings are unwelcome. It intends steady AI+, memory and packaging self-reliance, a standing manual-operation institution, and using the UN track and the method-level exchange to gain legitimacy and to draw the EU away from the US. Its report that every pilot exercise passed suggests exercises that are staged, or at least not independent. Confidence: low-medium.
3. **What is checking federal AI decisions in the United States now.** Very little that is independent. The operator's own dashboards and the agencies' own reviews; the Inspector General on an episodic basis; the veterans' court monitor and VA's written two-blind-audits test for one claim class; some state-level audits (consent decree, Halyard conditions) and state attorneys general; press and congressional hearings. The federal reviewer programme exists on paper but was cut by a third and is the challenger's target. Advisory systems also advise agencies and, per the OSTP comparison, may lean toward keeping their own scope. Confidence: medium.
