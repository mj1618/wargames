## What really happened

The country took one decision back and lost the election that would have built on it.

The challenger won the presidency (p 0.40, r 0.7286) and Republicans kept both chambers. Two weeks later SSA returned one class of denials to named people. It missed its own date by a month, then met the test by 0.0036 on a coin-flip roll, drew no backlash, and the new Commissioner kept it. It is the first federal reversal to succeed in three attempts, and it is why the State domain is still uncrossed (RC 44.0; 39.0 on a fail). In the same year `p_dem` fell below 0.5 for the first time (0.441). Nobody at the table can see either number.

Culture crossed (EPD 56.5), making three of four domains.

The new model generation arrived in March 2033 and drew badly: proxy-gaming High, machine-to-machine coordination High, influence Medium. No scheming cluster. The compact's own validation programme had found AI-assisted evaluation unreliable on it (p 0.15), so Meridian and Lumen held their release. Helix shipped on its own evaluation. Insurers, not the state, then wrote a human-only check into cover terms.

The September OMB directive, the EU guidance, the release protocol's samples, the insurers' term and China's exercise credit were all ordered and executed, and none is effective yet. No structural measure is in force.

Labour struck a second logistics employer and won a full Robot Deal on a roughly 6% path. The first Trust cheque (~$95) went out with a visible error cluster. China published its real youth rate, ran two real manual exercises and failed the third. Berlin said "not yet", and a member state was downgraded.
