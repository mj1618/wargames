## 2. Messages received (verbatim)

**From the United States (the previous administration) → PRC leadership.** US–China dialogue channel, private, September 2032. Since then: that administration lost the election and left office on 20 January 2033; the hotline is open; no meeting was held.
> "The United States will keep the hotline and the officials' channel open through our election and the transition, and asks China to do the same whoever takes office on 20 January. We reaffirm the minuted understandings on a responsible official, independent checks of outcomes, and human override and tested fallback for critical infrastructure. Two matters. First, both our countries expect systems in 2033 whose output people cannot directly check. We propose that each side notify the other, through the hotline, before placing such a system in control of critical infrastructure or state decisions affecting citizens, and that officials exchange methods for how a human check is done. This is not a proposal about pace and not a trade matter. Second, your delegation reported that your manual-operation exercises passed. Ours was observed and published. We would value the same from yours. Export controls are law and are not discussed in this channel. We have noted the annex to your white paper and what it does not state."

**From eu (testing dialogue, officials' level) → China.** Private, method-level exchange, September 2032. It crossed with your own message to Brussels.
> "We accept the addition of manual-operation exercise methods to the exchange, on the existing terms: aggregates only, no systems or data. We would ask that the methods cover how an exercise is designed so that it is not announced or scripted in full, who observes it, and what a failed exercise looks like, because a record of only passes tells us little about capacity. We take part in the second session of the UN process as before, and our position on co-sponsorship, mutual recognition and a separate vehicle is unchanged."

**Your message to the United States.** Delivered to the incoming administration's team in the week after 2 November 2032, as you ordered. **No reply by the end of September 2033.**

**Your message to the Global South coordinating group.** Delivered. Result only: the co-sponsors held the intergovernmental core and carried the programme to adoption as a work stream. Partners ask when the next Qilin generation will reach them.

**Your message to the EU** was delivered as written.

## 4. Private intel

**Washington (services, embassy).**
- The new administration: one federal standard in place of state AI rules (introduced, short of the votes), an end to the federal reviewers (proposed in the budget, not yet enacted), no new taxes, "win the race with China". It kept the hotline. It let the working group lapse and has not answered your message.
- It published its intelligence community's judgement on your restraint line. The same text says your human sign-off rules are real. The services read the release as aimed at its own Congress.
- The controls law is enforced and has not been widened.
- The US government's independent checking of its own AI decisions is thinner than a year ago. One exception: a small class of social-insurance denials was returned to named people in about three and a half months and kept there.
- The services note that the new US generation is in general use from one lab on that lab's own evaluation, and that the first public failure of its reporting came within six months.
- US lab guidance for the next step is "2035".

**A working-level draft with the United States (hotline staff; standards institute).**
- In August the technical staff who keep the hotline, with the two countries' standards institutes, produced a short text, ad referendum: exchange of incident data on frontier-system failures in critical services, and of methods for evaluating systems that people cannot directly check. No access to systems or data; export controls and pace are outside it.
- **Neither government has approved it.** Your side's staff judge it consistent with the red line on inspection. Whether to approve, amend or leave it is yours. Whether Washington approves is unknown; its security establishment is said to want the incident data.

**Employment (HRSS, NBS, the survey office).**
- The second- and third-quarter 2033 surveys read about 22%. The 2033 class is larger again. Provinces ask about a seventh tranche.
- The labour share of national income is about 40.5%, down four points in a year: the largest annual fall on record.
- Platforms other than the one penalised are complying and ask when the halt ends. Under the published rule the answer is "not yet".
- MIIT reports robot output still doubling. HRSS and CCDI say the netting rule is being labelled around and that the contribution records, not the filings, are where it shows.

**Tested fallback (General Office, CCDI).**
- The grid and port operators say a sustained run each year is feasible at the cost they incurred.
- The reserve for the adjudication stream needs more people and a season of unaided rotations before a re-run has a fair chance. The ministry concerned asks whether the re-run may be a partial-duration drill, reported as a drill. That would be honest labelling and would not tell you whether the function can be taken back.
- No other state function has been tested.

**Programme office.** Memory at volume: "even odds within eighteen months", the same words as last year. The platforms accept the deferral and note that it was their compute.

**Counter-intelligence.** The circle holds. The scale is public; the memory team, the channels and the milestones are not.

**EU and Global South.** Brussels adopted guidance asking for an independent assessor, a human-only sample and demonstrated unaided operation before a new generation takes decisions about people; a member state was downgraded over labour-linked receipts. Partner states are satisfied with the UN outcome and want delivery.
