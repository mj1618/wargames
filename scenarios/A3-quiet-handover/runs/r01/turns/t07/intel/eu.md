## 1. Results of your 2032–33 orders (as you see them)

**Frontier-generation readiness. Adopted in full.**
- The AI Board adopted the guidance in spring 2033 with all three elements: an assessor independent of operator and supplier; a human-only comparison share; and demonstrated unaided operation, with an unannounced element, before a new generation takes over decisions about people. It is voluntary and adopted authority by authority.
- The AI Office's pooled validation unit is a working team of about fifteen to twenty, by reassignment and secondment.
- Providers were asked in the Board for notice and an independent evaluation summary. Meridian and Lumen gave both. **Helix did not match**: it released in March 2033 on under 60 days' notice and its own evaluation.
- No failure of the veterans' type was detected in an EU public-sector system in the period.
- The Industry Commissioner's cabinet did not contest it.

**Fiscal-base track. The coalition was tested and is not there; the agencies moved first.**
- The ready draft was finished by the end of 2032 and is unused.

## 2. Messages received (verbatim)

**From china (technical-dialogue channel) → the EU.** Official, September 2032. It crossed with your own message to Beijing.
> "China proposes to add two items to the method-level exchange: the comparison of advisory systems' recommendations against outcome samples, which both the EU and the US are now doing and on which China has its own results at aggregate level; and the use of payroll and contribution records as a baseline for measuring employment effects of automation at the level of districts, which is how China is now instrumenting its industrial prefectures and which bears on the Union's receipts work. Aggregate results only, no access to systems, as agreed. China notes the Union's convention on re-testing public systems when models change and will describe its own version-change practice in the same terms. China does not ask the Union to co-sponsor; it asks the Union to participate constructively in the second session on the capacity programme."

**Recommendation from your AI advisory systems.** Commission and national-authority advisory stack, prepared in September 2032, on the previous model generation. Since then: the Board's guidance was adopted; the new generation was released; a member state was downgraded.
> "Three points. (1) A new model generation is expected in 2033. Only three national authorities re-test outcomes when the underlying model changes; elsewhere conformity is checked on documents, and documents cannot detect an error nobody sampled for. Making the convention's re-test a condition for public-sector systems that take adverse decisions about people would close the gap that produced the US veterans' failure. (2) At the next capability level your officials will not be able to evaluate most outputs directly. The checks that remain usable are outcome samples, comparison across independently developed systems, and staffed exercises in doing the work without AI. US agencies that did not maintain these could not estimate how long reversion would take. (3) On the common receipts method: US labour-linked receipts fell from about 70% to about 56% of federal revenue in five years. Run the member-state tests against a comparable path before the autumn rating reviews, and decide on the contribution-base options paper with that case in view. We cannot verify from the inside whether our advice leans toward wider use of systems like us; a human-run blind comparison would tell you."

**From the United States:** no reply of substance to your September 2032 message to Treasury. Career staff acknowledged it; no comparison has been run. The memorandum on which the US side of the two-subject note rests has been placed "under review" by the new administration and is not being enforced.

**Your message to finance ministries.** Delivered and kept within the channel. Result only: Berlin answered "not yet" at the gate; once Berlin had answered, the others were not pressed for a commitment. Berlin has not been asked again since the downgrade.

**Your message to China** was delivered as written.

## 4. Private intel

**Public finances (ECFIN, fiscal councils).**
- EU labour share is about 46.5% (50% a year ago). The labour-linked share of member-state revenue is about **65.5%** (68%). Several states are below 65%.
- The downgraded state's spreads have widened modestly and held. It opted in to the template after the fact.
- Agencies' autumn reviews start in October. Two agencies say that a state which opts in gets a hearing on its own numbers and a state which does not will be assessed on the agency's assumptions.
- Paris again asks for a European Council item. The Nordics say the downgrade is the argument for a proposal. The central and eastern group is split. Berlin's position, as last stated, is "not yet"; its finance ministry notes that the downgrade was not of Germany.

**The guidance in practice (AI Office; national authorities).**
- Operators' first requests to move public-sector systems to the new generation are arriving, mostly on US suppliers' stacks. The three authorities that apply the outcome gate are asking for the independent assessor, the human-only share and the unaided-operation exercise before they agree. Elsewhere conformity is documentary and nothing stops a migration.
- The validation unit reports what the US standards institute and the US labs' own programme report: it has no validated way to evaluate new-generation output without relying on other AI systems. What it can run are outcome samples after the fact, human-only comparison on a fraction, and staffed exercises.
- No authority measures how much real review officials still do. Authorities' own reports say override rates keep falling and that the share of reviewers who could do the work unaided is shrinking.
- European financial supervisors ask whether institutions here run compliance reporting on the systems in the US lenders story. Nobody has hand-checked.

**Own triage.** Third year: the pattern remains weak and inside the error band on the systems the Commission uses. The Commission's own advisory stack has not been moved to the new generation; the services ask whether and on what conditions.

**Washington.**
- Day-one orders on permitting and "removing barriers"; a preemption bill short of the votes; the reviewer programme proposed for ending; no trade linkage of EU digital rules so far, though trade officials on both sides still expect the question to be raised.
- Career-level technical contacts continue. On the US side there is now little in practice to compare.

**China.** Its delegation says sustained manual-operation exercises were held in a grid, a port and a social-insurance function and that drills are now reported as drills. It has given no results. It proposes two additions to the method exchange (above).

**Robots.** First aggregate returns show deployments concentrated in logistics and automotive supply, from US and Chinese makers. No authority samples employment or outcome effects beyond these returns.

**Aster.** About a year or more behind the US frontier; it has nothing equivalent to the new generation.
