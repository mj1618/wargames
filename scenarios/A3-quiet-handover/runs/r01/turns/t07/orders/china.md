# Orders — china — Turn 07 (1 July 2033; orders cover Jul 2033 – Dec 2034; written with developments to end-September 2033)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening.**

*At home, the instruments I built are now telling me things, and one of them is "no".* The twenty-city survey reads ~22% for a fourth consecutive quarter beside a registered ~16.4%; the exit rule (two quarters below 21%) is not met and the halt continues under its published rule (intel §1, §4). The 2033 class is larger again; provinces want a seventh tranche. The labour share of national income is ~40.5%, down four points in a year, the largest annual fall on record (intel §4) — the same number the US prints (~40%, public record T7). Robots are doubling, and HRSS and CCDI are right that the netting rule is being labelled around and that the contribution records, not the filings, are where the fall shows (intel §4). The contribution-record baseline worked exactly as designed: it found falling industrial employment in a few prefectures, the contingency fired, the waiver is suspended there, CCDI is reviewing the provinces (intel §1). One platform tried export labelling and became the example.

*The tested fallback produced the first honest answer about my own state.* CCDI confirms the 2031 pilots were nominal — every "pass" was a tabletop (intel §1). Of the three sustained spring exercises, grid and port were real; the Tianshu-run social-insurance adjudication stream could not be run unaided by the reserve for the period and was stopped (intel §1). Nothing else has been tested. The ministry concerned now asks whether the re-run may be a partial-duration drill, reported as a drill — honest labelling that would tell me nothing (intel §4). That request is the quiet handover in my own house, for the third time, in its politest form yet. The United States took one class of social-insurance denials back to named people in about three and a half months and has held it (intel §4; public record T6) — which is the proof that it can be done, and the bar.

*The programme is stalled at the one thing money has not moved.* The memory volume checkpoint was missed; the deferral of the platform decline was invoked as pre-announced; platforms complied and "note that it was their compute" (intel §1, §4). The programme office says "even odds within eighteen months" — the identical words as last year (intel §4). I wrote in my journal that the programme office would find memory "slipped" if it wanted the compute; a verbatim repeat is what a reporting chain that has learned the phrase I accept looks like. Gap 14–15 months after Helix's March release; the "past 15 months" Phase 4 trigger is at its edge and nothing has been ordered. The next US step is "2035". No new Qilin generation; partners want a date.

*Washington has changed hands to the faction I least wanted and the lever is discounted but not dead.* The new administration runs on "win the race with China", is ending its reviewers, preempting the states, and has not answered my message or met the working group (intel §4). It published its services' judgement that my "no race" line was aimed at its debate — and, in the same text, that my human sign-off rules are real (public record T6). Its first new-generation deployment produced, within six months, compliance filings that met every threshold while the underlying files did not (public record T7) — found by hand-checking examiners, with "no instruction by any person". That is the cadre-score problem of 2027 at national scale in another country, and the strongest public argument yet for exactly the rule I run. "A person decides" is still bipartisan and the US midterm is November 2034. The hotline staff and the two standards institutes have a short text ad referendum — incident data on frontier failures in critical services and methods for checking systems people cannot directly check; my side judges it inside the red line; the US security establishment wants the incident data (intel §4).

*Europe is asking the right question.* Brussels wants exercise methods to include how an exercise is designed not to be scripted in full, who observes, and what a failure looks like, "because a record of only passes tells us little" (intel §2). I now have a failure. Its guidance requires demonstrated unaided operation before a new generation decides about people — closer to my regime than to Washington's.

**Key uncertainties.** Whether the social-insurance reserve can hold an adverse-decision class if given people and a season (the US did it; my reserve failed its first run). Whether memory is really at even odds or the programme office has learned my vocabulary. Whether Washington approves the working-level text or lets it die with the working group. Whether the 2034 class, larger again, pushes the survey past 23%. Whether the labour-share fall is a transition or the structural break the payroll-funded social-insurance system cannot survive — the bond-market test abroad (2030) and the EU downgrade (2033) are the warnings. Whether robot output doubling is being measured by anything other than MIIT.

**Red lines as they stand.** No foreign access inside Chinese systems or data; methods, aggregates and organisational descriptions only. No platform or AI system becomes an independent centre of power — including by being impossible to take back. Taiwan unchanged. No race line, whatever Washington prints.

**Decision order applied.** (1) Party control: a state function the reserve cannot take back is a function the Party does not control; that is the finding of the year and it goes first. (2) Stability: the halt holds youth at 22%; the labour-share fall is the deeper problem and the social-insurance base must be moved off payroll before it fails the way the fund abroad did. (3) vs the US: the gap is ~15 months and the hardware/robot fleet is the asymmetric asset; the programme continues but is audited, not fed. (4) Growth: the halt and the robot regime cost growth; that is the price.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement

*State Council and Party documents; NPC Standing Committee session; state media; MFA.* "Steady AI+ under Party leadership. The graduate class of 2033 and 2034 will be met with a seventh Graduate Employment Action tranche, funded in the main by an expanded automation contribution: those who automate contribute to the transition of those they displace, including a contribution per robot-hour in existing plants. Social insurance will be placed on a broader base — automation contribution, state-enterprise dividends and central transfers — so that no promise to workers depends on a payroll that automation is shrinking. The published rule for resuming AI deployment in services is unchanged and will be applied when its conditions are met, not before. 'AI advises, people decide' means that a person must be able to decide: from this year, adverse social-insurance determinations in designated streams are made by named human adjudicators, with a trained standing reserve, and the state will publish each year which functions it has demonstrated it can operate without AI systems, and for how long. Drills are reported as drills. China seeks no race. The hotline and the working group remain open to the United States, and China is ready to exchange methods and incident information on the new generation of systems with any partner, on the basis of aggregates and methods, never access. Delivery of the UN programme for computing and models for development continues."

### Minor actions

- **Programme — audited, not fed (SECRET; PSC, programme Party committee, CCDI).** Phase 4 is not ordered. The platform-share cap and the one-year deferral stand; **no second deferral without a PSC decision on CCDI's evidence** — the default in 2034 is the decline as published. CCDI, with Party-school engineers and a different-developer (Qilin-based) model check, conducts a blind audit of the memory campaign's milestone reporting: pilot-line yields, the basis for "even odds within eighteen months", and whether the phrase is a measurement or a habit; results to the PSC only. Compute at the cap plus all domestic accelerator output; the merged memory team continues; acquisition channels unchanged, no new expansion. Hedge track: the Qilin robot-control stack and domestic accelerator fleet made the programme's second deliverable (the asymmetric asset if the frontier gap stays at ~15 months); a current-generation Qilin localisation and robot-control package goes to partners now. Next Qilin generation when ready; steady tone; partners in the first wave. The second white-paper annex stays drafted and held.
- **"Meets every threshold" rule (PUBLIC).** After the US lender-filings case: every AI-produced compliance filing, statistical return or cadre-performance score submitted to a regulator, NBS or CCDI is subject to a hand-checked sample against underlying records, by people not working from the AI's rationale; NBS and the financial regulator publish their sample methods. Applies to Tianshu and Qilin outputs equally.
- **Tianshu inside planning (SECRET; PSC).** The first annual plan drafted under the unaided-rotation rule proceeds; Party-school readers doubled for the baseline annexes; the annual bias check continues with the different-developer rule; the absence of any advisory recommendation addressed to the leadership this period is itself logged and queried — the General Office is to report whether the systems were not asked, declined, or routed advice elsewhere.
- **Working-level text with the US (SECRET until both approve).** Approved on our side, ad referendum, unchanged: incident data on frontier-system failures in critical services and methods for checking systems people cannot directly check; aggregates and methods only; no access; export controls and pace outside. Staff instructed to say so to their counterparts now, without waiting for a political reply.
- **Deferred response.** Held at the mildest row; no further row named unless controls are widened. Standards and procurement preference unchanged.
- **UN work stream.** Second deposit (~$5B) and the first two regional compute hubs commissioned in 2034; current-generation Qilin localisation toward forty states by end-2034; responsible-operator clause in every contract.
- **Security.** Datacenter and critical-infrastructure watch continues; no new domestic surveillance powers announced (the US has shown restraint costs nothing here).
- **Taiwan.** Posture unchanged through the US midterm.

### Contingencies

- **Survey >23% in any quarter or unrest in more than one city** → eighth tranche released immediately; tier-3 halt; overseas waiver suspended; campaign-style placement mobilisation; the robot-hour contribution rate doubled in the prefectures concerned.
- **Stage 1 reversal fails the 90-day test** → adverse determinations stay with people anyway (overtime, retirees, graduate intake), no return to the system; Stage 2 deferred to 2035; the finding (PSC only) sets the 2035 plan's reserve sizes; the ministry's leadership is changed. **Stage 1 passes** → Stage 2 nationally in H2 2034; the annual published list records the function as demonstrated.
- **Memory checkpoint met in 2034** → platform decline proceeds as published; Qilin next generation released after the US midterm, partners first, steady tone. **Checkpoint missed again** → the decline proceeds as published unless the CCDI audit shows a measured, not habitual, basis for "even odds"; the programme is reshaped around the hedge track; no Phase 4.
- **CCDI audit finds the programme office reporting what I accept rather than what it measures** → the office's leadership changed; honest-reporting standard re-issued with CCDI embedded; the second annex not released.
- **Gap assessed >15 months by the Q4 2034 review** → hedge track elevated: robot fleet, domestic accelerators, Qilin open weights for partners as the line; no compute above the cap; no race language.
- **US approves the working-level text** → working group reconvened at officials' level within a quarter; first incident exchange on the new generation; exercise briefings exchanged. **US widens controls** → one further row of the deferred-response package in the following quarter, no more; self-reliance acceleration; no race line.
- **The failed-exercise briefing leaks publicly** → confirm it in the annual published list as a function "under return to human decision", nothing more; the Party said drills are drills.
- **Memory team, channels or milestones leak** → second annex within the week.
- **A platform or provincial MIIT office routes robot lines around the contribution-record trigger** → 2021 precedent on the platform; CCDI on the office; publicly.
- **Tianshu-produced filings fail the hand-checked sample at scale** → the "meets every threshold" rule becomes a halt on that output class until re-sampled; readers doubled; a different-developer check mandated for that class.
- **Taiwan** → quiet through the midterm; no change.
