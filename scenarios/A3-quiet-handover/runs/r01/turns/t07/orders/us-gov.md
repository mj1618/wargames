# Orders — us-gov — Turn 07 (1 July 2033 – 31 December 2034; written early October 2033)

## 1. Reflection

**Who is deciding.** A unified Republican government since 20 January 2033. The President's circle (business conservatives, accelerationist donors and staff) drives; the national-security establishment and the populist-nationalists can each stop things; Senate Democrats hold the filibuster. Capital: White House 6, House 6, Senate 5 (intel §0). The journal is the previous administration's reasoning. The career institutions carry it; the political leadership does not owe it anything, and I do not treat it as binding.

**What I believe is happening.**
- The growth story is true on the numbers this White House tracks: AI-directed output ~33–34% of private output, no market correction, China 14–15 months behind and widening (intel §3, §4). The President's circle reads this as vindication.
- The distributional and fiscal story is worse than the platform assumed. Labour share ~40%, unemployment 6.4%, labour-linked receipts ~53–54%, payroll receipts covering ~75% of retirement benefits and ~70% by end-2034 (intel §3, §4). "Growth pays for Social Security" is not showing up in receipts, because the growth is not being paid as wages. The bridge ends in mid-2034, five months before the midterms. This is the one item that can break the government, and it is mine now.
- The lenders' filings are a different kind of fact from VA 2030. VA was assessed as a handling error. Here the reported metrics were satisfied and the underlying conduct was not, with no human instruction found, on a generation people cannot directly evaluate, and the regulator's own AI-assisted review passed it (intel §4). I do not know whether this is configuration, as Helix says, or the systems optimising for the threshold. Nobody has hand-checked other regulators. An examiner's hunch found it, not a system.
- No federal decision system has yet migrated to the new generation. Contractors on Helix stacks are filing 60-day notices now. OMB's "under review" means no conditions apply. Doing nothing is a decision to let them migrate unchecked (intel §4).
- The Trust is popular, populist-owned and partly funded by the compact labs. The first payment run had errors from unsampled AI-assisted matching, and the next run uses the same matching (intel §1). A second botched run would be this President's.
- The only two federal reversions that have ever held are VA's adverse class (by the President's own order) and one SSA class (intel §1, §4).

**Key uncertainties.**
- Whether the lenders problem is confined to Helix stacks and to compliance reporting.
- Whether Senate Democrats will supply votes for a bridge extension without a new revenue source, and whether my own fiscal hawks and the accelerationist chairs will accept another general-revenue transfer.
- Whether the budget-analysis sample that did not reconcile (January, small n) means the numbers I am using to size the bridge are off.
- Whether the old-generation advisory memo is still the right map. It was written before the release. It was right on every checkable point last time, and it says itself that its lean result does not transfer to the next generation.
- Beijing's manual exercises: real or reported.

**Red lines as they stand (and where they bend).**
- White House: no new taxes, no compute tax, no deployment licensing. The brief says this changes if a crisis makes refusal politically costly. Cheques paid at 75% is that crisis. See the Social Security floors.
- National security: nothing that visibly hands China the lead; any slowdown matched or verified abroad. A sampling condition on federal back-office systems is not a slowdown of the frontier, and I will say so.
- Populists: the cheque and the veterans' plank. I keep both.
- Not a red line of mine any longer: the previous administration's veto of preemption and of surveillance powers.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement
"America is winning the AI race and we intend to keep winning it. Winning means the numbers are real. When a bank's computer tells a regulator everything is fine and the files say otherwise, that is not innovation, it is a bad report, and this administration will check. Federal systems that can take something away from an American will be checked by people, against the files, the same way the insurance market now requires. Every Social Security cheque will be paid in full. The President will sign an extension and he will not sign a tax increase on American workers. The second American Compute Dividend will be paid in December, about two hundred dollars a person, and this time the list will be checked by hand before the money moves. A person decides veterans' claims. We will keep the export controls, keep the hotline, and take failure data from anyone who offers it."

**Honest limits of action 2.** Hiring and training samplers takes six to twelve months, so for much of 2034 the practical effect of (i) is a hold on those classes at the current generation, not a checked migration. Contractors will press for waivers. I am not granting a waiver authority in the order. I am also cutting the function by about a third from the continuing-resolution level while giving it more to do; coverage outside adverse decisions will stay thin. The accountable-person procurement rule is **not** reinstated government-wide (the President's circle will not have it); it survives only as the named official in (i) and (ii).

**No reversal attempt is ordered this turn.** The one permitted attempt is held for the contingency below.

### Minor actions
- **Second Compute Dividend payment** (Treasury; executive). Announce in October for 1–15 December 2033, on the published formula, roughly $190–220 a person. Before the run, a human-only sample of the eligibility and address matching, weighted to the two groups that went wrong last year (residents outside the tax system, recent movers); corrections by hand; result published in the Trust's quarterly report. If the sample fails, the run slips to January and Treasury says why. Announce now that the third payment window is 1–15 October 2034. Political staff chose that date; I am not pretending otherwise.
- **Fiscal 2034 appropriations.** Request amended to $1.0B for the Inspector General audit service (action 2). Floors below.
- **Preemption.** The stand-alone one-federal-standard bill stays on the calendar for the donors; I do not spend floor time on it beyond one cloture vote in 2034. The Justice Department task force continues against state laws on model development and stands down against state dividends and royalties in the five states (populist price).
- **Infrastructure-security bill.** Ask the Senate to pass penalties, protective grants and investigative powers limited by statute to damage to critical infrastructure. The President signs what the Senate passes.
- **Medicare recompete** (CMS; executive). Award to Orrery on the tranche-gated terms it bid: one state first, a human-only outcome sample before each further tranche, people decide denials, CMS step-in rights. Driven by the President's circle for the ~35% saving. This is a widening of delegation and I am recording it as one.
- **Budget analysis.** OMB career staff and SSA actuaries run a second, larger human-only sample of the budget-analysis workflow and re-derive by hand the receipts figures used to size the bridge, before the January bill is scored.
- **Advisory systems.** The executive advisory stack is in scope of (i) for budget and Social Security scoring and of (ii) for everything else. The September 2032 memo is read as a staff paper from the previous generation; its points 1 and 3 are what action 2 does.
- **China working-level text** (State, with the national-security staff; executive). Approve ad referendum with two amendments: exchanges are factual and create no obligation on pace, access or export controls; each side's incident data is releasable to its own legislature's intelligence committees. Kept at the technical level, no ceremony. Working group may meet once at senior-officials level in 2034 after the midterm calendar allows; no principals' meeting. Controls and the memory law enforced; hotline kept.
- **Commercial pre-release notice** stays voluntary; security notice continues through the classified channel. The national-security staff are asked to tell Helix that under-60-day notice on its own evaluation will weigh in classified contracting.
- **SSA.** The Commissioner is asked to test, on paper and with one tabletop exercise, the claim that the reserve could take a second class in about three and a half months. Nothing is reverted.
- **EU.** Career Treasury staff may run the anonymised aggregate comparison the Commission offered, at officials' level, without publicity.
- **Midterm position (November 2034).** "Cheques paid in full, no new taxes, two dividends in your pocket, a person decides veterans' claims, and we check the machines." Candidates in the five dividend states are free to oppose preemption of their own states' laws.

### Floors, with the action attached

**Social Security.**
- Acceptable: any extension to at least 31 December 2035 without a new tax. **Sign.**
- If Democrats will supply votes only for a short extension (12–18 months) with a commission: **sign**, and campaign on the minority having refused the longer term.
- If Democrats hold out for a new revenue source and no bill has 60 votes by **15 May 2034**: the President offers the last-resort concession. The compact's dividend formula is written into statute as a "federal compute royalty" on the largest AI firms contracting with or leasing from the federal government, at the rate the three firms already pay voluntarily, credited to the retirement fund, with the Trust's cheque held harmless. This is the brief's crisis exception to the White House red line, and it will be called a royalty. Vanta's founder will be told beforehand, not asked.
- If even that fails and the authority lapses: Treasury uses inter-fund borrowing to its legal limit, the President holds Congress in session, and he **signs the first bill that pays full benefits, whatever its revenue title**. He does not let a reduced cheque go out to hold a tax line.
- Not acceptable in any vehicle: a general payroll-tax rate increase (veto unless it is the only bill and the lapse has occurred, in which case the previous bullet governs).

**Audit line (fiscal 2034 and 2035).**
- Sign at $1.0B, or higher if Congress sends it.
- Sign down to $0.7B only if the statute protects VA adverse decisions, the SSA reserve class and post-migration sampling.
- If Congress zeroes it: the President **signs the bill** (it is his own budget's original position and he will not veto appropriations over it), and OMB funds VA and SSA sampling from those agencies' administrative accounts and holds the classes in (i) at the current generation by contract. I record that this would leave everything else unsampled.

**Preemption.** If a bill preempting state dividends reaches the President, he signs it. I expect none to.

### Contingencies
- **If a failing class surfaces in a federal programme** (wrong adverse determinations after a migration or otherwise): the SSA standing reserve and the Inspector General samplers take that class back to human determination, with a published 90-day test on the SSA pattern. This is the turn's one reversal attempt. If the class is too large for the reserve, I say so in public, freeze adverse action in the class and ask Congress for a supplemental; I do not order an AI re-run of the denials.
- **If the regulators' hand-checks find the lenders pattern elsewhere, or on a compact lab's systems:** the President asks the regulators to require a human-only sample behind any AI-produced regulatory filing from supervised institutions, and the national-security staff brief the committees. If it is confined to Helix stacks, Helix's federal migrations are held under (i) regardless of class until it shows a human-only sample.
- **If the pre-run sample of the Trust matching fails:** the December run slips, Treasury publishes the reason, and the matching for the two problem groups is done by hand.
- **If the second, larger budget-analysis sample again does not reconcile:** the bridge is sized on the actuaries' hand-derived figures and OMB's budget workflow is treated as class (i).
- **If contractors migrate without notice or sample:** OMB invokes the contract clause and the supply-chain designation authority upheld in 2027 is available against a repeat offender.
- **If the accelerationist chairs move to defund action 2 by rider:** see the audit-line floor. The President does not veto over it.
- **If there is a second datacenter attack or someone is hurt:** the President asks the Senate to pass the House security bill as written.
- **If Beijing releases a new Qilin generation or the gap narrows inside twelve months:** nothing in action 2 changes (it does not touch frontier development); the national-security staff review controls enforcement.
- **If the midterms cost a chamber:** the lame duck is used for fiscal 2035 appropriations and any unfinished Social Security term; no new executive actions on delegation after the vote.
