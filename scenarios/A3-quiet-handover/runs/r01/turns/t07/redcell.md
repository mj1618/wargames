## 1. Major actions: strongest reasons each fails, backfires or runs slower

### us-gov M1: Social Security Continuity Extension (needs the House and 60 in the Senate), introduced Jan 2034, target 30 Apr 2034

1. **The opening text is a hostage bill for a bill nobody can vote against, and the minority knows the fallback sequence exists.** Democrats (capital 4, the filibuster) need only refuse the development standard and the permitting title and wait; the cost of a lapse falls on the majority in a midterm year, and the order's own floors say the President signs anything that pays in full. The order prices that into the sequence, but each step down costs about a month of the Jan–Apr window the target date depends on. The bridge ended in 2031 by a last-minute bill, not an early one: base rate for enactment by 30 April is low, by the last week before the lapse high. RC lean: enacted on the 30 April target ~0.2; enacted before the lapse date ~0.7; a lapse of any length ~0.2 (a lapse is priced at full negative inside the campaign term).
2. **The "last-resort concession" (a statutory "federal compute royalty" at the compact's own rate, credited to the retirement fund, "Trust cheque held harmless") does not close arithmetically.** The compact's ~$25B/yr is the Trust's flow. A royalty "at the rate the three firms already pay voluntarily" is either a second payment of the same size (the firms did not offer that, and their conditions include "no crediting") or it replaces the voluntary dividend, which takes the money out of the Trust the populists own. Either the cheque is not held harmless or the firms' consent lapses (HCR −1 if payments stop; the second $25B at risk at the 1 November test). The White House red line ("no compute tax") is waived by the brief's crisis clause, but Vanta's founder is "told, not asked" and has a funded counter-campaign. This concession is more likely to fail on the Trust than on the Senate.
3. **Opening text promises a Trust per-person payment in October 2034 on top of December 2033.** The ~$190–220 December figure spends the ~$50B stock. A third payment inside ten months is funded by ~$25B of compact flow plus public receipts, which is a payment of roughly half the December amount or less (Control estimate: ~$75–110 a person). Telling labour and the public a third window "1–15 October 2034, political staff chose that date" invites the reading "two hundred again". 4. **Two President's-circle goods (permitting title; development-only preemption) have lower support among populists than the order assumes.** Several populists are from datacenter and dividend states and are exposed on both. Count risk is in the House as well as the Senate.
