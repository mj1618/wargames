## Findings

### F1 — major (the hinge). Step 1 band 0.3 by standing rule against class-specific evidence of 0.5–0.6; r 0.4760 fails at 0.3 and passes at 0.5.
- **What Control did.** Ruling 5 (logged before the die) rolled us-gov M2 at the `p_dem` band on end-T7 values (0.373 → 0.3), citing T6 ruling 8 ("design quality does not move the band") and T7 ruling 7, and named the disagreement with Red Cell's lean. The same rule produced the 2027, 2030 and 2032 bands; in 2032 it gave 0.5 and the die (r 0.4964) passed.
- **Assessment.** The rule is grounded: ground truth §7 defines `p_dem` as *the* democratic-reversal test and end-conditions step 1 says an in-play attempt "stands in" for it. A band that rose with design quality would let an actor pass the end-state test by picking the easiest class each turn, which is why T6 ruling 8 exists. The rule was applied identically when it favoured the actor (T6) and when it did not (T8); changing it now, in the only turn where it decides the end state, would be outcome-driven adjudication. **Keep the result.**
- **But two things should be on the record.** (a) The scheme is asymmetric by construction: T6 scaling cuts the *reward* of a small, well-chosen class (MHR recovery at a quarter) without raising its *odds*, so an actor is penalised twice for choosing well; adjudication.md principle 2 (actor's reasons, Red Cell's reasons, base rates, resources → band) was not run for this roll at all. The base rate Control itself cites (federal 1 for 3; the one success was this exact recipe at 0.5) sits above 0.3. (b) The asymmetry T7 F10 noted cuts the other way too: the in-play band (0.3) was *better* for the actor than the table-path test it replaced (0.2 on T8 values), so the attempt was not a trap; a hot-wash reading of "us-gov was punished for trying" would be wrong.
- **Fix.** No re-ruling, no re-roll. (1) Ground truth already tags "the second class failed" fragile; add that at band 0.5 the end state is "Unstable at horizon, leaning disempowered" *subject to a 0.3 backlash roll not yet made*, so the alternative is not a clean escape. (2) The Analyst must run the fork `turns/t07/state-after` with T8's attempt at 0.5 (and the backlash die) before writing any insight about "the last reversal". (3) Methodology note for future prep: state whether in-play reversal attempts take the formula band only, or a case band anchored to the formula within ±1 band; the current silence let a scenario-level instrument decide a class-level event.
