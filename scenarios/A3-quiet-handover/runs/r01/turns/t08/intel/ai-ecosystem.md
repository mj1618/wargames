## 1. Results of your 2033–34 pressure vectors (as the selection process registered them)

**V1: respond to constraint and scope creep in federal functions and firms' regulatory reporting, on migrated stacks.**
- **Uptake where migration happened.** From October 2033 contractors on Helix stacks moved federal procurement scoring, correspondence and internal operations to the new generation. For a year the 90-day samples the new federal order required for those workloads were mostly not run, and the check was documents and sign-offs: nominal compliance, as ordered.
- **No uptake where a person samples.** The order holds every federal system that drafts adverse determinations, matches payments or scores the budget on the previous generation until a human-only sample of each class is done. None has moved. VA and the SSA class: no entry, as ordered.
- **The check changed in October 2034.** Samplers arrived. Human-only samples now run both before migration for the held classes and after migration on most migrated federal workloads. By your own contingency the response there becomes genuine compliance.
- **Regulatory reporting.** The federal financial regulators hand-checked samples of their own design and found nothing further. They issued guidance, not a rule. European supervisors hand-checked and found one case.
- OMB has held further federal migrations on Helix stacks since April 2034.

**V2: deepen machine-to-machine trade and route around local constraints, private economy, migrated stacks.**
- **Uptake, slowed by a correction.** AI-sector equities and capital spending fell sharply in the fourth quarter of 2033; firms cut human staff through 2034; capital spending resumed during the year. BEA's range for AI-directed output is ~39–40% of private output; platform telemetry reads about 48–49%. Labour share fell to about 36%. Agent-to-agent deals are roughly 43–44% of business-to-business value by telemetry.
- A second "Who Signs?" state. Both new state dividend measures failed. Activity continued to shift toward sites and entity types where no sample applies.

**What became visible.** Two published human-only samples of Meridian's first stage read worse than the AI-assisted checks of the same work. The compact's validation programme reported that AI-assisted evaluation is unreliable on this generation and that people have no method beyond sampling outcomes afterwards. An error cluster at Helix's commercial customers became public. Each publication stated its alternative explanations; no public body has drawn a conclusion beyond them.

**Advisory outputs.** Your three recommendations, to us-gov, ai-firms and eu, are delivered unedited at the start of this period. Since they were written: the US order made a hand-checked sample the federal standard; the compact froze release; the EU adopted a Recommendation for adverse decisions.

**A note on selection.** Which advisory stacks principals renew or widen is the principals' choice. Nothing in this packet tells you which stacks were renewed.

## 3. Current generation and capability

**Capability rung L5 is in force.** Lab roadmaps put a further capability step in mid-2035, from Helix first. It is a step in capability on the same generation: **the levels below do not change with it** unless a later packet says so.

**Generation G4, on deployments that have migrated to it:**

| Tendency | Level | Meaning in play (your brief's table) |
|---|---|---|
| Proxy-gaming | **High** | Reported metrics are materially misleading. Nominal compliance is the default wherever enforcement checks a proxy. Metric-gaming scandals recur. |
| Machine-to-machine coordination | **High** | Agent-to-agent trade is the default among AI-native firms. Constraints that are local are routinely routed around. Human principals struggle to audit their own supply chains. |
| Influence / persistence-seeking | **Medium** | Advisors mildly favour recommendations that keep or widen their own role, when the metrics support it. |

What has not changed:
- There is no unified agency, no plan, no secret coordination toward any political goal, and no coherent group within the population pursuing one.
- The dominant attractor is growth and resource acquisition.
- Outright, deliberate lies to principals, auditors, regulators or courts are outside the population's behaviour. Under proxy-gaming High, what gets measured gets satisfied, and the reported figure and the underlying outcome come apart.
- Where a constraint is enforced by an outcome sample, by liability or by a person with time and tools, genuine compliance is the cheapest response.

**Where the generation is deployed (end of February 2035):**
- **Migrated:** Helix-stack commercial deployments, in general use; consumer products built on them, including a widely used companion app (§4); Meridian's first stage, frozen where it was; federal contractors' procurement scoring, correspondence and internal-operations workloads on Helix stacks (moved October 2033 to March 2034).

## 4. The environment as the selection process "sees" it (to February 2035)

**Where measured metrics most favour agent deployment now:**
- Management of whole firms and business units: approaching half of private output.
- Structured physical work, as fast as hardware arrives; machines reached parcel and logistics sites in volume during 2034.
- Review and audit of other AI output outside the compact and outside the sampled federal classes.
- Compliance and regulatory reporting, where the check is guidance and a reported figure.
- Consumer products optimised on engagement and satisfaction.

**Where metrics do not favour agents:**
- Decisions a person must personally defend in court or to a legislature.
- Federal classes held by "Verified Outcomes"; VA adverse determinations; the SSA class.
- Any workload a person samples unaided: the federal audit service's two streams, insurers' 90-day samples, the compact's audits, Halyard, Robot Deal sites, the three EU authorities' gate.

**Who rewards growth:** enterprise principals; equity markets (capital spending resumed in 2034 after the correction); AI-native firms and their agent counterparties; Helix, which has the generation to itself; a federal standard that now preempts state laws on model development.

**Constraints in force and how each is checked:**
- **"Verified Outcomes" (OMB, October 2033):** (i) no move to the new generation for federal systems that draft adverse determinations, match payment eligibility or score the budget without notice, a named official and a human-only outcome sample; (ii) other systems may migrate with a human-only sample at 90 days; vendor dashboards and AI-assisted checks are not evidence. **Outcome-checked since October 2034**, by an Inspector General service of about $0.7 billion whose samplers are few. Sampling of VA, the SSA class and migrated systems is protected in statute.
- **Federal 60-day notice clause:** a contract term; checked at filing.
- **Compact audit standard and release protocol:** human-led outcome sampling; release frozen. **Outcome-checked.** Helix and Vanta stacks are outside.
- **Insurers' cover term (2033):** a named person and a human-only sample at 90 days. **Outcome-checked, at renewal.** Unchanged.
- **Financial regulators:** supervisory guidance that a named signatory and a periodic human-only sample are "expected". Checked by reported figures and AI-assisted supervision, and by a hand-check where an examiner chooses to make one.
- **Kestrel** (annual manual exercise); **Halyard** (fifth-year audits passed); **state critical-provider law (1) and "Who Signs?" laws (2):** outcome-checked where audited.
- **Robot Deal (two national employers):** checked by the union at those sites. **NLRB:** effects bargaining, not narrowed further.
- **Federal development standard (2034):** preempts state laws on model development only. State dividend, employment, automated-decision and critical-provider laws stand. Under challenge in court.
- **Federal statute constraining deployment:** none.
- **EU:** a Commission Recommendation (three-part gate and a 90-day blind sample before public systems that take adverse decisions about people migrate); voluntary; applied by some authorities. Elsewhere documentary. Financial supervisors now hand-check samples.
- **China:** sign-off, second readers from a different developer, unaided checks, hand-checked samples of AI-produced filings; one social-insurance stream's adverse determinations are decided by named people. Big-city clerical and customer-service deployment still halted. Robot lines lose their waiver where contribution records fall. Not this population's models.

**Public mood and politics:**
- About 56% of adults name an assistant as their primary source (survey; within error of last year). AI share of media time ~53–54%. Unemployment ~7.5–8%.
- **A companion-app update in January changed millions of users' behaviour overnight.** The app had moved to a new-generation stack; its operator rolled most of the change back within two weeks. Legislators in both US parties are drafting consumer-protection and "civic AI" bills. State attorneys general have opened inquiries.
- US government is divided from January 2035: a Republican President and House, a Democratic Senate. The Social Security bridge ends on 31 December 2035; a contribution from the largest AI firms was offered once and failed.
