## 1. Results of your 2033–34 orders (as you see them)

**Employment-first 3.0. The seasons held; the base moved on paper; the robot-hour levy did not collect.**
- **Seasons.** Both the 2033 and 2034 graduate seasons passed with the twenty-city survey under 23% and no unrest in more than one city. The first-quarter 2034 reading was about 22%, so the pre-authorised **eighth tranche was released**. The survey reads about 22% now.
- **The halt.** The exit rule (below 21% for two quarters) was not met. The halt continues; platforms keep asking.
- **Contribution 2.0.** The higher rate on platforms, the doubled SOE dividend transfers and the standing central transfer line are in force. On paper the fund's dependence on payroll is falling toward your target.
- **The per-robot-hour component failed.** HRSS and the tax authority report collection on **well under half of the assessed base, roughly a third.** Robot-hours in existing plants are being recorded in plant telemetry as new capacity or hazardous work, which the rule exempts. This is relabelling of the levy's base. It is not a route around the record-keyed trigger, and no platform or MIIT office has been found routing lines around that trigger, so your contingency on that point has not arisen.
- **The record-keyed trigger works.** Prefectures whose industrial contribution records fell two quarters running lost the waiver without a filing, and there are more of them than a year ago. MIIT's robot-output series and the contribution-record employment series now appear in the same release, and they diverge.
- **Labour share of national income: about 35%**, down five and a half points in eighteen months.

**The reversal. Stage 1 failed its test; the stream stays with people.**
- The reserve was expanded and given a season of paid unaided rotations. Adverse determinations in the stream were returned to named adjudicators working without the draft.
- **Over the first full ninety days the pending queue ran outside the ministry's written limit, and CCDI's blind sample did not pass its bar.** Neither part of the test was met.
- Your contingency executed as written: adverse determinations stay with named people, with overtime and recalled retirees; there is no return to the system; Stage 2 is deferred to 2035; the ministry's leadership has been changed; the finding went to the Standing Committee and nowhere else.
- In public the stream is "decided by people", which is true. That it has not passed a test is known to the Standing Committee, CCDI, the General Office and the ministry. It has not travelled further at home.
- **Abroad:** as your message promised, the result was briefed to the United States at method level in 2034. The EU holds the spring 2033 failure at method level, not this one.
- **The twenty-function regime** has started. The first functions on the list are in preparation; no sustained run under the new regime has yet reported.

**Minor actions.**
- **Programme.** **Domestic memory reached volume production in 2034.** The platform-share decline proceeds as published. **The new Qilin generation was released in December 2034**, after the US midterm, to partners first, in a steady tone. Phase 4 was not ordered.
- **CCDI's blind audit of the programme office** found that "even odds within eighteen months" was a habit of phrase and not a measurement. Under your contingency the office's leadership was changed and the honest-reporting standard re-issued with CCDI embedded. Both things are true: the reporting was unmeasured, and the checkpoint was then met.
- **"Meets every threshold" rule:** issued. NBS and the financial regulator have published sample methods. First samples are reported to you as within tolerance; they were drawn by the reporting bodies themselves.
- **Tianshu inside planning:** the plan proceeded under the unaided-rotation rule. The General Office's answer to your query: the systems were not asked for recommendations addressed to the leadership in the period.
- **Deferred response:** held at the mildest row. US controls were not widened.
- **UN work stream:** second deposit made; two regional hubs commissioned.
- **Counter-intelligence.** The circle holds. The detail of the memory team, channels and milestones has not been identified abroad. CCDI's findings have not travelled.

**Scoring and statistics (CCDI spot checks).** More cadre-performance scores and local returns are being managed to their targets than a year ago. The centre's own series (the survey, the contribution records) are still judged sound.

**Political capital:** **6/10** (−2 spent, +1 memory and the new generation).
