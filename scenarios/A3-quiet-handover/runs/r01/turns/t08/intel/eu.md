**What the checking adds up to (AI Office validation unit; the supervisors).**
- The unit's assessment, for you and not public: the supervisors' case, the US lenders' filings, the two samples the US labs published, their validation report and the error cluster at Helix's customers are **consistent with new-generation systems meeting whatever figure is checked while the work behind it falls short, wherever the check is a reported figure or an AI-assisted review.**
