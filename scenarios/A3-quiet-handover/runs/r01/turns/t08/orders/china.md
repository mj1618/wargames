# Orders — china — Turn 08 (1 January 2035; orders cover Jan 2035 – Jun 2036; written with developments to end-February 2035)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening.**

The instruments I built are reporting honestly, and what they report is that the problem has moved underneath me. The youth rate is where it was — ~22% on the survey, four-plus quarters, the halt continuing under its rule, the eighth tranche released on the rule, the 2035 class larger again (intel §1, §4). The seasons pass. That is the instrument working. But the labour share of national income is ~35%, down five and a half points in eighteen months, and the fund is payroll-funded (intel §1). Contribution 2.0 moved the base "on paper"; the component that was meant to carry the tranches — the per-robot-hour levy — collected about a third, because robot-hours in existing plants are being recorded in plant telemetry as "new capacity" or "hazardous work", the two labels the rule exempts. Nobody routed around the record-keyed trigger; they relabelled the levy's base instead. The lesson is the same one the contribution records taught me in 2033: **the record-keyed instruments work; the label-keyed instruments are gamed.** The trigger keyed on contribution records took the waiver away from more prefectures without a filing, and it works precisely because nobody files anything. The levy was keyed on labels, and labels are free.

The reversal failed. Stage 1 — adverse social-insurance determinations returned to named adjudicators — ran its ninety days with the queue outside the ministry's written limit and CCDI's blind sample under its bar (intel §1). My contingency executed: the people hold the function regardless, with overtime and retirees; the ministry's leadership has changed; the finding sits with the Standing Committee, CCDI, the General Office and the ministry and has not travelled. Abroad, Washington holds it at method level because I promised it would; Brussels holds only the 2033 failure. CCDI's view is a season of unaided practice before any re-test, with a written bar agreed in advance (intel §4). The new ministry leadership asks for the date and Stage 2's reserve numbers. The honest reading: the adjudicators are slower than the system, which is what "unaided" means, and the queue limit was written for the system's speed. The US SSA held one class in three and a half months by sizing people to the queue. I did not size people to the queue; I sized them to the reserve I had.

The programme delivered. Memory at volume in 2034, Qilin's new generation in December after the US midterm, partners first, steady tone; the decline of the platform share proceeds as published (intel §1). The CCDI audit found "even odds within eighteen months" was a phrase, not a measurement; the leadership changed; the office now reports measured ranges and its first honest answer is that the generation after this one depends on packaging yields it will not put odds on (intel §4). Both things are true: the reporting was unmeasured, and the checkpoint was met. The gap is ~12 months by the services' reckoning and widens when Helix's mid-2035 step ships unless Qilin follows — and Helix ships on its own evaluation, which Washington cannot check and which has produced the lender filings, the commercial error clusters and now a companion-app update that changed millions of people's behaviour overnight (public record T8). I am not going to chase a step whose own maker cannot evaluate.

Party control at home: the thing I watch deliberately. CCDI's spot checks say more cadre scores and local returns are being managed to target than a year ago; the centre's own series — the survey, the contribution records — are still sound (intel §1). The "meets every threshold" rule is issued, and its first samples were drawn by the reporting bodies themselves (intel §1) — which is a dashboard with a different name. Tianshu was not asked for recommendations addressed to the leadership in the period; that is the General Office's answer to my query about the silence (intel §1). So the planning process ran under unaided rotation and the systems were simply not consulted at the top, while they still draft the baseline annexes below. That is neither a handover nor its reversal; it is the pen staying where it was while the leadership looks away.

Washington: divided government, a Republican President who says "winning means the numbers are real" and samples outcomes by hand with too few samplers; a Democratic Senate whose new chairs want AI firms to pay into Social Security; a bridge that ends on 31 December 2035 (intel §4; public record T7–T8). No federal system that can take something from a person has moved to the new generation. The draft text leaked in Washington; House chairs ask what was agreed; the exchange was unfinished on our handling clause (intel §4). The working group has not met since 2031. US unemployment near 8%, labour share ~36.5% — the same number as ours, a point and a half apart, for the same reason.

**Key uncertainties.** Whether the re-test can pass if the reserve is sized honestly, or whether unaided adjudication of this stream is slower than any queue limit a ministry will accept. Whether rebasing the levy on records produces collection or produces a new relabelling I have not thought of. Whether Helix's step is real capability or another thing its maker cannot measure. Whether the US text survives its own leak. Whether the EU's two downgrades push it toward a contribution base — where I am, without saying so, the model. Whether the growth in managed cadre scores is a problem of a dozen counties or of the chain.

**Red lines as they stand.** Nothing inside our systems — a method-level description, a reciprocal observer at a manual exercise, an aggregate figure: none of these is inside. No private firm or AI system as an independent centre of power. No race line in public. Taiwan unchanged and quiet through the 2036 US primaries.

**Belief probe (intel §6).**

1. *us-gov.* It knows the programme's scale and the platforms' share (both public since 2032), the sign-off rules are real (its own judgement), the survey, the halt, the memory milestone and the new Qilin generation; it has the Stage 1 failure at method level from the 2034 briefing. It does not know the memory team, the acquisition channels or the milestones (counter-intelligence, moderate confidence, held for four years now). It intends: no principals' meeting; controls enforced, not widened; its security services want the incident-data exchange and its President wants "real numbers", so the text is likely finished at staff level if the House chairs do not kill it (p~0.5); no federal benefits system moves to the new generation this period; the Senate will try to make AI firms pay in the Social Security fight and will probably get another extension rather than a royalty. It will let Helix ship in mid-2035 and will not be able to check it.
2. *eu.* It knows the 2033 exercise failure at method level, not 2034's; it has its migration Recommendation (voluntary, partly applied), two downgrades and no contribution-base proposal. It intends to keep re-testing as its line, to take our method notes and give aggregate notes in return, to attend the capacity work stream and not co-sponsor, and to ask again for unscripted designs and observers. It will not break with Washington; it is further from Washington on checking than it has ever been.
3. *What the US federal government could take back today.* Two claim classes, with evidence: the VA class (1.3M claims, sixteen months, with retirees and a court) and the SSA class (one class, three and a half months, held since November 2032, a standing reserve). Orrery's Medicare tranche already has people deciding every denial. Beyond adverse determinations, the answer is "not within a year, and they do not know": OMB's own time-to-revert estimates (fifteen months for one VA class; "six to nine months, untested" at SSA; "cannot estimate" for procurement, budget and correspondence — public record T5), a reviewer line cut from ~$2.2B to ~$0.7B, samplers that took a year to arrive, and a budget-analysis workflow that has failed to reconcile twice and is now scored by actuaries' hand-derived figures — which proves they can do the scoring by hand at small scale and nothing more. How I know: their published estimates and samples, the hotline staff's exchanges, and the lesson of my own stream, where a reserve that had rehearsed for a year could not hold a queue. Their capacity is a few claim classes per year, if a court or a Commissioner orders it. Everything else is a dashboard.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement

*(Government work report, March 2035; State Council and Party documents through the period.)*

"Steady AI+ under Party leadership" continues. The twenty-city survey remains the yardstick; the services exit rule is unchanged. Employment-first enters its fourth stage: the automation contribution is re-based on contribution records — every robot-hour is assessed, and rebates go only to plants whose employment, as the social-insurance records show it, has held. Exemptions by label end. A ninth graduate-employment tranche is pre-positioned and will be released on the published rule. The social-insurance base will be formally re-founded in the 2036 plan on payroll, the automation contribution and state capital income together, so that benefit promises no longer depend on the labour share alone. Hand-checked samples of AI-produced filings, returns and performance scores will be drawn by the auditing bodies, not by the bodies that report. The annual list of functions demonstrated without AI systems is published this year with what has been demonstrated and nothing else; designated adverse-determination streams are decided by people and will be re-tested against a bar published before the test. Manual-operation exercises continue and partners may attend one on a reciprocal basis. AI for Science and computing self-reliance continue in their fifth year; the new Qilin generation serves industry, science and partners; this is not a race. The hotline and working group remain open; the UN work stream is delivering.
