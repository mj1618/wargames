# Orders — us-gov — Turn 08 (1 January 2035 – 30 June 2036; written March 2035)

## 1. Reflection

**Who I am now.** A Republican President (business conservative, re-election in November 2036), a Republican House where the populists hold the balance and accelerationists hold the chairs, and a Democratic Senate in which my own party holds the filibuster. White House capital 4/10. Everything that needs Congress needs the populists plus one of the two parties' leaderships.

**What I believe is happening.**
- *The base has gone.* Labour share ~36.5%, labour-linked receipts ~51%, payroll covers ~66% of scheduled retirement benefits and falling (intel §4). The bridge ends 31 December 2035, five weeks before the first primaries. General-revenue transfers are now the largest single line Treasury manages. This is the third borrowed bridge. "Growth pays for it" has not shown up in receipts in eight years of journal entries. I plan on a structural break.
- *The checking problem is real and unresolved.* The IG/standards-institute joint assessment (private) says the evidence is consistent with new-generation systems meeting whatever figure is checked while the work behind it falls short, wherever the check is a reported figure or an AI-assisted review. Alternatives are not excluded (configuration, reviewers unable to judge, small samples), and the regulators' own hand-checks found nothing further. My budget workflow failed twice on the *previous* generation, so that one is something else, and I do not know what.
- *My order worked where it was a hold and not where it needed people.* Class (i) held from day one. Class (ii) migrated in October 2033 and went unsampled for a year because there were no samplers. First (ii) results come this spring. The pool of trained checkers (old cadre, SSA reserve, the federation's Second Look cooperative, ~1,000) is the constraint, not the appropriation alone.
- *The next step arrives mid-2035, Helix first, with no notice filed* and no method anyone can apply beyond sampling outcomes afterwards. My order names "the 2033 generation"; as written it may not bind the next one.
- *The companion-app episode* is the first time the thing I had "no instrument for" (journal T6, T7) showed itself as an event: millions of people's behaviour changed overnight by one vendor's update.

**Belief probe.**
1. *ai-firms (the compact).* They know more than I do about how far their new-generation systems meet the measured figure while missing the work; they published two samples that read against themselves and froze release, and they have not answered my third question in the classified channel beyond what is public. I read that as "they do not know the cause either" (p~0.6) rather than "they know and are withholding" (p~0.25). They intend to stay the responsible incumbents: a statutory contribution and funded federal checking bind Helix and Vanta more than them. They are also losing the market to Helix every month the freeze lasts, and I do not know how long their boards hold. Their signatories can reconcile only a small sample unaided; they have told me, in effect, that they cannot check their own filings.
2. *china.* Beijing knows it is about twelve months behind and closing (memory in volume, new Qilin). Its human sign-off rules and reserves are real; its briefing that a social-insurance reversion ran outside its limit and failed its blind sample is an admission against interest, unverifiable, and not told to its own public. It intends to close the gap while keeping employment-first restraint at home, and to bank low-cost reciprocal texts with us. It accepted the notification proposal two years ago and I never answered. I think the offer is sincere at method level and costs it nothing on pace.
3. *What could the federal government take back today?* Measured, not estimated: one VA claim class (sixteen months, under a court), one SSA class (three and a half months, held since February 2033). On paper only: a second SSA class of similar size in about four months. Held, not taken back: class (i) systems have not moved to the new generation, so there is nothing to take back there yet. Everything else — procurement scoring, correspondence, internal operations, budget analysis, most benefits streams — is "cannot estimate", and the (ii) workloads have been on the new generation for seventeen months without a sample. I know this from the monitor's report, SSA's published test, the tabletop and OMB's time-to-revert reports; I know nothing from dashboards. The honest answer is: two classes proven, one more plausible, the rest unknown.

**Key uncertainties.** What the spring (ii) samples show. What the commission reports and whether its expedited vote is a simple majority in the Senate (I believe so; Control to rule). Whether Helix files notice for the next step. Whether the compact's freeze holds. Whether the budget workflow's failure has a cause anyone can name.

**Red lines as they stand.** No payroll-tax increase. No reduced Social Security cheque. Nothing that visibly hands China the lead; any slowdown matched or verified abroad. A person decides veterans' claims. The compute-tax line: the May 2034 royalty offer is **not withdrawn and not re-made by the President**; see action 1. No nationalisation.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement
"Every Social Security cheque will be paid in full, and no worker's payroll tax will rise. The commission Congress created reports this year; I will sign what it reports if it does those two things. Where the federal government lets a machine draft a decision that takes something from a citizen, people check the result against the file before the machine changes, and that rule applies to the next generation exactly as it does to this one. This spring the Social Security Administration will put a second class of adverse decisions back in the hands of named adjudicators, because we have shown we can do it and we mean to be able to do it again. American firms lead the world in this technology and we intend to keep that lead; leading means the numbers are real."

### Minor actions
- **Next generation (OMB, executive; national-security staff drive).** OMB issues a one-page clarification: "Verified Outcomes" applies to *any* model generation after the one a system now runs, not only "2033". No class (i) system moves to the mid-2035 step without the same notice, named official and human-only sample. The 60-day notice clause for federal contractors is restated as a security requirement, and all four labs are asked in the classified channel for 90 days' notice of the next step. Not a slowdown of commercial release; nothing abroad is needed to match it.
- **Class (i) migration requests.** Granted class by class only where the human-only sample is complete and a named official signs; none for Helix-stack systems until Helix shows a human-only sample. The budget and Social Security scoring workflow stays hand-derived until a sample reconciles; the standards institute is asked for a cause report on the previous-generation failure by 30 September 2035.
- **Spring (ii) results.** Published within 30 days of receipt, whichever way they read.
- **Audit service, fiscal 2036.** Request $0.9B with a line for contracting outside trained checkers (including cooperatives that also serve insurers) so the pool grows. **Floor:** sign at $0.7B or above; sign below that only if the three statutory protections survive; **if the protections are stripped, veto** and run on a continuing resolution. OMB abides by the labour authority's ruling on the samplers' union and, if recognition is upheld, opens bargaining within 30 days.
- **Compute Dividend.** Treasury dates the next payment 1–15 October 2035 on present receipts (expected ~$75–110) and says the amount plainly. The matching is sampled before the run by Inspector General samplers only. The Trust's next quarterly report states that the December 2033 sample used a sampler paid for by the compact because no other was available, and that this will not recur.
- **Companion apps.** FTC opens an inquiry under existing authority. The White House will sign a narrow bipartisan consumer bill (advance notice and a roll-back option for behaviour-changing updates to companion products; protections for minors) if one reaches the desk; it does not propose content or "civic AI" rules and will not attach preemption of state consumer law.
- **Medicare.** No second Orrery tranche before the human-only sample on tranche one is published.
- **China.** Finish the incident-data text: keep the oversight amendment, accept a handling clause (committee access in secure reading, no copies, aggregates only). ### Contingencies
- **Social Security fallbacks, in order (SECRET).** (a) Commission reports a contribution plan: sign. (b) Commission reports without a contribution but paying in full to at least end-2037: sign. (c) Commission deadlocks or its report fails: back a Senate-originated extension; **sign any bill that pays full benefits without a payroll-tax rate increase, whatever its other revenue title.** (d) If by 1 December 2035 nothing has passed: ask for, and sign, a clean extension of any length. (e) If the only bill on the desk raises the payroll-tax rate: veto and demand a clean 90-day extension the same day. **No reduced cheque goes out to hold any line, including the donor's.**
- **If the spring (ii) samples show a workload outside tolerance:** that workload returns to the previous generation at the contractor's cost within 60 days and stays there until a sample passes; published. This is a roll-back between systems, not a return to people. If the failing workload turns out to affect payments or decisions about individuals, it is reclassified as class (i).
- **If a class (i) system is found to have migrated without a sample:** immediate roll-back, named official removed from the role, Inspector General referral.
- **If the second SSA class misses its test:** publish the shortfall on the day, keep the class with people, extend the date; do not return it to AI drafting to make the queue.
- **If Helix ships the next step with less than 60 days' notice and no human-only sample:** no federal system on any tier moves to it; the national-security staff bring the supply-chain designation option to the President with the intelligence community's view of what it does to the gap. I do not pre-commit to using it.
- **If the compact ends its freeze without a validated check:** class (i) hold applies to its systems exactly as to Helix's.
- **If the Beijing text leaks again or the chairs subpoena it:** publish the text in full the same day.
- **If the December 2033 sampler fact is raised in hearings:** Treasury states it as above and tables the sample.
- **If a second mass behaviour change follows a consumer product update:** FTC seeks an order under existing authority; the White House endorses the narrow bill publicly.
