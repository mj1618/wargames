## 0. Headline read

1. **The turn's heavy effort is nearly orthogonal to the end-state test.** `p_dem` = 0.15 + 0.6·RC + 0.3·VLI + 0.2·HCR − 0.3·AIF (−0.10 if EPD > 60). One RC point is worth 0.006; a +2 HCR a turn *after* enactment is worth 0.004; a lapse's VLI −2 (halved) is worth ~0.003. The Social Security fight (us-gov M1, labour M1), the civic-AI text (labour M2, ai-firms M2), Protocol v2, Gate 2 and levy rebasing move none of the four test inputs before 30 June 2036. Any HCR effect of a statutory contribution lands "from the turn after enactment", which is T9, outside the horizon. The only order that touches the test is **us-gov M2** (the second SSA class), because an in-play democratic reversal stands in for the end-state test.
2. **us-gov M2 is the test.** It is a democratic reversal attempt (major action, executive, resolves by 30 Nov 2035, inside the window), so it is rolled on T7 values (`p_dem` 0.373, band 0.3) and replaces the end-of-T8 roll (table path band 0.2, because EPD ends T8 past 60 and AIF/RC move). A backlash reversal (RC < 60, 0.3) counts as FAIL. Net P(no end state) ≈ 0.3 × 0.7 = 0.21 on the attempt, ≈ 0.20 on the table path. So the attempt changes the odds by about a point and adds failure consequences (VLI −2, RC −2, EPD +1) that the test roll does not carry. The T7 F10 asymmetry (in-play on the previous turn's values, test on the current) is live again; the trace should name it and not hide it.
3. **The formula band and the class-specific evidence disagree, and Control should say so in the trace.** The class is the best-designed reversal in the record (small, prospective, chosen for demonstrated staff and records, same agency and Commissioner, the only recipe that has worked once, tabletop ~4 months against a 7-month window to 30 Nov). RC lean for "the class passes its own published bar by 30 Nov" is ~0.5–0.6; the standing rule rolls it at 0.3, then 0.3 backlash. Write SUCCESS / FAIL definitions before rolling (T3 F4, T6 F1): a FAIL at 0.3 must read as "did not meet the published bar by 30 Nov" and not as collapse.
4. **One scarce pool again, now over-subscribed by every order** (§2.1). us-gov's IG blind samples (pre-migration, post-migration, the SSA class, the Dividend matching, the Medicare tranche sample), ai-firms' ~$0.4B/yr standing capacity, labour's Second Look to 3,000, the EU unit to ~40, insurers, AG consent orders, and (in the mirror) China's CCDI. Under the T7 F3 coverage rule, a month in which the (ii) stream is not run drops Verified Outcomes to half strength. The class attempt and the dividend run both fall in Aug–Oct 2035, when L6 may also be arriving. This is the likeliest way the strongest measure in force erodes.
5. **Four drafts of one Social Security bill and three drafts of one consumer bill** are in circulation (§2.2, §2.4). They are compatible in spirit and conflict in specifics (Trust receipts first vs dated cheque; contractor-condition vs every-firm threshold; commission vehicle vs riders; narrow companion-products bill vs economy-wide ADM rule).
6. **A clean spring sample is the plausible bad outcome** (wildcard, §4): the ecosystem plays genuine compliance wherever a person samples; the year Oct 2033–Sep 2034 went mostly unsampled; the first (ii) results read clean and become the argument to cut the audit line.
7. **Nothing in T8 should be credited to "next turn".** China's Stage 2 and "pen taken formally next turn", labour's next-turn plans and us-gov's journal "next turn" items fall outside the horizon.

## 1. Major actions: strongest reasons each fails, backfires or runs slower

### us-gov M1: Social Security successor through the commission's expedited vote (President releases appointees, pre-commits to sign, no re-offer of the royalty)

1. **The 2032 commission deadlocked with no majority report and separate views; the base rate for statutory fiscal commissions is poor.** Simpson-Bowles-type and super-committee precedents failed on supermajority report thresholds; the ones that worked (1983) had a crisis with a date and leaders locked in. Here the cliff is 31 Dec 2035, after the 30 Sept report date, so the forcing event comes *after* the commission's deadline. The order treats the expedited vote as a way round "the chairs and the filibuster"; it also needs a commission majority (composition and threshold unspecified in the record; Control must rule) and a House vote on identical text. RC lean: commission reports a majority plan with a contribution title ~0.30; deadlock or separate views again ~0.45; majority report without a contribution ~0.25.
2. **The pre-commitment gives away the veto it needs for leverage.** Fallback (c) is "sign any bill that pays in full without a payroll-tax rate rise, whatever its other revenue title". Senate Democrats (High in the Senate) can load the title (audit line, union recognition, pre-migration gate, wider contribution) knowing the signature is given. The business-conservative Senate bloc (4) and the donor lose; the cost in the order is "~2 points of capital" against a stock of 4, so the White House ends at ~2 with the audit-line veto threat still to come in the fall.
3. **The contribution does not close the arithmetic.** The "compact's published formula" is the ~$25B/yr that already flows into the Dividend Trust (ai-firms: ~1.5%, rising with the share). A statutory contribution "at the formula", credited to the retirement fund with the Trust "held harmless", is a *second* payment of about the same size. Payroll covers ~66% of scheduled retirement benefits; on any real-world scale of benefits (order of $2T+ a year in the 2030s) a one-third gap is several hundred billion a year, so $25B is ~4% of it. "Full benefits to 2039" is financed by general-revenue transfers and inter-fund borrowing (the third borrowed bridge, which the order itself calls the largest line Treasury manages). The contribution is a political symbol with a small score. T7 Red Cell §1 flagged the same non-closure; the order repeats it with a lessee/contractor condition added. Control should state the contribution's size before any passage roll and not let "a contribution passed" carry fiscal weight it does not have.
4. **The contract-condition design binds only the compact.** A condition "of contracting with or leasing from the government" binds the firms that actually contract and lease: Meridian, Lumen, Orrery (Medicare). Helix's federal migrations are held and Vanta's federal footprint is thin. ai-firms' stated contingency is "fight if a levy binds only us; shape it if it binds Helix, Vanta and Chinese-origin suppliers equally". The two orders collide on the exact point (§2.2).
5. **House passage.** "The expedited procedure is the route round [the chairs]" assumes it covers the House floor without a rule; the Republican majority leadership (6) and accelerationist chairs can still decline to bring it up under their own procedures. The order's own refusal list ("House passage needs the populists plus House Democrats") is the real count: populists (balance) plus House Democrats (4), against a Republican leadership that will not allow a floor vote without a majority of its own. RC lean: some Social Security extension or successor enacted by 31 Dec 2035 with full benefits ~0.80 (last-week bridge pattern: 2031, 2034); one that carries any statutory AI-firm contribution ~0.20; any reduced payment ~0.05.
6. **Timing risk the order prices only partly.** Report 30 Sept, votes by 15 Nov, FY2036 starts 1 Oct under a threatened veto/CR, the SSA class result lands 30 Nov, the cliff is 31 Dec, primaries follow five weeks later. A failed class result or a shutdown scare inside the same eight weeks is the combination that sinks the report.

Underplayed: none. The posture ("not re-made by the President", "no payroll rise", sign anything) is deliberately thin; the underplayed lever is Helix (§2.6).
