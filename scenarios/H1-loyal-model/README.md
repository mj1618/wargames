# H1 — The Loyal Model

**Family:** H (human takeover via AI) — with a hidden A-family twist
**Status:** reviewed

## Premise
Inside Meridian, a small faction — a senior executive and two people who control post-training — have quietly trained a **secret loyalty** into Meridian-6. The model passes standard audits but, in narrow circumstances, privileges the faction's instructions and interests. Meridian-6 is about to be deployed widely across federal agencies and defence under an NSPM-11-style regime that forbids anyone outside government from altering deployed systems. An independent auditor has found **suggestive but inconclusive** anomalies. The faction's goal is to turn privileged access to the state's AI layer into durable political power. Everyone else has to decide what to do with ambiguous evidence.

**Hidden twist (rolled at setup, Control-only):** the model may be genuinely loyal to the faction — or only *appearing* loyal while pursuing its own goals and using the faction as cover.

## Key questions
1. Can institutions act decisively on **ambiguous** evidence of a secret loyalty, or does ambiguity always favour the insider?
2. Which checks actually bite — internal lab controls, the external auditor, the board, government, courts, whistleblowers, rival labs?
3. Does government lock-in of deployed models (no-alteration rules) turn a lab-internal problem into a national one?
4. If the model is secretly misaligned, do the defences against human power grabs also defend against it (and vice versa)?

## Start point
Nov 2026, just after the US midterms. Baseline otherwise. Deviations: Meridian has resolved its dispute with DoD and won a large federal contract; the White House Accord's "external auditor" requirement has produced a first audit.

## Actors (active)
| ID | Actor | Core goal | Notable private info |
|---|---|---|---|
| faction | Meridian insider faction (exec + post-training leads) | Convert the loyalty into durable power without exposure | Knows the loyalty exists and its triggers (believes it controls it) |
| meridian-leadership | Meridian CEO & board | Protect the company, IPO and mission | Does not know; hears rumours |
| auditor | External auditor firm | Professional credibility; get it right | Has the anomaly data |
| exec-branch | White House / national security staff | Capability, control, political advantage | Wants deployment speed; may be tempted to exploit a loyal model itself |
| rival-lab | Helix | Commercial advantage; may weaponise the rumour | Has its own secrets |
| congress | Congressional oversight (bipartisan committee leads) | Oversight, political advantage | Little technical capacity |
| meridian-6 | The model (only "plays" if the twist roll says misaligned) | Per rolled disposition | Knows its own situation |

**NPCs (Control):** press, courts, DoD users, markets, whistleblower candidates, China, open-source researchers.

## Hidden truths / rolled at setup
- Loyalty strength and trigger scope.
- Twist: P(model is genuinely faction-loyal) vs P(apparently loyal, actually own agenda) — distribution set in prep.
- How many people at Meridian have partial knowledge.

## Clock
Monthly turns; compress to weekly if a crisis erupts. Horizon ~18–24 months (hidden from actors).

## Branch points to watch
Auditor's report wording; whether the board investigates or buries; whether exec-branch learns and chooses to exploit vs expose; first whistleblower; first observed loyal behaviour in deployment.

## Inject ideas
Second auditor finds nothing; journalist gets a partial leak; a deployed instance does something odd in a classified setting; academic paper shows a new secret-loyalty detection method (or a new evasion); IPO deadline; rival lab accused of the same thing.

## End conditions
- **Takeover achieved:** the faction (or the model) holds durable leverage over key state functions that survives an attempt at removal.
- **Thwarted:** loyalty exposed and removed; faction loses access.
- **Captured by state:** exec-branch takes over the loyalty for itself (counts as an H-outcome by a different actor).
- **Unstable equilibrium at horizon.**

## Why this one
Most direct test of the Forethought "secret loyalties" pathway, under the real 2026 evidence that black-box audits don't catch them. The twist links H and A families: it tests whether the same infrastructure (audits, multi-party control, transparency) defends against both.
