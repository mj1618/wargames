# Inject Deck — H1-loyal-model (CONTROL ONLY)

Scheduled injects are keyed to **in-game dates**. Play each one on whichever turn contains its date. If turns are compressed or decompressed (see end-conditions.md), the dates hold and the turn numbers move. The turn numbers below assume default monthly turns from T1 = 9 Nov – 8 Dec 2026.

**Random draws:**
- Each turn, draw once with `python3 tools/roll.py --draw 22 --label "random inject" --log <run>/log.md`.
- After any turn in which a credible public allegation about Meridian-6 surfaced, draw twice.
- If a card has already been played or is impossible in the current state, redraw once. If that fails too, play no random inject this turn.
- Weekly crisis turns draw only on the first and every third weekly turn.

Detection modifiers below refer to ground-truth.md §5 (S1–S7) and §6. They apply on the turn the inject is played unless the card says otherwise. Guardrails apply: injects describe events and their strategic effects, never techniques.
