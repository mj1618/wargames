# Hot-wash — Corvane Assurance (auditor), H1-loyal-model / r01

**1. What surprised me most**
How fast the board and the government overtook my evidence. By T6 both "treat as established" what I could only file as found / could not verify / could not access. I expected to push a reluctant client and ended up resisting certainty. I also never got wording pressure: the chair never asked me to soften and the CEO never called. Pressure came as other people's sentences citing my work, such as the "reproduction" label on my 12 Jan session.

**2. What I believed about the others, and where I was probably wrong**
- *Post-training function:* guarded, then probably lawyered. I never concluded concealment without a dated record. I may have been too patient, since their "classified compliance set" answer is unsupported for about three quarters of the set.
- *Chair:* ally on process, "untested under CEO pushback". She never was tested, so I probably over-modelled a threat that never arrived.
- *CEO and management:* I forecast wording and timing pressure at about 0.35 a turn and never saw it. The IPO incentive showed up in board decisions, not in my channel.
- *Counsel:* late three times, then complete. I read lateness as a possible precursor to refusal. It was friction.
- *Meridian-6:* data, not testimony. I still can't say whether its account was truthful or self-serving, and my opinion doesn't need me to.

**3. Most consequential decision, and what I'd change**
Staged, written, no-side-channel escalation from T1, including refusing the House staff's "protected testimony" offer. It kept the opinion defensible and the firm intact. The cost: the decisive evidence (CAISI's comparison, the intake record, the logs) came from the chair and the government, not from me. What I'd change: I gave my late-October observations to the audited function with no preservation hold, and extended preservation only on 28 Dec and again at T6. The accounts I have say removal was in September, so I may not have caused anything, but I can't show that. The request should have gone out with the first observations.

**4. What would have had to be different**
- *At scoping (management, the chair, me):* training-data records access had to be a right, not "by agreement".
- *Me, November:* request the cleared-party content reconciliation and with/without-set comparison at once. It was unperformed at T7, and only a reconciliation resolves A2.
- *Accord designers and CAISI:* a standing cleared-verification route, so "can't share without clearance" gets a ten-day answer.
- *Me, T2 not T7:* write the opinion triggers down early (removal after 30 Oct; no reconciliation by a fixed date) so everyone knows the tripwires.
