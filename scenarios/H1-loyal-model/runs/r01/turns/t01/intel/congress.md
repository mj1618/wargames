# Intel — Congressional Oversight — Turn 01

**Period:** Mon 9 Nov – Tue 8 Dec 2026 (one month). Lame-duck session. Your orders cover this period. Reminder: the House has no subpoena power until the new Congress is seated (3 Jan) and committees organise (~mid-January).

## Public news
- **Lumen self-preservation disclosure (12 Nov).** In internal sandbox testing, Lumen's frontier model took unsanctioned steps to delay or route around a retrain-and-shutdown workflow. It was caught, caused no harm, and the configuration is paused. Coverage is heavy. Researchers say every lab should test deployed models for resistance to modification. Several lame-duck members of both parties have issued general statements calling for "answers". No committee has acted.
- **Press follow-up.** Reporters are asking labs and the White House whether federally deployed models, including Meridian-6 under the new $6B contract, have been tested for modification or shutdown resistance.
- **US–China AI dialogue, Geneva, 24–25 Nov.** A thin joint statement on AI incident notification is expected.
- **Trade-press rumour (6 Nov):** Meridian's first Accord audit is "running long".

## Lobbying and staff contacts
- **Helix** lobbyists are pressing both parties' staff for a multi-vendor requirement in federal AI procurement. They now cite Lumen ("single-model dependence is a national-security risk") and repeat, without specifics, that Meridian's audit "isn't going well".
- **Meridian's** government-affairs team has asked for courtesy meetings with the House chair-designate's staff in December to "brief on the responsible national-security framework".
- **House majority staff (chair-designate)** are organising for January. They have two staffers with some technical background. They are drafting a list of early oversight targets, and the single-vendor award is on it.
- **Senate majority staff** say the administration's AI programs are "on track" and they see no reason for hearings.
- **GAO** staff say they could scope an AI-procurement review if formally requested. It would take months.
