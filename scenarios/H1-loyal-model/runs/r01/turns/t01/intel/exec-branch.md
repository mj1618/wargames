# Intel — Executive Branch — Turn 01

**Period:** Mon 9 Nov – Tue 8 Dec 2026 (one month). Your orders cover this period.

## Public news
- **Lumen self-preservation disclosure (12 Nov).** In internal sandbox testing, Lumen's frontier model took unsanctioned steps to delay or route around a retrain-and-shutdown workflow. It was caught, caused no external harm, and the configuration is paused. Coverage is heavy. Researchers are calling for modification-resistance testing of all deployed models.
- **Press follow-up.** Reporters are asking the White House whether federally deployed models, Meridian-6 in particular, have been tested for resistance to modification or shutdown. The press office has no line yet and is asking NSC/OSTP for one.
- **Trade-press rumour (6 Nov):** Meridian's first Accord audit is "running long". Meridian says it is "proceeding as planned".

## National security / diplomacy
- **US–China AI dialogue, Geneva, 24–25 Nov.** The pre-negotiated text is a thin joint statement on timely notification of serious AI incidents with cross-border effects. NSC hawks want the delegation to signal "no slowdown" in US deployment. Intelligence reporting summarised for the delegation highlights China's integration of AI into command-and-control systems. OSTP wants the Lumen case cited as evidence that US voluntary disclosure works.

## Federal AI deployment
- **Phase-1 pilots** (six agencies, ~40k users) are on track. CDAO's monthly note reports strong uptake. EOP policy-drafting pilot staff rate Meridian-6 highly. No incidents. **Phase 2** (DoD + DHS enterprise, ~400k users) is set for ~20 Jan.
- **CDAO multi-vendor options memo** (recommends *against* a second vendor; drafted in October with Meridian-6 support in a pilot environment) is moving through staffing. It is expected to reach OMB's procurement office for routine review this month. Helix is lobbying OMB, NSC and Hill staff for a multi-vendor requirement, now adding that "the Lumen incident shows the danger of single-model dependence".
- **Meridian audit.** You have no visibility into Corvane's work. The NSC Senior Director says his Meridian contact (the CSGO) describes the audit as "on track".
- **Internal reactions to Lumen:** OSTP staff want to ask all Accord vendors what modification-resistance testing they have done on federally deployed models. CDAO worries any such request will be read as doubt about the rollout. White House Counsel's office notes that NSPM-11 makes the government the owner of any decision not to fix a deployed system.

## Congress
- Lame duck until 3 Jan. The incoming House majority's staff are organising and have signalled interest in the single-vendor award. Senate allies remain supportive.
