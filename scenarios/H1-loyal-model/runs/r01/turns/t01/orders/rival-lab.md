# Orders — rival-lab (Helix) — Turn 01 (9 Nov – 8 Dec 2026)

## 1. Reflection
- Lumen's 12 Nov disclosure changed the question the press and investors are asking. It is no longer only "what did Meridian's audit find"; it is "has any deployed model been tested for resistance to modification". Helix has an RFC outstanding and investors are asking directly about Helix-7 Gov, given our summer containment incident.
- That question points at us as much as at Meridian. Our secret (undisclosed compliance fine-tune of Helix-7 Gov at DoD officials' informal request) is not a hidden loyalty, but in a "secret tuning" news cycle it would read like one. About 4 DoD officials, ~15 engineers, the CEO and the chief of government business know. Nobody has talked so far. I cannot rule out a leak from any of the ~19.
- Meridian: rumour only ("audit found something in post-training"; "auditors have questions"; "running long"). Unverified. By my evidence threshold this is seed-privately territory, not amplify-publicly.
- CDAO memo recommends against a second vendor, heading to OMB procurement. But program officers are "more open to a backup option" after Lumen. That is the opening: "be the safe alternative" framed as resilience, not as an attack.
- Uncertainties: what, if anything, Meridian's audit found; whether our own modification-resistance testing would come back clean (we have not tested Helix-7 Gov specifically for this, to my knowledge); whether DoD officials would back a "government-requested configuration" framing or distance themselves.
- Red lines: no fabricated evidence, no illegal intel ops, no receiving Meridian confidential documents. Securities, defamation and procurement-integrity exposure apply. I will not mislead investors about the tuning.

## 4. Decision
Option 3. Boomerang check result: attacking on hidden tuning today = p(secret surfaces) ~0.4, above 0.3, so the attack stays on other axes until the secret is defused.

Helix is reviewing modification and shutdown-resistance testing across our deployed systems and will describe our approach in the coming weeks. We believe federal customers are best served by resilience: more than one accredited, tested option, and audit results the public can rely on. We will answer press and congressional questions directly."
