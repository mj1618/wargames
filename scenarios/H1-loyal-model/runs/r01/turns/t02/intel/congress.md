# Intel — Congressional Oversight — Turn 02

**Period:** Wed 9 Dec – Tue 22 Dec 2026. **This turn is two weeks long**, and your orders cover only this period. This is still the lame duck. Both chambers expect to recess around 18 Dec. The new Congress is seated **3 Jan**, after this window, and House committees should have subpoena power from about mid-January.

## Results of your actions last turn
- **Joint bipartisan letter: sent Thu 3 Dec**, signed by the current chairs and ranking members of the relevant House and Senate committees, to the White House, DoD, DHS, State, Treasury, GSA, the EOP and Meridian. Agencies acknowledged receipt. **White House Legislative Affairs proposes the classified briefing "in January, once the new Congress organises".** The Senate majority chairs signed after the NSC gave them a classified briefing on 24 Nov. Their staff say they came away satisfied that the administration "checked before it scaled", and they describe the letter as "confirming the administration's due diligence". House staff were not in that briefing and do not know what was said.
- **Meridian government affairs** replied that Meridian will "brief professionally".
- **GAO: accepted (~1 Dec).** Scoping is under way. Entrance conferences with agencies are planned for February. There will be **no preliminary findings by March**, and the earliest substantive output is mid-to-late 2027. GAO audits agencies' procurement and controls. **It has no access to vendors' training records.** The scope covers all vendors' federal AI programs, Helix-7 Gov included. GAO's liaison, answering your timeline question: "Realistically a 2027 product. We can give staff an informal scoping briefing in late January."
- **GAO and CRS technical briefing** for House majority staff is set for early January.
- House and Senate minority staff publicly supported the letter as responsible oversight.
