- Whether the Committee will act under CEO pressure. The chair told the CEO on 4 Dec. I have no response yet.
- A3 is still open. The control-set false-positive rate is due mid-December.

**Red lines as they stand.**
- No opinion the evidence does not support, in either direction. No loud claim of tampering. Nothing is proven, and I have no evidence of concealment.
- No clean opinion with A2 unresolved.
- Breaking confidentiality without legal cover or Committee consent is a last resort.
- Whatever I write must survive being read aloud at a hearing.
