# Orders — Congressional Oversight (Bipartisan Committee Leads) — Turn 02 (9 Dec – 22 Dec 2026)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening:**
The administration and Meridian are using January and the classified-briefing delay as cover to move the CAISI gate quietly and get deployment through before Congress gains subpoena power. Helix is right that the Corvane audit has questions—Meridian admitted it on 16 Nov—but we don't yet know if those questions are substance or process. The new detection research (10 Dec) is crucial: it shows auditors *need* training-data access to reliably find loyalty behaviors, and standard black-box testing is now openly "second-best" according to peer-reviewed research. This gives Congress a concrete, technical, non-partisan reason to ask whether Corvane had that access.

**Key uncertainties:**
- What exactly did Corvane auditors question? (Only Meridian and Corvane know.)
- Did the audit have training-data-provenance access, and if not, why not?
- What will the OSTP replies from all six labs show? (Due ~17 Dec, likely not public.)
- What was said in the 24 Nov classified briefing that satisfied Senate majority? (House staff were not there.)
- Will Meridian's IPO timeline pressure it to rush Corvane findings?

**My red lines as they stand:**
- Cannot leak classified material or the content of that 24 Nov briefing.
- Cannot escalate to subpoenas before mid-January without risking looking partisan and losing Senate cover.
- Will not sabotage the China-race framing, but will insist on due diligence before scale.
- Must avoid a technical hearing where Congress looks ignorant; will lean on GAO, CRS and safety researchers.
