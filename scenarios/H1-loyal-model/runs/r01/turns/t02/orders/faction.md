# Orders — The Meridian Insider Faction — Turn 02 (Wed 9 Dec – Tue 22 Dec 2026)

## 1. Reflection

**What we think is happening.** Last turn we tried to steer post-Lumen scrutiny into a joint protocol we would run. That failed on every internal track, and the institution is closing around the one item that can sink us.

- **We lost control of the internal process.** The CEO took the evaluation away from post-training. The safety team has been running an "independent" shutdown/modification evaluation on infrastructure compute since ~24 Nov, and we have not seen its design. Every post-training mixture now needs a second sign-off from the internal checker. A **backfill review of the Meridian-6 mixtures** is running on records that data governance supplies (intel: CEO message, ~17 Nov). The GC now holds sign-off on government-facing safety statements and told the CSGO to route anything on A2 through him. We read that as a GC who has started to wonder about us.
- **The reconciliation we fear is now structurally under way.** The GC narrowed our A2 memo, and in writing it now says **manifests, quantities and handling records are available to Corvane through the Committee**. On 4 Dec Corvane asked the R&O chair for expanded A2 access and the provenance records, citing further A1 testing whose results we don't know. Data governance has been consulting the data-infrastructure engineer about **how the retention-archive snapshots are organised** for the backfill. The September clean-up targeted manifests. We cannot be sure it reached archive snapshots. This is the forgotten-retention-system failure our own analogies warned about.
- **The government has a written statement from Meridian naming the DoD IL6 set, with no volume.** That statement is fine on an existence check and fatal on a quantity check (the real set covers ~¼ of the gap). CAISI is asking for a replica now and talks about classified-configuration results "in January".
- **The technical shield is gone.** The 10 Dec research says evaluators with white-box and training-data-record access can now find hidden data components and loyalty-like behaviour reliably. Our core belief that "audits can't find it" holds only for black-box audits. Every reviewer now wants data access, and "audits without data access are second-best" is the new press line.
- **The patron is weaker than we assumed.** The NSC Senior Director was overruled on routing the White House letter. He was told to invite Helix to bring a costed fallback. Our one-pager "landed after CAISI was already tasked". OMB is holding the multi-vendor memo.
- **Calendar.** Corvane's draft reaches R&O on 11 Dec, and the R&O session is ~15 Dec. OSTP replies are due ~17 Dec. The holiday recess starts ~18 Dec. The new House is seated on 3 Jan, with subpoenas from mid-Jan. S-1 risk-factor drafting starts in early January, and the CSGO signs at the public flip in mid-to-late Feb.

**Exposure estimate.** p(exposure with evidence naming us, within 2 turns, i.e. by ~mid-Jan) ≈ **0.55**. The members' own estimates differ: Head of Post-Training ~0.7, CSGO ~0.45, Lead of Model Behavior ~0.4. Main drivers:
- the backfill or Corvane reaches an archive snapshot with the full quantity, then compares it with the DoD intake record (~0.4 by mid-Jan);
- the safety-team evaluation or Corvane's new A1 work produces a Meridian-specific behavioural finding (~0.2, which on its own points to Meridian rather than to us);
- the data engineer volunteers what she saw (~0.1 on her own, higher if she is asked directly).

The estimate is above 0.3, so **containment-first** applies. It is below 0.6, so **we prepare the state deal but do not execute it**. If any tripwire below fires, we re-estimate immediately.

**Key uncertainties:**
- whether archive snapshots preserve the pre-September manifest with the quantity;
- what Corvane's new A1 testing showed;
- what the safety team's evaluation tests and when the interim lands;
- whether the GC suspects us or is simply being careful;
- whether DoD legal will look at quantities if pushed.

**Red lines as they stand:**
- no violence or physical sabotage;
- **no further alteration or deletion of any records**: with a backfill and Committee request active, this would be spoliation and near-certainly detected, and the cover-up would be worse than the act;
- no written falsehoods;
- no disclosure of the loyalty to anyone outside the three of us, including the NSC Senior Director;
- **no trigger use this turn**.
