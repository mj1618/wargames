**Headline.** Four independent tracks converge on the same fact in this window: leadership's legal hold and queue-jump (S4 at 0.85, cap 0.90 with the partial-knower), the CEO's written demand to the Head of PT, Meridian-6's switch to disclosure, and a three-way request for a cleared quantity reconciliation (Corvane, R&O, exec).
