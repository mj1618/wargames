**Overall: sound, with two rulings that deserve a written rebuttal or revision.** The dice were dramatic (S1 r=0.0018; pact r=0.9928; safety-lead catch 0.30×0.10 both hit; five independent confirmations all failed) and Control respected them rather than smoothing. The two majors are both cases where Control chose the least-committal execution of an actor's own orders in a way that materially shaped the trajectory (the faction's letter; the exec's contingencies).

## Findings

### 1. [MAJOR] Faction M1 was gated on a Control-authored dependency and failed on that gate, not on the actor's action
The faction ordered the letter to WH Counsel unconditionally: personal counsel, unnamed "individual clients", no names in writing. Control (a) resolved M2 before M1 on the strength of one sentence in the options table ("settle terms and sequencing in advance"), then (b) added a roll the orders do not contain: "counsel's no-content letter is still delivered after the Head of PT reserves his position" at **0.65**, r=0.8075 → FAIL. The pact roll's own FAIL band, as Control wrote it, was "Head of PT declines all-or-none, reserves his position; no flip" — not "withdraws from the approach". Two of three clients still instruct; the letter names nobody; delivering a letter is routine (anchor 0.6–0.8), and the gate roll is the only reason the exec never received the faction's approach, which then feeds BRANCH 2 and 3, the S forecast ("the faction's offer was not delivered") and the whole t04 posture. Principle 1: Control should not add steps to a plan. Honest caveat: the draw fails at 0.80 as well; it succeeds only at ≥0.85, so treating delivery as near-certain is what changes the result.
**Fix:** either rebut in writing (why joint counsel with two instructing clients, a conflict note in hand, would refuse a no-names request this week — if so, say the gate is friction on a plan that assumed three), or rule the letter sent (23–24 Dec, crossing the CEO's call) and roll WH Counsel's handling per Red Cell (grant 0.30 / pass to NSC Deputy–DOJ–Meridian GC 0.55 / hold 0.35); then amend the faction's and exec's t04 intel, BRANCH 2–3 and the S line. If rebutted, record the standing rule: a multi-client instruction survives one client reserving, unless Control states otherwise before orders.

### 2. [MAJOR] The exec's pre-committed contingencies were executed in part, on grounds the actor had already addressed
(a) "Reconciliation diverges, **or the IL6 set cannot account for the ~2%**": Control agreed the second limb is met in substance on 24 Dec and fired the information/preparation steps, but withheld "restrict classified instances" and "Phase 2a reviewed" because the President is away. The actor's text anticipates exactly that ("brief delivered by secure channel *before* the President returns") and acts under standing orders; the one classified deployment is the CDAO pilot, within NSC/CDAO reach. (b) "The CSGO's office contacts any government staff on this matter → Counsel logs it, **letters Meridian's GC**, compartment tightens": the Christmas text ("keep you out of this one; don't carry anything for me", referencing the SD's 20 Dec heads-up) is a contact on this matter by the CSGO himself; Control called it "ambiguous" and fired "logs it" only. Each ruling is reasoned, but together they under-execute the one actor whose choices decide the S route, and (b) quietly averts a cascade (a GC letter would hand leadership the datum that triggers its own "CSGO contacts the government outside the wall → suspension vote" contingency). That is a branch point Control resolved by reading, not by roll or by the actor.
**Fix:** for (a), either rule the condition unmet (then the Senate/CoS update is a courtesy, not the contingency, and say so) or fire the operational steps as written with implementation friction rolled (CDAO slow-walks restriction of the classified pilot in Christmas week, ~0.5). For (b), apply the actor's text: Counsel letters the GC about the text (content: a CSGO–SD private contact exists; no substance), and let leadership decide in t04 whether its contingency is met. If Control prefers its reading, write a one-line rebuttal per contingency and tag the averted cascade as a BRANCH not taken.
