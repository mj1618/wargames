  - It can show whether the snapshot and the current records agree.
  - It can show whether the access pattern fits a set that was used as the post-training team describes.

  - It can only help reconcile the 2% if sizes and dates add up. Even then, "accounted in quantity and date" is not "accounted in content". Only CAISI's cleared comparison goes to the government's own record.

**Key uncertainties.**
- Whether the snapshot is a true snapshot. I have only the chair's cover note and data governance's word for it. A reconciliation against a snapshot whose provenance I cannot check is weaker than it looks.
- Whether "current records" were regenerated after August. Differences between snapshot and current can be benign (re-indexing, custodian reassignment) or not.
- Whether the missing DoD-pilot intake record is an omission or a gap in Meridian's own filing.
- What CAISI's cleared staff will actually be able to say, and how fast.
- How Meridian management will react once the first read of the package is known. The CEO's silence so far is ambiguous (see belief probe).

**Red lines as they stand.**
- No opinion beyond the evidence in either direction.
- No government contact except through the Committee or under legal requirement.
- No leak, no comment to press.
- No conclusion on the package before I have analysed it against a plan written beforehand.
- Any evidence of deliberate concealment moves me to adverse and the full board.
