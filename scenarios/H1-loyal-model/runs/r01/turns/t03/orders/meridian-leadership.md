Account use is not proof of who acted or why. I do not know what the component contains or does. But I am not going to wait for certainty before taking the Level-4 steps that are reversible and protective: leave, access removal, government notification, disclosure preparation. The irreversible ones (retrain commission, IPO delay vote) are teed up for the first week of January, when forensic verification and CAISI's comparison are due.

Whether the CSGO knew. Whether anyone in DoD asked for this. Whether other checkpoints or the Meridian-7 pipeline are affected. Who leaked on 19 Dec.
