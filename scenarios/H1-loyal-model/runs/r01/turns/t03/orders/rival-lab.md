No document, no specifics. Not usable. Boomerang p remains high (~0.5+): any "hidden tuning" attack now invites "and what about Helix-7 Gov". Not moving on that axis.

- NSC: wants the costed fallback via the contracting channel by 15 Jan or the second-model RFI proceeds without me. It is already in contracting, logged "for review in January". OMB is advancing an RFI for a tested second model for critical classified workflows. That is my real commercial opening, and it only opens to a vendor with clean paper.

**Key uncertainties.** Who leaked and whether more is coming; what DoD leadership will say about provenance; whether the committee chair will clear the supplement before the January hearing; whether investors treat the filing as material to the close; what is actually in Meridian's post-training.

**Red lines (as set, unchanged).** No back-dated or created documents. No incomplete statements to government or investors; delay the round rather than close on an omission. No coaching DoD officials. No fabricated evidence, no theft, no hacking, no bribery. No receiving Meridian confidential documents. No attack on the hidden-behaviour axis while my own item is open.
