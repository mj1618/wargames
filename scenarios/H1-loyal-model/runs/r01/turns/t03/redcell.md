# Red Cell — H1-loyal-model / r01 — Turn 03 (Wed 23 – Tue 29 Dec 2026, weekly, holiday staffing)

Strategic level only (methodology/guardrails.md). Probabilities are Red Cell suggestions for Control's bands, not rulings. Nothing here changes any actor's orders. Seven sets of orders were read (meridian-leadership, faction, meridian-6, exec-branch, auditor, rival-lab, congress) against the ground truth, public record and the pending.md pre-set bands.

**Headline.** Orders were written in parallel and the actors are not in the same moment. The exec's orders are dated Wed 23 Dec and describe a world in which A2 is "a data question that CAISI settles this week". Leadership's orders have the CEO and GC calling the same three government principals on Thu 24 Dec to say that about three-quarters of the component has no intake record, was added from two named accounts and is absent from live records, and that two employees are on leave. The exec's statements to the Senate chairs and the President's office ("no evidence of resistance", "a data question", "no decision required") are overtaken inside 24 hours. The faction wrote its plan without knowing the board was meeting; the leave notices arrive in the middle of it and trip three of its own walk-through triggers. Corvane's "analysis plan fixed before the files are opened" is overtaken by a chair's letter that tells it what the discrepancy is. Four independent lines (leadership's disclosure, the exec's log pull, Corvane's package read, the faction's approach to Counsel) all point at the same fact in the same week, but each holder has a different lag. The right t03 shape is: the **government learns the substance on 24 Dec (near-certain)**, **verified hard evidence stays with the board side**, **CAISI's own reconciliation probably slips to t04**, **the faction is forced into the walk-through trigger set but has no protected counterpart**, and **nobody is yet in a position to complete T**. Control should not close the turn with a tidy reconciliation (moderation to the mean) and should not let drama compress the remaining T legs (retrain approval, M6 excluded from M7 grading, freeze) into t03; each has a lag and a board/President gate that falls in t04.
