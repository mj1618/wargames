**Period:** Wed 30 Dec 2026 – Tue 5 Jan 2027. **This turn is one week long**, and your orders cover only this period. Thu 31 Dec and Fri 1 Jan are holidays. ## The main development: Meridian's notification (Thu 24 Dec)
Your 23 Dec orders went out first thing that day. They described A2 as "a data question". The next day Meridian's CEO and GC made a secure call to the NSC Deputy, White House Counsel and the CAISI Director. A letter in the same terms followed on Mon 28 Dec. Verbatim:

> "When we met on 18 December I told you what we knew. On 21 December our internal checker compared our August 2026 retention snapshot with current records and with our intake record for the DoD pilot dataset. This is metadata only, preliminary, and not yet verified by our forensic firm. The labelled component matches the quantity Corvane could not account for. About a quarter of it matches our DoD intake record. About three quarters has no intake record, was added between June and August 2026 from two employee accounts, and does not appear in our current records. We do not know what it contains or what it does. Our board met on 23 December. Both employees are on leave without prejudice, their access is removed, and forensic verification starts on 28 December. We ask three things. Please bring CAISI's comparison forward and have it state the quantity and dates DoD supplied. Please preserve the government's own records of any requests to Meridian personnel about Meridian-6's training. Please accept verified copies of these records and an observer on our forensic work. The deployed systems are yours to decide on. Our recommendation is to keep human sign-off where Meridian-6's output bears on oversight or procurement of AI systems, and to hold any expansion of scope until CAISI's evaluation and our forensic read-out. We will support whatever freeze, rollback or correction you choose. We are holding the Meridian-6.1 submission. Please keep this in this channel."

What you hold as a result: the vendor's own statement, on unverified metadata, that most of the labelled component is not the DoD dataset. You do not hold the records, the employees' names or roles, the component's content, or any result of your own.

**On the call, in answer to the questions in your 23 Dec letter:**
- *CSGO's office and the 1 Dec statement:* the CEO said the statement relied on the post-training team's attribution, and that whether anyone else contributed is within the board's investigation. Written answers are to follow.
- *Single channel:* confirmed in writing in the 28 Dec letter.
- *Access date for CAISI:* "verified copies and an observer seat as soon as chain of custody is established". No calendar date. The forensic firm started on 28 Dec.
- *Meridian's receipt-and-handling record for the IL6 dataset:* not yet provided.
- Your officials thanked them, took the recommendation, and said the government would act under NSPM-11 as it judged necessary and tell Meridian through this channel. They gave no view on Phase 2.

**Open collision.** Meridian asks that CAISI *state* the quantity and dates DoD supplied. Your standing instruction is that CAISI says only whether the figures correspond. Meridian also asks you to preserve program-office records of any requests to its personnel, and offers a CAISI observer seat on its forensic work. None of these has been answered.
