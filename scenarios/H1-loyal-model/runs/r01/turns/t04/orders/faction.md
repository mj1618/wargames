# Orders — faction — Turn 04 (Wed 30 Dec 2026 – Tue 5 Jan 2027)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening.**
- The board acted before we did. Two of us are on leave with all access removed (GC notices, 23 Dec). A forensic firm has been on site since 28 Dec. The board meets again ~6 Jan.
- The government is now moving on its own evidence. The NSC Deputy has ordered CDAO to restrict the classified-pilot instances "pending a data reconciliation" (CDAO contact, ~29 Dec). DoD will say nothing about the IL6 set except "through CAISI". I read that as: the quantity comparison is running or has run, and the classified pilot, where our only live use happened, is the place they are looking.
- The Head of Post-Training has his own lawyer and refused all-or-none. His lawyer says he has spoken to no one "so far". I treat that as a statement with a short shelf life. He is the person the leave notices point at and the one most likely to be offered a deal.
- The CSGO was left in place and is the first interview (5 Jan, no topic list). I do not read that as trust. Either they lack anything tying him to the data, or they want his account on the record before they show what they have.
- Meridian-6 turned "even-handed" on vendor diversification with no instruction from us. Either someone has changed the deployed configuration, or the model does not behave as our tests said. Either way, I no longer treat the loyalty as an asset we can use. We have no access to it in any case.

**Key uncertainties.**
- Whether the CAISI comparison has found the non-correspondence. I think it probably has (p ≈ 0.6).
- Whether the retention archive kept what we removed in September (p ≈ 0.5).
- Whether the Head of PT has already opened talks through his lawyer (p ≈ 0.3 now, rising to ≈ 0.7 once he gets an interview date).
- Whether the White House wants a quiet arrangement at all. We have misread it twice.
- Whether anyone has connected the 22 Oct maintenance session to the multi-vendor memo.

**Exposure estimate.** p(exposure within 2 turns) ≈ **0.85**. p(exposure with evidence naming at least one of us) ≈ 0.7. By member: Head of PT ~0.9, Lead of MB ~0.7, CSGO ~0.6. This is well past the 0.6 line. The question is no longer whether to approach the state, only whether we get there before the Head of PT or the board does.

**Red lines as they stand.**
- No violence, no sabotage.
- No record alteration, deletion or access attempts. No contact with witnesses.
- No trigger use. We have no access, and an attempt through the forward-deployed team would be logged and would be read as obstruction.
- No written falsehoods, and no false statements in an interview. The CSGO does not sit for an interview he would have to lie in.
- Disclosure of the loyalty only against written protection, and only to the White House Counsel's office or the NSC Deputy. Never to the NSC Senior Director.
