## 1. Reflection
**What I believe.**
- The binding constraint on everything Helix wants (close the raise, a credible fallback offer, a hearing posture) is now the board safety committee chair. She controls the supplement date. - DoD is silent and has given no written provenance account. The ~4 officials have counsel. This is both my only corroboration and my largest exposure. I will not coach, chase or characterise.
- Meridian: nothing new. Board split, outside counsel, legal hold (all secondhand), and a listing decision expected early January. Their trouble helps me only if I look like the vendor that is fixing its process, not the one adding to the noise. The 28 Dec analyst-note story about Meridian-6 and vendor diversification is the kind of story that comes from inside the federal pilot, not from me, and it is best left alone. Boomerang risk on any hidden-behaviour axis remains above 0.5. No attack.
- Hill: incoming House majority staff cite both stories and will call me alongside Meridian. They have not replied to my last message. They told me rigor earns credit; advocacy does not.

**Key uncertainties.** How long the chair takes after she has the account; whether the lead investor's revised terms are survivable; whether the leaker is on the investor side and will leak again; whether DoD leadership produces a provenance account before the hearing; what the CAISI results say about Helix-7 Gov.

**Red lines (unchanged).** No back-dated or created records. No incomplete statements to government or investors; I move the close rather than close with an omission. No Meridian documents. No coaching of DoD officials. No public statement about the committee's conclusions before she reaches them.
