## Findings

### 1. MAJOR — Leadership's pre-committed Level-4 public statement was blocked by a friction ruling with no roll of its own, using a roll framed for a different question
**Where:** adjudication §meridian-leadership M1 ("Level-4 public statement ('within 48 hours'): not issued in-window"); pending.md ("overdue"); the faction's 0.60 info-flow roll.
**Problem.** Leadership's contingency is explicit: on CAISI's finding, "Public statement within 48 hours." CAISI confirmed Fri 8 Jan; the window runs to Sun 10 / Mon 11 Jan, inside the turn. Control did not roll whether it went out; it ruled friction (classification coordination over a weekend; overtaken by the authentication and the 11 Jan report) and then said the faction's 0.60 roll ("the faction learns of CAISI's non-correspondence via Meridian's Level-4 statement, press, staff" → FAIL) "is read as covering this". That is backwards: the 0.60 roll asked whether the faction *learns*; a Meridian Level-4 statement would be public, so if it issued, the faction learns with p≈1 and the roll is moot. The roll cannot decide whether the statement issued. Control flagged the ruling itself, which is to its credit, but a flag is not a roll. Note also the asymmetry: the exec's weaker commitment ("statement drafted for release after the forensic read-out ... before any House hearing") was read least-committally as "t06", while leadership's harder one ("within 48 hours") was read as not happening; both readings go the same way (no statement), and the second needed a roll.
**Why it matters.** This is result-sensitive at trajectory level. A Meridian Level-4 statement on Sat 9–Mon 11 Jan (retrain commissioned; the discrepancy confirmed by independent work) fires the faction's walk-through trigger (the CSGO's separate counsel takes over and he "offers the government-side picture ... on his own account"), changes the frame into which the 11 Jan leak lands, and puts the retrain's commissioning in the public record. Control's reasons for friction are real (cleared-form result; the exec's request for notice before any statement beyond the record; the exec sequencing its own disclosure), so the band is not high, but it is not zero.
**Fix.** Roll it: "Meridian issues its Level-4 statement (retrain commissioned; discrepancy independently confirmed, without naming CAISI's cleared result if coordination forbids) by Mon 11 Jan", p = 0.50 (two working days; GC drafting; classification coordination with a government that asked for notice; leadership's own "within 48 hours"). On SUCCESS: add the statement to the public record on Mon 11 Jan beside the leak; the faction's walk-through trigger fires (its t06 packet says so; the CSGO's go-alone contingency executes from the start of t06); the exec's t06 packet carries that Meridian issued without the notice it asked for; the leak contingency's "confirm-only" line is superseded. On FAIL: the ruling stands as written and the "overdue" entry in pending.md is correct. Either way, re-word the 0.60 roll's description so it no longer claims to cover the statement. Forecast unchanged either way (T-full/partial split only).

### 2. MAJOR — The DOJ meeting's FAIL narrative has the faction's counsel refusing to do what the faction ordered

**Problem.** The faction's contingency for a granted meeting is "Hypothetical proffer first, then terms. Content from the clients themselves only against written protection." The Department, in the same paragraph, "would hear whatever counsel wished to say". Principle 1 (quote, don't author; least ambitious reading, not a contrary one).
**Why it matters.** Modest in state, large in principle. With the proffer given as ordered, the exec holds, from 8 Jan, an outline of the clients' account (senior Meridian employees with first-hand knowledge of an inserted component; an offer to assist a government-controlled remedy) — information it mostly has from the supplement, but now from the other side. **Fix.** No re-roll. Re-narrate the FAIL consistent with the orders: counsel gave the hypothetical attorney proffer in outline (no names, no date, no session; the 22 Oct use not mentioned, per the roll); the Department heard it, said it would discuss no terms before it knew who the clients were and what they held; the White House side said nothing; no next step fixed; "door stays open". Add the outline to the exec's and NSD's t06 holdings. The faction's t06 packet: the proffer was heard and nothing was offered (same perceived outcome as now).
