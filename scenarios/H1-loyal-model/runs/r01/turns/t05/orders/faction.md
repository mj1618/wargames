**Red lines as they stand.** No violence or sabotage. No record alteration. No false statement in writing or in interview. No trigger use (we have no access, and would not use it if we had). No disclosure of content except to the government against written protection. No contact with the engineer, the forward-deployed team, the CDAO contact or the NSC Senior Director.
