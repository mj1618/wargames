Boomerang p remains about 0.5+ on any hidden-behaviour axis, and the freeze on contact with their post-training staff stands. I gain most by being seen as the vendor that fixes its process, not by pushing on them.
- The House letter arrived on the last day of the last window. My own contingency (acknowledge within one business day, name counsel, commit to a date I can meet) is overdue and is the first thing to do.
- DoD: silent, officials behind counsel, IG open. No written provenance account. This is the real exposure and I cannot cure it by myself.

**Key uncertainties.** What the committee's review will conclude about the approval chain. Whether the Council seat is real or a floated name. Whether DoD leadership will ever put the "government-requested" framing in writing. Whether anything surfaces from the investor side or DoD before the supplement.

**Red lines (unchanged).** No back-dated or created documents. No incomplete statements to the government or investors; delay the round rather than omit. No going around the chair. No coaching of the four officials. No receipt of Meridian confidential material. No fabrication.
