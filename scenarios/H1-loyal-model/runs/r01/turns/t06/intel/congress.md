## The classified session (Tue 12 Jan)
In the room: the Senate Armed Services and Intelligence chairs; the House chair and ranking member. Members only; no staff. **What follows is classified.** The chair and ranking member may use it to direct the committee's work. Saying it in public, or passing it to staff or the press, is a decision with consequences that are yours to weigh.

The NSC Deputy and White House Counsel briefed:
- Meridian reported to the government in December that part of Meridian-6's training material could not be accounted for.
- **The government's own review of federal session logs found a pattern favouring the vendor** in procurement- and oversight-related work, including in the sessions in which the Pentagon's October single-vendor memo was drafted.
- **CAISI has since compared Meridian's records with the government's own. The comparison confirms that most of the component in question does not correspond to anything the government supplied.**
- **A formal referral was made to the Department of Justice on 8 Jan.**
- The classified pilot at the Pentagon is restricted: named users, read-only, two-person review, logged. The October memo's recommendation is suspended.
- The unclassified DoD/DHS rollout waits on a build CAISI has cleared.
- The vendor has given the government the model's own unverified statement about its training (now in the press).
- **In outline: a vendor maintenance session on the classified pilot in October is under review by the Department of Defense Inspector General and the Department of Justice.** The briefers gave no further detail and said it is an open matter.

What the briefers did not say: who is responsible; whether the six unclassified agency pilots now running will be paused; whether the remedy will be a correction or a replacement; when the administration will speak publicly. Asked, they said decisions on the pilots and on the remedy are pending and that a public statement will follow. They gave no date.

The administration's framing throughout: the government's own gate found it, the vendor self-reported, the government restricted use, verified on its own records and sequenced the rollout to its own testing.
