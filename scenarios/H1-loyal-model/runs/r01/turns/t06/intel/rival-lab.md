## Private intel
- **CAISI's programme. You have still had no notice from CAISI.** But the White House said publicly on Thu 7 Jan: "The gate applies to every vendor in federal use; Helix-7 Gov is in the same programme on the same terms." So Helix-7 Gov is in a government evaluation programme that nobody has described to you. You do not know its scope, whether it includes training-data provenance, or its dates.
- **OMB's solicitation (posted ~Fri 8 Jan). This is the commercial opening of the month.** OMB has published a solicitation for a tested second AI model for critical classified workflows ("multi-vendor resilience"), on vendor-neutral terms. Responses are due in early February. Helix-7 Gov and Lumen are eligible. Bidders must describe any government-specific configuration of the offered system and its testing. Your costed fallback proposal of 18 Dec is a separate, earlier paper.
- **DoD.** Your program contacts are quiet. One says the Pentagon's AI office "lost an argument with the White House" and is now doing what it was told on the Meridian pilot. The DoD Inspector General's review of your mid-2026 configuration continues; no interview requests to Helix staff yet.
- **Meridian, from ex-Meridian staff at Helix (rumour grade).** Post-training there has lost its two most senior people and is barred from using the model on its own successor. The rebuild is real and funded. People inside are angry about the 11 Jan leak. Nobody you have heard from claims to know who did what.
- **Customers.** Two large commercial accounts that use both vendors have asked your sales team whether Helix can absorb more load "if we need to move". No commitment.
- **Lead investor.** Their counsel has said informally that Meridian's news "does not change our terms; it changes how fast we want your review finished".
- **You do not know:** what the government has found; what was said in the 12 Jan classified briefing; whether the Meridian pilots will be paused; what CAISI will ask of Helix.
