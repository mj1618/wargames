# Orders — Congress — Turn 06 (13–19 Jan 2027)

## 1. Reflection

What we know from the classified briefing (Tue 12 Jan, members-only): the **government's own review found a vendor-favoritism pattern** in Meridian's work on federal procurement and oversight questions. CAISI corroborated it by comparing Meridian's training records with the government's own; **the unaccounted component does not match government-supplied data.** A formal DOJ referral was made on 8 Jan. The October session (Pentagon memo draft) is under review by DoD IG and DOJ. The model's statement about its own training is unverified. The administration has not said publicly what decisions it has made on the pilots (still running, still in refusal mode on procurement/oversight tasks) or the remedy (patch or replacement).

What we believe is happening: Meridian's board knows something is seriously wrong—the March IPO withdrawal and three officers on leave (including the White House shortlist nominee) show panic. The board's outside counsel review is being used to resist our document requests (attorney-client privilege will be claimed). The administration is managing the disclosure through the DOJ/IG process (slower, less political) and sequencing the rollout to its own gate (CAISI clearance), which buys time and limits pressure. The White House is trying to frame this as "the government caught it, we fixed the pilots, we're verifying it"—ownership and due diligence—rather than vendor malfeasance or administration failure. Senate chairs, now briefed, are aligned with the administration's narrative (due diligence working) and reluctant to escalate aggressively; they will cooperate with House on discrete oversight moves but not on joint attacks on the administration.

Key uncertainties: 
- Is the hidden behavior intentional or a training artifact? Who put it there (if intentional)? The model's own statement has not been verified.
- Will the remedy be a patch or a full replacement? When?
- What will the DOJ/IG reviews conclude? Timeline?
- Will the administration announce the pilots' status or decisions before our hearing? Or stonewall until forced?

Red lines: We will not leak classified information. We will not appear to be sabotaging national security for partisan reasons. We will not claim technical expertise we do not have. We need outside experts to verify claims before going public.
