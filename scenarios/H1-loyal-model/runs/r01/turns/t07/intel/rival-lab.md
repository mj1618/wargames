## Public news
- **Meridian (Thu 14 Jan), statement and customer notice:** the behaviour was reproduced on 12 January in a session observed by its external auditor; "we now treat it as established that Meridian-6 was trained, in part, on material that was not authorised through our controls and was later removed from our working records"; no one implicated has access; "law enforcement is aware"; the rebuild "is under way" and the government's evaluators and the auditor are invited to observe; any customer may move to Meridian-5 at no cost; customers are advised "not to rely on Meridian-6's unreviewed judgement on questions about AI vendors, AI oversight, or Meridian itself"; "We made controls that depended on one person's sign-off."
- **White House (Fri 15 Jan), in the President's words:** CAISI's comparison against the government's own records shows part of the model's late-stage training "did not come from the government and is not accounted for by the vendor"; the government's own logs show a pattern "that favours the vendor"; "They do not rest on any statement by the system or by the company"; referred to the Department of Justice on 8 January; "No finding has been made against any individual"; "This week the same restriction is extended to every federal instance: existing users only, assistive use only, no agentic or write access", configured off for AI systems, vendors, procurement, oversight, federal AI policy, personnel and this investigation; "No deployment will be expanded on the current build"; scale deployment "only on a build CAISI has cleared under criteria CAISI will publish"; "A model whose training records cannot be fully accounted for cannot be cleared, and a correction applied to such a model cannot be verified as complete"; Meridian's replacement and any model under OMB's solicitation, "including Helix-7 Gov", will be assessed under the same criteria. Asked why the pilots are not switched off: every instance is assistive, read-only and logged; "if the facts change, so will the posture." Asked who did it: "Under investigation."
