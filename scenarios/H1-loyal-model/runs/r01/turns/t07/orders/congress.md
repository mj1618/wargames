# Orders — Congress — Turn 07 (20–26 Jan 2027)

## 1. Reflection

**Beliefs based on intel and journal:**
What has changed since T06: the White House disclosed on Fri 15 Jan that CAISI found unaccounted training records and a pattern in Meridian-6 favoring the vendor, both from government sources (not the model's self-report). This disclosure strengthens Congress's hand—we now have government evidence, not just vendor evasion. Meridian itself advised customers to consider leaving, cancelling its IPO and putting three officers on leave; this credibility collapse is new. The President's statement says the model's use is restricted everywhere, but our intel says GSA switched off on its own and we don't know if the other four agencies applied the restriction. Senate chairs are satisfied with the President's response and won't join our hearing, but this is a manageable split if we frame our role as verification, not partisanship.

**Key uncertainties:**
- What exactly did Meridian's personnel do and when? We have a pattern but no mechanism or intent yet. The three officers on leave are the key—none has come forward.
- Are the five federal pilots actually restricted as the President stated, or is GSA the only agency that acted? We need to know what Meridian's answers reveal.
- Will CAISI publish its criteria before the hearing? The silence since the Friday statement is suspicious; either CAISI is still drafting or the White House is delaying to avoid committing to a standard that rules out Meridian-5 as interim.
- Has the Department of Justice opened anything beyond a referral? The White House said "no finding against any individual" but we don't know if that's true or a statement of intent.

**Red lines:**
- House majority: will not wait forever for DOJ; if Meridian's answers are evasive, we subpoena the three officers and their records.
- Senate majority: will not join public attacks on the administration; will cooperate on "due diligence" and "verification" framing, but not on "sabotage" language.
- Both: will not leak classified information; will protect any whistleblower who comes forward.
