# Orders — Executive Branch — Turn 07 (Wed 20 Jan – Tue 26 Jan 2027)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening.** The President took every recommendation on 13 Jan and said it in his own words on 15 Jan; the standard is now public and it is his. But two of his sentences are not yet true. "This week the same restriction is extended to every federal instance … configured not to operate" — no instance has been configured; the directive binds as a rule only (intel §Results 1(a)). "Criteria CAISI will publish" — not written, fourth week on the same two cleared staff. The hearing chronology will show both, and the press is already asking "has it been applied at each agency". The gap between the President's words and the facts on the ground is now my biggest exposure, bigger than anything Meridian can do to me this week.

The fact question has moved from "what" to "who". Meridian's own maintenance records put the 22 October session on the classified pilot under the access of the Lead of Model Behavior (intel §Results, action 2). Two of the three officers have been heard under proffer and their accounts cover that session and agree with the match; the former CSGO is heard Thursday. My contingency for this case runs as written: NSD leads, nothing from us to Meridian, referral supplemented, Senate chairs told in outline. I do not know what the two have said about scope, method or each other, and NSD has asked me for nothing. That is as it should be.

One collision is coming that nobody has planned for. The House asked Meridian on 15 Jan to describe "all access by Meridian personnel to federal instances since June 2026, including maintenance sessions", answers due Thu 21 Jan. Meridian will compile that from the same records it gave us on 19 Jan. Either Meridian notices the October entry itself while compiling, or the committee receives it on Thursday, or both. NSD's hold on telling Meridian is about to be overtaken by Meridian's own production to Congress. I owe NSD that fact today, and I owe the committee a classification review of material about a classified system that is routine and must not look like a stall.

Meridian is cooperating at every step I can see (intel §Private): it told its customers to consider leaving a day before I spoke, offered Meridian-5 twice, produced the records complete and on time, confirmed Meridian-6 has no role in Meridian-7, invited observers to a rebuild I have not staffed. The base checkpoint shows no self-continuity signals above control; the rebuild runs 10–14 weeks from 16 Jan. The hawks and CDAO have stopped fighting the standard and now fight on time, and on time they have a point: ten to fourteen weeks with nothing cleared and nothing decided about an interim is a real capability gap and a real hearing question. Meridian-5 has never been tested for any of this, and the two people most likely to have altered Meridian-6 were in post when Meridian-5 was made. So it is a candidate, not an answer, and the government environment that stood up last week and has run nothing is the place to find out.

CAISI is asked this week for six things and can do two. The criteria are an unclassified document; the two cleared staff are not needed to write them. Observing a configuration session is not evaluation; IG staff can do it. The detailees are blocked by a reimbursable-detail agreement Commerce has not signed — paperwork, not policy — and that is something OMB can force in a day.

**Key uncertainties.** What the three have told the Department; whether the model's account of itself is complete; whether Meridian has seen the October entry; whether Meridian-5 is clean; whether the sessions actually complete by Friday at five sites with the only qualified team; whether the House leaks the match.

**Red lines as they stand.** Nothing on the October session to Meridian or Congress without NSD. No advice on the remedy from the system under review or from the lawyer's clients. No patch to the current build. No expansion on the current build. The President's words made true by Friday, by configuration or by pause.
