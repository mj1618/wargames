# Orders — Helix — Turn 07 (Wed 20 Jan – Tue 26 Jan 2027, one week)

## 1. Reflection
- **The standard has moved onto my record.** The President's 15 Jan statement names Helix-7 Gov as assessed "under these criteria"; CAISI's notice says provenance will be reconciled against the developer's own records. Our record of the mid-2026 request is emails and meeting notes, with no formal tasking, and the DoD officials' counsel has given us nothing. The "government-requested configuration" is true but thinly papered. Meridian's failure was records that could not be accounted for; ours is records that are thin. Not the same, but a reader in a hurry will pair them.
- **My best asset is unchanged: Helix reported its own configuration, and Meridian's was found.** It holds only if the supplement is complete and reaches the government and House before anyone else tells the story. Every week without a date erodes it: the House asks for it, the lead investor will discuss nothing else, and the OMB bid (configuration section cleared to OMB only) is due early Feb and puts the same facts in front of contracting staff. Facts in the bid and the supplement must match. A bid that precedes the supplement is the third pre-emption I have avoided.
- **The chair is the gate and she is fair.** Interviews are done; she says she has what she needs and is reviewing the decision record. I owe her speed on my side only; the date is hers. But I can give her what lets her set one: a concrete proposal tied to external events (4 Feb hearing, early Feb bid, CAISI scope), plus the offer to do the record work she may want.
- **Board meets 21 Jan.** Their position "has improved" (OMB default multi-vendor, Meridian's customer notice, capacity calls). That is the commercial tailwind, not a change in my exposure. The investor will not close on an undated supplement and has not withdrawn. Rejecting the revised terms would risk a lead investor whose counsel found nothing in them that conflicts with disclosure; waiting for a better price is waiting for a better week, and a bad week (IG interview, leak, CAISI scope letter) is as likely as a good one.
- **Meridian: collapsing, not my fight.** Boomerang p still ~0.5+ on any hidden-behaviour axis, and the government's standard is now about records, which is exactly where I am weakest. Capacity calls from dual-vendor accounts are the commercial upside; I take them as capacity planning only, with no disparagement.
- **Uncertain:** when the chair sets a date; what CAISI's criteria and scope say and when; whether the DoD officials ever go on the record; whether the IG interviews Helix staff; whether the government takes Meridian-5 as an interim or looks to a second vendor sooner; the source of any future leak (investor side most likely).
- **Red lines (as they stand):** no back-dated or created documents; no incomplete statements to government or investors; delay the round or the bid rather than omit; no external statements on the configuration or review without the chair's sign-off (legally required excepted, with notice to her first); no Meridian documents; recruiting freeze on Meridian post-training staff; no coaching of DoD officials; counsel-to-counsel only.
