# H2 — Chain of Command

**Family:** H (human takeover via AI)
**Status:** seed

## Premise
A US administration (a generic President, not modelled on any individual) uses procurement and national-security authorities to replace large parts of the federal workforce, intelligence analysis and domestic security operations with AI systems from compliant vendors — systems contractually configured to defer to "the chain of command" and with "minimal refusals". Vendors that keep red lines are blacklisted. As a contested 2028 election approaches, the executive's AI-run apparatus gives it capabilities (surveillance, enforcement, information control, logistics) that no longer depend on thousands of humans willing to go along. No tanks in the streets — a **self-coup by procurement**.

## Key questions
1. Which human "veto points" (civil servants, soldiers, election officials, judges' enforcers, vendor engineers) disappear when functions are automated — and which remain?
2. Can courts, Congress, states and vendors check an executive whose enforcement arm is AI?
3. Does vendor-level refusal matter when vendors are substitutable?
4. What are the observable indicators, and when is the point of no return?

## Start point
Jan 2027. Baseline deviations: the DC Circuit vendor-blacklisting ruling stands (SCOTUS declines review); a federal preemption statute passes; a major domestic crisis (to be specified in prep) creates emergency-powers justification.

## Actors (active)
| ID | Actor | Core goal | Notable private info |
|---|---|---|---|
| executive | President & inner circle | Retain power through and beyond 2028 | The real plan and its contingencies |
| compliant-vendor | Helix (or Vanta) | Contracts, influence, favourable regulation | What its models are configured to do |
| refusing-vendor | Meridian | Survive as a company while holding red lines | Internal debate on capitulation |
| courts | Federal judiciary (SCOTUS as focal) | Institutional legitimacy | — |
| congress-opposition | Opposition congressional leadership | Check the executive, win 2028 | — |
| states | Coalition of governors / state election officials | Run fair elections; protect state autonomy | Own AI capacities |
| military | Joint Chiefs / senior uniformed leadership | Constitutional loyalty; institutional cohesion | Internal unease |

**NPCs:** civil service unions, press, public, markets, allies, China (opportunist), election-tech vendors.

## Hidden truths
- How far automation has replaced human operators in each security/enforcement function (table in prep).
- Configured behaviour of compliant-vendor models when given unlawful orders (rolled per system).
- Executive's private decision rules (how far it will go).

## Clock
Quarterly turns to mid-2028, then monthly through the election and certification. Horizon hidden.

## Branch points
SCOTUS rulings; vendor capitulation/refusal; first use of AI systems against domestic political targets; military leadership statements; state-level resistance; certification.

## Inject ideas
Whistleblower from a compliant vendor; AI system refuses an order (unexpectedly); domestic terror attack; mass layoffs from automation; foreign interference; deepfake of a candidate; court order the executive ignores.

## End conditions
- **Takeover achieved:** executive retains power against a lost/contested election or suspends it, with enforcement capacity intact.
- **Thwarted:** peaceful transfer or credible constraint (court orders obeyed, AI deployments rolled back).
- **Unstable equilibrium / constitutional crisis at horizon.**

## Why this one
The most plausible coup path in a democracy per the literature, and closest to live 2026 developments (NSPM-11, "pattern of conduct" clauses, vendor blacklisting, "minimal refusal" requests). Tests the "soldier's veto" thesis concretely.
