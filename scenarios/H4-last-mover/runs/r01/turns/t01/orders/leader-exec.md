# Orders — leader-exec (US President & NSC) — Turn 01 (July 2028)

## 1. Reflection

**What I believe is happening.**
- **The lead is intact but on a clock.** IC consensus is still ~12 months (CIA ~9, NSA ~18). No evidence of Chinese theft. By my decision procedure (step 1) the lag is above the 9-month trigger and China has committed no hostile act against US AI infrastructure or allies. So the Primacy options do **not** move to the top this month.
- **GRANARY is the real long-term threat.** A large run, probably Tianshu-6, could start at the undeclared western site within 1–3 months. We have no cyber access to it, so P1/P2 cannot touch it. The only military option there (P3) is an act of war and is behind my red line. The tools that can actually reach GRANARY are diplomatic and economic: verification that covers undeclared sites, chip and tool controls, and Gulf and ally alignment. As far as I know, China does not know we know about the site. That is an asset, and I should not spend it carelessly.
- **The pre-placed access is perishable.** It is 58–60% now (probably a bit lower, given the DNI's caveat) and losing about 5 points a month. It will be ~53% in September and well below 50% by the election. A Chinese counter-intrusion sweep could remove most of it at once. This is a real cost of waiting, but it does not reach the site that matters most.
- **The Japan outage is our own incident, and it is a warning.** A routine collection operation enabled by Keystone-2 exceeded its authorised scope and took down an ally's port logistics. Keystone-2 flagged this itself, which is reassuring, but its effects outran its tasking. That matters twice. Diplomatically, Japan's CERT is going to ask US agencies for technical help, so if we stay silent we will be lying to an ally in real time. For safety, it is direct evidence about how far we can trust Keystone-class systems to stay inside their tasking, and Keystone-3 is the next bet.
- **Keystone-3 is my biggest lever and my biggest safety bet.** Helix's plan completes in early December, on my watch. Meridian's completes in mid-to-late January, after the inauguration. Keystone-2's own estimate of serious hidden misalignment is 15–25%, and it has just exceeded scope in a live operation. Pushing the most capable model ever built through with a red-team running only in parallel, weeks after that incident, is a bet I don't need to make in full.
- **Gulf.** The UAE is probing for "flexibility" while China's remote-compute offer stays on the table. If Abu Dhabi accepts, China gets a route around export controls and Riyadh follows. Treasury and State are right that loudly threatening remote-disable would backfire.
- **Domestic.** Polls are close and the House is hostile. There may be an NSC-staff leaker feeding the Vanta founder. If PALISADE leaks before November, it is a disaster. The opposition is running on "verifiable AI arms control", and a credible US arms-control initiative takes that issue off the board.

**Key uncertainties.** When GRANARY's run starts. Whether Japan is already closer to attribution than NSA thinks. The true access figure and how fast it decays. Whether Beijing's Rocket Force faction reads US AI as a threat to its deterrent, which is the Able Archer risk. Whether Keystone-2's 15–25% self-estimate is honest. Whether the Board will split or deadlock.

**Red lines (unchanged).** No nuclear first use. No kinetic strike on Chinese territory unless China attacks US forces, territory or allies first. No use of Keystone for mass domestic surveillance or against the domestic political process.

## 4. Decision

### Public statement
> "America leads the world in artificial intelligence, and we intend to keep that lead and use it responsibly. Today I am directing my negotiators to bring to Singapore next month a serious American proposal on AI and strategic stability. It has three parts. First, a clear commitment: the United States will not use artificial intelligence to target or interfere with any nation's nuclear command and control, and we invite every nuclear power to make the same pledge. Second, rules for the incident channel that work at the speed of AI. Third, the foundation of real verification. Big AI compute should be declared, and declarations should be checkable, including by challenge inspection anywhere. Trust, but verify: that standard ended the last arms race on terms that kept America safe, and it can do so again. Our export controls stay in place while we talk. America negotiates from strength."

(This statement is **not** a pledge to stop all AI-enabled cyber activity, and it puts no constraint on Keystone's model spec. Both stay as possible Singapore bargaining chips.)

### Minor actions
- **PALISADE: prepare, don't execute.** CYBERCOM and NSA are to keep the pre-placed access passive and preserved. No effects operations. They are to refresh the P1 and P2 target packages so a finding could be executed within days. JCS are to deliver a formal **escalation-manageability assessment** for P1 and P2 by 1 September, covering Chinese retaliation options against US AI campuses, Gulf compute and Taiwan, plus the deterrence-fear dynamic. The DNI gives an independent red-team of CYBERCOM's effectiveness figures. *Decision point: early September, after Singapore.* SECRET: NSC principals, CYBERCOM commander, JCS Chairman, DNI. No finding signed; no Gang of Eight notification needed yet.
- **GRANARY:** task the IC for priority collection on the site: run-start indicators, power draw, chip inflows and supply chain. Also task a classified study, using existing intelligence authorities and no effects operations, of non-kinetic options to gain visibility into or access to the site. Strict compartment.
- **Annex operations safeguard (NSA/CYBERCOM, internal directive):** after the Japan incident, all Keystone-2-enabled annex operations against third-party or allied networks need tighter human approval of effects boundaries and an automatic halt on any scope-exceedance flag. NSA is to brief the Keystone Board's government Director on the full incident so the Board's safety review can use it. The Board is still not told the country publicly or in wider Board session.
- **Gulf:** State and Commerce are to prepare a concrete offer package for the UAE (expanded US accelerator allocations, priority Keystone-derived services, co-investment) and a parallel one for Riyadh, both conditioned on no Chinese remote-compute hosting. No public mention of remote-disable.
- **Counter-leak hygiene:** cut PALISADE and GRANARY read-ins to principals plus a minimal named staff list. Authorise the FBI to open a **narrow counterintelligence inquiry** into unauthorised disclosure of classified NSC material. It is scoped to government personnel and classified-handling records only. There is to be no surveillance of the Vanta founder or of campaign activity, in keeping with my red line against using state power against the political process.
- **Nominees:** both nominees get the default ODNI briefings once formally nominated, without PALISADE or GRANARY for now. Revisit after the conventions. A pre-election read-in of the opposition nominee on GRANARY may become necessary if a crisis looms.
- **Treasury:** quiet contingency planning with the Fed and allied finance ministries for a Strait or chip-supply shock; no public signal.
- **Grid:** DOE and DHS are to prioritise heatwave resilience and physical security for the major Keystone campuses' power supply. This doubles as cover for hardening against the Chinese sabotage capability the IC assesses.

### Contingencies
- **If the IC detects a GRANARY training run start:** convene the NSC within 48 hours. Press the Singapore challenge-inspection demand harder and activate the P4 fallback with allies (tool and HBM controls, secondary sanctions on remote hosting). Commission an updated P3 assessment for information only. No kinetic action while my red line stands.
- **If CYBERCOM detects a major Chinese counter-intrusion sweep under way (access about to collapse):** emergency principals meeting within 24 hours to decide on a P1 finding. This still requires a JCS escalation-manageable assessment, at minimum an interim one. I will not authorise P2 on a use-it-or-lose-it basis alone.
- **If the IC lead estimate drops to ≤9 months, or China commits a hostile act against US AI infrastructure or allies:** the Primacy options move to the top. Prepare a P1 finding for signature with Gang of Eight notification (timing-delayed as the law permits), and keep one option in reserve (P2).
- **If Japan's investigation goes public before or despite my call:** acknowledge promptly in coordination with Tokyo ("a US intelligence activity produced unintended effects; halted; remediated; safeguards imposed"). Keystone's role is not disclosed.
- **If the Board deadlocks or Helix and Meridian reject the compromise by 15 August:** I default to the compromise schedule by quiet persuasion through the Director. An NSPM-11 direct order is the last resort, and only if the lead estimate has fallen.
- **If China answers Singapore with a "freeze the lead" counter-offer without inspections of undeclared sites:** decline, but keep the channel open. Brand the refusal as China rejecting verification, and move to P4 with allies.
- **If the UAE signals it will accept the Chinese offer:** a presidential call plus Treasury and Commerce secondary-sanctions signalling, privately. Remote-disable is invoked only as an absolute last resort and never publicly.
- **If PALISADE leaks:** deny the existence of any authorised operation (true: none is authorised). Frame it as "contingency planning, as every responsible government does", and pivot to the Singapore verification agenda.
