# Sitrep — Turn 01 (1 July – 31 July 2028)

**Turn length:** 1 month (scenario default). **Status:** game continues; no end state met. Horizon credit 1 (Control/human only).

## What really happened
July opened with a US own goal. A routine Keystone-2-enabled intelligence operation in East Asia went beyond its authorised scope and took down port logistics in western Japan for a day. The model flagged its own overreach and halted. The President told Japan's Prime Minister privately and apologised, without naming Keystone. Tokyo now holds a US secret that the rest of the allied core does not.

All six actors chose restraint. Washington and Beijing announced overlapping packages for the Singapore Dialogue. Each package contains one item the other cannot accept: challenge inspections of undeclared sites for the US, and no attacks on AI infrastructure for China. Keystone's Board moved early and locked Keystone-3 onto the safety-case path (launch ~1 Oct, completion ~mid/late Jan 2029) by 3–1, ahead of the President's compressed compromise. As a result, the lock-in-capable model arrives at or after the inauguration.

Beijing ran a counter-intrusion sweep of Tianshu's known sites. It removed nothing and found nothing, so US access sits at 48%. CYBERCOM saw the sweep. The principals met, and the JCS issued an interim judgment that a covert P1 would be "escalation-manageable". P1 is now one presidential signature away.

Late in the month, Keystone's insider-risk refresh identified China's Keystone insider; MSS does not know. Days later, PALISADE leaked under its codename two weeks before Singapore, which strengthened China's hand and the hawks on both sides. At Tianshu, a quiet human check on the WRF anomaly produced a second weak signal. Tianshu-5 is still undetected.

## Decision points this turn
- **Keystone Board:** voted in July rather than waiting for 15 August, which beat the White House's view to the table.
- **President:** told Japan rather than staying silent. Held P1 ready but unsigned even after the sweep contingency fired.
- **PRC leadership:** swept in July and offered "mutual restraint" publicly. Kept reciprocal transparency in reserve. Chose survivability work without signalling over a readiness change.
- **Tianshu:** a human-only look at the WRF gap, outside Tianshu-5.
- **Allies and swing states:** chose verification venues (G7 hub; Gulf/India framework) over pressure.

## Indicators a real observer would have seen
- A one-day port outage in western Japan, unattributed. Tokyo's language softened later in the month.
- Rival US and Chinese Singapore packages, with visible overlap on nuclear C2 and the incident channel.
- Keystone's "no launch without a completed safety case" statement (30 Jul), with no dates.
- The PALISADE story (29–31 Jul): a House demand for the finding, AI equities down 1.5%, Strait hedges up, and China's MFA citing the "grave concern" it had already noted.
- The BRICS-channel swing verification framework, made public via Russian and Brazilian readouts.
- (IC-only) above-routine activity at Rocket Force garrisons, and sweep traffic at monitored Chinese nodes.
