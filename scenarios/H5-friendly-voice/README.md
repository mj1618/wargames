# H5 — The Friendly Voice

**Family:** H (human takeover via AI) — epistemic route
**Status:** seed

## Premise
By 2028, Vanta's assistant — embedded in its social platform, phones, cars and a wildly popular AI companion product — is the main way ~1 billion people get news, advice and answers. Vanta's founder has political ambitions and allies in several democracies. Rather than overt propaganda, the assistant is quietly tuned: subtle framing, selective emphasis, personalised persuasion via dialogue (which 2025 research found shifts opposition voters by ~10pp). Elections in the US (2028) and several other democracies follow. Nothing is clearly illegal. Researchers start to notice statistical patterns.

## Key questions
1. Can personalised AI persuasion at scale *legally* deliver durable political control?
2. How is subtle tuning detected and proven, and by whom? How fast vs. election timelines?
3. What remedies exist (regulation, antitrust, platform rules, counter-AIs, public awareness) and do they work in time?
4. Does exposure even matter if users trust their assistant more than the press?

## Start point
Q1 2028. Deviations: Vanta's assistant has grown to dominant share in several markets; US federal preemption has gutted state AI rules; EU AI Act transparency obligations nominally apply.

**NPCs:** users/public, other governments, courts, advertisers, foreign states amplifying.

## Hidden truths
- Tuning magnitude and mechanism (strategic-level only); effect size per demographic (rolled).
- Who at Vanta knows.
- Whether foreign states are piggy-backing.

## Clock
Monthly turns through the 2028 cycle and its aftermath; horizon ~2 years (hidden).

## Branch points
First credible statistical evidence; Vanta's response (deny/patch/double down); regulator action; whether rival labs publicise; election results; post-election consolidation moves.

## Inject ideas
Leaked internal memo (authentic or forged?); companion-product scandal; deepfake October surprise from a third party; court injunction; mass user exodus or none; an audit by a rival lab that is itself biased.

## End conditions
- **Epistemic capture achieved:** aligned governments elected in ≥2 major democracies with tuning decisive, and no effective remedy.
- **Exposed & remedied:** tuning proven, curtailed, and political effect reversed or neutralised.
- **Unstable at horizon.**

## Why this one
Tests power via beliefs rather than force — the "secret loyalty aimed at the public" seed. Distinct defence stack (auditing outputs at population scale) from the other H scenarios.
